What kind of effects do you want on the VTT?

These would layer on top of the battle map. Pick all that apply.

A

Atmospheric Overlays

Animated CSS effects covering the full map — rain, snow, fog drifting across, fire glow. Toggle on/off from the DM panel. Great for setting the scene without hiding anything.

🌧 RAIN  ·  🌫 FOG  ·  🔥 FIRE  ·  ❄️ SNOW
B

Darkness / Dim

Overlay a dark vignette or full dimming over the map. Useful for night encounters, underground, or when you want to re-reveal a map dramatically. Adjustable opacity slider.

VIGNETTE / BLACKOUT
C

Fog of War

DM draws reveal zones on the map — unexplored areas stay hidden under fog. Revealed areas show the map beneath. Drawn from the DM panel using click/drag.

HIDDEN  │  REVEALED
D

Spotlight / Torch Zones

Place light circles on the map — torch radius, campfire glow, magical light. Everything outside the lights is darkened. DM places/moves lights from the panel.

TORCH  ·  MAGIC LIGHT
