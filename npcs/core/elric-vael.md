## Profile

**Appearance:** Tall, thin, always slightly hunched over a desk or a book; he wears reading glasses he forgets are on his head when not in use.

**Voice:** Measured and precise, with long pauses while he decides whether the next sentence is worth saying.

**Signature line:** "I've noted this before, actually. The second time a pattern appears, it's information. The third time, it's a warning."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The historical record of every documented threat the Wardens have faced in Northreach; the folklore and arcane theory underpinning most of the Season 1 mysteries; how the current incidents map onto older catastrophe patterns.
- **Wants:** To understand the larger pattern before it completes — he is increasingly certain that the Season 1 events are not isolated, and the urgency of that belief is the first strong emotion he allows himself to show.
- **Secret:** He has decoded enough to know that a convergence event is coming, but his confidence in the timeline is shaky. He has been presenting his certainty as higher than it is to motivate Brenna to act faster. He is afraid that if he admits the uncertainty, she will slow down.

## Season 1: Background Analyst

Elric is the party's primary lore resource — the person they bring strange objects, odd symbols, or recovered texts to. He works slowly and carefully, but his answers are always useful. Play him as someone who asks more questions than he answers, not out of evasiveness but because he genuinely wants to understand before he speaks.

He should have one or two Season 1 moments where his pattern-recognition visibly unsettles him — a brief crack in the calm, quickly smoothed over. These are foreshadowing seeds.

He keeps meticulous notes and will reference things the party mentioned two sessions ago as if they were written down (because, for him, they were).

## Season 2+: Alarmed Scholar

The pattern converges and Elric's facade of detached analysis begins to slip. He will admit to the party (if they ask directly) that he has been understating his certainty. He becomes a more active presence — requesting field accompaniment, pushing for faster action, occasionally getting in his own way with analysis paralysis.
