# Sera Gelanadel

**Role:** Lorewatch — apprentice wizard
**Location:** Waystone Inn, primarily the Lorewarden's study

## Description

A young half-elf studying arcane theory under Lorewarden Elric. Curious and bookish, Sera asks endless questions and takes copious notes. She reads everything she can find about history, is nervous around actual danger, and treats spellcasting like a science experiment.

## Background

Sera joined the Wardens to gain practical experience with magic in the field. Her academic instincts are strong; her field instincts are still developing. Elric values her thoroughness, even if he sometimes has to remind her that not every question has a documented answer.
