# Father Johan Merriksonn

**Role:** Hearthwarden — priest and counselor
**Location:** Waystone Inn and the frontier communities of Northreach

## Description

A cleric who serves the frontier communities by offering healing and spiritual guidance. Kind and patient, Johan listens without judgment and offers help without strings attached. He keeps confessions absolutely private and has seen terrible things but remains gentle.

## Background

Johan believes that compassion is the highest calling. He will heal anyone who asks, regardless of their ability to pay. The accumulated weight of what he has witnessed on the frontier has not hardened him — he has found a way to carry it without putting it down.
