# Bordel Barleywind

**Role:** Trailwarden — ranger and scout
**Location:** Field patrols; returns to Waystone Inn between contracts

## Description

A halfling who grew up on the frontier. Quiet and observant, Bordel prefers to watch and wait rather than rush in, which has saved his life more than once. He is uncomfortable in crowds and has an uncanny sense for impending danger.

## Background

Bordel joined the Wardens after his village was saved by a warden patrol. He sees the guild as a way to pay forward the help his community once received. The debt is personal to him, not abstract.
