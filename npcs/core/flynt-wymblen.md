## Profile

![Flynt Wymblen](/portraits/FlyntWymblen.jpg) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** A gnome in perpetually mismatched clothes with an instrument case on his back and the look of someone who is already composing something about whatever is happening right now.

**Voice:** Quick, musical, and layered — he speaks in puns and asides, but if you listen to the actual words, the information is always real.

**Signature line:** "Don't mind me. I'm just the bard. Nobody ever minds the bard, which is incredibly useful for a man in my line of work."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The current gossip, rumor, and underground chatter of every tavern and waystation within a day's ride; which stories are being suppressed and by whom; the difference between a lie being circulated deliberately and one that grew organically.
- **Wants:** To be in the room when the song that will outlast everything gets written. He is genuinely courageous when it matters — not because he doesn't feel fear, but because good material requires proximity to danger.
- **Secret:** Several of his "performances" have been sanctioned intelligence operations for Brenna. He is more formally embedded in the Guild's covert work than his casual manner suggests. He keeps this compartmentalized because his value depends on no one taking him seriously.

## Season 1: Comic Relief & Prophecy Delivery

Flynt is the party's primary source of rumor-as-clue and tonal relief after heavy sessions. Use him to surface information that would be awkward to deliver directly, wrapped in a story or a song that the party might not parse immediately.

His prophecy-by-song delivery is a low-key foreshadowing tool — he can plant a refrain that lands differently once the party knows what it was pointing at.

Do not let him become pure comic relief. He should have one moment per arc where he sets the lute down and says something true without deflection. Those moments should land hard.
