# Flynt Wymblen

**Role:** Hearthwarden — bard and storyteller
**Location:** Waystone Inn; follows contracts that will make good material

## Description

A traveling bard who joined the Wardens after spending a winter at the Waystone Inn. Cheerful and chatty, Flynt collects rumors, songs, and stories like some people collect coins. He knows a song for every occasion, pays for information with entertainment, and turns warden contracts into ballads (with varying accuracy).

## Background

Flynt realized he preferred stories he could participate in to stories he merely observed. The Wardens offered both safety and an inexhaustible supply of material. His ballads are popular; their relationship to the actual events is creative.
