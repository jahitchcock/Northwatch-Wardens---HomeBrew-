## Profile

![Tillus Merrion](/portraits/TillusMerrion.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** A well-fed merchant of middle years with the practiced look of a man who is always calculating what the current situation is worth and to whom.

**Voice:** Reasonable and measured, with a slight emphasis on terms of agreement — he speaks in the language of deals even when the subject isn't a deal.

**Signature line:** "I'm not saying burn the forest. I'm saying that the cost of not burning the forest needs to be weighed against the cost of burning it, and someone needs to make that case clearly."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The economic impact of the wolf attacks on Welton's trade and livestock; which council members will follow his lead and which are wavering; how to frame a decision to make the aggressive option look like the pragmatic one.
- **Wants:** A resolution that protects Welton's commercial interests and his own standing on the council — which he believes aligns with a quick, decisive end to the wolf problem, regardless of nuance.
- **Secret:** He has already had preliminary conversations with a hunting company from the nearest city about a contract to clear the wolves. He has not formally authorized anything, but he has not discouraged them either. If the party's approach looks likely to fail or to take too long, he will quietly accelerate that.

## Season 1: Wolves of Welton — Political Obstacle

Tillus is the party's main source of institutional resistance to a diplomatic resolution. He is not a villain — he genuinely believes that efficiency and decisiveness are virtues and that sentiment about wolves is a luxury Welton cannot afford. He will obstruct, redirect, and outmaneuver if he can.

His leverage comes from his council position and his commercial contacts. The party can counter him with evidence (if the resolution they're pursuing demonstrably protects Welton's interests) or outmaneuver him through other council relationships.

He can also be used as a lever: if the party needs something fast and is willing to give him a win, he will deliver.
