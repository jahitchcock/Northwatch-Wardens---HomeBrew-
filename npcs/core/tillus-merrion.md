# Tillus Merrion

**Role:** Village council member
**Location:** Welton

## Description

A respected farmer who serves on Welton's council. Pragmatic and direct, Tillus represents the no-nonsense attitude common in frontier farming communities. He has farmed the same land for thirty years, doesn't believe in magic until it affects his crops, and takes village safety seriously.

## Background

Tillus speaks for the agricultural community and advocates for practical solutions. He is a reliable contact for the Wardens when working contracts near Welton — he won't waste their time and won't ask for more than he needs.
