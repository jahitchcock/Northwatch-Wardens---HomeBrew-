# Ariodh Highwhirl

**Role:** Lantern Guard — monk and observer
**Location:** Waystone Inn; field deployments

## Description

A refugee from distant lands. Quiet and watchful, Ariodh listens more than he speaks and acts with precision when violence is necessary. He fights without weapons and wins. He notices details others miss, and meditates in the coldest part of the morning.

## Background

Ariodh found work with the Wardens after proving his skills in an impromptu tavern brawl that he didn't start but did finish. He rarely discusses his past. Whatever drove him from his homeland, he has not left it entirely behind.
