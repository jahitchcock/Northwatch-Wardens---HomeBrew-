# Finethir Shinebright

**Role:** Lorewatch — eccentric wizard
**Location:** His tower west of the Waystone Inn (locals avoid it)

## Description

A gnome wizard of considerable power and questionable judgment. Brilliant but unpredictable, people respect his magical skill while being wary of his experiments. He has been seen talking to inanimate objects, owns a tower west of the Waystone Inn that locals avoid, and once turned a bandit into a sheep (the bandit reportedly deserved it).

## Background

Shinebright has a reputation for both brilliance and chaos — often simultaneously. The exact history of how he came to affiliate with the Wardens is unclear, possibly even to him. He is a useful resource when dealing with unusual magical phenomena, provided the cure is not worse than the problem.
