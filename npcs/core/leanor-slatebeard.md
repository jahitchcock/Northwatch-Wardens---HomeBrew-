# Leanor Slatebeard

**Role:** Tracker and hunter
**Location:** Welton; tracks predators through the surrounding wilderness

## Description

A dwarf who tracks predators for the village and keeps watch on animal movements near farmland. Skilled and cautious, Leanor doesn't take unnecessary risks and teaches others to do the same. She can track wolves through rain and snow, lost a brother to a winter bear years ago, and warns people about dangers they don't take seriously enough.

## Background

Leanor knows the wilderness around Welton better than anyone. The loss of her brother to a winter bear was not a lesson she chose to learn, but she has made use of it — her caution comes from experience, not timidity.
