# Galvena Aballon

**Role:** Lantern Guard — disciplined protector
**Location:** Waystone Inn; regular field deployments

## Description

Former city guard from a southern settlement. Serious and steadfast, Galvena takes her oaths literally and holds others to their word. She practices with her weapons every morning, doesn't drink on duty (which is most of the time), and will stand between danger and innocents without hesitation.

## Background

Galvena came north seeking a fresh start after her city fell to political corruption. She believes in order, structure, and protecting those who cannot protect themselves. The corruption she witnessed did not break her faith in institutions — it sharpened her personal code instead.
