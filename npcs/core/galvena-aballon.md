## Profile

![Galvena Aballon](/portraits/GalvenaAballon.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** A tall dwarf woman in immaculate armor with her holy symbol polished to a mirror finish; her posture is always exactly correct.

**Voice:** Even and formal, with a precision that suggests she has thought about what she is going to say before saying it.

**Signature line:** "I took an oath. Not to the outcome — to the conduct. Whatever happens after, I need to be able to stand behind what I did."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Healing protocols, field triage priorities, and the theological framework of several regional faiths — useful when dealing with temples or sacred sites; also knows which of the Wardens are likely to break under moral pressure and has been quietly shoring them up.
- **Wants:** To ensure that the Guild operates with integrity, not just effectiveness — she worries that pressure and expedience will erode the ethical standards that justify the Wardens' mandate.
- **Secret:** She was once assigned to a paladin order that disbanded under scandal — not her scandal, but she was there and said nothing when she could have. She transferred to the Wardens partly out of guilt and partly to build something she can actually believe in. The memory is a wound she doesn't pick at, but it shapes every hard call she makes.

## Season 1: Moral Compass & Field Healer

Galvena is the party's clearest voice for "how we do things matters as much as what we accomplish." She is not self-righteous — she will acknowledge when hard calls have no good answer — but she will name it when the party crosses a line, and she expects to be heard.

Use her as a steady, grounding presence in morally complex situations. She can provide healing and protection in the field, but her primary value is social: she represents the standard the Wardens are supposed to hold themselves to.

She takes the safety of civilians seriously to the point of overriding tactical considerations, which can create productive tension.

**Actions**

**Multiattack.** The knight makes two melee attacks.

**Greatsword.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) slashing damage.

**Heavy Crossbow.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +2 to hit, range 100/400 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d10) piercing damage.

**Leadership (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).** For 1 minute, the knight can utter a special command or warning whenever a nonhostile creature within 30 ft. that it can see makes an attack roll or saving throw. That creature can add a d4 to its roll if it can hear and understand the knight.

**Reactions**

**Parry.** The knight adds 2 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. The knight must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.
