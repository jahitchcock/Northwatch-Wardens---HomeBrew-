# Guz

**Role:** Lantern Guard — bruiser with a past
**Location:** Waystone Inn; field deployments

## Description

Guz doesn't talk about where he came from, and his accent suggests somewhere far south. He has old scars that tell old stories. Gruff and blunt, Guz has a short temper but an ironclad code — he protects the innocent, even when it costs him. He doesn't lie, even when it would be easier, and he respects strength but despises bullies.

## Background

He is trying to live better than he used to, and the Wardens gave him that chance. The specifics of his past remain his own.
