# The Dragonknights

**Role:** Ancient order of warriors
**Location:** Their temple in the northwest mountains

## Description

Little is known for certain about the Dragonknights beyond their temple in the northwest mountains. Heroic and mysterious, they represent an ideal that may or may not have existed as people remember it.

## Background

Local stories credit the Dragonknights with protecting the region from great evils in ages past. They fought alongside dragons, sealed something terrible beneath their temple, and their spirits may still guard the mountain passes — or so the stories say. The gap between legend and history is one the Wardens have not yet crossed.
