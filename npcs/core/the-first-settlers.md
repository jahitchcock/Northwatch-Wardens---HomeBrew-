# The First Settlers

**Role:** Founders of Northreach communities
**Location:** Throughout Northreach (historical presence)

## Description

The first humans, dwarves, and elves who settled in Northreach generations ago, whose descendants still live in the region. Tough and determined, the first settlers are remembered for surviving conditions that would have driven others away.

## Background

They came north seeking freedom from southern wars, made pacts with local communities already living here, and learned the hard way which places to avoid. Their legacy is less a set of specific stories than a cultural attitude: the frontier demands endurance, and the people who stayed are the proof of it.
