## Profile

![Elro Aldataur](/portraits/ElroAldataur.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** A weathered elf who looks like he has been cold for a long time and has made peace with it; there is a watchfulness about him that suggests he is always calculating what the worst case is and whether it is already happening.

**Voice:** Careful and tired — he chooses his words like a man who knows that the wrong ones can make things worse.

**Signature line:** "I'm asking you because I have no one else to ask. Please understand what that means before you decide."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The full scope of the sickness spreading through Palebank; which residents are affected and how quickly it progresses; the connection to Urgon's funeral and what it implies about the source; the limits of what the village can handle on its own.
- **Wants:** To stop the sickness before more people die, and to understand what is causing it before it spreads further. He needs the party to go where he cannot.
- **Secret:** He has already lost someone close to the sickness before the party arrives — not a character with a separate sheet, but someone personal enough that his professional calm is paper-thin. He will not show it unless the party creates a genuine moment of trust.

## Season 1: Frozen Sick — Quest-Giver & Protective Leader

Elro is the emotional anchor of the Frozen Sick arc's opening. His exhaustion and protectiveness are not weaknesses — they are the source of his urgency, and the party should feel the weight of what he is asking them to do.

He is the party's best source of confirmed information about the sickness and the most reliable person in Palebank. He cannot go himself, and this limitation should be played as frustrating to him, not just narratively convenient.

He will debrief the party after every significant discovery and his reactions — careful, always processing — are a good barometer for the arc's stakes.
