# Elro Aldataur

**Role:** Village leader and retired ranger
**Location:** Palebank Village

## Description

A wood elf who helped found Palebank Village decades ago. Weathered and wise, Elro still watches over the community with the same vigilance he once used in the wilderness. He has seen winters that would break lesser folk and leads with quiet authority.

## Background

Elro led the defense of Palebank during multiple crises. He knows every resident by name and trusts his instincts about trouble. His age and experience give him a perspective on the region's dangers that few others have — he remembers threats that younger residents only know as stories.
