# Aurixean Valignaak

**Role:** Lorewatch — sorcerer
**Location:** Waystone Inn; insists on being at the front during deployments

## Description

A brass dragonborn whose magical talents manifested during puberty. Dramatic and confident, Aurixean treats every mission like a performance and insists on being in the front. They breathe lightning when angry (or excited, or bored), refer to themselves in the third person sometimes, and are actually skilled despite the theatrics.

## Background

Aurixean joined the Wardens for "adventure, glory, and a chance to prove draconic superiority" — their words. The theatrics are genuine, not a pose, which makes them both endearing and exhausting depending on the day.
