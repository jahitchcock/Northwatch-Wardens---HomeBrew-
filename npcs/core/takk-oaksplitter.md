# Takk Oaksplitter

**Role:** Lantern Guard — front-line fighter
**Location:** Waystone Inn; first to volunteer for dangerous contracts

## Description

A half-orc who earned his name by splitting a tree trunk with a single axe blow during a logging competition. Loud, loyal, and quick to laugh, Takk is usually the first to volunteer for dangerous work and the last to leave a fight.

## Background

Takk found his calling with the Wardens after years of drifting between jobs. He is strong enough to carry an injured person for miles and has never met a problem he didn't think could be solved with direct action. He is terrible at cards but plays anyway.
