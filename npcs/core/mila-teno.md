# Mila Teno

**Role:** Trailwarden — rookie scout
**Location:** Waystone Inn; volunteers for every available patrol

## Description

Fresh from her training, Mila is eager to prove herself. She volunteered for every patrol until the guild leadership told her to pace herself. Enthusiastic and green, her energy is both an asset and a liability — she learns fast but sometimes rushes ahead.

## Background

Mila volunteered for the Frostwatch (Palebank's frontier militia) before joining the Wardens. She is trying very hard to be taken seriously and has yet to face something truly dangerous alone.
