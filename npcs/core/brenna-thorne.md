## Profile

**Appearance:** A lean, weathered woman with close-cropped grey-brown hair and a jaw that looks like it was set in iron; the scar bisecting her left eyebrow is the first thing anyone notices.

**Voice:** Clipped, low, and unhurried — she never raises her voice because she never needs to.

**Signature line:** "I don't want a good plan. I want a plan that survives contact with something going wrong."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The full threat assessment behind every mission she assigns; the political pressures bearing on the Guild from Northreach's council; which wardens are burning out.
- **Wants:** To keep her people alive and the Guild's mandate intact without compromising either for the other.
- **Secret:** She approved a mission two years ago that got four wardens killed because she trusted an informant she shouldn't have. She has never spoken of it. The informant still operates in the region under a different name.

## Season 1: Field Commander

Brenna is the party's primary authority figure and assignment giver. Play her as someone who respects competence and distrusts cleverness for its own sake. She will push back on creative solutions that introduce unpredictable variables. She is not cold — she genuinely cares about the wardens — but she won't show it until trust is earned.

Use her as a pressure valve: when the party succeeds, her approval is meaningful. When they cut corners, her disappointment has weight. She will never apologize for a hard call, but she will acknowledge it.

She is quietly aware that something larger is moving behind the Season 1 threats. She does not share this until she has evidence, and even then she'll frame it as a working hypothesis, not certainty.

## Season 2+: Strained Commander

The pattern Elric has been tracking is no longer deniable. Brenna begins accepting outside help she would have refused in Season 1, and the party may catch her in a rare moment of doubt. The informant from her past resurfaces as a plot thread — if the party ever learns what happened, her authority with them depends entirely on how she handles the confrontation.
