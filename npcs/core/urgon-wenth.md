# Urgon Wenth

**Role:** Explorer
**Location:** Palebank Village

## Description

A dwarf who spent a year exploring the ruins of the Far North before returning to Palebank Village. Brave and ambitious, locals remember Urgon as someone who took risks to provide for the community.

## Background

Urgon brought back several artifacts from his expedition into the ruins of the Far North. His time there changed him in ways he doesn't always discuss. The artifacts he returned with attracted interest — and possibly attention — that has not fully settled.

## Reputation / What People Say

- Spent a year in the ruins of the Far North
- Brought back artifacts from his expedition
- Remembered as brave and willing to take risks for the community
