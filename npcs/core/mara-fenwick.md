## Profile

**Appearance:** Round-faced, always smiling, with ink-stained fingers and a ledger that seems to materialize from nowhere whenever numbers are discussed.

**Voice:** Warm and quick, with the practiced ease of someone who has talked their way through a dozen difficult conversations before breakfast.

**Signature line:** "Oh, I can get that. I know a person. Well — I know a person who knows a person. The important thing is it arrives."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The Guild's full supply chain, every vendor contact in Northreach, and the names of at least three fence operations the Wardens officially have no dealings with.
- **Wants:** To keep the Guild solvent and the wardens equipped without Brenna having to know how some of the sourcing works.
- **Secret:** She maintains a quiet relationship with a black-market supplier in the lower city — not for personal gain, but because legal channels can't always get what wardens need in the field fast enough. If it came out, it would be a crisis. She's made peace with that risk.

## Season 1: Quartermaster & Fixer

Mara handles all gear requests, resupply, and resource allocation. Play her as relentlessly upbeat without being naive — she knows exactly what the party is walking into and equips them accordingly. She is a useful information broker: she has heard things through her supply network that official channels don't carry.

She is one of the first NPCs who might treat the party as equals rather than probationers. Use her for warmth and levity, but let her occasionally signal that her cheerfulness is a professional tool as much as a personality trait.

Good scene hook: she is unusually curious about what the party brings back from ruins or unusual locations — she's always looking for new sourcing opportunities.

## Season 2+: Strained Resource Manager

The demands of a larger threat begin to strain the Guild's supply lines. Mara's black-market contacts become both more valuable and more dangerous. She may ask the party for a favor that sits in an ethical grey zone — and she'll ask it with the same bright smile she always uses.
