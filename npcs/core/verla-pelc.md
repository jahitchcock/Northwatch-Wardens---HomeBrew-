# Verla Pelc

**Role:** Merchant and collector — runs Pelc's Curiosities
**Location:** Palebank Village

## Description

An introverted elf who runs Pelc's Curiosities, trading in artifacts and relics from Eiselcross. Reclusive but knowledgeable, Pelc can identify almost any artifact that passes through their shop. They open the shop on their own schedule, will pay fair prices for genuine relics, and guard their privacy fiercely.

## Background

Pelc values objects more than social interaction. The shop is a useful resource for parties returning from Aeorian ruins, though getting useful conversation out of Pelc requires patience and, ideally, something interesting to show them.
