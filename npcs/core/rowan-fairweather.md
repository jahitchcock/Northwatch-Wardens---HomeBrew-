# Rowan Fairweather

**Role:** Trailwarden — druid and guide
**Location:** Field patrols and wilderness routes; Waystone Inn between contracts

## Description

Rowan speaks for the land in a region where most folk see nature as something to survive rather than understand. Calm and practical, they offer advice in terms of natural balance — not preachy, but persistent. They can read weather patterns with unsettling accuracy and treat injured animals with the same care as injured people.

## Background

Rowan joined the Wardens to help people navigate the frontier without destroying it. They use their druidic knowledge not as a philosophy lecture but as a practical survival toolkit, and they expect others to engage with it on those terms.
