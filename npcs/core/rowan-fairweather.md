## Profile

![Rowan Fairweather](/portraits/RowanFairweather.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** A slight human in their early thirties with sun-bleached hair and a cloak that always has something living on it — a beetle, a bit of moss, once a small finch that nobody mentioned.

**Voice:** Unhurried and gently wondering, as though they are hearing interesting things for the first time in most conversations.

**Signature line:** "The forest has been wrong here for about three weeks. I don't mean dangerous — wrong. Like it's waiting for something."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The natural and spiritual health of Northreach's wilderness; where the land's patterns have shifted, sickened, or gone quiet in ways that precede larger disturbances; a working relationship with several druidic circles that have no formal Guild ties.
- **Wants:** To keep the wild places of Northreach intact while the Wardens do their work — they are quietly protective of nonhuman communities and ecosystems that get caught in the Guild's missions.
- **Secret:** Rowan has received a message through the Druidic network warning of something moving in the deep wilderness that predates the current season's threats. They are sitting on it while they decide who to tell and how much to reveal — not out of distrust, but because the warning was cryptic and they don't want to cause a panic over something they don't fully understand yet.

## Season 1: Wilderness Guide & Natural Oracle

Rowan is invaluable for wilderness travel and for reading the health of the land around mission sites. Use them to telegraph that something is wrong before the party sees direct evidence — their unease is always meaningful, even if they can't explain it yet.

They are a gentle presence at the Waystone who can defuse tense moments without forcing resolution. They are also willing to advocate for nonhuman NPCs (wolves, fey, creatures the party might dismiss as monsters) when the party's instinct is to fight first.

They work especially well in the Wolves of Welton arc as a voice for the awakened wolves' perspective.
