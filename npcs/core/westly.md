# Westly

**Role:** Shepherd
**Location:** Westly's Farm, on the western edge of Welton's territory

## Description

A human shepherd whose farm sits on the western edge of Welton's territory. Hardworking and isolated, Westly manages his farm with minimal help and deals with problems on his own. He raises sheep and goats, farms in one of the more dangerous areas, and recently had trouble with wolves.

## Background

Westly's livelihood depends on protecting his flock from predators. He is self-reliant by necessity — the farm is remote enough that help is rarely close when trouble arrives. His wolf troubles are a potential entry point for Warden involvement.
