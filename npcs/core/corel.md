# Corel

**Role:** Trailwarden — veteran tracker and wilderness guide
**Location:** Northreach wilderness (rarely indoors); returns to Waystone Inn between contracts

## Description

Corel has spent more time in Northreach's wilderness than in its settlements. Originally from a nomadic trading family, he learned tracking from his parents and expanded his skills through years of hard experience. Dependable and laconic, Corel doesn't waste words, and when he speaks about the wilderness, people listen.

## Background

His upbringing with a nomadic family gave him an early education in reading land, weather, and animal behaviour. The years since have hardened those instincts into something close to instinct — he trusts the land before he trusts people.

## Reputation / What People Say

- Can track anything that leaves a mark
- Prefers sleeping outdoors to sleeping indoors
- Has survived situations that would have killed lesser scouts
