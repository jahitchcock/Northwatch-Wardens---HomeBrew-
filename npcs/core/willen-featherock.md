# Willen Featherock

**Role:** Hearthwarden — shepherd and survivor
**Location:** Waystone Inn; field support

## Description

Nervous but earnest, Willen struggles with confidence but pushes through fear when others need help. He still has nightmares about what attacked his sheep, is surprisingly good at first aid, and tries harder than anyone to be brave.

## Background

A former shepherd whose flock was attacked by something he couldn't explain. Willen survived and joined the Wardens to help others avoid his fate. He is not a natural fighter and knows it, but the memory of his helplessness that night is a stronger motivation than courage.
