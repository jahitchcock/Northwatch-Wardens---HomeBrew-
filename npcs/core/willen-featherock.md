## Profile

![Willen Featherock](/portraits/WillenFeatherock.jpg) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** A young human man who flinches at sudden movement and keeps his back to walls when indoors; he was broader before — something has been eating at him from inside.

**Voice:** Hesitant and circling, like someone who knows what he wants to say and keeps deciding it sounds too strange.

**Signature line:** "It looked at me. Not like a wolf looks at you. Like — it was deciding something. About me."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** First-hand account of a wolf attack that doesn't match any normal wolf behavior; specific details about what the wolves did and didn't do that only a direct witness would notice; the names of the other shepherds who were present at various incidents.
- **Wants:** To be believed. He has been telling this story for weeks and the village keeps softening it into something more manageable.
- **Secret:** He ran. When the wolves came and behaved strangely, the other shepherd who was with him froze and Willen ran. The other shepherd was injured. Willen has not told anyone which decision was his, and the guilt is compounding his trauma.

## Season 1: Wolves of Welton — Ordinary Witness

Willen is the "ordinary person" perspective the party needs to ground the Wolves arc in human stakes. His testimony is the clearest first-hand account of the awakened wolves' behavior, and getting him to tell it fully requires patience and the sense that he won't be judged.

If the party creates space for his guilt to surface, the story becomes more complete — and Willen becomes more useful as an ally rather than just a witness. He will help with a plan that treats the wolves as intelligent creatures rather than monsters to be killed.

He is not heroic, but he is honest when he trusts someone. That is worth a lot.
