## Profile

![Tulgi Lutan](/portraits/TulgiLutan.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** A compact, dark-haired human woman with the particular tension of someone holding themselves very carefully together; she keeps her hands busy when she's not sure what to say.

**Voice:** Defensive and clipped at first contact; warmer if the party demonstrates they are not there to accuse her.

**Signature line:** "I'm doing what I can. I would do more if I knew what I was dealing with. If you figure that out, come back and tell me."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The full medical picture of the sickness — symptoms, progression, what helps and doesn't; which victims deteriorated fastest and what they had in common; more about the source than she has told Elro.
- **Wants:** To save the people she can save, and for the investigation to produce something she can actually treat.
- **Secret:** She received a sample of the contaminated substance weeks ago and ran tests on it — she knows more about its arcane-alchemical nature than anyone else in Palebank. She has not shared this with Elro because she obtained the sample from Verla Pelc under circumstances that would implicate Verla and create a conflict she doesn't know how to manage. She is sitting on the information while she decides what to do.

## Season 1: Frozen Sick — Medical Resource & Reluctant Suspect

Tulgi's defensiveness makes her read as a suspect when she is actually an ally who is protecting someone else. Perceptive parties will pick up on this quickly. Pressure makes her more closed; genuine partnership and evidence that the party can handle complexity opens her.

Her information about the substance is the key that unlocks the next phase of the investigation. How the party earns it — and how they handle the Verla implication that comes with it — determines the arc's social texture.

She is one of the few characters who should remain an ally regardless of how the arc resolves, because Palebank still needs its healer.
