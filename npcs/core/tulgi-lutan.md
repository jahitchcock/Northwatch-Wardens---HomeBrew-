# Tulgi Lutan

**Role:** Trapper
**Location:** Palebank Village; trapping grounds in the surrounding wilderness

## Description

A mountain dwarf who makes a living trapping in the wilderness around Palebank. Gruff and self-sufficient, Tulgi doesn't ask for help and doesn't offer it unless pressed. She is one of the best trappers in the region, keeps to herself, and has a sister named Hulil whom she rarely mentions.

## Background

Tulgi is known for her independence and her refusal to rely on others. Her skill in the wilderness is respected, but she is not someone who invites familiarity.
