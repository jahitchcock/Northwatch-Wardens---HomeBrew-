# Baleth Cindermoon

**Role:** Lorewatch — charming warlock
**Location:** Waystone Inn; attached to field contracts he finds interesting

## Description

An elf with an easy smile and magic that doesn't quite feel like normal spellcasting. Charming and evasive, Baleth deflects questions about his patron with jokes and subject changes. He never seems to run out of magical energy, always knows more than he's saying, and people trust him despite not quite trusting him.

## Background

Baleth claims his powers come from "a mutually beneficial arrangement" and declines to elaborate. The nature of his patron is unknown to the Wardens formally, though Elric has noted that his magic has no arcane signature he can classify.
