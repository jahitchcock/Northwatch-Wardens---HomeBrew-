# Baleth Cindermoon

## Profile

![Baleth Cindermoon](/portraits/BalethCindermoon.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** An elf with an easy smile and magic that doesn't quite feel like normal spellcasting — the kind of person who can make you feel like the most interesting person in the room without revealing anything about himself.

**Voice:** Warm, unhurried, and slightly too smooth; he tells you just enough to keep you from asking the next question.

**Signature line:** "It's a mutually beneficial arrangement. Aren't all the best things?"

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** More about eldritch patron structures than a field warden should; which members of the Guild are likely to ask too many questions and which can be usefully distracted; where several old magical agreements sit dormant and why.
- **Wants:** To keep his arrangement quiet while extracting maximum value from his Guild position — not maliciously, but with the practised self-interest of someone who has learned that transparency is expensive.
- **Secret:** His patron is not a standard warlock patron. The "mutually beneficial arrangement" is with a specific entity that has an interest in Northreach — and that interest predates the Wardens' existence. Elric has noted that his magic has no arcane signature he can classify. Baleth would prefer that investigation stops there.
