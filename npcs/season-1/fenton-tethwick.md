## Profile

**Appearance:** A lean, mid-thirties human with the look of a man who has been useful to a lot of different people and is still deciding whether that was a good idea.

**Voice:** Careful and adaptive — he mirrors the register of whoever he's talking to faster than most people notice.

**Signature line:** "I don't have a side in this. I just know things that some people on both sides would find interesting."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Fragments of information about several of the other Welton residents' private affairs; the location of at least one thing in Welton that is not supposed to be there; what happened the night of the worst wolf incident, from a vantage point no one knows he had.
- **Wants:** To stay useful enough that no one has a reason to look too closely at his own situation.
- **Secret:** He has been doing small favors for Tillus Merrion — nothing overtly harmful, mostly information sharing — in exchange for Tillus overlooking a minor debt. He is not proud of it and is aware he is being used. He will cooperate with the party if they offer him a cleaner out.

## Season 1: Wolves of Welton — Flexible Lever

Fenton is a DM resource for filling gaps in the investigation. He works as a reluctant witness (he saw something and needs to be convinced it's safe to say so), a rival (if someone in the party rubs him wrong early), or a low-stakes ally who can provide access or confirm a detail the party couldn't get another way.

His connection to Tillus is available as leverage in either direction — the party can use it against Tillus, or Tillus can use Fenton against the party.

Deploy as needed; don't force him into every scene.
