## Profile

**Appearance:** A heavyset older man with the particular authority of someone who has lived in one place long enough that his opinions about it carry weight without him needing to justify them.

**Voice:** Deliberate, with long beats before conclusions — he has opinions and he wants you to feel the full weight of how long he has held them.

**Signature line:** "I've lived here forty-three years. The forest has never been what people say it is, and it hasn't started now."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The multi-generational context of Welton's relationship with the surrounding wilderness; old stories about past unusual animal behavior that the younger villagers don't know; the unspoken rules of community life that predate the current council.
- **Wants:** For Welton to handle this problem the way Welton has always handled problems — internally, without outside interference changing the shape of the place.
- **Secret:** He is more frightened than he appears. He has never seen wolves behave this way in forty-three years, and the thing he is not saying is that this particular pattern matches a story his grandfather told him once, dismissively, as a legend.

## Season 1: Wolves of Welton — Community Voice

Arl is the grounded, skeptical "town voice" who gives the party a sense of what the village actually thinks beneath the fear and the politics. He is not welcoming of outside intervention but he will respect competence and directness when he encounters it.

His grandfather's legend is a DM tool — deploy it when the party needs a folkloric reference point or when you want to suggest that this situation has history.
