## Profile

**Appearance:** A broad, weathered human man with a northern accent and the easy stillness of someone used to being the most dangerous person in the room — until the day he wasn't. Studded leather worn soft with use; a scimitar and a brace of daggers.

**Voice:** Blunt and transactional, but not cruel. He talks like a man running a business in a place with no law, because that's exactly what he is.

**Signature line:** "We thought they were collector's items. I swear on my mother's grave — we didn't know."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The icefield route to Salsvault and what lies in its outer chambers; the identity of the relic dealer who hired him to retrieve "anything glowing blue" from the ruins; that one of his own crew (Sett) is now dying the same way as the man in Palebank.
- **Wants:** Coin, and his crew alive. The first he can usually get; the second has just become a problem he can't fight his way out of.
- **Secret:** He had no idea the vials were deadly. The guilt lands hard once he connects them to the sickness — and once he looks at Sett. He'll trade everything he knows for any chance to save her.

## Season 1: Frozen Sick (Croaker Cave) — Morally Grey Fence

Brynn is the cure path's human gate, not a boss fight. He won't attack first, calls for surrender at 50% casualties, and cooperates fully if the party offers to cure Sett. He is the living thread that connects the Palebank vials to the northern supply line and to [The Buyer](npcs/season-1/the-buyer.md).

Play him as cold and hungry rather than evil. If the party handles him with anything other than violence, he becomes a genuine (if disreputable) source — drawing maps, naming his employer, and owing the party a favor if Sett survives. He is statted in full in the Croaker Cave scene.
