## Profile

**Appearance:** An adult silver dragon — enormous, beautiful, and unsettling in the way that things are unsettling when they are clearly more intelligent than the situation assumes; her scales catch light like polished steel.

**Voice:** Measured and formal in the way a scholar is formal — she chooses words precisely and speaks in complete sentences that have clearly been composed before delivery.

**Signature line:** "You may call me Rorn. My full name is given to those who earn it. I have not yet determined whether you have."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The Pinebrook situation in full, from a vantage point the town guard lacks; the history of the region extending back further than any human record; the capabilities and likely intentions of any significant threat in the area.
- **Wants:** To protect the wyrmling in her care and to resolve the Pinebrook situation without violence — but she will not sacrifice either if forced to choose between them. She is willing to negotiate, but she is operating from a position of genuine power and expects that to be acknowledged.
- **Secret:** The wyrmling is not her offspring but she treats it as if it is. The actual parent trusted her with it before dying. She has not told anyone this because she judges it irrelevant to the current negotiation, but it explains the depth of her protective instinct.

## Season 1: Peril in Pinebrook — Intelligent Negotiating Party

Rorn is the most powerful NPC the party will encounter in Season 1, and she should feel like it — not through aggression but through the quality of her attention. She knows more than she lets on, asks better questions than most people expect, and evaluates the party's answers carefully.

She is willing to help with the Pinebrook situation if approached correctly. The correct approach is: honest, direct, respectful of her intelligence, and not presuming that she will simply do what the party needs because it is convenient.

She is a potential long-term contact of significant value. The nature of that relationship depends entirely on whether the party earns "Rorn" — and whether they ever learn what that means.

## Season 2+: Occasional Ally

Rorn appears rarely and always purposefully. If the party earned her respect, they have a contact of enormous capability who will assist in situations of genuine significance — but she is not a cavalry call. She may also bring the wyrmling (now somewhat older) into later arcs as a hook.
