## Profile

**Appearance:** A compact halfling with trail-worn leathers and a habit of standing with his back to the nearest wall, watching the room.

**Voice:** Sparse and direct — he gives you exactly the words you need and stops.

**Signature line:** "I scouted it. Three exits, two guards, one dog. Dog's the problem."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The terrain and patrol patterns of most of Northreach's wilderness approaches; which trails are watched and which aren't; how long it takes to get anywhere on foot in any season.
- **Wants:** To be assigned field work, not garrison duty. He respects Brenna but chafes under the Guild's increasing paperwork overhead.
- **Secret:** He has been running unofficial reconnaissance on a location that wasn't part of any Warden assignment — something he noticed on a routine patrol that he hasn't reported yet because he doesn't know what to make of it.

## Season 1: Steady Scout

Bordel is a reliable source of pre-mission intelligence and a competent field backup if the party needs a fifth on a dangerous assignment. He is not interested in conversation for its own sake, but he will talk freely about terrain, creature behavior, and tactical assessment.

He is a useful contrast to more talkative NPCs: scenes at the Waystone that include him tend to get quieter and more focused. Play him as the warden who already knows the answer to the question everyone else is still asking.

His unreported location becomes a useful mid-season hook or foreshadowing element at DM's discretion.
