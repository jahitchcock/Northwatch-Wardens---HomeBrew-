## Profile

**Appearance:** Described by those who knew him: a mountain of a dwarf with a laugh you could hear from the other side of the village; the kind of person whose absence makes a place feel smaller.

**Voice:** (From accounts) Loud and certain — he said what he thought and expected you to say the same back.

**Signature line:** (From accounts) "If it's worth worrying about, it's worth doing something about. What are we doing?"

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** N/A — deceased at arc start. His knowledge is the investigation: what he handled, where he got it, and who else touched it.
- **Wants:** N/A — but his community wants the same thing he would have wanted: an answer, and action.
- **Secret:** He knew the object he brought back from his last trading run was unusual — he showed it to at least one other person before the sickness started. That person is either already sick or too frightened to come forward.

## Season 1: Frozen Sick — Funeral Hook & Community Keystone

Urgon is dead at the start of the arc and his death is the inciting incident. He is present in the arc through the grief of the people who knew him, the investigation of what killed him, and the occasional sense that the community is still organized around the space he occupied.

Use him to establish stakes through character rather than exposition — the party understanding that a specific, beloved person died makes the sickness real in a way that a number count doesn't.

His last trading run and its contents are the investigation's first thread. The person he showed the object to is a discoverable witness; their reluctance to come forward is a scene the party can navigate.
