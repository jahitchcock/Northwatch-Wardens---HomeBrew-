## Profile

![portrait](/portraits/OrvoMustave.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** A young gnome with the look of someone who has been frightened for long enough that it has become his resting expression; he keeps his pack on even when seated, as if leaving may become necessary at any moment.

**Voice:** Fast and slightly too loud, with the cadence of someone who has been alone with the same frightening information for too long.

**Signature line:** "You need to understand — this can't wait. The window is not — I've seen what happens when you wait and I am telling you it cannot wait."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The most recent state of Salsvault from direct observation; specific details about what is in there that the party should know before they go in; the urgency of the timeline in a way that no one who hasn't been inside would.
- **Wants:** To get out and stay out, and to make sure someone with the capability to do something about it actually goes in and does it.
- **Secret:** He left someone behind inside Salsvault. Not intentionally — there was no choice, or there was no choice that didn't require more courage than he had in that moment. He is managing his urgency partly through genuine concern and partly through guilt-driven need to see someone go back in so that it means something that he got out.

## Season 1: Frozen Sick (Salsvault) — Urgency Engine

Orvo's primary function is to make the Salsvault situation feel immediate. Use him to feed the party time pressure and concrete stakes before they enter the ruin. His information should be accurate but filtered through fear, which means the party gets the right facts in a somewhat scrambled order.

His guilt about the person he left behind is a quiet secondary thread — it doesn't need to become a full plot beat, but it should be visible in how he talks about what's inside.
