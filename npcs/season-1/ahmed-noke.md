## Profile

**Appearance:** A slight human man who is clearly aware that he does not look threatening and has decided to make that into a weapon — dressed carefully, posture controlled, every element of presentation calibrated.

**Voice:** Precise and condescending, with a specific quality of patience that implies he is humoring you.

**Signature line:** "I want you to understand that I am not afraid of you. I want you to think about why that is."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The full workings of his tower and the defenses within it; the specific capabilities of the polymorphed prisoners and why they were polymorphed; the value and function of the item he stole from Shinebright.
- **Wants:** To be left alone to pursue his work — he genuinely believes his research justifies the methods, and he finds the party's intrusion an irritating interruption of something important.
- **Secret:** He is terrified of Shinebright and always has been. The polymorph was not cruelty — it was the only way he could ensure the competition was neutralized before Shinebright realized what was happening. His entire presentation of fearlessness is load-bearing. The right question, asked the right way, can crack it.

## Season 1: Wild Sheep Chase — Petty-Genius Antagonist

Noke is a tonal chameleon: he can be played as pathetic (a self-important minor wizard who overreached) or genuinely unsettling (a man with no moral floor for "acceptable research methods" who happens to be competent). The party's approach will determine which register he falls into.

His fear of Shinebright is the crack in the presentation. The party may not discover it, but if they do, it changes every interaction they've had with him.

He should not be disposable even if defeated — the item he stole, the research he was doing, and the question of what he knows about Shinebright's capabilities are all threads with later value.
