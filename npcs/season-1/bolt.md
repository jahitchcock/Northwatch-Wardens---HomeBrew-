## Profile

![Bolt](https://i.imgur.com/4oD3lBF.jpg) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** A large grey wolf with pale amber eyes that hold still a moment longer than natural animal eyes do — there is something reading the situation behind them.

**Voice:** Speaks in short, careful sentences; has learned language the way someone does who finds the grammar of it mildly alien but is working hard at it because it matters.

**Signature line:** "We did not want this. We did not ask for this. But we cannot go back. So we ask: what is possible from here?"

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Exactly what Alexi did and how it affected the pack; the current state of the wolf territory and the internal divisions it has caused; the difference between what Flame wants and what Bolt believes the pack needs to survive.
- **Wants:** A stable existence for his pack that doesn't require ongoing conflict with humans — he is frightened of what Flame is becoming and afraid that without a diplomatic solution, the pack will be destroyed either by humans or by Flame's ambitions.
- **Secret:** The awakening is not complete and not fully stable. Bolt has moments where instinct overwhelms the new intelligence — moments of fear, of hunger, of territorial rage that the human-reasoning part of him has to fight back down. He does not tell the party this because he fears it will be used to argue against the pack's right to exist as they are.

## Season 1: Wolves of Welton — Diplomatic Nonhuman Ally

Bolt is the party's access point into the wolf perspective and the clearest voice for a nonviolent resolution. He will initiate contact carefully — he needs to assess whether the party is trustworthy before he exposes himself or the pack.

His relationship with Flame is the internal tension driving the arc. If the party can support Bolt's position within the pack, the diplomatic resolution becomes viable. If they undermine it (or if they deal with the humans in a way Bolt reads as bad faith), Flame's faction gains ground.

He is a recurring nonhuman ally with genuine depth. Treat him with the same respect you would a humanoid NPC who is trying to do right by his people.

## Season 2+: Established Ally or Lost to Flame

If the Welton arc resolved diplomatically, Bolt becomes a long-distance contact — the pack maintains its territory and Bolt occasionally has information the party can't get any other way. If Flame won, Bolt's fate is either tragic (dead, exiled) or complicated (still alive, still trying to hold the line from a weaker position).
