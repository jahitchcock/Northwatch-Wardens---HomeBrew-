## Profile

**Appearance:** A broad human man whose fervor shows in his posture — chest out, jaw set, the look of someone who has found something to believe in and is daring the world to argue with him about it.

**Voice:** Loud and declarative, with the specific cadence of someone who has rehearsed the answer to the question you're asking.

**Signature line:** "You don't understand what we're building here. Nobody does, until they see it completed."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The cult's local operation and personnel; the ideological framework of the cult's mission as he understands it; the location of cult resources within Croaker Cave.
- **Wants:** To prove his worth to the cult's patron — he knows Hulil Lutan looks down on him and he is trying to close the gap through zeal.
- **Secret:** He has doubts he cannot admit. Specifically, he has seen one person die from the sickness and it was not the abstract statistical harm the cult framed the operation as — it was a person, dying badly, and he watched it. He has been pushing the memory down with more zeal. If the party creates the right conditions, the memory is a crack they can work with.

## Season 1: Frozen Sick (Croaker Cave) — Breakable Lieutenant

Raegrin is the more manageable of the two Croaker Cave antagonists. He can crack under pressure — the right approach (confronting him with the human cost of what the cult is doing) can produce a confession, a surrender, or at minimum useful information.

He can also double down, in which case he is a dangerous and committed fighter who needs to be taken seriously in combat.

His dynamic with Hulil is worth playing: she doesn't respect him, he knows it, and the gap between them is a lever.
