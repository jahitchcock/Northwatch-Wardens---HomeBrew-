## Profile

![Alexi Merriksonn](https://i.imgur.com/UUuiBcg.jpg) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** Described by those who knew them: slight build, restless hands, always carrying something to read; the kind of person whose energy is bigger than their body and frequently gets them into trouble.

**Voice:** (If encountered) Rapid and self-correcting — they think faster than they speak and speak faster than they should.

**Signature line:** "I thought I understood what I was doing. I mostly understood what I was doing. The part I didn't understand — that's the problem."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** What actually happened with the wolves — the specific sorcerous accident that triggered the awakening, and how much of it was intentional versus runaway; the current state of their own transformation (if any).
- **Wants:** To fix it, if fixing is still possible. And if it isn't, to at least explain it to someone before the consequences fully land.
- **Secret:** The awakening was not entirely an accident. Alexi was trying to reach the wolves — to communicate, to befriend, out of a loneliness that the village never understood and Johan never fully addressed. The magic went further than intended. What Bolt and Flame have become is partly Alexi's doing and wholly Alexi's responsibility, and they know it.

## Season 1: Wolves of Welton — Mystery & Consequence

Alexi is the answer to the central mystery of the Wolves of Welton arc, but they are also a person — not a MacGuffin. If the party finds them, the scene should prioritize their humanity: the fear, the guilt, the complicated love of someone who caused a disaster while trying to do something genuine.

The resolution of the arc depends on what happens in this encounter. Alexi can help undo the awakening (if the party wants that), advocate for the wolves' right to exist as they are (if the party leans diplomatic), or simply explain enough for the party to make an informed choice.

Their survival past the arc and their relationship with Bolt and Flame are both ongoing hooks at DM's discretion.
