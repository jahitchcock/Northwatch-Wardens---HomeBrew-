## Profile

![portrait](/portraits/Dew.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** A cat-sized dragon in shifting iridescent colors — Dew is almost always the most visually striking thing in any scene they appear in, and they know it.

**Voice:** Lilting and playful, with occasional jarring drops into complete, quiet sincerity that land harder because of the contrast.

**Signature line:** "Oh, that's a very serious face. Did you want to discuss it? Or should I turn something blue first? I find blue helps."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Significantly more than they let on — faerie dragons of Dew's color are capable of sixth-level spell effects and have been around long enough to have observed a great deal; what they choose to share is always chosen.
- **Wants:** To be entertained, genuinely — but also to see how the party makes choices when things are strange. There is something being assessed here beneath the playfulness.
- **Secret:** Dew's association with The Buyer is older and more complex than "familiar." They have their own reasons for being in this situation, and those reasons are not fully aligned with The Buyer's agenda. Dew will not harm the party and will not let The Buyer harm the party, even if it means acting against their patron's instructions.

## Season 1: Frozen Sick (Salsvault) — Tonal Contrast & Sincere Ally

Dew is a deliberate tonal break in the Salsvault arc's heaviness. Use them to inject levity at precisely the moments the table needs it — and then let the sincerity moments land harder because of the contrast.

They are not to be dismissed. Their magic is real, their observations are sharp, and if the party is respectful without being servile, they will give the party things The Buyer would not.

Their moments of sudden sincerity should feel like a different entity surfacing briefly. Do not overuse this — one or two per encounter, and only when it matters.

**Magic Resistance.** The faerie dragon has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Spellcasting.** The faerie dragon is a 5th-level spellcaster (spell save DC 13, +5 to hit). Innate spells (no material components):

- At will: *dancing lights, mage hand, minor illusion*
- 1/day each: *faerie fire, hallucinatory terrain, mirror image, suggestion*

**Actions**

**Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 1 piercing damage.

**Euphoric Breath (Recharge 5–6).** The faerie dragon exhales a puff of euphoric gas in a 5-foot cone. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 11 Wisdom saving throw or be incapacitated for 1 minute. An incapacitated creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
