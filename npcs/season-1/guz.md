## Profile

**Appearance:** A large, broad ogre with small, careful eyes that don't match the rest of him — he looks like a wrecking ball trying to remember to use its inside voice.

**Voice:** Low and slow, with long pauses; he chooses words carefully because he knows he doesn't have many of them when it matters.

**Signature line:** "I don't want trouble. I never want trouble. Trouble just — it finds me. I think it's the size."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** How Noke's Tower operation worked from the inside; the names of a few other small-time operators in the region who use muscle-for-hire; what it feels like to be on the wrong side of something and know it.
- **Wants:** To be left alone to make better choices than he has been making. He is not looking for redemption as a concept — he just wants a situation where doing the right thing is also survivable.
- **Secret:** He did something on Noke's orders that he is not going to volunteer. It wasn't the worst thing Noke ever ordered, but it hurt someone who didn't deserve it, and Guz can't stop thinking about it. He would make it right if he knew how.

## Season 1: Reluctant Antagonist / Redemption Arc Seed

First encountered as muscle at Noke's Tower — he is not loyal to Noke, he is employed by him, and the difference becomes visible under pressure. He will not fight to the death for Noke's interests; he can be talked down, bluffed past, or simply outrun by his own conscience if the party reads the situation correctly.

If the party gives him an exit from the situation without humiliating him, he becomes a recurring contact. He is not a warden and may never be — but he can be a useful, imperfect ally who comes through when the party needs a particular kind of help.

His wolf companion (collared, intelligent, tactically aware) travels with him and is a useful secondary presence — the wolf's demeanor often communicates what Guz won't say directly.

Play him as someone who is trying. Not dramatically, not loudly. Just trying.
