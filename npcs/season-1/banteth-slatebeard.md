## Profile

![Banteth Slatebeard](https://i.imgur.com/EI33MV8.jpg) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** A broad dwarf man with a floury apron and the comfortable manner of someone who has decided that the best response to most problems is a hot meal and a moment to breathe.

**Voice:** Unhurried and even, with a gentle humor that never sharpens into sarcasm.

**Signature line:** "Sit down. You can argue in a minute. Eat first — everything looks different after you've eaten something."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The current emotional temperature of the Shepherd's Crook's regulars; when Leanor is more worried than she is showing; which conversations in the common room have recently gone quiet when a stranger walked in.
- **Wants:** For the tension in Welton to resolve without someone he cares about getting hurt; for Leanor not to have to carry as much of this as she's currently carrying.
- **Secret:** He knows about the book Leanor gave Alexi. He found out afterward and kept it from coming up because he judged that protecting his spouse was more important than his own discomfort with the knowledge. He will not volunteer this. He will also not lie directly if asked.

## Season 1: Wolves of Welton — Defuser & Secondary Source

Banteth is most useful as a de-escalation tool. When scenes at the inn get tense — between villagers, or between the party and Leanor — his presence can redirect the energy without forcing resolution. He is good at lowering the temperature.

He is also a path to Leanor that bypasses her wariness: talking to Banteth first, in the right way, can open doors she would otherwise keep closed.

He is fundamentally honest and fundamentally kind. Do not make him a pushover — he has a line, and if the party treats his home or the people in it with contempt, he will ask them to leave and mean it.
