## Profile

![portrait](/portraits/PimTosscoin.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** Curly-haired halfling with perpetually windblown locks and a leather courier bag plastered in regional pins and settlement badges from every town he visits.

**Voice:** Fast, breathless, sentence-jumping — speaks as though delivering urgent news even when the topic is the weather.

**Signature line:** "Road's clear to Welton — well, mostly clear — there's a downed tree at the fork but you can go around — anyway here's your package, sign here, also Brindle says hi."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Road conditions between all Northreach settlements, who is receiving what from whom, and the fastest alternate route around any obstacle
- **Wants:** To work up the courage to tell Sylra he's been leaving her anonymous poetry — and to eventually be taken seriously as more than "the messenger"
- **Secret:** He has a profound crush on Sylra Moonwhisper and has been leaving her unsigned poetry tucked behind bottles on the bar. The poems are, objectively, worse than hers.

## Season 1: Network in Motion

Pim is the fastest way to send a message anywhere in Northreach. He appears at the Inn between runs and can be hired for delivery jobs. He is also a passive intelligence network — he notices things on the road and will share them if asked. The Sylra situation can become a running comedic thread; PCs who figure it out can help or hinder.
