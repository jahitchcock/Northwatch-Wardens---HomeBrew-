## Profile

![portrait](/portraits/ElaraMoonstone.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** Half-elf teenager with round spectacles and ink-stained hands perpetually in motion — always carrying at least two books, always with a third balanced somewhere impractical.

**Voice:** Precise and serious, with the particular intensity of someone who has decided competence is the only currency that matters.

**Signature line:** "I've cross-referenced it against three sources and the discrepancy is consistent. That means it's deliberate. That means someone changed it."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The Guild archive contents, research methodologies, how to navigate scholarly sources, and the location of books that seem to have been deliberately misfiled
- **Wants:** To prove to Elric Vael that she is capable of real work, not just copying — and to understand what she found before it becomes someone else's problem
- **Secret:** While doing archival research, she found references she shouldn't have — texts connecting local history to cult activity. She is too scared to tell Elric and too curious to ignore it. She has been sitting on the information for weeks.

## Season 1: The Protégé in Danger

Elara is an active research asset — she can investigate topics for the party far faster than they can and knows how to find what archives are hiding. Her discovery of the cult-adjacent texts is a plot trigger: she will eventually share it with someone, either the party or Elric. If she shares it with the party first, she becomes a person who needs protection. Her safety can be used as genuine dramatic stakes without being cheap.
