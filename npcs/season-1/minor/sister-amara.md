## Profile

**Appearance:** Human woman in simple brown road-worn robes, carrying a walking staff carved with seven divine symbols from different traditions, with kind eyes that hold more sorrow than her calm expression suggests.

**Voice:** Quiet and unhurried — she speaks as though she has time, and she extends that gift to whoever she's listening to.

**Signature line:** "I won't tell you what the gods want. I can only show you what I choose to do, and let that speak."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The spiritual and emotional state of settlements she passes through, who is suffering quietly, basic healing and blessing practices of seven different divine traditions
- **Wants:** To help enough people that the weight of the ones she failed becomes bearable — she does not expect to be forgiven, only useful
- **Secret:** She failed to save her congregation from a plague — she had the resources to act sooner and delayed due to doubt. She wanders as self-imposed penance. She does not speak of it; she simply keeps moving and helping.

## Season 1: Grace in Motion

Sister Amara appears where there is suffering — a village after a hard winter, a road after an ambush, an inn where someone is clearly not alright. She does not seek converts or announce her presence. She offers what she can and leaves when she is no longer needed. Her minimal healing should feel meaningful without making her a free resource. PCs who sit with her long enough will sense the grief underneath her calm.
