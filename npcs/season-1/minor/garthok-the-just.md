## Profile

**Appearance:** Half-orc with graying hair and well-groomed tusks, wearing simple robes with a small justice scales emblem stitched at the collar — the only mark of his former office he still wears.

**Voice:** Considered and measured — he listens fully before speaking, asks clarifying questions, and never raises his voice.

**Signature line:** "Tell me what you believe happened. Then tell me what you know happened. They're not always the same thing."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Law, mediation, the history of disputes in Pinebrook, and how to tell the difference between someone who did wrong and someone who was wronged
- **Wants:** To give people the fair hearing he failed to give — to make right through process what cannot be undone in fact
- **Secret:** Early in his magistrate career, he convicted a man who was later proven innocent — too late. The man had already died in custody. Garthok bears this guilt daily and it has shaped everything about how he practices justice since.

## Season 1: Ethical Anchor

Garthok is the moral center of Pinebrook. He can mediate disputes, advise on legal matters, and offer the party a principled perspective when they face ethically complex choices. He does not preach — he asks questions. His insight into rehabilitation vs punishment can generate useful roleplay. His secret gives weight to any trial or judgment scene the party participates in.
