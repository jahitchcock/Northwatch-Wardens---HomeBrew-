## Profile

![portrait](/portraits/LyssaBrightsong.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** Half-elf with auburn hair shot through with gray streaks, scarred hands and face from years in the field, and a slight limp on the right side that she doesn't acknowledge and doesn't let slow her.

**Voice:** Witty and dry, with the particular cynicism of someone who loved something dearly and quit it deliberately.

**Signature line:** "I retired for a reason. Several of them. You want advice or a room?"

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Adventuring tactics, dungeon survival principles, how most overconfident parties die, and what separates the ones that don't
- **Wants:** To run her inn in peace and to stop caring every time a young party walks through her door looking exactly like her old one did
- **Secret:** Every member of her adventuring party died in a ruin thirty years ago. She was the only survivor. She walked out and never went back into a ruin again. The crossbow behind the bar has not been fired in a decade.

## Season 1: The Cautionary Mentor

Lyssa is the party's best source of tactical advice in Pinebrook — if they can get past her sardonic exterior. She doesn't romanticize adventuring and won't pretend the party is special. Her critique is honest and useful. If a party member is seriously hurt or if the party is clearly heading into something similar to what killed her friends, she will give them the truth without being asked. The crossbow behind the bar is a detail the DM can use when things go wrong at The Crossroads.
