## Profile

**Appearance:** Half-orc wrapped in bandages from at least two separate injuries, missing his pack, cradling a broken arm, somehow still wearing an expression of battered optimism.

**Voice:** Cheerful against all evidence — describes catastrophes in the same tone as minor inconveniences, and always follows misfortune with a plan for the next attempt.

**Signature line:** "The good news is I found what you're looking for. The bad news is there's a very large— actually, it's gone now. The other bad news is that's my arm."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Where dangerous things are, because he has recently survived encountering them — his bad luck is a map of hazards if interpreted correctly
- **Wants:** To have one adventure that goes correctly, from start to finish, without a single thing going catastrophically wrong
- **Secret:** A hag cursed him years ago: "May misfortune follow your steps." The curse is literal. Things go wrong around Grimbosh in ways that are statistically impossible. He suspects the curse but hasn't confirmed it, and wouldn't stop adventuring even if he had.

## Season 1: Disaster-Adjacent Discovery Engine

Grimbosh is the party's accidental scout. Every encounter with him involves something having just gone wrong, or going wrong in real time. His disasters are a reliable mechanism for the DM to introduce locations, threats, or items the party would not otherwise find. He is not dead — his survival instinct is genuinely remarkable. The curse can be confirmed, investigated, or potentially lifted as a side quest. He is always grateful for rescue and always optimistic about the next attempt.
