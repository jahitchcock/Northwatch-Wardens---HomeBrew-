## Profile

**Appearance:** Willowy half-elf with short silver hair, a collection of patron-gifted earrings that sway when she moves, and ink-stained fingers that betray the journal she keeps behind the bar.

**Voice:** Bright and darting — jumps between topics, interrupts herself to ask a follow-up question, ends observations with a hopeful uplift.

**Signature line:** "Wait, that's fascinating — can I write that down? Just the broad strokes, I promise."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Every rumor that passes through the Inn, traveler routes, local relationship drama, and which merchants are actually solvent
- **Wants:** To compile enough stories for a published oral history of Northreach — and to find a story worth telling that's truly her own
- **Secret:** Her gossipy journal is far more than personal notes — it is a structured oral history she intends to publish. She has overheard things that could embarrass or endanger people if the journal were read.

## Season 1: Best Rumor Source in Northreach

Sylra will trade information for "interesting stories" — specific, personal, vivid ones she hasn't heard before. PCs who share genuine experiences earn her trust and a flowing stream of local intelligence. She is oblivious to Pim's crush and the unsigned poems left on the bar. If the journal is ever threatened or stolen, she will be far more distressed than seems proportionate — it is her life's work.
