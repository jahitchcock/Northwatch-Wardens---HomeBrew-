## Profile

![portrait](/portraits/EliraDawntracker.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** Wood elf with long auburn hair threaded with wooden beads, smelling of pine and fresh mint, always with a worn canvas gathering bag slung over one shoulder.

**Voice:** Slow, deliberate, and reverent — she speaks about plants the way a scholar speaks about ancient texts, with patience for those willing to listen.

**Signature line:** "This one shouldn't grow here. The soil shouldn't support it. And yet — here it is, and it's been here a long time."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Northreach's flora in exhaustive detail, how to identify Echo-touched or Aeorian-affected plant growth, and which remedies work in the field versus in a proper lab
- **Wants:** To understand what the Echo is doing to the region's plant life — and to prove her research is a gift, not a danger
- **Secret:** She was exiled from the Nullwood Enclave for pursuing "dangerous curiosity" about Aeorian-affected flora. She is still experimenting, still cataloguing, and still sending observations back toward the Enclave through intermediaries, hoping to be vindicated.

## Season 1: Herbalist with a Hidden Edge

Elira sells standard potions and trail remedies near the Inn or at the edge of the market. She is a capable survival teacher and will share foraging knowledge freely. PCs investigating Echo phenomena may find her knowledge unsettling — she knows more than she lets on, and her specimens are not all benign. If approached with genuine curiosity rather than alarm, she becomes a critical information source on the region's environmental changes.
