## Profile

**Appearance:** Lean and weathered human with a burn scar across his right shoulder, moving with deliberate, unhurried calm around the animals in his care.

**Voice:** Quiet and measured — speaks rarely, chooses words carefully, more at ease talking to a horse than a person.

**Signature line:** "She's favoring her left fore. Give her two days. I'll tell you when she's ready."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Horse care, wilderness survival, how to read animal behavior as an early-warning system, and which roads are safe based on what the animals smell coming back
- **Wants:** To be left alone with his work and to carry his guilt quietly until it stops mattering
- **Secret:** He deserted his military unit during the chaos of the Calamity's aftermath to return home. The unit was not in immediate danger when he left, but people died later. He tells himself it would have made no difference.

## Season 1: Quiet Competence

Torven is rarely in the main room. PCs who seek him out in the stables find a man who will teach animal handling and wilderness sign-reading without being asked twice — he respects people who think ahead. He will never volunteer his military past, but if a PC notices the burn scar or presses gently, the truth can surface. He is not redeemable in one conversation; trust builds slowly over multiple visits.
