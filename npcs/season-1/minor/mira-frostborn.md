## Profile

![portrait](/portraits/MiraFrostborn.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** Tall half-orc with braided black hair, wearing furs she trapped and cured herself, the tip of her right tusk broken off in a way she has never bothered to explain.

**Voice:** Plain and direct — no social cushioning, no apology, just what needs to be said in as few words as possible.

**Signature line:** "Two days out, one day back. Bring your own food. I won't carry your pack."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Northern wilderness routes, glacier approach lines, every large predator's territory in the region, and where the weather turns dangerous before it looks like it
- **Wants:** Space and competent traveling companions — she will work with people who don't slow her down and don't complain
- **Secret:** During a blizzard two winters ago, she sheltered five lost villagers in her personal camp, sharing food and warmth for three days. She has consistently described this as "common sense," not heroism, and becomes irritated if anyone calls it the latter.

## Season 1: Best Guide in the North

Mira is the party's most valuable asset for wilderness travel in the Palebank region. She doesn't advertise her services — she must be sought out. She will not work with people who treat animals badly, complain without good reason, or ignore her instructions in the field. If the party earns her respect through demonstrated competence, she teaches them her skills actively. She can rescue the party during a blizzard encounter — this should feel like common sense to her, not a dramatic moment.
