## Profile

**Appearance:** Thin human with oiled hair and expensive but gaudy clothing — every piece chosen to signal wealth without suggesting taste, and a cold smile that never quite reaches his eyes.

**Voice:** Smooth and precise — every word calibrated, never raised, always reminding you of the document you signed.

**Signature line:** "Everything is in order. You agreed to the terms. I simply hold you to them. That's not cruelty — that's commerce."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Who in every settlement is in debt, who is desperate enough to take bad terms, which caravan guards are underpaid and unhappy, and which shipments are worth hitting
- **Wants:** To expand his financial control across Northreach through debt traps, and to ensure the violence he funds stays deniable
- **Secret:** He is the financial backer behind local bandit operations — taking a percentage of stolen goods and providing "recovery loans" to the desperate victims afterward. The loop is profitable and elegant.

## Season 1: The Legal Obstacle

Merrick is an antagonist the party cannot solve with violence — not without consequences. He operates within the law and hides behind it. Taking him down requires investigation, evidence collection, and either legal process (via Garthok, perhaps) or social exposure. PCs who default to threats find him unmoved — he has people for that. The bandit connection, if discovered, is the lever. Haggard Ironfist is one of his victims; that relationship illustrates his operation clearly.
