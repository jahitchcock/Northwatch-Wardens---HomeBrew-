## Profile

![portrait](/portraits/KorrinShale.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** White-bearded dwarf whose beard genuinely has small icicles frozen into it — a look he insists is deliberate fashion — wearing a seal-fur coat that smells of cold water and fish brine.

**Voice:** Doomsaying delivered cheerfully, as though bad news is the funniest thing he's heard all week.

**Signature line:** "Oh, the ice'll crack there. Always does by Harvestide. Lost three nets last year. Four the year before. Very consistent, really."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Glacier behavior, ice fishing techniques, safe crossing points, the history of past glacier accidents, and what "the voices in the ice" sound like when they're getting louder
- **Wants:** To keep fishing, keep laughing, and to not think too hard about why the ice sounds different this season
- **Secret:** His wife died in a glacier accident twenty years ago. His pessimism is grief wearing a comedy mask. He will never say this unless extremely pushed.

## Season 1: Cheerful Prophet of Doom

Korrin is the first person in Palebank to mention the "voices in the ice" — delivered as an aside in a story about a bad fishing day. He treats it as a long-running nuisance, not a threat. PCs who take it seriously and ask follow-up questions will find his knowledge of glacier behavior genuinely useful. His information about ice conditions and glacier safety can prevent disasters. Do not let him be merely comic relief — his grief is real and his expertise is genuine.
