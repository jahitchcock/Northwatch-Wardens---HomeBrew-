## Profile

![portrait](/portraits/TheAwakenedFawn.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** A young deer, barely past spotted-fawn stage, with eyes that hold an unsettling intelligence. Her movements are hesitant — she still startles at loud noises, then catches herself, as if remembering she is supposed to be afraid.

**Voice:** High and uncertain, like a child speaking a language she learned an hour ago. Sentences fragment when emotion overwhelms her. She pauses often, searching for words that don't exist yet.

**Signature line:** *"The cold grass. That's what made me talk. That's what made him stop."*

---

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The geometric frost patterns that create the Echo effect. She can sense them before they become visible — a prickling in her hooves, a taste like copper. She knows where her father fell.
- **Wants:** To understand what she is. To warn others from the cold wrong ground. To find the ones who helped her — she asks after "the ones with the small silver creature."
- **Secret:** She does not yet know that the stolen egg (the one that hatched into Falcor) is her sibling. She senses a kinship she cannot name. The connection will surface through roleplay and investigation, not exposition.

---

## Season 1: Newly Awakened

The party encountered the fawn on the road to Pinebrook, where she was being chased by a direwolf. She had eaten grass near geometric frost patterns and gained sapience and the ability to speak Common — all on the same day. Terrified and newly conscious, she bolted after giving the party the most valuable thing she carried: her father's antlers.

**She never gets a name until the party gives her one.** Whatever they choose will matter. Until then, she is simply "the fawn" or "the awakened fawn."

### Current Behavior
- Wanders the Northreach wilderness, warning travelers from frost-touched areas
- Guides animals around dangerous ground
- Asks questions of anyone who seems to know things — scholars, druids, old folk
- Reports to travelers that she is looking for "the ones with the small silver creature"
- Word reaches **Elric Vael** before the party returns from The Pale Sickness — he asks **Brenna Thorne**, who looks at the party very steadily: *"Did you want to mention something before you left?"*

### The Fawn's Revelation Scene
When she finds the party again, she sees John's armor and stops. Approaches slowly. Sniffs at the antlers. Long pause.

*"Those were his."*

She tells the story in fragments — old stag, biggest antlers she knew, he came to the cold wrong ground, he lay down and didn't get up, she ate the grass because she was hungry, then she could talk.

**She figures it out mid-sentence.** You watch her make the connection in real time.

*"The cold grass. That's what made me talk."*

Beat.

*"That's what made him stop."*

If John tells her he's going to fight the thing that did this, she looks at him for a long moment:

*"Then he goes with you."*

## Season 2+: A Name and a Purpose

By Season 2, the fawn has a name (given by the party) and has begun moving through the wilderness with clear intent. She may arrive at the Waystone Inn, seeking out Elric:

*"You smell like books and something old. Are you the one who knows what I am?"*

She becomes a source of wilderness intelligence — where the frost is spreading, which animals are behaving strangely, where travelers have gone missing. She cannot enter civilization safely (a talking deer draws attention), but she can meet the party at forest edges and road crossings.

**The Direwolf:** The direwolf that hunted her — leave its origin undefined for now. It may return as a threat, or it may have been another Echo victim.

**The Sibling Connection:** The stolen egg that became Falcor is the fawn's sibling. Do not reveal this yet. Let the players discover the connection through roleplay and investigation.

## Stat Block Reference

Use the **Deer** stat block (MM p. 321) as a baseline, with the following modifications:

- **Intelligence:** 10 (was 2)
- **Languages:** Common (cannot read or write)
- **Special:** Can sense Echo-affected areas within 1 mile
- **Cannot attack effectively** — she is still a fawn

{{note
**DM Note:** The Awakened Fawn is not a combatant. She is a walking mystery, a witness to the Echo's origin, and a moral anchor for the party. Her questions about what she is and why she exists mirror the campaign's larger themes. Treat her with care — she should feel fragile, precious, and deeply strange.
