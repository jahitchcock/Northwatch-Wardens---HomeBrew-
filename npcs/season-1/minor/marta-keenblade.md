## Profile

![portrait](/portraits/MartaKeenblade.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** Muscular human woman with close-cropped gray hair, a forge-scorched leather apron, and the left ear missing its upper tip — the remnant of a past she doesn't advertise.

**Voice:** Clipped and direct — no pleasantries, no cushioning, assessment delivered like a blacksmith's hammer.

**Signature line:** "Your blade's got edge rust in the fuller. That tells me everything I need to know about how you handle gear — and yourself."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Weapon quality, who in Northreach can actually fight, what the Guild is short on, and how to spot adventurers who won't survive their next contract
- **Wants:** To keep the frontier well-equipped and to maybe — just once more — take on a contract herself before she's too old
- **Secret:** She was an adventurer before settling into the trade. She lost part of her ear to a saber-tooth tiger during an expedition gone wrong. She still dreams of "one more adventure" but tells herself the smith work matters more.

## Season 1: Honest Appraiser

Marta is the Guild's primary equipment supplier. She will assess PC weapons and armor unsolicited, comment plainly on their condition, and offer maintenance or upgrades at fair prices. She respects competence; she is contemptuous of carelessness. PCs who maintain their gear well earn her genuine respect — and access to better inventory. Her adventuring past surfaces if PCs ask about the ear or push on her history.
