## Profile

**Appearance:** Round halfling woman with rosy cheeks, a flour-dusted apron, and an infectious laugh that fills whatever room she's standing in.

**Voice:** Warm and encompassing — she asks questions, remembers the answers, and greets returning guests by name and preference.

**Signature line:** "You look like you haven't eaten since Palebank. Sit down. The bread just came out."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Every piece of Welton village gossip, which families are struggling, and what travelers have been through based on how they sit down at her table
- **Wants:** To fill her inn with noise and laughter and to make sure nobody who walks through her door goes to bed hungry or alone
- **Secret:** Her daughter died of winter fever three years ago. She treats young adventurers as surrogate children — buying them extra food, checking on their wounds, worrying about them after they leave. She has never spoken of the loss directly.

## Season 1: Welton's Hearth

Brindle's inn is the social hub of Welton. She knows who is feuding, who is courting, and what the farmers are worried about this season. Her bread is genuinely remarkable. PCs who stay more than one night find her maternal attentions can be touching or smothering depending on temperament. Her grief surfaces if a young party member is seriously injured — she will insist on caring for them herself.
