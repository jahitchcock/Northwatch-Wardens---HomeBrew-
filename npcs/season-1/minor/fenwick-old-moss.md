## Profile

**Appearance:** Human elder with a long matted beard and clothing so overgrown with actual moss and lichen that he appears to be part of the forest floor — this is entirely intentional and he will tell you so.

**Voice:** Eccentric and discursive — speaks to plants in the same conversational register as people, and is more lucid when addressing them than when addressing visitors.

**Signature line:** "She says you've come a long way. The oak, I mean. She can tell by your boots."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The Northreach wilderness in exhaustive ecological detail, which areas have been touched by the Echo and how, and the difference between natural wildness and something wrong wearing the shape of nature
- **Wants:** To monitor the region's health and keep people away from the parts that aren't safe anymore — whether or not those people understand why
- **Secret:** He withdrew from civilization after the Calamity shattered the natural order he had devoted his life to maintaining. The trauma runs deep. He monitors the region's ecological health as the only thing he knows how to do, and the Echo-affected zones are getting worse.

## Season 1: The Wilderness Oracle

Fenwick is found by accident, not intention — he encounters the party when they stray into his monitored territory. He will warn them away from Echo-affected areas with cryptic specificity. PCs who treat him with curiosity rather than dismissal will find his knowledge irreplaceable. His plant-talking behavior conceals genuine druidic awareness; the plants are not answering him out loud, but he reads them like text. He is the early warning system for escalating wilderness threats.
