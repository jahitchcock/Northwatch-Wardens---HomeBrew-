## Profile

![portrait](/portraits/TorbinAshfall.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** Scruffy human boy in patched clothing, with quick hands, a defensive set to his shoulders, and eyes that are always reading the room for exits and threats.

**Voice:** Guarded and clipped with strangers — short answers, deflections, no personal information until he decides you've earned it; warm and unguarded with people he trusts.

**Signature line:** "I don't know what you're talking about. And even if I did, why would I tell you?"

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Street-level intelligence in Welton, who is moving where at what hours, where the orphan children sleep, and what the masked figures he saw were doing and where they went
- **Wants:** To keep the younger orphans safe — everything else is secondary, and he will trade information for protection of that group
- **Secret:** He witnessed cult agents recruiting or conducting a ritual near the village. The details of what he saw are significant. Someone wearing a mask noticed him noticing, and he has been watched since. He knows he is in danger but has nowhere to go.

## Season 1: Information and Stakes Combined

Torbin will not trust easily — he has been let down by adults before. PCs must demonstrate they protect rather than exploit before he opens up. Once trust is established, he is an exceptional street-level intelligence source with genuinely dangerous information. His personal danger should be real: the cult operatives are aware of him. Using him as a loose end creates good narrative pressure. The younger orphans in his care are additional stakes the party may choose to take on.
