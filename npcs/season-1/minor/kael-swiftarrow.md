## Profile

**Appearance:** Wood elf in a weathered traveling cloak, a recurve bow always within reach or already in hand, with alert eyes that assess every new person as a potential threat before downgrading to something warmer.

**Voice:** Cautious and spare — watches longer than he speaks, asks clarifying questions before offering anything, values demonstrated character over stated intention.

**Signature line:** "I've seen you for two days. You haven't made a bad choice yet. That earns you this much: I'll answer one question."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Travel routes through the northern wilderness, current threat positions, Nullwood's patrol patterns (from memory, not current contact), and where it is and isn't safe to camp in any weather
- **Wants:** To find a purpose that doesn't require belonging to a community that will eventually judge him — and, quietly, to not be alone in the way he has been since his partner died
- **Secret:** He was exiled from the Nullwood Enclave for pursuing a relationship with a human — forbidden by enclave custom. His partner survived the exile and died in the wilderness months later. He does not speak of it. He has been in the wilderness alone for years.

## Season 1: The Wary Ally

Kael crosses paths with the party during wilderness travel. He will not attach to them immediately — he observes before engaging, and engages minimally before trusting. The trust arc is a long one and worth developing: once he commits to the party, his scouting and wilderness intelligence are exceptional. His exile backstory can surface through patient roleplay. He will not discuss his partner unless asked directly and carefully.
