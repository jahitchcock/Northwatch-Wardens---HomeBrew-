## Profile

**Appearance:** Stocky dwarf with a white beard braided with copper rings, a stained apron worn over an old chainmail shirt, and a left hand missing two fingers.

**Voice:** Gruff and economical — short sentences, long pauses, food metaphors for everything.

**Signature line:** "Sit down. Eat first. Stories after. That's how it works here."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Frontier survival wisdom, old Warden operational history, Marshal Brenna's early career, and which travelers can be trusted by the way they treat the kitchen staff
- **Wants:** To keep feeding people well and die knowing the frontier is safer than when he patrolled it
- **Secret:** Lost his two fingers defending a caravan from an owlbear — not a glorious battle wound. Keeps his old Warden badge tucked in his apron pocket "for luck," though he tells no one.

## Season 1: Reliable Fixture

Garth is the heart of the Waystone Inn's common room. He notices everything from behind the serving counter. If PCs earn his respect (by finishing their food, treating horses well, or showing competence), he'll share Warden history and offer genuine tactical advice. He knew Marshal Brenna when she was a raw recruit and will test whether the PCs are worthy of her trust. His war stories are long but always contain a useful survival detail buried inside them.
