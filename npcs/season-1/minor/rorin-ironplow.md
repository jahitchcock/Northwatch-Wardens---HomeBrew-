## Profile

![portrait](/portraits/RorinIronplow.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** Broad-shouldered human with sun-darkened skin, hands calloused from years of field and sword work, wearing simple practical clothing that tells you nothing about his authority until he speaks.

**Voice:** Measured and deliberate — he weighs words like he weighs risk, doesn't rush, and expects to be heard once.

**Signature line:** "You want to help this village, you earn it. Show me what you can do first."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Every path through Welton's surrounding farmland, the capabilities of each militia member, and where threats have come from in the past
- **Wants:** To make Welton strong enough that no family loses someone the way he lost his brother — and to find outsiders worthy of trusting
- **Secret:** His brother was killed in a bandit raid five years ago. Rorin blames himself for not having organized the village defenses sooner. The militia he built is as much penance as protection.

## Season 1: The Gatekeeper

Rorin is the first obstacle to earning Welton's cooperation. He is slow to trust outsiders and will test PCs with practical requests before sharing information or opening doors. Once trust is earned, he is a loyal and capable ally who knows the local terrain better than anyone. He can mobilize a small but determined militia. PCs who show they protect the weak rather than exploit them win him quickly.
