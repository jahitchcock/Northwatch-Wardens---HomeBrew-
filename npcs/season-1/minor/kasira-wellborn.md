## Profile

![portrait](/portraits/KasiraWellborn.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** Gangly teenager with freckles, messy braided hair, and perpetually hay-dusted clothing — her borrowed sword tucked at her hip looks too big but she wears it like it belongs there.

**Voice:** Eager and rapid — asks twice as many questions as she waits for answers to, and punctuates everything with "— right?" seeking confirmation.

**Signature line:** "Is it true Wardens have to fight alone sometimes? How do you know when to call for help? — Actually, what does 'when to call for help' even feel like?"

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The sheep routes and pasture layout around Welton, where the soft ground is after rain, and which farmers are hiding livestock from the levy
- **Wants:** To become a Warden — to protect her village the way the adventurers do, with a sword and a purpose instead of a shepherd's crook
- **Secret:** She practices swordfighting alone at night with a blade borrowed from a neighbor's barn wall. Her family needs her on the farm; she has never told them she wants to leave.

## Season 1: The Eager Future

Kasira is an enthusiastic helper who can get PCs into places adults can't — she knows every shortcut and every gossip thread among the village youth. She is a future recruit if the campaign continues. Her potential danger moment: she may try to "help" during an actual threat, putting herself at risk and creating a moral weight for the party. Rorin watches her closely; he sees something in her.
