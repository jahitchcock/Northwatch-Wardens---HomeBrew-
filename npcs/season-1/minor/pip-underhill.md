## Profile

![portrait](/portraits/PipUnderhill.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** Halfling child with curly red hair, a missing front tooth, and a toy sword on his belt worn with complete seriousness — as though it is just as real as any other weapon.

**Voice:** Fearless and declarative — says dangerous things with cheerful confidence, asks adults questions they find uncomfortable, and follows up on answers he doesn't understand with more questions.

**Signature line:** "I'm going to be a Warden. I've already decided. Can I come with you? Just to the edge of town. Just to see."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Every shortcut in and around Welton, which adults are fun and which are boring, and where all the other children go to avoid supervision
- **Wants:** A hero to look up to — someone who shows him that bravery and goodness go together, not separately
- **Secret:** His father drinks heavily and is largely absent. Pip doesn't frame it as suffering; he simply looks outward for the models he isn't finding at home.

## Season 1: Innocence and Stakes

Pip is the party's conscience made small. He will follow them, idolize them, and occasionally put himself in danger because he doesn't yet understand that danger is real. Protecting him is a low-stakes rehearsal for protecting the community. If the party behaves well around him, the DM can reward them with his trust as an in-world validation. If a party member becomes his hero and then fails him, the scene should land with weight.
