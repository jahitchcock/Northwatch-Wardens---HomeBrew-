## Profile

![portrait](/portraits/JessamineColdwater.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** Blonde teenager with blue eyes and her mother's amulet at her collar, perpetually practicing chord progressions on a lute that goes slightly out of tune in the cold.

**Voice:** Optimistic and warm, with a habit of composing observation-songs about mundane things in real time, quietly, as though she's not aware she's doing it.

**Signature line:** "It's a simple melody — just three chords, but I think the third verse is starting to work. Do you want to hear it? It's about the fish market."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The rhythms and mood of Palebank, who is happy and who is sad this week, and which travelers have passed through the market with interesting stories
- **Wants:** To leave Palebank eventually and find audiences larger than her mother's market stall — but she's not ready, and she suspects it
- **Secret:** She has written a collection of love songs about a traveling Warden — one of the PCs, possibly. They are sincere and not performable in public. She is far too shy to ever play them to the subject.

## Season 1: Optimism Under Gray Skies

Jessamine is a tonal counterpoint to Palebank's cold, subdued atmosphere. Her enthusiasm is genuine. She cannot sing well — the DM should be honest about this — but her compositions have heart. She represents the village's hopefulness and is fragile in that way: if something threatens Palebank, she is among the first to be affected emotionally. Her songs about a PC can surface as a comedic or touching moment depending on the table.
