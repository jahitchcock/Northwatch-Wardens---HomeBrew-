## Profile

![portrait](/portraits/VexNighthollow.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** Tiefling with crimson skin and filed-down horns, wearing black leather armor with a crossbow over one shoulder and shackles hanging from a belt hook — all of it functional, none of it decorative.

**Voice:** Polite and precisely professional — even warmth is deployed deliberately, and nothing is said that doesn't serve a purpose.

**Signature line:** "I have a contract. You're not on it. We have nothing to discuss unless that changes."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Underworld contacts across the region, bounty markets, how to find people who don't want to be found, and the specific behavioral patterns of someone who fits a description they carry but haven't shared
- **Wants:** To find the person from their own past — the current contracts are real work, but they are also cover
- **Secret:** Vex is using legitimate bounty work as a reason to travel Northreach while searching for someone specific from their own history — someone who wronged them, or someone they wronged, or someone they lost. The nature is deliberately ambiguous and DM-assignable.

## Season 1: Ally, Antagonist, or Something Else

Vex's alignment with the party depends entirely on whether their targets overlap or conflict. They may be hunting someone the PCs are protecting, or hunting someone the PCs also want. They are a professional and will not take unnecessary risks or make things personal unless the situation becomes personal. Their private search is the lever that turns them from a neutral into something more invested. PCs who earn their professional respect get access to underworld contacts they couldn't reach otherwise.
