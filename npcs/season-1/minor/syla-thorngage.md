## Profile

**Appearance:** Half-elf in flashy, well-polished armor with a cape that is more dramatic than protective, weapon always displayed at a visible angle, posture permanently arranged for maximum impression.

**Voice:** Grand and self-narrating — speaks in the third person occasionally, uses dramatic pauses, and describes her own deeds while they are still allegedly happening.

**Signature line:** "Ah — you've arrived. I was wondering if you'd manage it in time. The credit for the setup is mine, of course, but I'll mention you in the account."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Which contracts are high-profile, which clients want to be seen hiring impressive-looking adventurers, and how to make a hired job appear to be her own accomplishment
- **Wants:** Fame — specifically, to be the most celebrated adventurer in Northreach, regardless of whether she has earned it
- **Secret:** She is a poor adventurer. She hires capable people, sends them to do the dangerous work, and arrives afterward to collect the credit. None of her advertised accomplishments are entirely fabricated — someone did do those things — just not her.

## Season 1: The Rival Who Needs Rescuing

Syla is primarily an annoyance — she competes for contracts, claims credit, and spreads unflattering versions of the party's reputation. The comedic potential peaks when she is genuinely in danger and the party must choose whether to rescue her. A rescue earns no gratitude she'll acknowledge publicly. The truth about her operation can surface through investigation, through the people she hires, or by catching her before she arrives at the ending she's taking credit for.
