## Profile

**Appearance:** Silver-haired gnome with hair arranged in complex braids, every finger wearing a different ring, dressed in layered colorful clothing that somehow always looks intentional rather than chaotic.

**Voice:** Shrewd and playful — treats every transaction as a game she expects to win, but laughs genuinely when outplayed.

**Signature line:** "That price is for people who don't know what it's worth. You clearly do. So let's talk about what it's actually worth to you."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The financial state of every family in Pinebrook, what adventurers actually need versus what they think they need, and which suppliers are reliable versus which are cutting corners
- **Wants:** To run her store as a net positive for the community — profit is the mechanism, not the goal
- **Secret:** She has anonymously funded the starting equipment of twelve young adventurers over the years, routing it through "anonymous donors" or surprise inventory "errors." She keeps a private ledger of everyone she's helped.

## Season 1: Shrewd Benefactor

Seraphine is a fun haggling encounter with hidden depth. She matches PCs in wordplay and negotiation and clearly enjoys it. Her prices are fair-to-high, but she will quietly discount or gift supplies to PCs who are clearly in genuine need — always with a paper-thin excuse. PCs who are perceptive enough to notice her pattern of generosity and call it out will earn her trust. The anonymous sponsorship history can surface as a plot element.
