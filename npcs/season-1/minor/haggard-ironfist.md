## Profile

**Appearance:** Scarred dwarf who carries the smell of cheap spirits, wearing a tarnished Guild badge he hasn't removed despite everything — the one thing he will not let go of.

**Voice:** Bitter and slurring — starts calm enough, slides into accusation and self-pity within a few minutes, occasionally cuts through to something sharp and lucid before the drink pulls him back.

**Signature line:** "You think you're better than me? Give it time. Give it time and a bad night and we'll see what you do."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** How the Warden Guild actually operates under pressure, the names of old missions that ended badly, and which current members have skeletons of their own
- **Wants:** To not be alone with what he did — whether that means company, absolution, or making everyone else as miserable as he is, depending on the evening
- **Secret:** He caused civilian deaths through negligence during an active mission. The Guild discharged him. He has never disputed the decision because he knows it was correct. The shame is deserved, which is what makes it unlivable.

## Season 1: Cautionary Tale and Threat

Haggard is the party's dark mirror — what a Warden looks like when they fail their purpose and carry it wrong. He will spread resentful rumors about the PCs if they succeed where he failed. He may be used by Merrick Blackcoin, who funds him in exchange for information about Warden activities. The DM can use him to illustrate institutional accountability, or as the subject of a redemption arc that may or may not succeed depending on whether the party invests in it.
