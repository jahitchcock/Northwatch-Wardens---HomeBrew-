## Profile

**Appearance:** Lean human with short dark hair and well-worn leather armor, moving through any environment with a predator's quiet economy of motion.

**Voice:** Confident and appraising — talks to strangers like they're being evaluated, softens only when the evaluation goes well.

**Signature line:** "You track or you don't. There's no middle. But you're welcome to try to keep up."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Forest terrain around Pinebrook, the behavior of every large creature in the region, and how to read a trail days after it was made
- **Wants:** To be recognized as the best tracker in Northreach — on his terms, proved by skill, not by others' judgment
- **Secret:** Years ago, he entered a regional tracking competition and lost to a city-born ranger visiting the area. He has been trying to prove himself ever since — to himself more than anyone else.

## Season 1: Rival or Asset

Drake is a natural foil for any ranger or Scout-type PC. He is not hostile, but he is competitive, and he will issue or accept challenges related to wilderness skills. Befriending him requires demonstrating genuine skill — flattery gets nowhere. Once he decides the party is competent, he shares forest danger intelligence freely. The losing-competition backstory can be teased out through rivalry roleplay and surfaces as an opportunity for character growth.
