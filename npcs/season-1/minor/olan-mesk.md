## Profile

**Appearance:** Lanky human with sawdust perpetually worked into his hair and spectacles held together with a knot of cord, wearing an apron that has more pencil marks than tool marks.

**Voice:** Distracted and enthusiastic — starts sentences about one thing, pivots mid-way to something more interesting he just thought of, then forgets where he was.

**Signature line:** "— and the joinery here is completely wrong for post-Calamity construction, which means — sorry, what were we talking about?"

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The age and origin of any structure in Northreach by its construction methods, local architectural history, and where foundations don't match the buildings above them
- **Wants:** To document everything before it's lost or demolished — and to find someone who thinks old buildings are as interesting as he does
- **Secret:** While repairing Palebank's foundation drainage, he discovered pre-Calamity stonework beneath the village — foundations of something much older. He hasn't told anyone because he fears the discovery would cause panic or be dismissed.

## Season 1: Key to the Ruins

Olan is the connective tissue between Palebank Village and any Echo-related dungeon content beneath it. PCs who engage his historical enthusiasm genuinely — rather than just pumping him for information — will find he opens up readily. The pre-Calamity foundation discovery is a potential plot hook: once he trusts the party, he will show them what he found. The ruins are not empty.
