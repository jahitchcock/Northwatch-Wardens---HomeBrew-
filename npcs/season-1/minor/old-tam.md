## Profile

![portrait](/portraits/OldTam.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** A bent, white-haired human leaning on a walking stick, with a clouded left eye that seems to look through people rather than at them — permanently occupying the chair closest to the Shepherd's Crook fireplace.

**Voice:** Wandering and slow, mixing digressions and tangents freely, until a moment of sudden, startling clarity cuts through like a blade.

**Signature line:** "That reminds me of a story — well, it's more of a thing that happened, really. You have a moment? Good. Sit."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The pre-Calamity geography of the region, old names for landmarks, where ruins were before the settlements grew over them, and what the land looked like when it was different
- **Wants:** To be listened to — and, on some level, to tell the true story at least once before he dies
- **Secret:** He witnessed the Calamity as a young man. The trauma is buried beneath seventy years of story and exaggeration, but it surfaces in nightmares and in the moments when his stories get too specific to be invented.

## Season 1: Living Archive

Tam's rambling is a delivery mechanism for genuine historical intelligence. PCs willing to listen — and to sift the embellishment from the fact — can extract useful information about ruins, old roads, and pre-Calamity locations that no map shows. The DM can seed specific clues into his tangents. If asked directly about the Calamity, he goes very quiet before the words come.
