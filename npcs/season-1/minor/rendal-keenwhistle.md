## Profile

![portrait](/portraits/RendalKeenwhistle.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** Balding halfling with impressive sideburns pushing a cart piled with tools, small mechanisms, and oddities — always with something interesting sitting on top and something more interesting underneath.

**Voice:** Chatty and affably curious — he asks about everyone's business while appearing to volunteer his own freely, though nothing he says about himself is quite complete.

**Signature line:** "Oh, I heard something about that! Well — not exactly about *that*, but related. Pull up a crate, I'll tell you what I know, and you can tell me if I've got it right."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Road conditions across all of Northreach, which settlements are growing and which are struggling, trade route patterns, and fragments of intelligence from every conversation he's had in the last month
- **Wants:** To keep moving, keep gathering, and keep delivering intelligence that earns his continued employment — and to avoid anyone finding out who employs him
- **Secret:** He is a paid spy for the Solace Nexus merchant guild, mapping northern trade routes and assessing settlement stability. Some of the rumors he shares are deliberately planted. He is not malicious about it — it is simply his job.

## Season 1: Friendly Face with an Agenda

Rendal appears in multiple locations across the campaign — he is always plausible wherever he shows up. His information is a mix of genuine and curated. PCs who treat him as a passive resource will receive useful but shaped intelligence. PCs who investigate him or press his inconsistencies will begin to see the pattern. He is not an enemy — he is a professional with competing loyalties, and how that resolves depends on what the party does with the knowledge.
