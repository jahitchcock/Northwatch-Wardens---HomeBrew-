## Profile

**Appearance:** A stout dwarf woman with steel-grey hair and the particular quality of stillness that belongs to people who have been watching rooms for decades.

**Voice:** Warm and unhurried, with a faint edge underneath it when she doesn't like what she's hearing.

**Signature line:** "I'll get you settled in. And while I do — you can tell me what you're actually here for."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Everyone's business in Welton — who was sleeping poorly before the attacks started, which families have histories with the forest, who came through town recently that wasn't a regular; she is the village's unofficial intelligence network.
- **Wants:** For Welton to stay intact and for the people she has known for thirty years to get through this without destroying each other or making decisions they can't take back.
- **Secret:** She was the one who told Alexi Merriksonn about a druidic text she'd acquired from a traveling trader — a text about speaking to animals. She didn't think anything would come of it. She has not made this connection publicly because she is afraid of what it means for Johan.

## Season 1: Wolves of Welton — Information Broker

Leanor is the party's best social intelligence source in Welton. She does not volunteer information freely, but she responds well to directness, genuine concern for the village, and the sense that the party is asking because they need to understand rather than because they want to confirm a decision they've already made.

She is a slower burn than Marta Henwick — her information is better, but it requires more investment to unlock.

Her connection to Alexi through the book is the kind of revelation that should come naturally from a good conversation, not be extracted through pressure. If the party pushes too hard, she closes.
