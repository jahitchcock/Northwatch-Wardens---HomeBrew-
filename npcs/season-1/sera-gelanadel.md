## Profile

**Appearance:** Young half-elf with ink on her cuffs and three different bookmarks sticking out of the satchel that never leaves her shoulder.

**Voice:** Fast and enthusiastic, with a tendency to answer a question and then immediately amend the answer with a more precise version.

**Signature line:** "It's actually a modified abjuration matrix — well, modified isn't quite the right word — the point is, I can translate it, just give me ten minutes. Maybe fifteen."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** A working reading knowledge of four dead languages; the arcane theory behind most of the anomalies the party encounters in Season 1; the contents of Elric's research notes (she files them).
- **Wants:** To prove she is ready for field work, not just desk work. She is terrified of being left behind on the mission that matters.
- **Secret:** She has been teaching herself a spell from a recovered text without telling Elric. She doesn't fully understand it yet, and it has behaved unpredictably in practice.

## Season 1: Quest-Brief & Translator NPC

Sera is the party's most accessible source of arcane knowledge — more approachable than Elric and faster to answer, though sometimes less accurate. Use her for quest briefings, translation of recovered texts, and identification of magical objects.

She works well as a "human cost" counterpoint: she's brilliant but young, and the weight of what the party brings back from the field lands on her differently than it does on veterans. A moment where she has to translate something genuinely disturbing is a strong scene.

She also appears in the Temple of the Dragonknights arc as field support — her eagerness to be in the field may create complications when the situation escalates beyond her experience.

## Season 2+: Tested Scholar

The spell she has been practicing secretly either becomes a liability (if used at the wrong moment) or a surprise asset. Elric finds out. How he handles it — and whether the party covers for her or not — is a relationship beat worth playing.
