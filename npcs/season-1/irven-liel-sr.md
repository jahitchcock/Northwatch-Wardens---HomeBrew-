> **Note:** Not to be confused with [Irven Liel](npcs/season-1/irven-liel.md), the courier of Welton (*Wolves of Welton*). They share a name only. This is the older Palebank merchant — "Sr." — whose family is infected in *The Pale Sickness*.

## Profile

**Appearance:** A careworn human man in his late forties, hands never quite still, eyes going constantly to the next room where his children rest. Whatever composure a merchant's life taught him is gone.

**Voice:** Frayed and pleading, breaking on the edges. He apologizes for things that aren't his fault and bargains with people who haven't asked for anything.

**Signature line:** "Look at their hands. Please. I'll give you anything — just tell me there's something to be done."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** That he bought two pieces of "decorative Aevorian glass" from a traveling fence named Brynn as a gift for his partner; the fence's description and that he spoke of more coming from the north, near Croaker Cave.
- **Wants:** His family saved. Nothing else exists for him right now. He will hand the party every coin and every scrap of information he has without being asked twice.
- **Secret:** None worth hiding — his guilt is fully on the surface. He blames himself for bringing the vials home, and he is right to, and knowing that is its own wound.

## Season 1: Frozen Sick (Palebank) — The Stakes Made Personal

Irven Sr. exists to make the disease a face rather than a number. His partner and two children are infected — one child already showing crystallization at the fingertips. He is the cleanest, most cooperative source of the Croaker Cave lead (no Persuasion required) and the emotional core of the "who do you cure?" decision if antidote runs short.

He should never be played as a suspect. He has nothing to hide and everything to lose. Use him to raise the cost of every wasted day.
