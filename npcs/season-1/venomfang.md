## Profile

![portrait](/portraits/Venomfang.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** A green dragon of significant size who has made the Temple's interior specifically comfortable for herself and specifically uncomfortable for anything else; the smell of acid and old death is the first thing the party encounters.

**Voice:** Smooth and amused, with the specific quality of patience that belongs to a creature that expects to outlive everything in the room by centuries.

**Signature line:** "A bargain implies I need something from you. I'll let you think about whether that's the situation we're actually in."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The Temple's full history and the significance of what it houses; the capabilities of the party, observed or estimated; what value any particular bargain would actually deliver and what it wouldn't.
- **Wants:** To maintain her position in the Temple and to accumulate the specific kind of power the Temple's artifacts represent. She is not actively seeking conflict with the party — she simply does not weight their lives very heavily.
- **Secret:** She has already entered into one bargain that she has not honored and does not intend to. The party may not discover this in Season 1. If they do, her position shifts from "treats bargains as traps" to "is actively managing a situation where she is exposed."

## Season 1: Temple of Dragonknights — Campaign-Tier Threat

Venomfang is the most dangerous direct threat in Season 1 and should feel like it. A direct confrontation at the party's Season 1 level is likely fatal. The correct play is: rescue Clementine without triggering a confrontation, or negotiate from a position of leverage the party has to find before the negotiation.

Any bargain the party makes with her should be treated with extreme suspicion — she keeps agreements only until keeping them becomes more costly than breaking them.

She is a recurring threat. If the party solves the immediate situation without destroying her, she will be present in the region for every subsequent arc.

## Season 2+: Active Regional Power

Venomfang's position in the Temple has expanded. Whatever she was working toward in Season 1 is now further along. The broken bargain (if applicable) is now actively relevant. The party's Season 1 choices about how they handled her are load-bearing for how this arc plays.
