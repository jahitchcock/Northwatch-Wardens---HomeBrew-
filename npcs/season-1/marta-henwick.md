## Profile

**Appearance:** A sharp-faced human woman in her forties who keeps her shop immaculate and her eyes moving; she notices who comes in, how long they stay, and who they talk to.

**Voice:** Quick and conspiratorial, always pitched slightly below the ambient noise — everything she says sounds like it's being confided.

**Signature line:** "I'm not saying it's connected. I'm just saying — the timing is interesting, isn't it?"

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Surface-level gossip about most of Welton's residents; which buildings in the village have had unusual activity; the general direction of local opinion on the wolf situation, day by day.
- **Wants:** To be the person who knew something important before anyone else did. Information is her currency and her sense of self.
- **Secret:** She has been exaggerating her accounts of the wolf attacks to paying travelers who pass through — embellishing details for drama without caring about the effect on the village's fear level. She knows she is making things worse. She can't seem to stop.

## Season 1: Wolves of Welton — Gossip Hub & Clue Leak

Marta is the easiest information source in Welton to access and the least reliable. She will tell the party things, but the party should quickly learn to calibrate her accounts — genuine observations buried in dramatization. With the right approach (flattery, the sense that she's being brought in rather than questioned), she will surface useful details she doesn't realize are useful.

She can also be used as a pressure point if the party needs leverage: her embellishment habit is something she would very much prefer the council not know about in detail.
