## Profile

**Appearance:** A sharp-featured human woman in her fifties with the clothes of a successful trader and the eyes of someone who has been negotiating in bad-faith environments for decades.

**Voice:** Controlled and pleasant, with the specific quality of pleasantness that functions as a wall rather than an invitation.

**Signature line:** "I'm happy to help. I just need to understand what 'help' means in this specific situation before I commit to it."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The trade routes into and out of the frozen north; which goods move through Palebank and from where; the names of several players in the region's shadow economy who are connected to the sickness's point of origin.
- **Wants:** To protect her business and her position in Palebank — she has more to lose from exposure than from the sickness itself, and she is managing both crises simultaneously.
- **Secret:** She brought the contaminated goods through Palebank. Not knowingly, but not entirely carelessly either — she ignored a warning sign because the delivery was profitable. The guilt is real but so is the fear of what happens if anyone traces it back to her.

## Season 1: Frozen Sick — Leverage & Desperation

Verla is most useful as an information source who has to be motivated to share. The party can motivate her through threat (she is exposed if they dig), through appeal (she actually does not want more people to die), or through deal (she has something they need, and she knows it).

Her secret is not a full reveal early — let it emerge through the investigation. When it does come out, her reaction (guilt + self-preservation instinct in direct conflict) is a scene worth playing.

She can become a genuine, if complicated, ally once her exposure is on the table and she decides the party is more useful to her as an out than a threat.
