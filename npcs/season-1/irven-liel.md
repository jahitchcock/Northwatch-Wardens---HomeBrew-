## Profile

![Irven Liel](https://i.imgur.com/XRPU9yL.jpg) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** A wiry human man who always seems to be mid-transit — one foot out the door, pack half-shouldered, in the middle of something he was just about to do.

**Voice:** Friendly and slightly distracted, like someone who is mentally composing his next message while having the current conversation.

**Signature line:** "I can take word to — where did you need word taken? I was heading that way anyway."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Who sends messages to whom in the Welton area; which routes are currently safe to travel and which aren't; what he has seen and heard on the roads over the past few weeks.
- **Wants:** For the roads to be safe again. The wolf situation is directly affecting his livelihood and he is losing income he cannot afford to lose.
- **Secret:** He saw something on the road three weeks ago — a figure moving along the tree line that he couldn't identify — that has been living rent-free in his head ever since. He hasn't told anyone because he doesn't know how to describe it precisely enough to be believed.

## Season 1: Wolves of Welton — Breadcrumb Witness

Irven is most useful as a mobile information node — he knows who is talking to whom and has seen things the more rooted villagers haven't. Use him to create connections between separate clues or to confirm a theory the party is developing.

His sighting on the road is a flexible hook: it can point toward Alexi, toward the wolves' movements, or toward something else entirely depending on where the DM wants to guide the investigation.

He can also function as a quick-hire courier if the party needs to send word somewhere fast.
