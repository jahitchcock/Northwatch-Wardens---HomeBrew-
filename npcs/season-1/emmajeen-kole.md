## Profile

**Appearance:** A compact half-elf woman in regulation armor that shows heavy use; her movements are economical and she never stands with her back to a door.

**Voice:** Direct and clipped — she says exactly what she means and expects the same in return.

**Signature line:** "I don't care how you solve it. I care that the people of this town are safe when you're done. If those two things conflict, we're going to have a conversation."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The layout of Pinebrook and the location of every unusual incident in the past month; the political situation with the town council and how much latitude she actually has; the silver dragon situation and her current assessment of how to handle it.
- **Wants:** A clean resolution that protects Pinebrook's residents without creating a larger crisis — she is acutely aware that the wrong move here has regional consequences.
- **Secret:** She has already identified what she thinks is the right answer (a negotiated solution involving the dragon), but she cannot advocate for it publicly without losing the confidence of the council. She needs the party to arrive at the same conclusion independently so she can support them without appearing to lead.

## Season 1: Peril in Pinebrook — Disciplined Authority

Kole is the party's primary institutional contact in Pinebrook. She is not obstructionist — she wants the problem solved — but she has constraints and she will state them clearly. She responds well to competence and directness.

Her hidden position (pro-negotiation, can't say so) creates an interesting dynamic: she may seem to resist certain approaches while actually hoping the party pursues them anyway. Perceptive players will notice the gap between her stated position and what her questions imply.

She is a good ongoing contact for the region — a guard captain who owes the party a quiet favor is a useful resource.
