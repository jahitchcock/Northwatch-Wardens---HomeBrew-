## Profile

![portrait](/portraits/HulilLutan.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** A lean tiefling woman with the unhurried movements of someone who has thought through all available exits before entering any room.

**Voice:** Quiet and controlled — she speaks slowly because she wants you to hear exactly what she is saying, and because she has decided there is no urgency in this particular exchange.

**Signature line:** "You have two options. You can try to stop me here, or you can let me walk out and we can both think about whether this conversation needs to happen again."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The full cult operation at Croaker Cave; the supply chain of the contaminated substance and where it leads; who the cult's patron is and what they want from the Frozen Sick outbreak; the location of at least one more operation the party hasn't found yet.
- **Wants:** To complete the current operation and get out cleanly — she is a professional, not a fanatic, and she would prefer not to die for a job.
- **Secret:** She is not a true believer in the cult's ideology. She was recruited for competence and paid well. She has begun to have doubts about the patron's endgame — not moral doubts, but strategic ones. The operation is getting larger than she was told it would be, and she does not like surprises.

## Season 1: Frozen Sick (Croaker Cave) — Intelligent Antagonist

Hulil is the most capable direct antagonist in the Frozen Sick arc. She should never feel like a pushover — she will not fight to the last if fighting is losing, and she will use everything available to her including negotiation, misdirection, and an escape route she has prepared in advance.

If captured or cornered, she can be a source of information in exchange for something she wants. She is not loyal to Raegrin Mau and will not cover for him. She is not loyal to the cult beyond the contract.

If she escapes, she becomes a recurring complication — smarter the second time.

## Season 2+: Wild Card

Hulil's doubts about the patron's endgame crystallize into action. She may surface as an unexpected source of intelligence against a larger threat, or she may decide the party is the most reliable weapon against something she now needs stopped. She will not apologize for what she did. She will expect to be used, not trusted.
