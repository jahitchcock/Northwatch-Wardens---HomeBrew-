## Profile

**Appearance:** Currently: a sheep. A sheep with unusually alert eyes and a specific quality of attention that is deeply incongruous with the wool. When restored: an older gnome with the disheveled brilliance of someone who has never learned to care what they look like.

**Voice:** (As sheep) Unable to speak, but communicates urgency, disdain, and exasperation with remarkable precision. (As himself) Rapid, densely technical, and self-interrupting — he assumes everyone is following and is mildly irritated when they aren't.

**Signature line:** "Yes, yes, I'm a sheep. I'm aware. Could we please focus on the part where you get me out of here before I critique the situation further?"

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** A great deal of arcane theory and practical wizardry; the specifics of the polymorph affecting him and how to undo it; information about Noke's Tower and Ahmed Noke's operations that he gathered before being transformed.
- **Wants:** To not be a sheep anymore. After that, to ensure Noke faces consequences and to retrieve something Noke stole from him.
- **Secret:** The item Noke stole was not just valuable — it was dangerous, and Shinebright has not been completely honest about what it does. He judged that full disclosure might cause the party to make a different decision about the mission, and he needed them to go in regardless.

## Season 1: Wild Sheep Chase — Comic Lead & Recurring Contact

Finethir is the heart of the Wild Sheep Chase arc's tone: a comedy scenario with genuine stakes. The humor comes from the incongruity; do not undersell either the sheep absurdity or the underlying competence and arrogance.

Once restored, he becomes a recurring magical contact — knowledgeable, blunt, occasionally useful, and occasionally a source of new complications related to the item he hasn't been fully honest about.

His secret about the item is a hook the DM can deploy at any time. It does not need to surface in Season 1.

## Season 2+: Complicated Ally

The item's nature becomes relevant. Shinebright's relationship with the party depends entirely on whether they have figured out he wasn't fully honest and what they decided to do about it. He will not apologize, but he will do a significant favor in lieu of an apology if the party lets him frame it that way.

{{/season-gate}}
