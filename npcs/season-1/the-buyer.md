## Profile

**Appearance:** Always seen at a distance or in low light — a deliberately maintained presentation; the clearest observation the party is likely to get is a voice and a silhouette and the sense of someone who has made a specific choice about how much to be seen.

**Voice:** Measured and precise, with a warmth that feels genuine without revealing anything; they speak as though they have thought carefully about what to say and have decided that this version of the conversation is in everyone's interest.

**Signature line:** "I'm not asking you to trust me. I'm asking you to notice that I have been reliable — which is a different thing."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The full scope of what is in Salsvault and what it is worth; the larger network of which the Croaker Cult is a small part; what the party's involvement in the Frozen Sick arc means for their longer-term usefulness.
- **Wants:** Specific artifacts from Salsvault — and to establish the party as a recurring acquisition resource without revealing enough of their agenda to give the party the ability to refuse with full information.
- **Secret:** The Buyer is not independent. They serve a larger power whose goals are not aligned with anyone the party has met so far. The relic brokerage is infrastructure, not a business. They are not hostile to the party — they are investing in them — but investment is not benevolence.

## Season 1: Frozen Sick (Salsvault) — Long-Term Faction Seed

The Buyer should feel significant from first contact — not dangerous, but important in a way the party can't fully parse yet. Their generosity is real within its limits. Their agenda is real and hidden.

Use them sparingly in Season 1. One direct contact, well-played, is enough to establish the thread. The thread is most valuable when it has gone quiet for long enough that the party has moved on — and then it reappears.

The specific identity and faction of The Buyer is a Season 2+ reveal; keep it genuinely unknown in Season 1 even to the DM if possible — let the campaign generate the answer.

## Season 2+: Revealed Agenda

The Buyer's agenda becomes legible. Whether the party chooses to continue working with them or becomes their target is a major Season 2 branch. Their reliability record is either a genuine asset or the groundwork for a very effective betrayal.
