## Profile

**Appearance:** A girl of perhaps ten, with the specific quality of stillness that children develop when they have decided that panic is not going to help and they are waiting for something to change.

**Voice:** Careful and observant — she asks better questions than the situation implies she should be able to.

**Signature line:** "I knew someone would come. Dad doesn't give up. I didn't give up either."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** More about the Temple than she should — she has been conscious and observant since her capture, and she has been paying attention in the specific way of someone with nothing else to do.
- **Wants:** To go home. But she has also, in the time she has been here, started to understand that something is happening in this place that is larger than her situation.
- **Secret:** She found something in the Temple while waiting — a small object, carried in a pocket, that she has not shown anyone yet. She does not know what it is. The DM does.

## Season 1: Temple of Dragonknights — Rescue Stakes & Future Hook

Clementine is the rescue object of the capstone arc, but she should not be played as passive or merely symbolic. Her observations of the Temple are useful intelligence. Her composure under pressure should land as a character beat, not just precocity.

Her secret object is a campaign hook. Plant it here; pay it off in Season 2 or later. The specific nature of the object is at DM's discretion and should tie into the larger pattern Elric has been tracking.

If saved, she is a future recurring hook — a child who survived something significant and was changed by it in ways that will only become clear later.
