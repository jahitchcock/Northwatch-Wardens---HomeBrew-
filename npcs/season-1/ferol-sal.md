## Profile

![portrait](/portraits/FerolSal.png) {width:130px,float:right,margin:"0 0 10px 15px"}

**Appearance:** Whatever form the undead transformation has taken, there is still something recognizably deliberate about it — the impression is of a mind, working, even if the body is wrong.

**Voice:** Unhurried and precise, with the specific quality of someone who has no more urgency because they no longer have a timeline to worry about.

**Signature line:** "I have had a great deal of time to think about what I would do if someone came back here. I have prepared for most of the options. Please tell me which you are."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Everything about Salsvault — its layout, its history, the purpose of every artifact and trap within it; the nature of the contaminated substance and how it was created; the full history of the expedition that disturbed the ruin.
- **Wants:** To not be interrupted. And, if interrupted, to negotiate from a position of advantage — they have no interest in destruction for its own sake; they want their work left alone.
- **Secret:** The undeath was not accidental and not entirely unwilling. Ferol chose it as a mechanism to finish something they were not going to live long enough to complete. The work they are completing is genuinely dangerous — but it is also genuinely their work, and they believe in it.

## Season 1: Frozen Sick (Salsvault) — Intelligent Final Threat

Ferol Sal is the capstone challenge of Salsvault. They should not be played as a monster to be defeated — they are a mind that happens to be housed in something horrible, and they can negotiate. The party's best outcome may be a deal rather than a fight.

Their advantage position is real: they know Salsvault completely and the party does not. Any plan to fight them straight up should feel disadvantaged.

The negotiation options are: leave and let the work continue (bad long-term); destroy the work and accept the consequences (immediate but clean); understand the work well enough to evaluate it honestly (expensive but gives the party real information).

How the party handles this determines what they know about the larger arc going forward.
