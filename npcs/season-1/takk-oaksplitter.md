## Profile

**Appearance:** A massive half-orc with a gap-toothed grin and arms that look like they were designed for a creature twice his height; always in armor, even at meals.

**Voice:** Loud, cheerful, prone to accidental shouting when excited — which is frequently.

**Signature line:** "I'll go first. I always go first. That's not bravery, that's just — I like going first."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** The layout of every building in the Waystone district; who among the Wardens is reliable in a fight versus who freezes; how to keep morale up in a group that has been scared.
- **Wants:** To be useful. He has no ambition for rank or glory — he just wants to be the person who made the difference when it mattered.
- **Secret:** He can't read. He has hidden this from the Wardens for two years with a combination of memorization, bluffing, and quietly asking Sera to "remind him" of written details. He is deeply ashamed of it and would do almost anything to keep it from becoming common knowledge.

## Season 1: Morale Anchor & Front-Line Support

Takk is the party's most reliable source of uncomplicated warmth and enthusiasm. Use him to lighten the mood after difficult sessions and to model what healthy warden culture looks like. He is not stupid — he reads people well and has good tactical instincts — but he is unsubtle, and sometimes that's exactly what a scene needs.

His literacy secret is a low-stakes personal hook the party can either help with quietly or accidentally expose. It's a good character moment either way.

He volunteers for dangerous assignments constantly. If the party ever has to tell him he can't come on a mission, his disappointment is genuine and a little heartbreaking.
