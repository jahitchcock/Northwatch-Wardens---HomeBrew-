## Profile

**Appearance:** A dwarf with the particular physical economy of long-range wilderness travelers — nothing carried that isn't needed, nothing done that wastes effort.

**Voice:** Even and unhurried, with the specific quality of someone who knows the situation is bad and has decided that panic is a waste of time.

**Signature line:** "I'll give you my best assessment. I won't tell you what you want to hear, and I won't soften it. That's what I've got."

## Knows / Wants / Secret

- **Knows:** Salsvault's layout from his previous expedition; the hazards and the safe routes through the ruin; the history of the expedition that originally discovered the contaminated substance; who else was on that expedition.
- **Wants:** To get through this situation with as few additional casualties as possible — he has lost people in Salsvault before and he is not interested in losing more.
- **Secret:** He came back to Salsvault voluntarily after his first expedition ended badly, because he could not stop thinking about something he saw and did not take. He has not told anyone what it was. It is relevant to the Ferol Sal situation.

## Season 1: Frozen Sick (Salsvault) — Competent Field Contact

Morgo is a practical, reliable ally in the Salsvault section of the arc. His value is tactical knowledge and honest assessment — he will not tell the party the situation is fine when it isn't, and he will not pretend to know things he doesn't.

Use him to establish the ruin's stakes and layout before the party goes in. His hard truths about survival and risk are most useful when the party is considering a plan that sounds clever but has a flaw he can see.

His secret is a DM hook for the Ferol Sal confrontation or a later arc.
