## Special Abilities

### Divine Smite
**Trigger:** When you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack

Expend a spell slot to deal extra Radiant damage on hit: 2d8 per slot level (1st), +1d8 per slot level above 1st. +1d8 if the target is an Undead or Fiend. Maximum 5d8.

---

### Lay On Hands
**Healing Pool:** 15 HP / Long Rest (5 HP currently used)

You have a pool of healing power that replenishes when you finish a Long Rest. With that pool, you can restore a total of 15 HP.

**Heal (Bonus Action):** Touch a creature and restore HP up to the pool maximum remaining.

**Purify Poison:** Expend 5 HP from the pool to remove the Poisoned condition.

---

### Divine Sense
**Uses:** 2 / Long Rest (both currently used)

Until the end of your next turn, you know the location of any celestial, fiend, or undead within 60 feet of you that is not behind total cover. You also detect consecrated or desecrated places or objects.

---

### Fighting Style: Great Weapon Fighting
When you roll a 1 or 2 on a damage die for an attack with a two-handed melee weapon, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll.

---

### Sacred Oath: Oath of Devotion
**Tenets:** Honesty, Courage, Compassion, Honor, Duty

**Channel Divinity: Sacred Weapon** — As an action, imbue one weapon with light. For 1 minute, add Charisma modifier to attack rolls and emit bright light.

**Channel Divinity: Turn the Unholy** — Each fiend or undead within 30 feet must make a Wisdom saving throw or be turned.

---

### Resourceful (Human Variant)
Whenever you finish a long rest, you gain Heroic Inspiration.

### Savage Attacker (Feat)
