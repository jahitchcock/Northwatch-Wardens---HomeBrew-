## Otherworldly Patron: The Great Old One

### Awakened Mind
You can telepathically speak to any creature you can see within 30 feet. The creature does not need to share a language with you, but it must be able to understand at least one language.

You learn the *Find Familiar* spell and can cast it as a ritual. The familiar can be an imp, pseudodragon, quasit, or sprite (in addition to normal options). You can forgo one of your attacks to allow your familiar to make one attack of its own.
