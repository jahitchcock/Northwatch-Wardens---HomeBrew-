## Growth Stages

Falcor is a Beastmaster companion and scales with Perkia's ranger level. His power progression is story-driven and tied to Perkia's journey.

### Current Stage: Hatchling (Ranger Levels 3–5)
- **Flight:** Glide only — can leap and glide up to 20 ft. in a straight line. Cannot hover or gain altitude.
- **Cold Breath:** Cosmetic puff only — 1 cold damage, 5-ft. cone. Mostly harmless. He is very proud of it.
- **Size:** Tiny dragon. Adorable. Easily distracted by shiny objects.

### Future Stages

#### Youngling (Ranger Levels 6–9)
- **Flight:** Full flight — 30 ft. fly speed. Can carry small objects but not humanoids.
- **Cold Breath:** 2d6 cold damage, 15-ft. cone (1/day).
- **Size:** Small dragon. Still cute, but beginning to ask difficult moral questions.
- **Personality:** Curious, loyal, emotionally attached. Begins forming opinions about right and wrong.

#### Adolescent (Ranger Levels 10–14)
- **Flight:** Strong flight — 40 ft. fly speed. Can carry Perkia for short distances.
- **Cold Breath:** 3d6 cold damage, 15-ft. cone (1/day).
- **Size:** Medium dragon. No longer fits in a backpack. May challenge Perkia's decisions.
- **Personality:** Morally independent. Becomes a moral compass for the party — sometimes inconveniently so.

{{note
**DM Note:** Falcor should never overshadow the party. He is a Beastmaster companion first, a dragon second, and should always feel special but not overpowering. See the full companion progression guide in the DM resources for behavior logic, tension hooks, and loyalty tracking.
