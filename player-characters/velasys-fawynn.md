## Special Abilities

### Fey Ancestry
Advantage on saving throws against being Charmed, and magic can't put you to sleep.

### Trance
Meditate for 4 hours instead of sleeping for 8 hours to gain the benefits of a Long Rest.

### Keen Senses

### Fey Step
As a bonus action, magically teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest.)

### Eyes of Night
You have Darkvision out to a range of 300 feet.

### Vigilant Blessing
As an action, you give one creature you touch (including yourself) advantage on the next initiative roll the creature makes. This benefit ends after a roll or if you use it again.

### Heart of Darkness

### Channel Divinity (1/rest)
- **Turn Undead:** Each undead that can see or hear you within 30 feet must make a Wisdom saving throw. If it fails, it is turned for 1 minute or until it takes any damage.
- **Twilight Sanctuary:** As an action, you present your holy symbol and a sphere of twilight emanates from you in a 30-foot radius. The sphere is filled with dim light. The sphere moves with you, and it lasts for 1 minute or until you are incapacitated or die. Whenever a creature (including you) ends its turn in the sphere, you can grant that creature one of these benefits:
  - Give it temporary HP equal to 1d6 + your Cleric level.
  - End one condition or effect causing it to be Charmed or Frightened.
