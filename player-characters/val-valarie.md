## Special Abilities

### Darkvision
You can see in dim light within 60 feet as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light.

### Hellish Resistance
You have resistance to Fire damage.

### Legacy of Cania
- **Mage Hand:** You know the Mage Hand cantrip.
- **Burning Hands:** You can cast Burning Hands as a 2nd-level spell once per Long Rest.
- **Flame Blade:** You can cast Flame Blade as a 3rd-level spell once per Long Rest.
- Spellcasting ability for these spells is Charisma.

### Bardic Inspiration (d6)
You can inspire others through stirring words or music. As a bonus action, choose one creature other than yourself within 60 feet who can hear you. That creature gains one Bardic Inspiration die, a d6. Once within the next 10 minutes, the creature can roll the die and add the number rolled to one ability check, attack roll, or saving throw it makes.

**Uses:** 3 (regain all expended uses on a Short or Long Rest)

### Song of Rest
If you or any friendly creatures who can hear your performance regain HP at the end of a Short Rest, each of those creatures regains an extra 1d6 HP.

### Mantle of Inspiration (College of Glamour)
As a bonus action, you can expend one use of Bardic Inspiration to grant yourself a magical mantle. When you do so, choose up to 5 creatures you can see within 60 feet. Each gains temporary HP equal to your Charisma modifier + your Bard level (5 temp HP), and each can immediately use its reaction to move up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

### Countercharm
As an action, you can start a performance that lasts until the end of your next turn. During that time, you and any friendly creatures within 30 feet have advantage on saving throws against being Frightened or Charmed.
