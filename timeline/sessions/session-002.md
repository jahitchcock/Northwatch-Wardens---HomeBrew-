# Session Log — 2026-05-26

**Adventure:** The Awakened Fawn / Peril in Pinebrook  
**Party level:** 3  
**Real-world date:** 2026-05-26

---

## What happened

The party traveled the road to Pinebrook. En route, they encountered a direwolf chasing a spotted fawn. The fawn spoke Common — she had gained sapience that morning after eating grass near geometric frost patterns. The party drove off the direwolf and spoke with her. She was hours old as a thinking being, terrified, and had no framework for grief or language. She described her father's death near the "cold wrong ground" and gifted the party his antlers before fleeing into the wilderness.

In Pinebrook, the party investigated missing patrols, discovered a baby silver dragon egg, and defended it from ice troll attacks. The dragon hatched and bonded with Perkia as a Beastmaster companion. The party named him Falcor.

All characters advanced to Level 3 during this session. John took the Oath of Devotion and incorporated the Father's Antlers into his armor. Perkia chose the Beastmaster archetype and bonded with Falcor. Kuetis took the Pact of the Chain and selected the Armor of Shadows and Fiendish Vigor invocations.

The party returned to the Waystone Inn, debriefed with Marshal Brenna Thorne, Lorewarden Elric Vael, and Steward Mara Fenwick, and picked up the Pale Sickness contract.
