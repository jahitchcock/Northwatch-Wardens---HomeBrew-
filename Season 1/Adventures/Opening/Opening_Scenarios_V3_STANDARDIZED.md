## CHAPTER 1: FRAMEWORK OVERVIEW

### What Are the Opening Scenarios?

The **Opening Scenarios Framework** is not a single adventure but a **structured onboarding system** designed to:

1. **Establish party cohesion** — Bring together individual player characters into a functioning team
2. **Introduce campaign tone** — Grounded, frontier-focused, morally complex
3. **Teach game mechanics** — Combat, skill checks, investigation, social encounters
4. **Plant first mystery** — Whispers of the Aeorian Echo (geometric frost, wrong magic)
5. **Launch first mission** — **Wolves of Welton** becomes the party's first contract

### Campaign Context

**When This Happens**
- **Session 0** — Character introductions, world briefing, cold-open combat
- **Session 1** — Charter ceremony, first briefing, party assembly, travel to forward camp

**Best Played Before**
- Any major adventure (Wolves of Welton recommended as first full mission)

**Recommended Party Setup**
- **3–5 player characters** (flexible; can accommodate 2–6 with adjustments)
- **Levels 1–2** (ascending to 2–3 by end of opening scenarios)
- **No experience with guild mechanics** (opening teaches this)

### The Northwatch Wardens Identity

Players should understand the **guild's core identity** before moving forward:

**The Northwatch Wardens are:**
- A **professional organization** (not adventuring heroes; they're contractors)
- **Divided into three command roles** (Marshal, Steward, Lorewarden)
- **Merit-based** (advancement through successful contracts)
- **Guild-first** (loyalty to the wardens comes before individual gain)
- **Frontier-focused** (protecting settlements in Northreach and surrounding regions)
- **Grounded** (low magic, tactical thinking valued, heroics secondary to survival)

**Core Concept Players Should Internalize:**
*"You're not legendary adventurers. You're trained professionals hired to solve problems. Your payment reflects your skill; your reputation reflects your results."*

|---------|----------|-----------|

### Running for Different Party Sizes

**2 Players (Struggling)**
- Cold-open: 2 wolves (normal)
- Optional bandits: 1 bandit only (or skip entirely)
- Veteran Scout becomes **strong NPC ally** (third combat participant)
- Brenna maintains focus on practical support (supplies provided, short rests available)

**3 Players (Standard)**
- Cold-open: 2 wolves (normal)
- Optional bandits: 2 bandits (negotiable)
- Veteran Scout is **guide, not combat ally** (assists with skill checks)
- Recommended setup: plays exactly as written

**4–5 Players (Optimal)**
- Cold-open: 2 wolves (standard, but party shouldn't struggle)
- Optional bandits: 2 bandits as negotiation opportunity (not lethal threat)
- Veteran Scout is **mentor figure, not combatant** (focuses on teaching)
- Additional emphasis on party roleplay / character dynamics

**6+ Players (Large Group)**
- Cold-open: 3 wolves (extra threat for bigger party)
- Optional bandits: 2 bandits + 1d4 additional bandits (escalating threat)
- Veteran Scout may **ask for party's help on other problems** (leadership teaching)
- Emphasis on squad tactics and party roles

### Session Zero Guidance

**Before game starts, confirm with players:**

- [ ] "This is a **campaign onboarding framework**—get to know your party, learn the guild, receive your first mission"
- [ ] "Tone: grounded frontier; you're professionals, not legendary heroes on first night"
- [ ] "You'll start as strangers, face a shared threat, and become a team by session's end"
- [ ] "Combat is expected but not guaranteed—negotiation and smart tactics matter"
- [ ] "The first major adventure (Wolves of Welton) kicks off after this framework"
- [ ] "Expect 2–4 hours total for complete opening; can be split into two sessions"
- [ ] "One mystery thread begins here: strange magic is stirring in Northreach"

## SCENE 0: COLD OPEN — WOLVES AT WAYSTONE

**Scene Purpose**
Introduce the setting, thrust party into immediate danger, force cooperation, and establish the campaign's grounded tone.

**Time & Location**
- **Timing:** Evening (dusk, settling darkness)
- **Location:** Waystone Inn (exterior), common room, entrance
- **Duration:** 20–30 minutes
- **Atmosphere:** Sudden chaos, innkeeper panic, urgent cooperation

### The Arrival

**Read-Aloud Text:**

> *You arrive at the Waystone Inn as snow begins to fall. The building is warm hospice against the gathering dark—heavy timber construction, three wide chimneys spilling smoke. Lights glow in the windows. You can hear voices and laughter from inside: travelers, locals, the sound of a living place.*
>
> *You step toward the entrance, ready for comfort and lodging.*
>
> *Then: a **scream** from the back of the building.*

### The Wolf Attack

**Encounter Overview**

As party enters or moves toward the inn:

> *Three shapes burst from the darkness—three **wolves**, lean and desperate, running *straight at you*. Their eyes gleam in the lamplight. They're not fleeing game or other predators—they're hunting something. They're hunting you.*
>
> *One wolf lunges at the nearest party member. The others flank.*

**Wolves (Dire Wolf variant, CR 1)**

**Note:** These are larger, more intelligent than common wolves—foreshadowing the "awakened" wolves later in Wolves of Welton.

- **AC** 13 | **HP** 11 (2d8+2) | **Speed** 40 ft
- **STR** 15 (+2) | **DEX** 15 (+2) | **CON** 15 (+2) | **INT** 3 (-4) | **WIS** 12 (+1) | **CHA** 6 (-2)
- **Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +4
- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
- **Challenge** 1 (200 XP each)

**Special Traits:**
***Pack Tactics.*** The wolf has advantage on melee attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the wolf's allies is within 5 feet of the target and the ally isn't incapacitated.

***Pounce.*** If the wolf moves at least 20 feet straight toward a creature and then hits it with a claw attack on the same turn, that target must succeed on a **DC 13 Strength save** or be knocked **prone**.

***Keen Hearing and Smell.*** The wolf has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.

**Actions:**
- **Bite:** +4 to hit | 2d6+2 piercing (or **2d8+2 if using Pounce**)
- **Claw:** +4 to hit | 1d8+2 slashing

**Wolves' Behavior:**
- **Hungry, desperate, not suicidal**
- Attacked the innkeeper/staff earlier (drove them inside)
- Focused on food (party members look like viable prey)
- **Will flee when reduced to 3 HP or fewer** (desperation gives way to self-preservation)
- **Do NOT attempt to communicate** (wolves are animals, not awakened)

### Scaling by Party Size

| Party Size | Wolf Count | Innkeeper Support | Difficulty |
|-----------|-----------|------------------|-----------|
| 2 players | 2 wolves | Strong (Richard rescues party) | Medium |
| 3 players | 2 wolves | Moderate (Richard provides supplies) | Medium |
| 4 players | 2 wolves | Minor (innkeeper safe) | Easy-Medium |
| 5+ players | 3 wolves (extra threat) | Minimal (party handles it) | Easy |

**Richard & Lauraine Brown (Innkeepers, NPC Support)**
- **AC** 10 | **HP** 5 each | **Can't fight effectively**
- Will **barricade themselves inside** after first wolf appears
- Can be rescued/protected (adds emotional stake)
- Grateful if party saves them (provides information, free lodging)

### The Scene's Structure

**Round 1: Ambush**
- Wolves charge, melee combat begins
- Innkeeper screams; other guests slam the door (taking shelter inside)
- Party must respond immediately or be overwhelmed

**Round 2-3: Combat**
- One wolf focuses on strongest-looking target
- Others flank or threaten party formation
- If flank is successful, Pounce triggers (knockdown effect, tactical awareness)

**Round 4-5: Climax**
- Wolves at half health begin disengaging
- At 3 HP or fewer, **desperate wolf flees** (toward forest, away from inn)
- Last wolf makes tactical decision: **fight to victory or flee?** (rolls depend on remaining HP)

**After Combat: The Moment**

Once wolves are dead or fled:

> *Silence. The snow falls harder, covering blood and paw prints. The inn door slowly opens. Richard Brown peers out, shaking.*
>
> *"Thank the gods. We thought—they came so fast. I've never seen wolves like that. So organized. So... purposeful."*
>
> *Lauraine emerges, seeing blood: "Did you protect yourselves? There are supplies inside—bandages, blankets, fire. Come in. Please. This isn't normal."*

### Investigation Opportunities

**DC 10 Perception:**
- Notice the wolves' **unusual organization** (not typical wolf behavior)
- See claw marks on the inn's wooden door (the wolves deliberately attacked the building, not random hunting)

**DC 12 Survival:**
- Recognize that wolves are **well-fed, not starving** (unusual—starving predators wouldn't retreat; these are tactical)
- Track shows deliberate approach from **north** (toward forest, toward higher ground)

**DC 13 Arcana or unusual perception:**
- Notice thin **geometric frost** on the wolves' fur (same unnatural ice seen in later campaign)
- Wizard or magical perceptive PC might sense **faint magical aura** around the bodies (wrongness)

### The Inn Interior (First Look)

**Read-Aloud Text:**

> *The Waystone Inn is warm and comfortable. A hearth fire crackles in the common room. Tables are scattered with travelers—a merchant, two farmhands, a priestess in Unaligned robes. All stare at you as you step inside, blood and snow-tracked from combat.*
>
> *Behind the bar, Richard points to an empty table near the fire. "Please. Sit. Let me bring water, cloth for injuries. You're safe here."*

**NPCs Present:**
- **Richard & Lauraine Brown** (innkeepers, male & female, 60s, grateful)
- **Father Merriksonn** (priestess, human female, mid-50s, observing you with interest)
- **Traveling Merchant** (neutral bystander, not important)
- **Local Farmhands** (two young men, curious but irrelevant)

### Father Merriksonn's Approach

**If party is receptive (helped the innkeepers, seem competent):**

Father Merriksonn approaches your table as Lauraine brings water and cloth.

> *"You handled yourselves well against those wolves. Unusual creatures, weren't they?" He sits uninvited—a man comfortable with authority. "I'm Father Merriksonn, priest of the Unaligned Way. I came to Northreach three weeks ago investigating... strange occurrences. And it seems I'm not the only one interested in this region's troubles."*
>
> *He eyes the wolf blood on your armor.*
>
> *"Tomorrow morning, there's a guild called the Northwatch Wardens. They recruit occasionally. If you're the type to turn a profit from solving problems, and if you're smart enough to survive the wolves—you should speak to their Marshal. Her name is Brenna Thorne."*

**This is the hook.** Merriksonn essentially directs party toward the Northwatch and the charter ceremony.

### Optional: Innkeeper's Hospitality

Richard & Lauraine offer:
- **Free lodging** for the night (party can rest, heal, recover)
- **Basic supplies:** waterskins, rope, bandages
- **Healing:** minor first aid (1 HP recovery each, or verbal support)
- **Information:** local rumors (works lead to minor quests, but those are secondary)

**If party wants to investigate further (pursuing wolves, exploring forest):**
- **NOT recommended** before Scene 1 (morning preparation)
- But allowed if party insists (DM can use scouts or patrols to encounter them)

### Transition to Scene 1

**If party rests at inn:**

> *The night is quiet. The wolves don't return. Snow accumulates. By morning, the attack feels dreamlike—until you see the scratches on the inn door, the blood stains despite Lauraine's cleaning attempts.*
>
> *Richard brings breakfast. "Marshal Brenna from the Northwatch will be at the inn after dawn. A regular contact of ours. If you're interested in work, this is your chance."*

## SCENE 1: THE MORNING AFTER — CHARTER AND CONTRACT

**Scene Purpose**
Formally induct party into the Northwatch Wardens, establish guild structure, assign the first mission, and provide campaign direction.

**Time & Location**
- **Timing:** Morning (after sleep/rest)
- **Location:** Waystone Inn, charter room (private meeting room)
- **Duration:** 20–30 minutes
- **Atmosphere:** Professional, ceremonial, exciting (but grounded)

### The Briefing Room

**Read-Aloud Text:**

> *Richard guides you to a smaller room off the common area—the charter room. A wooden table dominates, worn smooth by years of use. A large parchment is spread across it: the **Northwatch Wardens Charter**. Beside it, a leather-bound logbook and several smaller contracts.*
>
> *Standing at the head of the table is a woman in practical leather armor. She's middle-aged, scarred, with the bearing of someone who's made life-and-death decisions. This is **Marshal Brenna Thorne**.*
>
> *Next to her: a younger man in scholar's robes, watching you with sharp eyes. This is **Lorewarden Elric Vael**.*
>
> *And seated near the supplies: a practical woman with a quartermaster's air, organizing gear. This is **Steward Mara Fenwick**.*

### Meet the Guild Leadership

**Marshal Brenna Thorne (Guild Leader)**
- **Appearance:** 50s, scarred, competent, no-nonsense
- **Speech Pattern:** Direct, efficient, expects obedience but grants respect
- **Attitude Toward Party:** Professional curiosity; they survived a wolf attack and came to the inn—that's noteworthy
- **Key Quote:** "The Northwatch isn't a family. It's a business. You work for us, you get paid, you earn advancement. Fail, and we find other contractors."

**Steward Mara Fenwick (Quartermaster)**
- **Appearance:** 40s, motherly, practical
- **Speech Pattern:** Warm but organized; speaks in logistics
- **Attitude Toward Party:** Wants to see them equipped and healthy (resource management)
- **Key Quote:** "You'll need a few things for work. We provide basics—rope, rations, camping gear. Better equipment, you buy with your wages."

**Lorewarden Elric Vael (Scholar & Lore Master)**
- **Appearance:** 30s, bookish, wears scholar's robes (unusual for a frontier guild)
- **Speech Pattern:** Thoughtful, asks questions, connects dots
- **Attitude Toward Party:** Intellectually interested in them; views their arrival as part of larger patterns
- **Key Quote:** "Strangers arrive. Wolves attack. The charter is signed. These are not coincidences. Magic is stirring, and we need people who can think, not just fight."

### The Charter Ceremony

Brenna presents the **Northwatch Wardens Charter**:

> *"You survived the wolves. Lauraine says you were competent, not reckless. That matters. The Northwatch runs on reputation and results. This charter is our oath—your oath—that you'll work for the guild's success, that you'll honor contracts, that you'll advance through merit."*
>
> *She unrolls the parchment. It bears the Northwatch emblem: a compass rose with a sentinel figure.*
>
> *"Read it or don't. The terms are simple: You take jobs. You complete them. You report findings. You advance. You fail, and we replace you. Questions?"*

**THE NORTHWATCH WARDENS CHARTER**

> *We, the Northwatch Wardens, are a professional organization dedicated to protecting frontier settlements and solving regional problems through competent action.*
>
> ***Core Oath:***
> *"I pledge myself to the Northwatch. My loyalty is to the guild's success. I will complete assigned contracts, report findings truthfully, and conduct myself with honor. I will protect the innocent and advance only through demonstrated merit. This is my oath, freely given."*
>
> ***Rights:***
> *- Fair payment for completed contracts*
> *- Guild housing and supplies at the Waystone Inn*
> *- Medical care for job-related injuries*
> *- Advancement through demonstrated competence*
> *- Right to refuse dangerous contracts (with consequence of demotion)*
>
> ***Responsibilities:***
> *- Complete assigned contracts to the best of ability*
> *- Report findings and discoveries to leadership*
> *- Maintain guild reputation through professional conduct*
> *- Support fellow Warders in field operations*
> *- Accept assignment demotion for contract refusal*
>
> *— **The Northwatch Wardens, established Age of Ascension, Year 47 of the Realms**

**Signature Moment:**
Party members sign (or make mark) on the charter. This is **ceremonial but real**—they are now formally inducted into the guild.

> *Brenna watches as you sign. When the last name is written, she nods.*
>
> *"Welcome to the Northwatch. You are now **Initiate-rank Warders**. Your first contract awaits."*

### The Mission Brief: Wolves of Welton

Mara presents a **contract document**:

> *"Welton village. South of here, two days' ride. They've reported wolf attacks. Livestock killed, one shepherd missing. They're requesting Warder investigation and... mitigation."*
>
> *She slides the contract across the table.*

**THE WOLVES OF WELTON CONTRACT**

> *Issuance: Welton Village Council*
> *Classification: Field Contract — Threat Assessment & Suppression*
> *Rank: Initiate-Eligible*
>
> *The settlement of Welton reports organized wolf activity. Livestock (goats, sheep) have been slaughtered. At least one shepherd—a veteran tracker—is missing. The attacks are unusual: coordinated, targeting farms rather than wild hunting patterns.*
>
> ***Objectives:***
> *1. Travel to Welton*
> *2. Assess the wolf threat (numbers, behavior, intelligence)*
> *3. Investigate the missing shepherd*
> *4. Neutralize or drive away the wolf threat*
> *5. Return to Waystone Inn with findings*
>
> ***Reward: 800 gold pieces on completion and confirmation by village council**
>
> ***Danger Level:** Moderate (likely combat with dangerous animals)*
> ***Duration:** Estimated 3-5 days*
> ***Contingencies:** If threat is greater than anticipated, contact Waystone Inn for reinforcements*

**Elric's Addition:**

> *"There's something more here," Elric says, studying you. "Welton is one of several settlements reporting unusual animal behavior. Awakened intelligence. We're not sure why. But I suspect the attacks are connected to... something stirring in the region. The wolves of Welton may be just the first symptom of larger problems."*
>
> *Brenna shoots him a look—*stop sharing classified information*—but doesn't contradict him.*

### Party Questions & Clarifications

**If party asks "How do we prepare?"**
Mara responds: "You'll receive standard equipment—bedrolls, rations, basic tools. Better gear, you earn with your wages. The Waystone provides arrows, healing potions, and rope at cost. Ask Richard for a supply list."

**If party asks "Can we refuse?"**
Brenna answers: "You can. Your rank drops. Another Warder takes the contract. You'll be reassigned to guard duty or supply runs. Refusal has consequences, but we don't force people into danger."

**If party asks "What happened to the missing shepherd?"**
Elric answers: "That's what you're being paid to discover. Information is part of your value. Find him—alive or confirm his death—and that's additional contract value (extra 200 gp)."

**If party asks "Why us? We just survived one night."**
Brenna responds: "Because you survived that night. You're competent, you came to us, and you're available. The Wardens doesn't recruit heroes—we recruit contractors who deliver results. Prove it, and you'll advance."

### Mara's Supply Handout

**Standard Initiate Kit (Provided by Guild):**
- Bedroll
- Waterskin
- 10 days of rations (bread, cheese, dried meat)
- 50 feet of hempen rope
- Lantern + 20 flasks of oil
- Crowbar
- Thieves' tools (basic)
- Pack (20 lb capacity)

**Available for Purchase (at-cost):**
- Healing Potion (50 gp)
- Healing Kit (5 gp)
- Additional rations (1 gp per day)
- Arrows/bolts (2 gp per quiver)
- Ammunition variety (cost varies)
- Rope, additional (1 gp per 50 feet)

**Not provided:** Weapons, armor, specialized equipment (party must bring their own or purchase at inflated market prices)

### Transition to Scene 2

Brenna concludes:

> *"You'll leave at first light tomorrow. The road south is clear but watch yourselves. One of our scouts will meet you at the creek crossing—about six miles south of here. She'll guide you into Welton territory, introduce you to the village council, and provide additional context.*
>
> *"You have the rest of the day to prepare. Rest, gather supplies, and if you have questions, ask Mara. She knows logistics better than anyone."*

## SCENE 2: RETURN TO WAYSTONE — ROAD TO FORWARD CAMP

**Scene Purpose**
Build party cohesion through shared travel, introduce Echo mystery through environmental clues, provide combat options, and transition to campaign's first full adventure.

**Time & Location**
- **Timing:** Next morning → late afternoon
- **Starting Location:** Waystone Inn (farewell)
- **Ending Location:** Forward Camp (north of Northreach, 6 miles, 2-3 hours' travel)
- **Duration:** 30–40 minutes (game time, compressed travel)
- **Atmosphere:** Growing trust, hints of wrongness, adventure beginning

### The Departure

**Read-Aloud Text:**

> *Morning light breaks over the Waystone Inn. Snow has settled overnight. Mara brings a packed lunch: bread, cheese, salted meat, waterskins. Brenna shakes your hands: "Come back with results. The Northwatch honors its contractors."*
>
> *You travel south on a well-maintained trade road. The snow melts as morning progresses. By noon, the road curves southeast toward Welton.*
>
> *About two hours of travel brings you to a **creek crossing** where twisted birch trees lean over clear water.*

### The Creek Crossing (Skill Challenge)

**Environment Description:**

> *The creek is perhaps 20 feet wide and 3-4 feet deep. A wooden bridge—merchant-built, solid—crosses it. The crossing is a natural meeting point: travelers rest here, wayfarers camp.*
>
> *But something is wrong.*
>
> *Geometric frost patterns coat the stones beneath the bridge in intricate designs. The frost doesn't melt despite the sunlight. The water flowing underneath is **unnaturally cold**—you can feel the chill from several feet away.*

### Optional Skill Challenge: Crossing

**No mechanical check needed** (party can cross bridge normally), but investigation rewards awareness:

**DC 10 Perception:**
Notice the **frost isn't natural**—it's too geometric, too perfect.

**DC 12 Arcana:**
Sense **faint magical aura** around the stones (same wrongness from campaign's larger mystery).

**DC 13 Survival:**
Recognize that **water this cold would require magical source** (seasonal weather couldn't explain it).

### Meeting the Veteran Scout

**Read-Aloud Text:**

> *As you reach the bridge's midpoint, a figure steps out from behind the trees: a human woman in practical leather armor, bow in hand. She's weathered, middle-aged, and assesses you with the gaze of someone who's survived hard things.*
>
> *"You must be the new Warders. I'm Captain Aldwin—no, wait." She gives a wry smile. "I'm a scout now. Name doesn't matter. I'm here to guide you to forward camp and brief you on Welton. Questions before we move?"*

**The Veteran Scout (NPC Companion)**

**Role:** Mentor, guide, tactical support, fountain of information

**Her Function:**
- Leads party toward Welton (knows the way)
- Provides tactical advice ("Wolves hunt in packs; ambush from flanks")
- Shares information about Welton (settlements, NPCs, local knowledge)
- Can assist in combat (adds support, not dominance)
- Acts as party's "window" into the frontier lifestyle

**Her Personality:**
- Experienced, dry humor, no patience for drama
- Respects competence; judges party on actions
- References past campaigns and veteran experience
- Becomes ongoing contact for future adventures

**Important:** Scout is NOT an NPC party member long-term. She's a guide for this specific arc. After forward camp, she returns to normal duties.

### Optional: Clue Discovery

If party investigates the frost patterns thoroughly:

**A piece of torn cloth hangs from a branch**, caught and partially buried in frost:

> *It's bloodstained, torn recently, and matches the description of clothing that a scout would wear. But more alarming: the cloth is **cold to the touch**, and a faint **humming vibration** emanates from it—feeling metallic, magical, wrong.*

**DC 12 Arcana:** Identify the humming as **magical resonance** (similar to aura but tactile/auditory).

**Implication:** Something magical is corrupting the natural world—trees, stones, cloth. This is the **Aeorian Echo's first manifestation**.

**Scout's Reaction:**
> *The scout's expression hardens. "I've heard reports of this. Three others described the same wrongness. Whatever it is, it's spreading." She carefully wraps the cloth in leather and pockets it. "We report this to Brenna."*

### The Road to Forward Camp (Skill Check)

**Optional Challenge: Road Navigation**

**DC 11 Survival check to maintain pace** (no penalty for failure, just slower travel):
- Success: Party arrives at forward camp by late afternoon
- Failure: Party arrives at forward camp by early evening (affects available interaction time)

**No mechanical penalty for failure**, but time is lost.

### Optional: Bandit Scout Encounter

**If DM wants additional skill-challenge or negotiation practice:**

Two human **Bandit Scouts** emerge from hiding on the road, demanding a "safe passage toll."

**Bandits x2**
- **AC** 12 | **HP** 11 (2d8+2) each | **Speed** 30 ft
- **Challenge** 1/8 (25 XP each)

**Actions:**
- **Shortsword:** +3 to hit | 1d6+1 slashing
- **Crossbow:** +3 to hit | range 80/320 ft | 1d8+1 piercing

**Bandit Motivations:**
- Want money or supplies (not heroic resistance)
- Will **negotiate if party shows strength** or **convinces them**
- Will **flee if combat turns against them** (cowardly, not brave)

**Resolution Options:**
- **Combat:** Simple fight; bandits flee after 1-2 rounds of losing
- **Intimidation (DC 12):** "We're Northwatch Warders; keep moving"
- **Persuasion (DC 13):** "We handle wolves for settlements; we're not treasure hunters"
- **Payment (10 gp):** Bandits accept toll and leave satisfied

**Scout's Commentary (if combat occurs):**
> *"Bandits are cowardly. Real threats are in the wilderness—wolves, cults, worse. These are small-time. Not worth the sword work."*

### Forward Camp Arrival

**Read-Aloud Text:**

## CHAPTER 7: QUICK REFERENCE & TROUBLESHOOTING

### Common Issues & Solutions

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| **Party doesn't want to take the charter/join guild** | Make joining **optional but incentivized** (better contracts, payment, equipment). Party can work independently but loses guild support. |
| **Combat drags on in cold-open** | Use **group rolls** for multiple wolf attacks. Speed up by resolving multiple rounds in single narrative pass. |
| **Party ignores Echo clues** | **Highlight them visually** ("The frost is *right there*, geometric and impossible"). Have Scout directly mention them ("Did you notice the wrongness?") |
| **Party wants to avoid Welton mission** | Allow refusal but **mark reputation down** (Brenna respects it but party is sidelined). Offer alternative contracts (guard duty, supply runs) with lower pay. |
| **Party rests/prepares excessively** | Timeskip with **DM summary** ("You spend the day training and preparing. The next morning..."). Don't let logistics become slow. |
| **One player dominates social encounters** | Redirect: "Brenna asks the *group* what you're thinking, not just you." Give others chances to speak. |

### Scaling Notes

| Party Size | Cold-Open Wolves | Optional Bandits | NPC Mentor Support |
|-----------|----------------|-----------------|--------------------|
| 2 players | 2 (normal) | 1 (or skip) | Strong (Scout fights) |
| 3 players | 2 (normal) | 2 (negotiable) | Moderate (Scout assists skill checks) |
| 4 players | 2 (normal) | 2 (easy) | Minimal (Scout guides only) |
| 5+ players | 3 (increased threat) | 2-3 (escalated) | Scout as mentor-only |
