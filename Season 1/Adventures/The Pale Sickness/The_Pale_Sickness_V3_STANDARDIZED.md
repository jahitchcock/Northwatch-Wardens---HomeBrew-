## Chapter 7: Aftermath & Revelation

### Scene Overview
**Duration:** 30-45 minutes (Session 3, Part 2)
**Type:** Resolution + Revelation
**Challenge:** Return to Palebank, cure infected, receive Elric's debrief
**Stakes:** Save lives, connect mystery dots, launch Arc 2 framework

### Return Journey

**Option 1: Uneventful (Reward for Success)**
Morgo guides party back to the Cold Anchor, then Palebank. No random encounterslet players celebrate victory.

**Option 2: Tension Maintenance (if DM prefers pacing)**
One random encounter on return (ice mephits, saber-toothed tiger) to maintain tension.

**Timeline Reminder:**
- 5 days travel back to Palebank
- Tulgi has ~2-7 days remaining (party should arrive in time if moved efficiently)
- Irven family has ~5-10 days remaining (safe margin)

### Curing the Infected

**Read-Aloud:**
> You return to Palebank Village as the sun sets on the fifth day. Elro meets you at the edge of town, eyes scanning your packs. When he sees the golden vials, relief washes over his face. "You found them. Thank the gods. Come quicklyTulgi is barely holding on."

**Tulgi's Condition (if ~7 days elapsed):**
> Tulgi lies in his cabin, skin blue-white, breath shallow. Ice crystals form on his beard. His eyes barely focus when you enter. "You... you came back..." He can barely speak.

**Administering Antidote:**
> You uncork the golden vial. Warm vapor risessharp contrast to the frozen room. As Tulgi drinks, color floods back into his face. The blue veins recede like ice melting. His breath deepens. Within minutes, he sits up, tears streaming. "I thought I was dead. I felt the cold taking me. Thank you."

**Irven Liel Family (early stage):**
Similar dramatic recovery. Family of four (Irven, wife, two children ages 8 and 12) all cured. Emotional gratitude scenechildren hug party members, wife weeps with relief.

**Moral Choice (if vials limited):**
If party only found ~10 vials instead of 20, DM can create moral tension:
- Cure Tulgi (thief who caused outbreak) vs. Irven's children (innocent victims)?
- Save adults vs. children?
- Use remaining vials for future outbreaks vs. cure everyone now?

### Palebank Gratitude

**Elro's Thanks:**
> "You saved this village. Twiceonce by recovering the vials, again by finding the cure. Palebank won't forget this. You're heroes here."

**Reputation Change:**
- **Palebank Village:** Friendly (future quests available, discounts at shops, free lodging)
- **The Cold Anchor:** Interested (southern cities expedition wants party for future ventures)

**Material Rewards:**
- 200 gp (original contract payment from southern cities buyers)
- 100 gp bonus (from grateful villagerspooled resources)
- *Potion of Greater Healing* (from Elro's personal stash)
- Aevorian artifacts kept by party (Ersatz Eye, historical coins)

### The Revelation: Elric's Debrief

**Transition Hook:**
When party returns to Waystone Inn (1-2 days travel from Palebank), Lorewarden Elric requests immediate debriefing.

**Read-Aloud:**
> Elric's study is cluttered with maps, journals, and Aevorian artifacts. When you mention Salsvault, he goes pale. "Say that again. Salsvault? You found the Aevorian crash site?" He spreads a map of Northreach on the table, marking locations with shaking hands. "Do you realize what this means?"

**Elric's Revelation Speech (CRITICAL CAMPAIGN MOMENT):**
> "Salsvault. Gods above. Aevor was one of the flying cities during the Age of Arcanumthe age when mortals challenged the gods themselves. The gods struck down Aevor during the Great Fall, shattered it, buried it beneath ice. That was over a thousand years ago."
>
> [He marks Salsvault on map, then traces lines to Welton, Pinebrook, Temple of Dragonknights]
>
> "If Salsvault has reactivatedif its magic is leaking againthen everything makes sense. The wolves at Welton: awakened by Aevorian spores. The magical instability across Northreach: arcane feedback from buried ruins. Alexi Merriksonn's death: he was investigating these disturbances, got too close to Salsvault."
>
> [He meets your eyes]
>
> "The Aevorian Echo isn't random. It's spreading from Salsvault. And if Urgon Wenth found it reactivated two months ago... someone triggered it deliberately. Someone wants Aevorian magic active again. The question is: who? And why?"

**What This Reveals to Party:**
-  **Salsvault is the SOURCE** of all magical disturbances in Northreach
-  Wolves awakening = Aevorian spore exposure
-  Alexi's death = investigating Salsvault, killed by magical feedback or guardians
-  Magical instability = Echo spreading from active Aevorian ruins
-  **NEW MYSTERY:** Salsvault was deliberately activated ~2 months ago
-  **NEW THREAT:** Someone seeks Aevorian weapons/magic (god-killing technology)

**Elric's Warning:**
> "The Aevorians built weapons to kill gods. If someone is excavating Salsvault, if they're trying to reactivate Aevorian technology... we're facing something far more dangerous than infected wolves or bandit raids. We need to find out who activated Salsvault. And we need to stop them before they unleash something that could shatter Northreachor worse."

# Conclusion
