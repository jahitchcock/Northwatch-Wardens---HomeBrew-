## CHAPTER 1: ADVENTURE OVERVIEW

### Campaign Context

**Where This Fits**
- **Campaign Arc:** Arc 3 — Convergence: Rising Threats (Part 1 of 1)
- **Best Played After:** Frozen Sick (to recognize magical convergence patterns)
- **Enhanced By:** Wolves of Welton (pattern of creatures exploiting magic)
- **Recommended Party Size:** 3–5 players, Levels 4–5
- **Tone:** Dramatic, faction-focused, dragon-centric

### Adventure Premise

*The Temple of the Dragonknights, an ancient burial ground for legendary dragon slayers, sits atop intersecting ley lines that are amplified by the Aeorian Echo. Venomfang, a young green dragon, senses this magical power and claims the temple as her lair, gathering kobold cultists to serve her ambitions. The party must investigate the threat, navigate the temple's dangers, and ultimately decide Venomfang's fate—knowing that they're witnessing a larger pattern: the Aeorian Echo is creating magical beacons that attract powerful, ambitious creatures.*

### Core Themes

1. **Convergence & Attraction:** Magic awakening draws creatures responding to it (dragons, cults, opportunists).
2. **Ambition Without Morality:** Venomfang isn't evil; she's ambitious and rational—a sympathetic antagonist.
3. **Root Cause vs. Symptom:** Defeating Venomfang doesn't stop the Echo; it will attract other predators.
4. **Faction Competition:** Multiple groups (dragons, cults, wizards) are mobilizing to exploit the same magical surge.

### The Aerorian Echo Connection

*What Players Learn:*

- The Temple is a **magical convergence point** (invisible to untrained eyes but detectable via Detect Magic)
- Salsvault's reactivation has created **ley line amplification** across Northreach
- Venomfang arrived **recently**, explicitly drawn by the magical power
- Kobolds performing rituals are **empowered beyond normal** by the amplified magic
- This is a **pattern**: powerful creatures are sensing and responding to the Echo

*What Remains Hidden:*
- Ultimate source (Salsvault) and how to stop it
- Scope of other convergence points across the region
- Whether Venomfang's intelligence is natural or Echo-influenced
- What other creatures may be responding to similar magical signals

### What Players Know (Before Starting)

- Kobolds have attacked Northcrest townspeople and farms
- The attacks are coordinated and unusual (not typical kobold behavior)
- A dragon may be involved (rumors, witness accounts vague)
- The village needs outside help; local militia is overwhelmed

### Pacing Overview

| Section | Duration | Key Moment |
|---------|----------|-----------|
| **Northcrest Introduction** | 30 min | Kobold attack, Joel's plea, investigation begins |
| **Andersmith Farm Investigation** | 30 min | Evidence gathering, trail discovery, puzzle clues |

| **Venomfang Confrontation** | 45–90 min | Combat OR negotiation OR escape/chase sequence |
| **Resolution & Return** | 15–20 min | Return to Northcrest, debrief, consequences |

**Total: 4–6 hours** (can be split into two 3-hour sessions)

### Common Pacing Issues & Solutions

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| **Cavern exploration feels repetitive** | Compress to 3–4 key encounters instead of 5 areas; use skill checks to skip empty rooms |
| **Multiple kobold battles feel tedious** | Use group rolls; resolve faster than standard combat; emphasize environmental tactics |
| **Players don't feel the stakes** | Repeatedly show echo-affected magic (traps work strangely, rituals are powerful, environment corrupted) |
| **Venomfang fight is anticlimactic** | Build tension before encounter; describe her lair with awe; make her dialogue matter |
| **Investigation stalls between locations** | Provide clear clues (tracks, kobold corpses, bloodstains); hints from NPCs; environmental storytelling |
| **Players ignore temples' mystery** | Plant obvious clues via Investigation checks DC 10; have NPCs mention strange magic; emphasize "ley lines" and "convergence" |

### Running for Different Party Levels

**For Level 3 parties (Struggling):**
- Reduce Venomfang's HP to 100 (from 136)
- Skip green dragon wyrmling (or make it much weaker)
- Have Venomfang flee at 50% HP (doesn't fight to death)
- Reduce kobold numbers by 25%
- Provide more healing potions / short rests

**For Level 4–5 parties (Optimal):**
- Standard adventure as written
- Full Venomfang stats (136 HP)
- All encounters at intended difficulty
- Venomfang is dangerous but not guaranteed victory

**For Level 6+ parties (Overkill):**
- Add reinforcements (additional zombies, more kobolds)
- Give Venomfang legendary actions
- Include green dragon wyrmling as additional encounter
- Increase trap damage and encounter complexity

### Session Zero Guidance

**Confirm with players before starting:**

- [ ] "This is a **capstone adventure** featuringa young dragon and faction conflicts"
- [ ] "Tone: dramatic and morally complex—the dragon isn't mindlessly evil; she's ambitious"
- [ ] "You'll investigate a town under attack, explore ancient temple ruins, and likely face a dragon"
- [ ] "This adventure bridges into a larger mystery: magical power is attracting dangerous creatures"
- [ ] "Combat is possible, but negotiation, stealth, and clever tactics are equally valid"
- [ ] "Expect 4–6 hours of play; this is a significant milestone adventure"

## SCENE 1: NORTHCREST UNDER SIEGE

**Scene Purpose**
Establish the threat, meet key NPCs, introduce the mystery's urgency, and set the foundation for investigation.

**Time & Location**
- **Timing:** Daytime (late morning)
- **Location:** Northcrest (frontier town) — The Flowing Mug Inn, market square
- **Duration:** 30–45 minutes
- **Atmosphere:** Chaos, fear, organized panic; townspeople mobilizing

### Read-Aloud Text: The Attack

> *You arrive in Northcrest, a modest frontier town known for trade routes and farming. The streets are normally quiet—but not today.*
>
> *In the market square, town guards are clashing with humanoid creatures: green-skinned kobolds with crude weapons. Screams echo from nearby buildings. A farmer lies bleeding against the wall of The Flowing Mug Inn, calling for help. The townspeople are organized but overwhelmed.*
>
> *You can sense this is not a random raid.*

### Encounter: Kobolds vs. Town Guards

**Kobolds (4–6, depending on party size)**
- **AC** 12 | **HP** 5 (1d8+1) each | **Speed** 30 ft
- **STR** 9 (-1) | **DEX** 12 (+1) | **CON** 13 (+1) | **INT** 10 (+0) | **WIS** 10 (+0) | **CHA** 8 (-1)
- **Skills** Stealth +3
- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft, Passive Perception 10
- **Languages** Common, Draconic
- **Challenge** 1/8 (25 XP each)

**Special Trait — Pack Tactics:**
Gain advantage on melee attack if ally within 5 feet of target.

**Actions:**
- **Shortsword:** +3 to hit | 1d6+1 slashing
- **Spear (ranged):** +3 to hit | range 20/60 ft | 1d6+1 piercing
- **Trap (improvised):** Kobolds may attempt to trigger alarm or activate traps if present in buildings

### Scaling by Party Size

| Party Size | Kobold Count | Town Guard Support | Difficulty |
|-----------|-------------|-------------------|-----------|
| 2 players | 4 kobolds | 3 guards assist heavily | Medium |
| 3 players | 5 kobolds | 3 guards assist moderately | Medium |
| 4 players | 6 kobolds | 2 guards, mostly defense | Medium |
| 5+ players | 6 kobolds + 1 kobold sergeant | Minimal guard support | Medium-Hard |

**Town Guards (Support NPCs):**
- AC 12, HP 22 each (5d8+5), **CR** 1/8
- Help party by flanking enemies and defending civilians
- Won't leave townspeople to pursue fleeing kobolds
- Can be injured (which adds emotional weight)

### The Scene's Structure

**Round 1-2: Chaos**
- Kobolds fight but aren't suicide-committed
- Guards hold ground but are barely managing
- Party's intervention immediately shifts the odds

**Round 3-5: Momentum Shift**
- Kobolds realize they're losing
- Those at 50% HP or fewer attempt to flee
- Final kobolds retreat toward the forest (north, toward Andersmith Farm)

**After Combat: The Moment**
Once combat concludes, there's a brief silence:

> *blood pools in the cobblestones. The screaming stops. The surviving townspeople stare at you with a mixture of fear and desperate hope. Then someone moves toward the fallen farmer.*

### Meeting Joel Andersmith

Joel Andersmith, a weathered farmer in his 50s, lies against the inn wall with a serious wound on his abdomen.

**Joel's Information (if stabilized or healed):**

- Kobolds attacked his farm north of town, killing his family
- His wife: murdered
- His son: murdered
- His daughter **Clementine**: **taken alive** (unclear why)
- He escaped and ran to town
- Kobolds came from north, from "somewhere deep in the forest"
- The raid felt **organized, not random**—the kobolds knew what they were targeting

**Joel's Emotional State:**
- Devastated, guilt-stricken (he survived; they didn't)
- Angry at the kobolds
- Desperate to find Clementine
- Willing to provide any information if party agrees to help

### Meeting the Town Leaders

**Richard & Lauraine Brown (Innkeepers)**
- Human, mid-60s, warm but terrified
- Offer free lodging to the party in exchange for help
- Provide information: "The kobolds came from the north. Nobody's ever seen them organized like this."

**Dorn Stoutheart (Blacksmith)**
- Human, late 30s, practical
- Offers equipment sales (standard DMG prices, slightly marked up due to panic)
- Mentions: "This isn't banditry. Bandits steal from merchants. These things targeted a family farm specifically."

**Northcrest Guard Captain Aldwin (Militia Leader)**
- Human, mid-40s, experienced but out of depth
- Admits: "We can't follow them into the forest. We need specialists. That's why we're grateful you're here."
- Clarifies terrain: "North through the farmland, then into dense forest. There are caves up there—everyone knows it, but nobody goes."

### The Investigation Begins

Party has several options:

**Option A: Immediate Pursuit**
- Head north toward Andersmith Farm
- Track kobolds while trail is fresh
- Risk: May encounter kobolds before full picture is clear

**Option B: Gather Information First**
- Investigate the crime scene (Andersmith Farm in depth)
- Talk to witnesses
- Gain tactical advantage before cave exploration
- Risk: Clementine is in danger; time matters

**Option C: Prepare & Resupply**
- Buy equipment, healing, provisions
- Talk to more townspeople
- Sleep and rest before venturing into caves
- Risk: Longer delay; increased pressure (time is running out)

**Recommended:** Use Option B (investigate farm, gather clues) into Option C (brief resupply/rest) before cave exploration.

### Transition to Scene 2

Once party commits to helping:

> *Joel clutches your arm: "She's still alive—I know it. I can feel it. Clementine is strong, clever. Bring her back. Please. And I'll... I'll pay you everything I have."*
>
> *Dorn wraps Joel's wound with practiced efficiency. The blacksmith looks at you: "The north path to the farm is clear enough. If there's more of them, you'll find evidence on the way. Mind yourselves—whatever brought them here is still out there."*

## SCENE 2: ANDERSMITH FARM INVESTIGATION

**Scene Purpose**
Gather investigative clues, establish horror and emotional weight, discover the trail to the caves, build environmental storytelling.

**Time & Location**
- **Timing:** Late morning → early afternoon
- **Location:** Andersmith Farm (north of Northcrest, bordering forest)
- **Duration:** 30–45 minutes
- **Atmosphere:** Desolation, grief, wrongness left behind; blood and ash

### Environmental Storytelling: The Scene Unfolds

**Read-Aloud Text:**

> *The farm is destroyed. Smoke still rises from the ruined barn. Crops are trampled and burned. Slaughtered animals lie in fields—goats, chickens, a plow-ox. The smell of blood and smoke hangs heavily.*
>
> *The farmhouse stands on a small hill to the east. Its door hangs from a single hinge. From here, you can see scorched wood and broken windows.*

### Investigation Areas

**The Barn (Primary Evidence Location)**

**DC 10 Perception:** Notice blood trails leading into the forest
**DC 11 Survival:** Track trails (lead toward a hidden thicket northwest)
**DC 12 Investigation:** Find overturned supply barrels (contents: grain, preserved fish, cloth—now scattered)

**Key Discovery:**
> *Claw marks scar the barn's interior—not animal claws, but **deliberate, methodical destruction**. Someone (or something) searched for something: supplies, shelter, or people.*

**The Farmhouse (Secondary Evidence)**

**DC 10 Investigation of Main Room:**
- Signs of struggle (overturned furniture, blood on walls)
- Family's possessions (toys, clothes, food)
- Personal items that suggest who they were

**Left Hallway — Bedrooms:**

**Left Bedroom (Son's room):**
- **Age:** ~12 years old (bed size, toys visible)
- **Contents:** Dead child, slashed wounds, simple shortsword at his side (he tried to defend himself)
- **DC 10 Insight:** This wasn't a quick death; the child fought back

**Right Bedroom (Daughter's room):**
- **Age:** ~16 years old
- **Contents:** Empty (signs of struggle: overturned furniture, bloodstains, torn clothing)
- **DC 12 Investigation:** Drag marks lead toward the back of the house (she was taken alive)
- **Emotional Detail:** A bloodstained teddy bear in the bed (old toy, suggests she kept it despite her age)

**Right Hallway — Bathroom & Master Bedroom:**

**Master Bedroom:**
- **Age:** Adult woman (~40-50) 
- **Contents:** Dead woman (Joel's wife), leaning against bed, slit throat (quick death—suggests mercy killing or priority on moving her out of the way)
- **DC 10 Medicine:** She's been dead 12+ hours (confirms timeline matches Joel's account)

### What the Scene Reveals (Via Investigation)

**DC 10 checks reveal:**
- Kobolds killed the family (not random carnage—targeted)
- Clementine was taken alive (drag marks, missing body)
- The attack happened quickly (family didn't have time to gather supplies)
- Kobolds knew the farm layout (they knew where to find family members)

**DC 12+ checks reveal:**
- Multiple kobolds were involved (different sized footprints)
- Something *larger* was present (large claw marks that would require 15+ foot tall creature)
- The family tried to hide (clothing bundles, scattered possessions suggest packing attempt)
- This was **not a random raid** (deliberate targeting, careful killing, purposeful abduction)

### Emotional Beats & NPC Reactions

If Joel is with party:
> *Joel stands in the doorway, staring at the bodies of his wife and son. His knees buckle. A guard stationed in town (if present) steadies him. He doesn't weep—he's beyond weeping. He just whispers: "Clementine..."*

This moment should hit the table. Children died. A child was abducted. This is **personal**.

### The Trail (Track Kobolds to the Caves)

**Following Blood & Drag Marks:**

**DC 10 Survival:** Party notices trail heading northwest toward forest
**DC 11 Investigation:** Drag marks become harder to spot as terrain becomes wild; vegetation trampled in direction of forest
**DC 12 Investigation:** Spot a piece of torn clothing (matches Clementine's description Joel provided) caught on a branch

**The Thicket (Hidden Entrance):**

Party discovers an **unnaturally dense thicket**—too organized to be natural, deliberately concealing something.

**Two Options:**
- **DC 11 Investigation:** Find a vine connected to a hidden mechanism (pull it; thicket opens — engineered to close behind travelers)
- **DC 13 Strength (Athletics):** Brute-force through thicket (takes time, noisy)

**If path is revealed:**

> *The thicket parts, revealing a narrow path barely 5 feet wide. Vegetation towers on either side. The path clearly goes **deeper into the forest, toward higher ground**. This isn't a natural trail—it's been deliberately maintained. Kobolds have traveled this way before.*

### Transition to Scene 3

Once the trail is found:

> *You have two choices: follow the trail now while light remains, or return to Northcrest to rest and prepare. Behind your decision is the knowledge that Clementine is ahead, perhaps alive, perhaps in danger. Every hour counts.*

## SCENE 3: THE TRAIL TO POISONTIP CAVERN

**Scene Purpose**
Build atmosphere and dread, introduce environmental hazards, deliver mystery clues about the Aeorian Echo, and transition to cavern exploration.

**Time & Location**
- **Timing:** Afternoon (late)
- **Location:** Forest path from farm to cavern, followed by cave entrance area
- **Duration:** 30–45 minutes (including optional encounters)
- **Atmosphere:** Growing darkness, wrong feeling, ancient place stirring

### The Forest Path (Skill Challenge)

Party travels the narrow 5-foot path for perhaps 2 miles. Use this as an opportunity for **environmental storytelling and mood building**.

**DC 10 Perception (Passive check)** detects:
- Bones of previous victims scattered along path (animal bones, possibly humanoid)
- Crude fetishes hung from branches (small skulls, feathers, totems—kobold ritual items)
- The air becomes **colder** as they travel (anomalous for the time of year)

**DC 12 Perception detects:**
- Geometric frost patterns on some plants and stones (the same **unnatural ice** from earlier campaign mysteries)
- This frost doesn't melt despite sunlight
- Makes party realize: **Something magical is happening here**

**DC 13 Arcana check** (if someone casts Detect Magic or similar):
> *You sense a concentration of magical aura, growing stronger as you travel deeper. It's old, powerful, and rhythmic—like a heartbeat. The magic feels **wrong**, not aligned with any school you recognize.*

### Environmental Hazard: The Creek

After 2 miles, party reaches a **narrow creek crossing** with slippery stones.

**Description:**
> *A creek cuts across the path, perhaps 15 feet wide and 3-4 feet deep. Smooth, slippery stones provide stepping stones, but they're moss-covered and treacherous. The water is cold, flowing from upstream where mist rises (anomalously cold for the season).*

**Skill Check Required:**
- **DC 12 Acrobatics (Dexterity)** to cross safely
- **DC 11 Athletics (Strength)** to wade through water
- Magic users can use levitation/water-walking (automatic success)

**Consequences of Failure:**
- Fall into creek (takes 1d6 cold damage per failure, slowing progress)
- Equipment may get damaged (rolls on items become disadvantage until dried)
- Noise of splashing alerts nearby creatures (kobold scouts may investigate in 1d4 hours)

**Optional: Quippers or Creatures**
If party splashes heavily:
- **DC 10 Perception:** Notice small fish darting in the creek (quippers, harmless unless party actively tries to bathe)
- Atmospheric detail: water is unnaturally cold (reinforces Echo theme)

### Optional: Bandit Scouts Encounter

**If party made significant noise or DM wants additional combat:**

Two human **Bandits** emerge from hiding on the far side of the creek, demanding a "road toll."

**Bandit x2** (CR 1/8 each)
- **AC** 12 | **HP** 11 (2d8+2) each | **Speed** 30 ft
- **Challenge** 1/8 (25 XP each)

**Actions:**
- **Shortsword:** +3 to hit | 1d6+1 slashing
- **Crossbow:** +3 to hit | range 80/320 ft | 1d8+1 piercing

**Bandits' Goal:** Extract coin/goods; **not** interested in actual combat if party shows strength

**Social Resolution:**
- **DC 12 Persuasion:** "We're on an urgent rescue mission; we can't afford delays."
- **DC 13 Intimidation:** "We have bigger problems to handle; move aside."
- **DC 10 Deception:** "We'll return with treasure; let us pass and we'll compensate you."

**Combat Resolution:**
- Use terrain (creek, stones) to advantage
- Bandits have crossbows (range advantage) but are outmatched if party closes
- After first round of combat or at 50% HP, **bandits flee**

**Mystery Clue (Optional):**
If party defeats or successfully negotiates with bandits, check dropped supplies:
- Metal buckle or coin with strange **humming vibration** (cold to touch)
- **DC 13 Arcana:** Identify the vibration as magical, similar to the aura sensed earlier
- **Implication:** The Echo's influence extends to the creatures it affects (via corrupted items, amplified magic)

### The Cavern Entrance

**Read-Aloud Text:**

> *The forest opens into a small clearing. The creek drops into darkness below. Before you stands the entrance to a massive cavern—an opening in the rock face perhaps 30 feet wide and 20 feet tall, ringed with dripping stone. Torchlight flickers deep inside.*
>
> *Crude totems line the entrance: skulls impaled on spikes, fetishes and kobold artwork screaming "DANGER." The smell of smoke and something worse (burned meat, filth, decay) drifts outward.*
>
> *You can hear voices echoing from within: guttural whispers, something larger moving in the dark, and... is that screaming?*

### Final Decision Point

Before entering the cavern, party should rest and prepare:

**Questions to Prompt:**
- "Do you rest tonight and enter at dawn, or press forward now?"
- "Do you want to scout the entrance first, or prepare for direct entry?"
- "Does anyone have abilities that could give advantage: darkvision, flight, invisibility?"

**If party rests outside cavern:**
- They may hear sounds from within (additional dread)
- Kobolds sleep in shifts; party can gain surprise with good Stealth
- Morning investigation reveals more (footprints, blood trails)

**If party enters immediately:**
- Fatigue penalties (disadvantage on Wisdom saves if night travel)
- But immediate action may save Clementine from immediate harm
- More dramatic tension (what's happening to her right now?)

**Narrator's Note:**
Lean into the dread. The cavern is dangerous. The dragon feels powerful, even from exterior. Clementine is in danger upstairs. There's no "right" choice—only different paths forward.

## SCENE 4: POISONTIP CAVERN EXPLORATION

**Scene Purpose**
Navigate expanding dungeon complexity, solve environmental puzzles, manage multiple combat encounters, and build toward the temple revelation.

**Time & Location**
- **Timing:** Night (if they enter immediately) or dawn (if they rest first)
- **Location:** Poisontip Cavern (multi-level complex: 3-5 key areas)
- **Duration:** 60–90 minutes
- **Atmosphere:** Underground oppression, kobold civilization, ritual preparation

### Cavern Overview & Scaling

The cavern has **5 distinct areas** (I'll present core areas and suggest compression options):

| Area | Primary Feature | Encounter | Puzzle | Treasure |
|------|-----------------|-----------|--------|---------|
| **1** | Winged Kobold Guard Post | 1 winged kobold (flying) | Rickety bridge over lake | Puzzle parchment (Dragonknight clue) |
| **2** | Sleeping Quarters | 3 kobolds (sleeping) | Secret door | 25 gp, 2 Potions of Healing |
| **3** | Underground Lake | Environmental hazard | Bridge crossing (Acrobatics) | Connection to multiple areas |
| **4** | Treasure Chamber | 1 piercer trap over chest | Trap detection | 25 gp (treasuretrove) |
| **5** | Ritual Chamber | None initially | Offering bowl puzzle (summons door) | Pathway to temple |

### For Faster Play (3-4 hours):

**Compress cavern to 3 key encounters:**
1. **Winged Kobold + Bridge Crossing** (Area 1)
2. **Sleeping Kobolds OR Secret Door Discovery** (Area 2)
3. **Ritual Chamber Puzzle** (Area 5)
Skip Areas 3-4 or combine them with Area 1

**For Full Exploration (4-6 hours):**
Run all 5 areas as written below

### AREA 1: The Entrance Guard Post

**Read-Aloud Text:**

> *The cavern interior is surprisingly organized. Torches in crude sconces illuminate carved stone passages. The air is thick with smoke and the smell of roasted meat.*
>
> *Immediately, you see movement above: a **winged kobold** perches on a ledge 15 feet up, watching the entryway. It shrieks an alarm, wings spreading.*

**Encounter: Winged Kobold Draconic (CR 1/4)**
- **AC** 13 (leather + dex) | **HP** 8 (2d6+1) | **Speed** 30 ft, fly 30 ft
- **STR** 9 (-1) | **DEX** 14 (+2) | **CON** 13 (+1) | **INT** 10 (+0) | **WIS** 10 (+0) | **CHA** 8 (-1)
- **Skills** Stealth +4
- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft
- **Languages** Common, Draconic
- **Challenge** 1/4 (50 XP)

**Special Trait — Flight:**
Can fly up and avoid melee; will position itself on high ledges

**Actions:**
- **Shortsword:** +4 to hit | 1d6+2 slashing (rarely uses in combat; prefers flying)
- **Spear (ranged):** +4 to hit | range 20/60 ft | 1d6+2 piercing

**Kobold's Tactic:**
- Shrieks alarm (alerts 1d4 additional kobolds in adjacent areas)
- Flies to high position and pelts party with spear
- If damaged, **flees deeper into cavern** to warn others
- Won't stands and fight (self-preservation)

**Scaling by Party Size:**
- **2 players:** Winged kobold alone
- **3 players:** + 1 regular kobold (enters fight from adjacent passage)
- **4+ players:** + 2 regular kobolds (area support)

### The Rickety Bridge (Environmental Challenge)

**Encounter Escalates:** If party tries to pursue the fleeing winged kobold, they discover its escape route: **a rickety wooden bridge spanning an underground lake**.

**Description:**
> *A massive underground lake stretches below—perhaps 50 feet deep. Stalagmites pierce the water like spears. A wooden bridge, clearly kobold-made and poorly maintained, stretches across the lake. Ropes are strung as handholds, but several are frayed. The bridge sways with every step.*

**Skill Check Required:**
- **DC 12 Acrobatics** to cross balance-beam style (requires light armor, no shields)
- **DC 11 Athletics** to cross carefully holding ropes (slower but more reliable)
- Magic levitation/flight: automatic success

**Failure Consequences:**
- **Miss by 1-4:** Slip but catch rope (no fall, no damage, embarrassed)
- **Miss by 5+:** Fall into lake below
  - Take 1d6 normal fall damage + 1d6 piercing  from stalagmites (lucky landing)
  - Or 2d6 piercing if landing on stalagmites (unlucky)
  - Lake has **quippers** (harmless fish, atmosphere only)

**Puzzle Clue on Bridge:**
Halfway across, a **torn parchment** hangs from a stalagmite spike:

> *Reading it (DC 10 Arcana): It's a torn page from an ancient tome, bearing the **emblem of the Dragonknights** (a snake-creature wrapped around a sword) and text in Draconic:*
>
> *"We brought the girl to the robed ones. Not sure why they wanted her, but whatever it takes to please the master."*

**Implication:** Someone **above** (in the temple) wants Clementine specifically. This isn't about ransom—it's ritual purpose.

### AREA 2: Sleeping Quarters (Kobold Camp)

After the bridge, party reaches the kobolds' **sleeping and living quarters**.

**Description:**
> *The cavern opens into a large chamber. Crude tents are arranged in a semicircle. A fire pit in the center contains dying embers. Three sleeping forms are visible in/near the tents. Supplies are stacked in corner: stolen barrels from Northcrest (foodstuffs, tools, cloth).*

**Encounter: Sleeping Kobolds (2-3, depending on party size)**

If party was loud/alert, **kobolds are awake and ready** (surprise is lost).
If party was sneaky, **kobolds are sleeping** (surprise is gained).

**Kobold x3** (CR 1/8 each)

- **North tent:** 25 gp, a torn parchment (see Bridge puzzle above), other mundane items

Secondary lake chamber (optional, bypassed if bridge is used). Function: atmospheric, provides alternative path, contains trap.

### AREA 4: The Ritual Chamber (Dragonknight Offering)

**Description:**
> *The cavern rises toward a set of **massive stone double doors** carved with the emblem of the Dragonknights (snake wrapped around sword). Before the doors: an **ornate stone offering bowl** filled with gold coins. Magical torches (unlit, but not requiring fuel) flank the doors. The chamber feels **ancient** — the air is cold and weighted with age.*

**The Puzzle: Bloodprice (Dragonknight Ward):**

The doors are sealed by an ancient **Dragonknight ward** requiring blood initiation (or violence to break).

**Solution 1: Blood Offering (Intended)**
- A party member cuts their hand (1 HP damage) and bleeds into the offering bowl
- The blood ignites in supernatural flames (no damage to caster)
- The bowl glows; the double doors **swing open**

**Solution 2: Dispel Magic (Arcane)**
- **DC 15 Arcana check** to identify and dismantle the magical seal
- Requires 1 minute of concentration
- Success opens doors without damage

**Solution 3: Brute Force (Class Skill)**
- **DC 16 Strength (Athletics)** to break doors with weapons/magic
- Doors are ancient stone (very tough)
- Breaking alerts creatures deeper in temple (adds time complications)
- Doors take 1d8+4 damage before opening (resource-intensive)

**Lore Implication (if players investigate):**
- Dragonknight emblem appears throughout cavern (this is prep for **Temple above**)
- The ward is designed to test blood commitment ("Only those willing to risk themselves may enter")
- **Inscription on bowl (DC 12 History):** "Sealed by divine will. Do not disturb. — Dragonknights, Age of the Calamity"

**Skill Check (Optional):**

## SCENE 5: TEMPLE OF THE DRAGONKNIGHTS EXPLORATION

**Scene Purpose**
Navigate the temple's multiple chambers, discover the ritual prepared for Clementine, face cultist encounters, and build toward Venomfang's lair.

**Time & Location**
- **Timing:** Dusk (cavern passage took several hours)
- **Location:** Temple of the Dragonknights (ancient burial site, 3 key levels)
- **Duration:** 60–90 minutes
- **Atmosphere:** Sacred profaned, ancient slumber disturbed, ritual power building

### Temple Overview

The temple has **6 key areas** (presenting core chambers; can compress to 4 for faster play):

| Area | Feature | Encounter | Significance |
|------|---------|-----------|-------------|
| **1** | Burial Chamber (Dragonknights) | Zombie encounter | Desecration puzzle |
| **2** | Armory (Torture Scene) | Cultist + rescue Sera | Moral choice point |
| **3** | Ritual Frieze (History) | Investigation | Lore discovery |
| **4** | Cult Assembly Hall | Cultist ritual | Clementine in danger |
| **5** | Private Chambers | Investigation | Supplies/history |
| **6** | Upper Lair (Dragon's Chamber) | Venomfang final encounter | Climax |

### AREA 1: The Burial Chamber (Dragonknights' Tombs)

**Read-Aloud Text:**

> *The chamber is circular, perhaps 60 feet across. Four massive stone coffins are arranged in cardinal directions. Each bears a carved effigy of a warrior in Dragonknight armor, weapons resting on their chests. The stone craftsmanship is exquisite—these were heroes, once.*
>
> *But the chamber has been **defiled**. Crude symbols are painted on the walls. Kobold refuse litters the floor. And in the center, disturbed as though recently opened: **broken stone, claw marks, blood**.*

**The Trap/Encounter: Risen Dragonknight Zombies**

Standing on the central emblem triggers the ward:

> *As your boots touch the carved emblem, the four coffins **erupt with unnatural light**. Stone lids are thrust aside. Four figures rise—skeletal warriors, still in ancient armor, moving with undead purpose.*

**Zombie x4 (Dragonknight Variants, Nerfed)**
- **AC** 12 (armor) | **HP** 11 (2d8+2) each (weaker than standard zombie)
- **STR** 13 (+1) | **DEX** 10 (+0) | **CON** 14 (+2) | **INT** 3 (-4) | **WIS** 8 (-1) | **CHA** 5 (-3)
- **Damage Immunities** Poison
- **Condition Immunities** Charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned
- **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft
- **Challenge** 1/8 (25 XP each)

**Actions:**
- **Greataxe (nerfed damage):** +1 to hit | 1d6+1 slashing (instead of standard 1d12+1)
- **Slam (weak):** +1 to hit | 1d4 bludgeoning

**Zombie Behavior:**
- **Undead Fortitude:** If reduced below 8 HP, make **DC 10 Wisdom save** or drop to 0 HP instead of running away (they stay animated briefly)
- **Slow Movement:** Zombies move at 20 ft (slower than party)
- **Mindless:** No tactics, just shambling attacks

**Scaling by Party Size:**
- **2 players:** 3 zombies only
- **3 players:** 4 zombies (standard)
- **4+ players:** 4 zombies (party should handle this easily)

**Tactics:**
- Let the party **dominate** this encounter (building confidence before Venomfang)
- Zombies are slow enough to be engaged at range
- Good opportunity to show control and tactics matter

### The Desecration Puzzle

**DC 12 Religion or Investigation:** Notice that the coffins have been **deliberately broken from the outside** (not the inside). The corpses were not naturally rising—they were magically forced upward by the cultists.

**Implication:** 
- The cultists are using profane magic to corrupt sacred ground
- Each desecrated tomb **weakens ancient wards** (Dragonknight seals that kept something beneath the temple contained)
- This is important for the campaign's long-term mystery

### AREA 2: The Armory (Torture Chamber)

**Read-Aloud Text:**

> *This chamber holds the military equipment of long-dead warriors. Weapon racks line the walls—spears, swords, shields, many engraved with the Dragonknight emblem. But the room's purpose has changed.*
>
> *In the chamber's center, suspended by crude magical bonds, is a **young woman**—an elf, perhaps 20 years old, wearing torn robes. She's been beaten, starved. Her name, if you ask later, is **Sera Gelanadel**, a wizard's apprentice from Waterdeep.*
>
> *Standing over her is a **robed cultist**, chanting in Draconic, a crude dagger poised at her arm.*

### Encounter: Cultist Torturer

**Cultist (CR 1/4)**
- **AC** 12 | **HP** 13 (3d8) | **Speed** 30 ft
- **Challenge** 1/4 (50 XP)

**Actions:**
- **Dagger:** +2 to hit | 1d4 slashing
- **Cantrip (Pain):** DC 11 Wisdom save or take 1 psychic damage (mostly for roleplay/torture)

**Special:** This cultist is **fanatical**. They **will not surrender**. They'll fight until death or flee to warn others.

**Scaling by Party Size:**
- **2 players:** 1 cultist alone
- **3 players:** 1 cultist + 1 kobold who heard the alarm
- **4+ players:** 1 cultist + 2 kobolds

### Rescuing Sera Gelanadel

Once the cultist is defeated:

> *The magical bonds dissolve. Sera falls to her knees, gasping. She's weak, injured, but alive.*
>
> *"Thank the gods," she whispers. "I was sent to investigate the temple's magical resonance. I found... something. The magic here is wrong. Old. And something is waking it up. But first—where's Clementine? The young girl? They were preparing something for her upstairs..."*

**Sera's Information:**
- She's a **1st-level wizard apprentice** (not useful in combat but adds to party)
- She sensed the temple is sitting atop **converging ley lines** (confirmation of earlier Echo-sensing)
- The cultists are preparing a **ritual to amplify the ley lines' power**
- Clementine is needed for the ritual (as sacrifice or focus; Sera isn't entirely sure)
- Venomfang is **upstairs** in the upper temple (confirmed dragon presence)

**Sera as NPC Companion:**
- Can cast cantrips and 1st-level spells
- Moderate HP (8, representing weakness from torture)
- Won't be leading the party; will follow and support
- Provides narrative value (outside perspective on the temple's magical nature)

### Artifact Discovery

**DC 10 Investigation of weapon racks** reveals a **+1 spear of the Dragonknights** (or +1 weapon of party's choice — DM should adjust to party's needs):

> *One spear stands apart, its craftsmanship finer than the rest. The haft is carved with Dragonknight runes, and the head glows faintly with residual magic. This is a**+1 Spear of the Dragonknights**.* 

### AREA 3: Ritual Frieze (History Chamber)

**Read-Aloud Text:**

> *This chamber contains a single, enormous carved frieze spanning the entire southern wall. The artwork depicts a **epic tale of dragonslaying**. Fierce warriors in Dragonknight armor battle **three massive dragons**—one red, one blue, one green. The dragons are piercing fortifications while the Dragonknights defend.*
>
> *The craftsmanship is masterful. You feel the weight of history here.*

**Historical Investigation (Optional but Rewarding):**

**DC 12 History or Arcana:** The frieze depicts a **Post-Calamity War** between the Dragonknights and surviving Aeorian constructs (the dragons are likely **dragon-like magical automatons**, not true dragons).

**DC 15 History (Deep Lore):** The battles shown are from the **Age of the Calamity** (ancient history, 1000+ years ago). The Dragonknights were **protectors who kept ancient things sealed beneath the earth**.

**DC 18 Investigation (Hidden Groove):** Discover a **hidden alcove carved into the stone**. Inside: a **platinum ring worth 250 gp** (no magical properties, just treasure).

**Implication for Campaign:**
The temple was built to **seal something beneath the earth** — the same ancient facility (Salsvault) that party will discover later. The Dragonknights' purpose was guardianship.

### AREA 4: The Cult Assembly Hall (Ritual in Progress)

**Read-Aloud Text:**

> *The chamber opens into an enormous space—clearly once a meeting hall for Dragonknight generals. Now, it's been reshaped into a **cult ritual ground**.*
>
> *A massive ritualistic circle is carved into the floor—concentric rings inscribed with Draconic runes. At the center, **suspended by magical threads of light**, is a young **girl**—Clementine Andersmith. She's conscious but magically bound, screaming soundlessly (the magic prevents her voice from reaching you).*
>
> *Standing in the outer circle: **5 robed cultists** chanting in unison, hands raised, pouring magical energy into the central sphere. The very air **crackles with power**. Ley lines are being channeled through the platform—you can *feel* it.*

### Encounter: Cult Ritual & Cultists

**The Situation:**
- Cultists are **concentrated on the ritual** (surprise is easy if party enters quietly)
- Interrupting the ritual causes **magical backlash** (environmental hazard)
- Cultists will **fight defensively,** trying to complete ritual while protecting themselves

**Cultist x5 (CR 1/4 each)**
- **AC** 12 | **HP** 13 (3d8) each
- **Challenge** 1/4 (50 XP each)
- **Special:** All cultists are **fanatical** (fight to death or until clear victory for party)

**Actions:**
- **Dagger:** +2 to hit | 1d4 slashing
- **Arcane Bolt (Cantrip):** +4 to hit | 1d8 force damage (range 120 ft)

**Ritual Mechanics:**

If cultists continue uninterrupted for 3 more rounds:
- Clementine is **transformed** by the ritual
- **DC 15 Insight:** Damage is permanent (removal requires Greater Restoration or similar)
- Party has **motivation to interrupt immediately**

**Interrupting the Ritual:**

Attacking cultists or dispelling the magic causes:
- All cultists **immediately disengage** from ritual and focus entirely on combat
- The ritual circle **destabilizes**—environmental hazard:
  - **Turn 1:** Magical discharge (all creatures within 30 feet must make **DC 14 Dexterity save** or take 2d6 force damage)
  - **Turn 2-3:** Ceiling **rumbles and shakes** (non-lethal, but dramatic)
  - **Turn 4**: Ritual chamber is safe again

**Scaling by Party Size:**
- **2 players:** 4 cultists (reduced for survivability)
- **3 players:** 5 cultists (standard)
- **4+ players:** 5 cultists (party should overwhelm)

### Rescuing Clementine

Once the cultists are defeated/fled:

The magical bonds release. Clementine falls gently to the ground.

> *Clementine gasps, eyes wide. She's traumatized but unharmed. She clings to whoever rescues her, sobbing: "Father? Is my father...? My mother and brother... I heard them screaming..."*

**Emotional Beat:**
This is the party's **moral payoff**. They've rescued an innocent child from a ritualabduction. Let them have this moment.

## SCENE 6: VENOMFANG & THE FINAL CONFRONTATION

**Scene Purpose**
Climactic encounter with the young dragon, moral complexity, multiple resolution paths, revelation of the Echo's role in attracting dangerous creatures.

**Time & Location**
- **Timing:** Dusk → Dusk (final chamber, final battle)
- **Location:** Temple Upper Lair (Venomfang's chamber)
- **Duration:** 45–90 minutes (depending on combat or negotiation)
- **Atmosphere:** Awe, danger, dragon presence, hoard glittering

### The Approach

**Read-Aloud Text:**

> *A final spiral staircase leads upward to a chamber far larger than the temple below. You emerge onto a **marble platform overlooking a massive vaulted space**.*
>
> *The chamber is flooded with golden light from crystalline formations in the ceiling. At the chamber's center, atop a **hoard of gold and treasures**, lies a **young green dragon**.*
>
> *She is beautiful and terrible: scales gleaming like emeralds, wings folded, eyes sharp with intelligence. As you appear, those eyes **open and fix on you**.*

### Venomfang's First Words

**Read-Aloud Text:**

> *"Adventurers," the dragon says, her voice a rich purr layered with danger. "How... expected. You've slaughtered my servants, disturbed my lair, and now you stand before me on MY pedestal. Brave or foolish? Perhaps both."*
>
> *She rises slightly, wings unfurling. The chamber echoes with her movement. "I sensed your coming the moment you crossed the threshold. My domain. My power. And now, you stand before **Venomfang, Emerald Sovereign**."*

### Venomfang's Stat Block (Complete)

**Venomfang (Young Green Dragon, CR 8)**

- **AC** 18 (natural armor) | **HP** 136 (16d10+48) | **Speed** 40 ft, fly 80 ft, swim 40 ft
- **STR** 19 (+4) | **DEX** 12 (+1) | **CON** 17 (+3) | **INT** 16 (+3) | **WIS** 13 (+1) | **CHA** 15 (+2)
- **Saving Throws** DEX +4, CON +6, WIS +4
- **Skills** Deception +5, Insight +4, Perception +7, Stealth +4
- **Damage Immunities** Poison
- **Condition Immunities** Charmed, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned
- **Senses** Blindsight 30 ft, darkvision 120 ft, Passive Perception 17

- **Languages** Common, Draconic
- **Challenge** 8 (3,900 XP)

**Special Traits:**

***Amphibious.*** Venomfang can breathe air and water.

***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If Venomfang fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.

***Innate Spellcasting.*** Venomfang's spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 14). She can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
- At-will: Mage Hand, Minor Illusion
- 3/day: Suggestion
- 1/day: Hold Person

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** Venomfang makes three attacks: one bite and two claws.

***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 15 (2d10+4) piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a **DC 14 Constitution save** or take 7 (2d6) poison damage.

***Claw.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6+4) slashing damage.

***Poison Breath (Recharge 5-6).*** Venomfang exhales poisonous gas in a **30-foot cone**. Each creature in that area must make a **DC 14 Constitution save**, taking 54 (12d8) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

### Legendary Actions

Venomfang can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Venomfang regains spent legendary actions at the start of her turn.

***Detect.*** Venomfang makes a Perception check.

***Claw Attack.*** Venomfang attacks with a claw.

***Wing Attack (Costs 2 Actions).*** Venomfang beats her wings. Each creature within 10 feet of Venomfang must succeed on a **DC 15 Dexterity save** or take 11 (2d6+4) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone. Venomfang can then fly up to half her flying speed.

### Combat: Three Potential Paths

**Path A: Direct Combat**

**Round 1-2: Dragon's Tactics**
- Venomfang uses Poison Breath immediately (recharge allows 1 use per 2 rounds)
- Then flies to **high perch** (ceiling at 60 feet — out of easy reach)
- Uses Claw attack if party approaches

**Round 3-5: Escalation**
- Continues Bite + Claw (multiattack) while maintaining flight
- Uses Legendary Actions to increase attacks
- Environment: Hoard piles provide **half-cover** (party can hide within, reducing Venomfang's effectiveness)

**Dragon's Escape Condition:**
- **At 50% HP (68 HP):** Venomfang **does NOT** fight to death
- She either:
  1. **Offers negotiation** ("You've proven yourself. Leave now, and I'll spare your miserable town")
  2. **Attempts to flee** through a **collapsed ceiling passage** (she can fit; party cannot easily follow)

**Recommended DM Approach:** Let Venomfang fight hard for 2-3 rounds, then allow the party to **force the issue** (negotiate, drive her away, or achieve tactical victory). Don't let this drag into a 10-round slog.

**Path B: Negotiation (Social Encounter)**

**Initial Negotiation DC:**
- **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion):** Appeal to her ambition("Why risk death against us? Claim the temple and move on.")
- **DC 13 Charisma (Intimidation):** Show strength ("You're outnumbered and outmaneuvered. We can end this if you push.")
- **DC 12 Charisma (Deception):** Lie ("There are dragon slayers coming from the west. Flee and live to fight better.")

**Venomfang's Response:**

If party succeeds on negotiation:

> *Venomfang's eyes narrow. She descends slightly, wings folding. "You are... interesting. Not terrified. Not stupid enough to die quickly. Very well. I will leave this region. In exchange, you will grant me two conditions:*
>
> *First: One magic item from your possession — as tribute to your victor. Second: You will not hunt me. I will not hunt your pathetic settlements. We part as... rivals with mutual respect."*

**This is a VALID ENDING.** Venomfang leaves, the party has won without lethal combat, and they've learned that dragons are individuals with agency.

**Variant:** Party can negotiate other terms (payment, safe passage elsewhere, dragon alliance under specific conditions).

**Path C: Escape / Collapse the Temple**

**Clever Destruction:**
Party realizes they can't win a straight fight. Alternative approach:

**DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation):** Notice that the temple's pillars are **fractured** from recent ritual magic — destabilized.

**DC 13 Strength (Athletics):** Topple a pillar, collapsing the chamber.

**Result:**
- Venomfang **can fly and escape** (moving ceiling doesn't kill her)
- But her hoard is **partially buried** and inaccessible
- Her lair is **destroyed**; she's forced to relocate
- Party achieves victory (denied her power base) without lethal combat

## CHAPTER 8: COMMON QUESTIONS & TIPS

### For New DMs

**Q: "Venomfang is a CR 8 monster. My Level 3 party will get destroyed!"**
A: Yes. This adventure is level 4-5 recommended for a reason. If you absolutely must run it for Level 3 party:
- Reduce her HP to 100 (from 136)
- Have her flee at 25% HP (not 50%)
- Give additional healing potions/short rests
- Emphasize negotiation and environmental tactics

**Q: "The cultist ritual feels railroaded — party has limited time. Is that intentional?"**
A: Yes. The 3-round timer creates **urgency** and **moral weight**. It prevents the encounter from being purely mechanical. Party should feel the pressure to choose: fight faster, negotiate harder, or accept Clementine's transformation. This is intentional design.

**Q: "My party wants to befriend Venomfang instead of fighting her. Should I allow it?"**
A: Absolutely. Negotiation is a valid victory. Make the DC high (DC 14 Persuasion), but allow success. This demonstrates player agency and morality-rich gameplay. Dragons are individuals; they don't have to die.

**Q: "How do I make this feel like a climax, not just another encounter?"**
A: Use **atmosphere, NPCs, and stakes**. Describe Venomfang with awe (beautiful, terrible, intelligent). Have Sera provide magical perspective on the ley line power. Most importantly: **remind the table that this is the culmination of everything learned so far** — the Echo brought them here, the cultists prepared this, Clementine's life hangs in the balance.

### For Experienced DMs: Depth & Variations

**Q: "Can Venomfang become a complex character arc extending across multiple campaigns?"**
A: Absolutely. If party negotiates/drives her away (doesn't kill her), Venomfang can:
- Return in future campaigns with better preparation
- Ask for party's aid against a greater threat (forcing moral choice)
- Become unexpected ally (if party's reputation grows)
- Develop as individual character (ambitions beyond mere territorial expansion)

**Q: "What if party tries something I didn't plan — like poisoning Venomfang's hoard?"**
A: **Allow creative solutions**: Poisoned gold, trapped hoard, magical contamination — all these change the encounter dynamics. If poison works, Venomfang gets sick/weakened (advantage on next attack roll against her). Reward creative thinking while maintaining challenge.

**Q: "How can I make the cult feel like genuine threat, not just minions?"**
A: Play cultists as **fanatical believers**, not mercenaries. They quote prophecies, refuse surrender, continue the ritual even as comrades die. When one dies, another steps in ("Our mistress will rise!"). This shows the ritual's *power* — creatures are willing to *sacrifice* for it. That's menacing.

**Q: "Can I tie Salsvault more directly into this adventure?"**
A: Yes. Have **Sera recognize Aeorian architecture** in the temple's lower sections. Have **cultists speak of contacting entities below**. Drop a *clue that the ritual is a *downward conduit* — attempting to communicate with or awaken something beneath the temple. This bridges Temple to future Salsvault-specific adventure.
