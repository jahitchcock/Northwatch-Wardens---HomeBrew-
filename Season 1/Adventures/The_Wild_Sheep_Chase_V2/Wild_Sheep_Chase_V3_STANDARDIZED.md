## CHAPTER 1: ADVENTURE OVERVIEW

### Campaign Context

**Where This Fits**
- **Campaign Arc:** Arc 2 — Revelation: The Hidden Truth (Part 2 of 2)
- **Follows:** Frozen Sick (optional but thematic)
- **Precedes:** Temple of the Dragonknights
- **Standalone Play:** Excellent as one-shot or palate cleanser
- **Recommended Party Size:** 2–5 players, Levels 1–2

### Adventure Premise

*A cultured elf wizard has been trapped in the body of a sheep for two years. His ambitious apprentice controls a magical wand that's supposed to transform creatures into stone—but the wand is malfunctioning, creating bizarre transformations instead. When the wizard finally escapes and finds adventurers, he begs them to help recover his wand and restore his true form. But the apprentice won't give it up without a fight, and his tower is defended by enchanted objects that are behaving unpredictably.*

This adventure provides comic relief while reinforcing a critical campaign mystery: magical artifacts are destabilizing. The Wand of True Polymorph's malfunction is proof that something is interfering with magic across the frontier.

### Core Themes

1. **Magic is Unreliable:** Not all problems have straightforward magical solutions. Enchanted items can fail.
2. **Absurdity as Horror:** The comedy of a sheep-wizard contrasts with the danger: what if YOUR magic suddenly stopped working?
3. **Ambition & Obsession:** Noke's desire for power blinded him to the consequences.
4. **Second Chances:** Helping Shinebright opens dialogue with a powerful ally.

### The Aeorian Echo Connection

*What Players Learn:*

- Magical items and enchantments across Northreach are **malfunctioning**
- The Wand of True Polymorph was meant to petrify; instead it polymorphs randomly
- External magical interference is **corrupting** even high-level artifacts
- If this continues, every wizard's tools become unreliable (major threat)

*What Remains Hidden:*
- Source of the interference (Salsvault — revealed in Frozen Sick)
- Scope of artifact failures (widespread across the region)
- Whether the malfunction can be reversed (campaign-ongoing mystery)

### What Players Know (Before Starting)

- A wizard was turned into a sheep by his apprentice
- The apprentice has a powerful wand
- The wizard needs help getting the wand back
- There's something strange about the wand's behavior (it's not working right)

### Common Pacing Issues & Solutions

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| **Guz fight drags** | Resolve in 1–2 rounds; let party dominate or escape quickly |
| **Tower infiltration feels like dungeon crawl** | Emphasize comedy: guards behave erratically, trapped objects argue, Shinebright comments snarkily |
| **Players don't want to fight Noke** | Allow negotiation; he'll agree to peaceful terms if intimidated, but remain paranoid and bitter |
| **Wand backfire too random** | Pre-roll wand effects; don't let randomness derail pacing |
| **Shinebright overshadows party** | Give him 1–2 meaningful moments, then stay supportive (suggests tactics, complains, doesn't fight) |

### Running as a Palate Cleanser

**For use between heavier adventures:**

- Resolve Noke fight quickly (wand backfires, combat ends)
- Focus on Shinebright's gratitude and humor
- Estimated time: 2–3 hours instead of 3–4

## SCENE 1: THE ACCIDENTAL MESSENGER

**Scene Purpose**
Introduce the adventure, establish the tone (comedy + stakes), meet Shinebright, understand the core problem.

**Time & Location**
- **Timing:** Downtime in town; party resting/drinking/wandering
- **Location:** Tavern, inn, marketplace, or street
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Atmosphere:** Chaos breaks loose; humor, urgency, and strangeness

### Read-Aloud Text: The Sheep Arrives

> *You're [at the tavern / walking through town / resting at the inn] when a sound like hoofbeats and bleating erupts nearby. Pedestrians shout and scatter. A SHEEP—fluffy white coat, black face, curled horns—bounds toward you, frantically waving an elaborate scroll in its mouth.*
>
> *The sheep is clearly intelligent, purposeful, and desperate to get your attention.*

**The Scroll**
If the party activates the Scroll of Speak with Animals (simple to do—just read it aloud), the sheep's bleating becomes a cultured, elven-accented voice:

> "Thank the gods! Adventurers! I am Finethir Shinebright, Master Transmuter and scholar of the highest order. I have been...transformed. Cursed into this ridiculous form. My former apprentice wields a Wand of True Polymorph that has gone catastrophically wrong. I need your aid to recover it and restore my proper shape. Will you help?"

### Shinebright's Personality & Motivations

**Appearance (True Form):** Thin, straw-haired high elf with an extremely long nose. Walks with a scholar's bearing.

**Current Form:** Sheep (much to his horror).

**Personality Traits:**
- **Arrogant:** Speaks of himself in grandiose terms ("Master Transmuter," "scholar of the highest order")
- **Pompous:** Lectures others on topics they don't care about; condescending
- **Cowardly:** Won't fight; prefers negotiation and has little combat prowess
- **Witty:** Makes sharp observations; sarcastic under stress
- **Occasionally bleats mid-sentence:** Reminder of sheep form; comedic moment

**What Shinebright Knows:**
1. His apprentice is Ahmed Noke, an elf who served for decades in ungrateful silence
2. Noke became obsessed with power and stole the Wand of True Polymorph two years ago
3. He transformed Shinebright into a sheep (intended effect: death; actual effect: polymorph into sheep)
4. Noke has been experimenting with the wand, creating an army of polymorphed guards
5. The wand is **unstable**—it malfunctions constantly, creating unpredictable effects
6. The instability started after Noke took the wand; Shinebright suspects external magic is interfering with it
7. His tower is located on the outskirts of town (2 hours by foot), in the woods

**What Shinebright Wants:**
- Recovery of the wand
- Restoration to his true form (via the wand)
- Revenge on Noke (though he'd deny it—he'll say "justice")
- Escape from the indignity of being a sheep

### Initial Offer

Shinebright offers:
- **100 gp** (plus an additional 200 gp once restored)
- **1 uncommon magic item** of the party's choice from his tower collection
- **Eternal gratitude** (and potential future magical assistance)
- **Access to his tower library** for research

Shinebright is a **noble liar**—he commits to deals he fully intends to keep but will rationalize if circumstances change.

### Social Encounter: Learning the Details

**If the party asks questions** (likely), Shinebright provides increasing detail:

- **DC 10 Insight:** "He's genuinely desperate and scared, but also arrogant—he doesn't think the party is capable, but he's out of options"
- **DC 12 Persuasion:** "Can you ensure my safety? I'm quite helpless in this form."
- **DC 13 Arcana:** "The wand's malfunction is unusual. Standard polymorph spells don't fail like this. Something is interfering with the magic itself."

### Transition to Scene 2

Once Shinebright has explained the situation, read:

> "The wand is at my tower, held by Noke himself. He rarely leaves, paranoid that someone will attack him. My former apprentice has collected guards—some voluntary, others...less so. If we're to recover the wand, we must move quickly. But first, you should know: Noke's hired muscle may already be looking for me. We should prepare."

## SCENE 2: GUZ'S AMBUSH

**Scene Purpose**
First combat encounter; establish stakes (Noke is serious); provide an opportunity to test combat mechanics with lower stakes; option for diplomacy/escape.

**Time & Location**
- **Timing:** Immediately after Shinebright's plea (same day, transition to journey)
- **Location:** Street, marketplace, road leading out of town (DM choice)
- **Duration:** 30–45 minutes
- **Atmosphere:** Action, chaos, dark comedy

### Read-Aloud Text: The Enforcer Arrives

> *As you prepare to depart town, the sound of heavy footsteps and animal snarls fills the air. Townsfolk scatter. A massive HALF-ORC strides forward—clearly a mercenary—flanked by two large WOLVES wearing iron collars and carrying intelligence in their eyes. Behind them lumbers a BROWN BEAR in a dirty cloak, moving with unsettling intelligence.*
>
> *The half-orc's eyes fix on Shinebright. His hand rests on a greatsword hilt.*

### Encounter: Guz & Polymorphed Allies

**Guz** (Half-orc, Thug variant, CR 2)
- **AC** 14 | **HP** 27 (5d8+5) | **Speed** 30 ft
- **STR** 15 (+2) | **DEX** 11 (+0) | **CON** 13 (+1) | **INT** 8 (-1) | **WIS** 11 (+0) | **CHA** 10 (+0)
- **Skills** Intimidation +2
- **Senses** Passive Perception 10
- **Languages** Common, Orc
- **Challenge** 2 (450 XP)

**Special Trait — Reckless:**
Guz can gain advantage on melee weapon attacks on his turn, but all attacks against him have advantage until his next turn.

**Actions:**
- **Greatsword:** +4 to hit | 2d6+2 slashing | Can make two attacks per turn

**Wolves (2 polymorphed assassins, intelligent)**
- **AC** 13 | **HP** 11 (2d8+2) each | **Speed** 40 ft
- **Skills** Stealth +4, Perception +3
- **Senses** Passive Perception 13
- **Damage Resistances:** Magic (resistance to spell damage)
- **Challenge** 1/8 (25 XP each)

**Actions:**
- **Bite:** +3 to hit | 1d6+1 piercing | Target must pass DC 11 Strength save or be knocked prone

**Brown Bear (polymorphed enforcer, intelligent)**
- **AC** 11 | **HP** 34 (4d10+12) | **Speed** 40 ft, climb 30 ft
- **STR** 19 (+4) | **DEX** 10 (+0) | **CON** 16 (+3)
- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
- **Challenge** 1 (200 XP)

**Actions:**
- **Claw:** +5 to hit | 1d8+4 slashing | Can make two claw attacks
- **Bite:** +5 to hit | 2d6+4 piercing

### Guz's Demands

Before combat (unless the party attacks first):

> "The sheep is Master Noke's property. He wants it back. NOW. Give it to me, or I take it by force. Master Noke is very powerful. You don't want him angry."

**If party refuses:** Guz attacks without hesitation. Wolves flank to grab Shinebright; bears charge.

**If party negotiates:** 
- **DC 12 Persuasion or Deception:** Guz agrees to leave if party offers 50+ gp or a compelling lie
- **DC 13 Intimidation:** Guz backs down if he believes he's outnumbered or outmatched
- **If intimidation fails but party is clearly strong:** Guz attacks but flees when reduced to 10 HP or fewer

### Combat Tactics

**Guz Strategy:**
- Charges the toughest-looking party member
- Uses Reckless to gain advantage (doesn't care about risk)
- Focuses fire on whoever damages him most
- Flees if seriously wounded (below 10 HP)

**Wolves Strategy:**
- Coordinate to surround and trip foes (knockdown combo)
- Attempt to grab Shinebright (low-priority; fleeing with him is secondary to combat victory)
- Intelligent—use terrain, flanking, hit-and-run tactics

**Bear Strategy:**
- Grapple and maul high-HP targets
- Focus on preventing escapes
- Surprisingly intelligent; will retreat to regroup

### Scaling by Party Size

| Party Size | Guz | Wolves | Bear | Difficulty |
|-----------|-----|--------|------|-----------|
| 2 players (Levels 1–2) | Guz only | None | None | Hard |
| 3 players (Levels 1–2) | Guz | 2 wolves | None | Medium |
| 4 players (Levels 1–2) | Guz | 2 wolves | 1 bear | Medium |
| 5+ players (Levels 1–2) | Guz | 2 wolves | 1 bear + 1 ape ally | Medium |

### Aftermath: Guz's Escape or Defeat

**If Guz is defeated:**
- He drops his greatsword and flees (or surrenders if trapped)
- Can be interrogated (DC 10 Intimidation): reveals Noke's tower location, paranoia, and weakness (wand obsession)

**If Guz escapes:**
- He returns to warn Noke; Noke preemptively flees the tower (complicates final encounter)
- Alternatively: Guz pursues party to tower, creating a climax surprise

**If party kills Guz:**
- Shinebright is horrified: "I didn't want him dead! He was a victim of Noke's magic too!"
- Reputational consequence: Noke becomes more aggressive; adds 1–2 additional guards to tower

### Shinebright's Response Post-Combat

Regardless of outcome:

> "Well done. You've confirmed that Noke is serious. The wand's power has made him bold—and desperate. We must reach my tower before he learns we're coming. Time is of the essence."

## SCENE 3: THE TOWER APPROACH & INFILTRATION

**Scene Purpose**
Exploration and puzzle-solving; allow players to use tactics, stealth, or negotiation before entering the tower; establish the tower as a strange, malfunctioning magical complex.

**Time & Location**
- **Timing:** Next morning (party rests after Guz fight) or immediately (fast-paced version)
- **Location:** Path through woods to Noke's tower compound
- **Duration:** 1–2 hours
- **Atmosphere:** Ominous, beautiful, slightly wrong—old magic gone unstable

### The Approach

**Read-Aloud Text:**

> *The path to Noke's Tower cuts off from the main road, leading through dense woods. After an hour of travel, you emerge into a clearing. Before you stands a structure unlike any normal building.*
>
> *The tower appears to be formed from living wood—massive oak trees shaped and woven together into three distinct platforms. The lowest platform, some 40 feet across, sits 10 feet above ground. A gentle slope of roots and branches connects it to the path. The middle platform is the largest—60 feet across, 20 feet high, enclosed with walls of twisted branches with window-sized gaps and a visible door.*
>
> *The highest platform, perhaps 30 feet up, is smaller and appears to be Noke's private study.*
>
> *Around the base: tents, wooden huts, outhouses. Several armed figures move about—some polymorphed (animals moving with human intelligence), others normal mercenaries.*

**Guards & Defenses:**
- **3 Apes** (MM p317, polymorphed mercenaries) playing dice on the lawn, greatswords nearby
- **1 Brown Bear** (polymorphed, currently in the outhouse—available later as reinforcement)
- **Total visible guard count: 4** (plus more inside the tower)

### Infiltration Options

**Option A: Direct Assault**
- Party walks up and fights all guards
- Least stealthy but most straightforward
- Difficulty: Medium (6–8 CR worth of enemies)

**Option B: Stealth Approach**
- **DC 12 Stealth:** Bypass ground-level guards undetected
- **DC 13 Perception:** Notice the circling patrols (guards rotate every 15 minutes)
- **DC 10 Investigation:** Find an alternate entry (kitchen window, back entrance)

**Option C: Distraction**
- Party creates a commotion (fire, noise, deception)
- Draws guards away; allows sneaking to tower
- **Requires DC 12 Deception or Charisma check**

**Option D: Social Engineering**
- Shinebright contacts someone inside (if party prevented his total isolation)
- Noke's staff (if any are loyal) might provide access
- **Requires high Charisma and creativity**

### Inside the Tower

**Ground Level (Entry Hall):**
- Scattered spellbooks, notes, adventuring gear
- 2 **Flying Swords** (MM p20, magical constructs) patrol here, behaving erratically
- **DC 12 Arcana:** Notice the swords are moving oddly—they're destabilized, sometimes attacking friendlies

**Second Level (Library & Study):**
- Hundreds of books on transmutation magic
- Comfortable sitting room, dining area
- Several **Animated Armor** suits stand inactive (can be reactivated by spells or loud noise)
- Noke's personal notes scattered about (reveal his paranoia, obsession, and guilt)
- **Optional NPC:** Polymorphed staff (cat butler, squirrel maid) who can provide information or aid if freed

**Third Level (Noke's Laboratory & Bedroom):**
- Alchemical equipment (dangerous: explosive potions, volatile vials)
- Wand of True Polymorph on an ornate pedestal OR in Noke's hands
- Noke's personal bedchamber (sparse, paranoid security measures)

### Tower Hazards (Magical Malfunction Theme)

**Destabilized Magic Environment:**
Every room has **DC 10 Dexterity save** once per encounter to avoid unpredictable magical effects:
- Animated objects behave erratically (attack random targets)
- Spells cast within tower may backfire (5% chance per spell)
- Teleportation circles malfunction (lead to wrong destinations)
- Traps trigger unexpectedly or fail to trigger

**DCs for Tower Navigation:**
- **DC 10 Stealth:** Move through hallways unseen
- **DC 12 Arcana:** Disable a magical trap or recharge a spell circle
- **DC 10 Investigation:** Find secret passages or hidden compartments
- **DC 13 Thieves' Tools:** Pick locked chest (Noke's spellbook or wand temporary storage)

### Scaling Tower Difficulty

| Party Size | Animated Armor Count | Flying Sword Count | Special Hazard |
|-----------|----------------------|--------------------|----------------|
| 2 players | 1 suit | 1 sword | Hazardous alchemy vial triggers (party can control) |
| 3 players | 2 suits | 2 swords | Unstable wand temporarily unattended (grab opportunity) |
| 4 players | 2 suits | 2 swords + 1 ape inside | Wand actively guarded; requires combat |
| 5+ players | 3 suits | 3 swords + 1 ape | Multiple guards, hazards, longer exploration |

## SCENE 4: AHMED NOKE & THE WAND

**Scene Purpose**
Climactic confrontation; option for combat, negotiation, or chaos; wand malfunction payoff; resolution of core adventure conflict.

**Time & Location**
- **Timing:** Party reaches the laboratory (3rd level)
- **Location:** Noke's Laboratory & Bedroom
- **Duration:** 45–60 minutes
- **Atmosphere:** Tense, theatrical, magical chaos

### Read-Aloud Text: Noke Revealed

> *The third-level laboratory is spacious and cluttered. Alchemical equipment covers worktables. Bottles of volatile reagents line shelves. In the center of the room stands Ahmed Noke, an elf in elaborate robes, holding a shimmering wand. His eyes are wild, unfocused—the look of someone who hasn't slept in days.*
>
> *The moment he sees you, his expression shifts from surprise to rage.*

### Ahmed Noke (Wizard / Mage, Custom)

**Ahmed Noke** (Elf, High Intelligence, Paranoid, CR 3)

- **AC** 12 | **HP** 45 (7d8+14) | **Speed** 30 ft
- **STR** 10 (+0) | **DEX** 14 (+2) | **CON** 14 (+2) | **INT** 18 (+4) | **WIS** 12 (+1) | **CHA** 13 (+1)
- **Saving Throws** Intelligence +7, Wisdom +4
- **Skills** Arcana +7, Insight +4
- **Senses** Passive Perception 11
- **Languages** Common, Elvish, Draconic
- **Challenge** 3 (700 XP)

**Spellcaster Features:**
- **Spell Save DC** 15
- **Spell Attack Bonus** +7
- **Spells Known (Wizard list, 3rd level):**
  - Cantrips: Mage Hand, Light, Fire Bolt
  - 1st level (4 slots): Magic Missile, Shield, Detect Magic, Sleep
  - 2nd level (3 slots): Scorching Ray, Blur, Enlarge/Reduce
  - 3rd level (2 slots): Fireball, Counterspell

**Special Feature — Wand of True Polymorph (Modified & Unstable):**
- **Charges:** 3 remaining (recharges 1d6 per day, unreliably)
- **Backfire Chance:** On wand use, if user rolls 1–3 on d20, wand backfires
- **Backfire Effect:** Caster OR random target within 30 ft transforms into random beast (sheep, frog, chicken, wolf, or bear) for 1d4 hours

**Actions:**
- **Polymorph Ray (Recharge 4–6):** +7 to hit | Range 60 ft | Target makes DC 15 Wisdom save or transforms into beast (Noke's choice, if possible)
- **Scorching Ray:** +7 to hit | 3 rays | 2d6 fire damage each
- **Sleep:** 5d8 hit points of humanoids within 30 ft, DC 15 Wisdom save

**Legendary Actions (if needed to survive):**
- **Move** up to speed
- **Cast a cantrip**
- **Use wand ability (if available)**

### Noke's Personality & Motivations

**Appearance:** Thin elf, dark robes, unkempt hair, wild eyes, nervous tics

**Personality Traits:**
- **Paranoid:** Convinced enemies are plotting against him
- **Ambitious:** Believes the wand proves his superiority over Shinebright
- **Theatrical:** Makes grandiose speeches; prone to monologuing
- **Guilty:** Deep down, knows transforming Shinebright was wrong, but won't admit it
- **Unstable:** Sleep needs broken by paranoia; mental state deteriorating

**What Noke Knows & Believes:**
1. Shinebright received decades of adulation while Noke worked as a servant
2. The wand is the source of Shinebright's power and respect
3. By controlling the wand, Noke became powerful (or so he tells himself)
4. The wand is becoming unstable (something is interfering with it), making it even more powerful and dangerous
5. Anyone who comes here is a threat to his power

### Noke's Monologue (If Uninterrupted)

> "You've come for my former master, have you? The great Finethir Shinebright? Let me tell you a secret: he was nothing without this wand. Decades I served him, catered to his every need, listened to his lectures, and received not a single word of praise. 'Keep studying, Ahmed. You're not ready yet, Ahmed. Perhaps in another century, Ahmed.' Well, NOW look at me! I have the wand! I have POWER! And with this wand, I can reshape reality itself!
>
> But you... you're just like the others. You've come to take it away. To restore the natural order where genius like Shinebright rules and ambition means nothing. Not this time. This time, I decide the outcome!"

### Noke's Tactical Approach (Combat)

**Phase 1 (First round):**
- Casts Blur (defensive +2 AC)
- Uses polymorph ray to attempt to transform a party member into a harmless creature

**Phase 2 (If outnumbered):**
- Uses Scorching Ray as area denial
- Casts Enlarge/Reduce on allies (polymorphed guards) to buff them
- Maintains distance, using spells from afar

**Phase 3 (If desperate):**
- Threatens to use wand on everyone in the room
- May actually attempt it, triggering backfire
- If truly cornered: **Wand explodes (see Wand Explosion below)**

### Scaling Noke's Threat

| Party Levels | Noke HP | Spell Load | Ally Support | Difficulty |
|-------------|---------|-----------|--------------|-----------|
| Levels 1–2 | 30 HP | 2nd-level spells only | 2 guards (apes) | Hard (consider reducing HP) |
| Levels 2–3 | 45 HP (full) | 3rd-level spells | Variable | Medium |
| Levels 4–5 | 45 HP + Legendary Actions | Full capacity | Multiple guards | Medium |

**To reduce difficulty for Levels 1–2 parties:** Reduce Noke's HP to 25, remove one spell level, have guards flee when Noke is injured below 15 HP.

### Alternative Resolutions

#### Resolution A: Defeat Noke in Combat

**If party defeats Noke:**
- He pleads for mercy or attempts escape (if physically possible)
- Party can choose to spare him (justice), capture him (authorities), or eliminate him (execution)
- **Option:** Wand backfires on Noke during final exchange, polymorphing him into a sheep (ironic justice)

#### Resolution B: Negotiate with Noke

**If party attempts negotiation (likely!):**
- **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion):** Convince Noke to surrender wand peacefully (unlikely unless party offers something valuable)
- **DC 13 Deception:** Trick Noke into lowering his guard
- **DC 14 Intimidation:** Frighten him into believing the wand is cursed and he should be rid of it
- **Outcome:** Noke agrees to terms if party offers asylum, gold, or a promise of non-violence toward his person

#### Resolution C: Wand Explodes

**If wand is used repeatedly or backfires without control:**

> *The wand glows with unstable magical energy. The air crackles. As Noke activates it one final time, the device shatters in a burst of wild magic.*
>
> *Everyone within 30 feet must make a **DC 15 Dexterity save** or be affected by wild magic surge: randomly roll 1d8 (1=sheep, 2=frog, 3=chicken, 4=wolf, 5=bear, 6=unchanged, 7=temporary wings 1 minute, 8=random mild beneficial effect)*

**Consequence:** The wand is destroyed; party must find another method to restore Shinebright (e.g., cast Dispel Magic at DC 15, seek Greater Restoration spell, negotiate with Noke if alive)

### Treasure & Loot

**From Noke's Study:**
- Spellbook (contains all spells Noke knows; valuable)
- 300 gp in coins and gems
- 2d4 spell scrolls (1st/2nd level)
- Component pouch, ink, quills
- Aeorian artifacts (if campaign emphasizes; 1-2 items, 100+ gp each)

**The Wand Itself:**
- **Unstable Wand of True Polymorph** (Rare, Cursed)
- Party must decide: destroy it, keep it (risky), or study it

**Result:**
- Noke is dead; no further threat from him

## CHAPTER 8: COMMON QUESTIONS & TIPS

### For New DMs

**Q: "This adventure feels very comedic. How do I balance humor with stakes?"**
A: Lean into the absurdity (sheep-wizard, ridiculous situation) while reminding players that magical items failing is actually terrifying. The contrast between comedy and existential threat makes the message more memorable.

**Q: "Shinebright is overshadowing the party. How do I manage his personality?"**
A: Give him 1–2 standout moments (his arrival, his gratitude), then reduce his involvement. He's great support NPC but shouldn't dominate combat or decisions. Use him to comment on party tactics or provide magical insight, not to solve problems.

**Q: "What if party wants to keep the unstable wand instead of destroying it?"**
A: Allow it, but enforce backfire mechanics strictly. Remind them: "Each time you use it, roll a save. On a failure, someone polymorphs unpredictably." Make the downside real.

**Q: "My party negotiates with Noke instead of fighting. Is that valid?"**
A: Absolutely valid. Negotiation is a legitimate victory. Just remember: Noke is paranoid and won't trust easily. High Charisma checks are required; success feels earned.

### For Experienced DMs: Depth & Variations

**Q: "How can I make Noke feel like a genuine threat, not just a comedic foil?"**
A: Play his paranoia seriously. He's mentally unwell and volatile. His decisions are erratic. Show his desperation. When he monologues, make it unsettling—a glimpse into a fractured mind, not just villain clichés.

**Q: "Can I expand this adventure into a longer campaign?"**
A: Yes. Add subquests: finding Noke's apprentices, locating other unstable wands, exploring the nature of magical corruption, hunting Noke's associates. The wand malfunction premise opens many story doors.

**Q: "What if party wants to study the wand and understand the corruption?"**
A: Excellent! **DC 18 Arcana check** reveals partial truth: external magical frequency is interfering—signature of something very old and powerful. Builds suspicion toward Salsvault (if running full campaign).
