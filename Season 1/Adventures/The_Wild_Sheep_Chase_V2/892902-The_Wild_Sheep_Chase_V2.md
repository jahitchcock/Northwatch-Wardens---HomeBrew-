#### Northwatch Wardens Integration (DM)

This adventure is the **tonal counterpoint** of Arc 2. Where Frozen Sick reveals the source of the Aeorian Echo with cold dread and urgency, **The Wild Sheep Chase shows it through absurdity**.

A wand that should have killed instead transformed. Magic that should be reliable has become chaotic. The Aeorian Echo is not only spreading disease and awakening wolves — it is **corrupting the fabric of spellwork itself**.

This is where the Wardens understand: ::

- That magical items across Northreach are destabilizing
- That even "safe" magic can now malfunction catastrophically
- That at least one scholar — Finethir Shinebright — has been tracking this silently
- And that the Echo's reach is wider and stranger than anyone suspected

- This adventure is set near **any established Northreach town** — Welton works well
- **Noke's Tower** lies southeast of town, in sparse woodland off the main road
- **Finethir Shinebright** is a traveling wizard and secret Aeorian Echo researcher
- **Guz** is a local enforcer hired by Noke; the "wolves" and "bear" are polymorphed hired thugs

The Wild Sheep Chase is the **tonal pivot** of Arc 2 — a comedy adventure with real campaign weight underneath it.

A wand malfunctioned. A wizard became a sheep. His apprentice seized power. Thugs hunt through town streets with wolves on leashes.

#### This adventure blends:

 **Comedy** :: (a snobbish wizard in sheep form with a Scroll of Speak with Animals)
 **Action** :: (street brawl, chase scene, tower assault)
 **Exploration** :: (a magical tree-tower with three platforms and an eccentric layout)
 **Revelation** :: (the Echo corrupts magic itself — this isn't isolated)

The Wardens must navigate absurdity while recognizing that underneath the bleating and the backfiring wands, something deeply wrong is happening to magic across Northreach.


### The Wardens must:

- Protect a polymorphed wizard long enough to hear his story
- Fight off a half-orc enforcer and his pack of suspiciously intelligent "wolves"
- Infiltrate a living tree-tower southeast of town
- Navigate magical chaos: animated furniture, backfiring traps, polymorphed guards
- Defeat or outsmart Ahmed Noke before he uses an unstable wand again
- Retrieve the wand and restore Finethir Shinebright to his proper form

*All while realizing that this **unstable wand is not an accident — it's a symptom**.*

# Summary

The party's attempt to grab a rare afternoon of downtime is interrupted by a frantic sheep equipped with a Scroll of Speak with Animals and a fierce determination to get their attention. This is no mere beast, however, but a wizard fallen victim to an embittered apprentice wielding a Wand of True Polymorph.

Transformed assassins are looking to grab themselves a mutton dinner, while the only object capable of restoring his opposable thumbs lies in the hands of his former pupil and current nemesis. Fortunately, the woolly wizard has found new allies he can rely on — right?

Can the heroes put an end to Ahmed Noke's transmutational tyranny and restore an innocent wizard to his true form? There's only one way to find out.

## Adventure Hooks

Getting the players into this adventure is straightforward. All that is required is that they be spending a few days in a town large enough to host a wizard.

If you want to plant seeds in advance, you may wish to introduce the character of Noke through rumours or idle conversation with shopkeepers and innkeepers, who view him with fear and awe.

Alternatively, Lorewarden Elric has been tracking unusual artifact failures near the town and may ask the Wardens to investigate reports of "unstable magic near the old tree-tower southeast of town."

# Baaaa-d News

The adventure begins as the party idle away an afternoon. They might be enjoying a drink at a tavern, resting at their base, or simply walking down the street without a care in the world.

{{descriptive
There is a sound of clattering hooves, surprised yelps, and frantic bleating. Before the party can react, a sheep bounds towards them — fluffy white coat, black face, curled horns — carrying an elaborate scroll in its mouth.

The sheep tries to get close to the most magically gifted member of the party and waves the scroll at them, letting them take it should they reach for it.
}}

A wax seal purports that it is a Scroll of Speak With Animals (Modified). If a character says this out loud — even to explain it to other party members — the sheep appears to nod and bleat enthusiastically.

Reading the scroll aloud activates the spell for the entire party.

{{descriptive
The bleating resolves instantly into cultured, elven-accented Common, albeit with a slight, unavoidable hint of a bleat.

"Thank the gods. Adventurers. I am **Finethir Shinebright**, Master Wizard of the Third Circle, and I have been most *grievously* transformed by my vile former apprentice, Ahmed Noke, who wields a Wand of True Polymorph that has — through some catastrophic malfunction I have had two years to contemplate — reduced me to this *absurd ovine form*."

A pause. He glances down at his hooves.

"I require your assistance. Will you help me?"
}}

He will attempt to tell his story, but shortly after he starts, the sound of howling fills the air.

## Roleplaying Shinebright

Arrogant, self-obsessed, and filled with an inflated sense of his own ego, Finethir Shinebright is one of the people worst suited to be transformed into a sheep. Still, no matter his form, the former elven wizard manages to exude an air of fussy, bookish intellectualism.

Despite his flaws, however, he is generally good-hearted. He will try to help others where possible, though he will loudly complain of their inability to grasp concepts as simple as poly-planal transweave theory.

Once upon a time the local nobles, merchants, and arcanists spoke the name of Finethir Shinebright with respect, awe, and grudging admiration. A master of transmutation, he would regularly be called upon to carry out grand tasks such as clearing blocked roads by turning massive boulders into rabbits. Two years as a sheep have taught him very little about humility, but a lot about grass. If he returns to his natural form, Shinebright is a willow-thin high elf with straw-coloured hair and an extremely long nose.

- Refers to himself in the third person occasionally ("Shinebright does *not* graze on demand")
- Complains about hooves at least once per scene
- Insists on his full title even when bleating
- Provides tactical advice during combat, hiding behind the nearest Warden
- Is genuinely good-hearted beneath the snobbery — and slightly surprised by this himself
- Can cast minor illusion and mage hand even in sheep form

**What Shinebright Can Share (Before Guz Arrives):** ::

- Two years ago, Noke stood over him with the wand. He became a sheep. He does not know why it didn't kill him.
- Noke still occupies his tower southeast of town
- The wand is on Noke's person at all times
- He snuck back in last night while Noke was away to steal this scroll
- He needs the wand used on him one more time to restore his form

**What Shinebright Will Not Share Yet:** ::
- That he has been secretly researching the Aeorian Echo for eighteen months
- That his tower contains a hidden archive with maps, notes, and Aeorian symbol sketches
- That he suspects the wand's malfunction was not random

{{note
**Insight DC 15:** Shinebright is choosing his words carefully. He is not lying — he simply isn't telling everything. He will share more if the Wardens earn his trust.

# Shepherds and Crooks

{{descriptive
A loud howling fills the air, accompanied by the sound of angry yells and the occasional scream drawing closer and closer.

The half-orc, Guz, is accompanied by a trio of collared wolves with strangely intelligent eyes, while the huge figure behind him is actually a polymorphed human in bear form.

He demands that he be given "Master Noke's sheep," which he claims has escaped and is of great "sent-ee-ment-all value" to his master. Any mention of Shinebright's true nature is met with hollow, mirthless laughter.

Guz is not a bright fellow, but he is very loyal and determined to complete his task. His trackers know that Shinebright is there no matter how well the party tries to hide him, and will not take no for an answer.

While he prefers violence and intimidation, he is also willing to offer bribes and promises of magical favours if that looks like the best option.

---

## Roleplaying Guz

A hulking brute of a half-orc, Guz views violence and intimidation as the only sensible methods for achieving his goals. He also assumes that most people feel the same way, and prefers to get his own violence in first.

Though to most outside observers he may appear evil, he is incredibly loyal to Noke — who genuinely doesn't care what a creature looks like on the outside — and owes him unswerving devotion.

Guz isn't particularly intelligent and can be easily confused by quick-talking foes, though this usually just makes him angrier. He is also fond of trying to use long, complicated words he has picked up from Noke — though the extent to which he actually understands what they mean is up for debate.

#### Negotiation Options

**DC 12 Intimidation:** Guz backs off briefly, then attacks anyway.
**DC 14 Persuasion:** Guz agrees to a temporary delay — "I will tell Master Noke you are *considdering* it." He returns with reinforcements within the hour.
**DC 10 Insight:** Guz genuinely believes the sheep is just a sheep. He has not been told the truth.

{{note
**If the Wardens mention the disease, the wolves, or the Aeorian Echo:** Guz stares blankly. "Master Noke doesn't pay me to understand things. He pays me to collect things."
}}

## The Cloaked Figure

The hooded figure behind Guz has not moved. Until someone targets it, attempts to get past Guz, or combat begins — at which point it lets its hood fall and runs forward with a roar.

{{descriptive
The cloak falls. What stands behind Guz is not a man — it is a brown bear the size of a cart horse, wearing the remnants of iron-studded leather across its shoulders. Its eyes are wrong. Too focused. Too angry.

It remembers being human.
}}

If Guz feels like he's not making progress, he will simply attack without warning. The wolves will follow him up, while the cloaked figure lets its hood fall and runs into combat with a terrible roar.

The exact details of any battle will depend on the location. If it takes place in an inn, tables and overturned chairs litter the floor. In a street, frightened passers-by form zones of difficult terrain.

All of the beasts still have an intelligent human mind and act intelligently. Guz is happy to charge into the front line and take on any heavy hitters alongside the bear.

The wolves will aim to cut round behind the party to grab Shinebright — he uses the statistics of a Pony but with INT and WIS scores of 18 and 14 respectively. They intend to capture Shinebright, not kill him.

**If Guz captures Shinebright:** He and his allies set off for Noke's tower. The party must decide whether to follow immediately (running confrontation) or track them (tactical approach).

## After the Dust Settles

Once Noke's goons have been defeated, distracted, or otherwise dealt with, Shinebright pleads with the party. Without their help, he is doomed. Noke still has many guards and eventually they will find him.

If they're feeling mercenary, he reminds the party that in his true form he is a powerful and wealthy wizard and promises great rewards. He is more than willing to commit to deals that he knows he can't actually fulfil.

In any case, Shinebright will explain:

- Until two years ago he owned and worked out of a tower on the outskirts of town, specialising in transmutation magic
- His most prized possession was an incredibly rare Wand of True Polymorph
- One fateful night he ended his meditative trance to find Noke standing over him, clutching the wand. The only noise he could produce was an angry "baaaah."
- The wizard became a virtual prisoner in his own garden, forced to graze while wolves and other beasts — actually polymorphed guards — looked on
- Last night Noke left without closing the door; Shinebright snuck in, made his way to an old bookshelf, and stole the Scroll of Speak with Animals
- In order to turn back into his original form he needs another dose of True Polymorph — which means he needs his old wand
- His former apprentice still dwells in his old home, southeast of town, and keeps the wand on him at all times

He knows the layout of his old home intimately and is more than happy to describe it in endless detail.

---

#### Useful Investigation

**DC 12 Arcana** (examining the wolves' collars): The iron is etched with the same rune-inscription found on Noke's modified wand. The collars *anchor* the polymorph — breaking one would restore the wearer.
**DC 10 Medicine** (examining Guz): He is entirely human. Not polymorphed. Just enormous.

### The House in the Woods

The path to Noke's Tower cuts off from a main road, darting off between a gap in the bushes. It is a well-trodden route that soon begins to weave its way between tall oak trees.

Anyone who examines the path for tracks will easily see the impression of many feet and paws, with the freshest sets matching the group led by Guz.

{{descriptive
Rather than stone or glass, the house before you seems to have been formed from the living branches of four sturdy oak trees, shaped and woven to create three thick platforms.

The lowest of these platforms is roughly 40ft across and sits about 10ft from the ground. The only obvious route up is a gentle slope formed of roots and branches. Branches curl around its base, creating a rough bowl shape full of flowers and small trees.

The largest platform is the middle one — roughly 60ft across, 20ft above the ground, fully enclosed with a wall of twisting branches and window-sized gaps. A door faces the garden platform.

The final, tallest platform is roughly 30ft above the ground and much smaller. It links to the central platform by another small slope.

Scattered beneath the platforms: two small wooden huts, a large outhouse, and three large figures who appear to be playing dice with oversized cubes. Greatswords are stuck in the ground beside them.
}}



### Reconnaissance

**DC 12 Perception:**:: The dice-players are not men. Their proportions are wrong — too long in the arm, too wide across the shoulder. Polymorphed apes.
**DC 10 Survival:** :: Fresh tracks from Guz's boots lead up the root-slope to the middle platform.
**DC 13 Arcana:** :: The entire tower radiates unstable magical energy. Several enchantments appear to be running in contradiction to each other.

## Ewe Are Very Clever...

Noke is naturally paranoid and Shinebright's recent escape hasn't exactly improved his outlook. This makes luring him away from the safety of his tower an extremely difficult task, though not an impossible one.

Noke's lust for fame and power slightly outweighs his fear of being unseated. This means that a very skilled set of liars with a sufficiently devious plan and a good helping of luck may be able to tempt him with the offer of a particularly prestigious, lucrative contract.

Even so, when Noke leaves he will take all of his remaining bodyguards with him and will be extremely quick to suspect the party of betrayal if there is any hint of a double-cross. He will, of course, have his wand to hand at all times.

Should he be given enough time to cast it, Noke will use a charge of True Polymorph to transmute some nearby item into a Wyrmling that uses roughly the same stats as the Bed Dragon Wyrmling. Depending on what it was made from, it may not have the same flavour and might need to have its resistances/vulnerabilities tweaked.

## Roleplaying Noke

Noke once hero-worshipped Shinebright and served as his apprentice for many years. However, as time dragged on there was never any change in their relationship. Decades passed and still the master transmuter treated him like a child, expected to cook, clean, and recite answers by rote. When Noke pressed him on it, Shinebright would explain that this was how he had been taught, and didn't seem to register Noke's claims that, as a human, he couldn't spare a century to serve out an apprenticeship.

At the same time, Noke began to realise that much of Shinebright's acclaim came not from his own abilities — extensive as they were — but from the Wand of True Polymorph he wielded. Eventually he cracked, and turned on his former master. However, a combination of guilt and suddenly unstoppered ambition spelled bad news for his mental state.

He is paranoid that someone will attack him as he attacked Shinebright, and rarely sleeps. This paranoia has led him to invest in a troop of guards, many of whom he has transmuted into stronger, more bestial forms.

**Theatrical** :: prone to monologuing, dramatic pauses, gestures with the wand
**Paranoid** :: constantly checking exits, deeply suspicious of social niceties
**Genuinely intelligent** :: he knows the wand is unstable; he uses it anyway because he has no other power
**Broken** :: if Shinebright is revealed to be alive, Noke's composure fractures

{{descriptive
**If Shinebright is present and Noke sees him:**

Noke goes very still.

"You're... you're still in there."

A long pause.

"I thought it would be easier if you were just... gone."
}}

This moment can shift the encounter entirely. A skilled Warden who plays this correctly may be able to negotiate — or watch Noke make a terrible decision with the wand in grief.

If they talk, Noke will reveal his reasons for hating Shinebright and boast disjointedly of his own achievements. He will also ask if they killed "my man, Guz," and be visibly upset if they have.

The most important part of any fight is Noke, who is offensively weak but can buff his allies dramatically. He will **cast Enlarge/Reduce on any polymorphed ally** still functional. ## The Bed Dragon

Three rounds after Noke flees to his bedroom, he casts True Polymorph on the large bed in the corner.

{{descriptive

The Wyrmling is not that smart. It will use its Splinter Breath as often as possible, simply trying to attack the closest target otherwise. Desperate and unwilling to let his old master win, a babbling Noke will then attempt to use the wand on himself, intending to transmute his form into that of some monstrous creature.

# Big Choices

Should the players recover the wand it becomes immediately obvious that it is damaged. Anyone proficient in Arcana can tell that it has been modified so it can be used more often, but overuse has rendered it extremely unreliable.

If they wish to speak to Shinebright, a quick search of the central platform will allow them to find another Scroll of Speak with Animals. At the sight of his former apprentice's ruined corpse, Shinebright may even undergo some realisation of how poorly he treated his former apprentice and turn sombre despite his triumph.

Even if he is made aware of the dangers associated with his old wand, Shinebright will still be willing to risk his life in order to be turned back into an Elf. However, he is also open to persuasion if the party tries to talk him out of it. A compelling argument and a **DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion)** will be enough to make the former Wizard reconsider his options.

Before undergoing the risk of having the spell performed on him, Shinebright will ask that if he does die, the party send word to his old college.

---

# Not Like This...

A stealthy party may be able to steal the wand while Noke sleeps, or a combination of lucky rolls and inventive tactics may allow a player to disarm the wizard.

Should this happen, don't force the story back towards the "ideal path" — allow it to develop naturally.

One thing you must do, however, is try to provide at least a hint of the dangers associated with the modified wand before the players try to use it. Possibly it sparks unnaturally when a character touches it, or Noke himself may feel honour-bound to warn them.

# Modified Wand of True Polymorph

Formed of a long, thin twig taken from an oak tree, this wand holds 1d4−1 charges of True Polymorph (minimum 1). These can be cast by anybody with a spellcasting ability who has spent at least one hour attuning to the wand. Charges reset at dawn each day.

In order to increase the amount of times the spell can be cast, the wand appears to have been fitted with a bulbous, rune-inscribed iron band and has cracks running along its length.

**Inspection (DC 15 Arcana):** The modifications have left the wand extremely unreliable.

Each time a charge is used the user must pass a **DC 17 Intelligence (Arcana)** check.

- **Success:** The spell casts normally, and the DC permanently increases by 1.
- **Fail by 5 or less:** The spell transmutes the target into a Gibbering Mouther — a hideous lump of random limbs, organs, and facial features that attacks mindlessly. This cannot be undone by any spell below 9th level.
- **Fail by more than 5:** As above, plus the wand explodes violently, dealing 1d12 force damage to all creatures within 10 ft. per charge remaining. This destroys the wand.

# Consequences

**If the party uses the wand and it succeeds**, Shinebright congratulates the party heartily. He will have to be reminded of anything he actually promised while he was still a sheep and cannot actually pay any financial reward — Noke was virtually bankrupt and had sold most items to pay for research materials and guards.

He will, however, be willing to perform transmutations of up to 5th level for them, charging just enough to cover the components. He will also take in any polymorphed guards who fled, promising to work towards returning them to their natural shapes.

**If the party uses the wand and it fails**, Shinebright dies permanently. The party is free to honour his final wish and leave the tower more-or-less intact, or loot to their hearts' content. The tower is surprisingly empty of anything of benefit to non-transmuters — valuables are restricted to expensive lab equipment and arcane paraphernalia estimated to be worth around 1,000 gp. If the players wish they can also take the entire tower complex as their own, though this may cause trouble when the local nobles try to pay Noke a visit and find him missing.

**If the party refuses to perform the spell**, Shinebright accepts but will not give up his hopes entirely. He will take back his old home and work towards a way to remove his curse. In any case, he heads to the bedroom and slips on one of his old robes. Though he will be upset with them, Shinebright will acknowledge that he owes the party a large debt.

In any case, any adventurers that help Shinebright deal with Noke each receive 500 XP.

### Adventure Overview (DM Lens)

**Theme:** Magic behaving unpredictably — the Aeorian Echo corrupts artifacts.
**Core Tension:** A polymorphed wizard needs the wand that transformed him to undo it — and that wand is in the hands of a paranoid, overmatched villain.
**Tone:** Absurdist comedy with genuine campaign stakes underneath.
**Level Range:** 1–2 (scalable to 4–5).
**Expected Duration:** 3–4 hours (single session).

---

### Common DM Pitfalls & Fixes

#### **1. Taking It Too Seriously**

**Problem:** :: The adventure loses its identity if played as straight horror or grim drama.

**Fix:** ::
- Shinebright bleats mid-sentence, every time, whenever he gets emotional
- Guz uses long words he clearly does not understand ("The master desires the sheep returned with all due *exped-it-ious* haste")
- The Bed Dragon has bedsheet wings and a pillow tail
- Let the Wand Instability Table fire as often as possible
- Reward creative solutions: distract enemies with actual sheep, polymorph Noke back, etc.

#### **2. Shinebright Is Just Cargo**

**Problem:** :: If Shinebright does nothing during combat, players stop caring about him.

**Fix:** ::
- He hides behind the nearest Warden and shouts warnings ("Watch your left! The bear flanks!")
- He casts *minor illusion* (sheep-sized ghost wolf) to distract an enemy at a critical moment
- He argues loudly about tactical decisions and is usually correct
- He makes promises he cannot keep with complete sincerity

---

{{pageNumber,auto}}
{{footnote Section 9: Return to Waystone}}

\page

#### **3. Noke's Tower Becomes a Slog**

**Problem:** :: Without personality, a three-floor dungeon crawl loses energy.

**Fix:** ::
- **The broom** is sweeping the same corner forever
- **The polymorphed staff** react to the Wardens with desperate hope
- **Animated objects argue** — the chairs refuse to move toward the bookshelf, the bookshelf has opinions about this
- Every time the wand fires, roll on the Instability Table out loud and visibly
- Noke's monologue should be uninterruptible for exactly one sentence before something goes wrong

---

#### **4. The Wand Feels Like a MacGuffin**

**Problem:** :: Party grabs wand, wins, leaves. No tension.

**Fix:** ::
- Noke uses it defensively in every round if cornered
- Each use requires a roll on the Instability Table — players will start dreading it
- The wand is obviously damaged: cracks, iron band, visible vibration
- When Shinebright sees it, he goes very quiet. He knows what almost happened.

---

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

**"This adventure feels too silly compared to the rest of the campaign!"**
→ Acknowledge the tonal shift: this is a palate cleanser — comic relief that still matters thematically. Enjoy the absurdity.

**"We want to kill Shinebright and keep the wand!"**
→ Allow it, but consequences: Shinebright's allies hunt the party. Noke escapes and becomes a recurring villain. Wand eventually backfires catastrophically.

**"Guz captured Shinebright and we can't catch him!"**
→ Introduce a helpful NPC who knows a shortcut. Or Shinebright casts *message* to guide the party. Or Guz stops to gloat.

**"Noke is too strong; he's killing us!"**
→ Wand backfires on Noke (roll Instability Table). Polymorphed staff rebel and attack Noke. Shinebright reveals he hid a *dispel magic* scroll in his tower.

**"We want to negotiate with Noke instead of fighting!"**
→ Valid choice. Noke is paranoid but might agree to a truce. He becomes a recurring villain — but that's a valid outcome.

### Polymorph Quick Rules

#### **True Polymorph (Noke's Wand):**
- Target becomes beast of CR equal to or less than target's level
- Retains INT/WIS scores (polymorphed assassins are intelligent)
- Can't cast spells or use equipment while polymorphed
- HP becomes beast's HP; reverting restores original HP
- Lasts until dispelled OR caster drops concentration

#### **Reversing Polymorph:**
- *Dispel Magic* (DC 15 to break True Polymorph)
- Noke's wand (he can reverse it willingly OR if party attunes and succeeds DC 17 Arcana)
- *Greater Restoration* (5th level spell, removes curse)

#### **Collars (Polymorphed Guards):**
- Collar AC 19, 5 HP — destroying it ends the polymorph
- *Dispel Magic* DC 13 — ends the collar's anchor enchantment
- Creature reverts to human form; stunned 1 round (DC 12 Wis save negates)

### Perception Checks

A player rolls a d20 and adds the modifier written next to the Perception skill on their character sheet to make a Perception check. The Difficulty Class to succeed on this check is 10 (written as DC 10), so if anyone gets a Perception check of 10 or higher, you explain that they hear a noise coming from the thorny forest underbrush.

If no character succeeds on the Perception check, Captain Kole points in the direction of the sound. Continue reading:

{{descriptive

### Roleplaying and Information

{{note
Players will ask questions throughout the adventure. To keep information clear and consistent, divide your answers into three categories:

#### **Information the Characters Know.**

#### **Information the Characters Might Know.**

# Conclusion
###### Estimated Time to Play This Encounter: 10 minutes

The angry silver dragon waits for the characters to explain themselves. This is an opportunity for the players to roleplay and speak on behalf of their characters.

During the conversation, the dragon may reveal her name: **Hysvearorn**, which translates to *Soaring Silver*. She invites the characters to call her **Rorn**, as it is easier for non‑dragons to pronounce.

If the players hesitate, prompt them with questions such as:

- “The dragon demands an explanation. What do you say?”
- “How does your character feel standing before a furious dragon the size of a house?”
- “What can you say to prove you came here to return her wyrmling?”

As long as the characters remain respectful, Rorn quickly realizes they are not thieves — they are rescuers.

---
{{note
#### DM Tip

Dragons are overwhelmingly powerful. If the players decide to attack Rorn, you can handle it in several ways:

- Rorn looks disappointed and tells them to stop being foolish.  
- A quick Nature check reveals she could defeat them effortlessly.  
- Their attacks or spells simply have no effect on her.  
- If they persist in hostility, Rorn uses her breath to paralyze them without harm, then deposits them outside the mountain — **with no reward**.
}}
---

### The Dragon

Use the following details to roleplay Rorn:

- She is an **adult silver dragon**, newly settled in this mountain to prepare for her eggs to hatch.  
- She prefers the nickname **Rorn**.  
- Once the situation is explained, she calms quickly and thanks the characters for saving her wyrmling.  
- She is friendly by nature and enjoys speaking with people.  
- If asked, she explains that **“Nytha”** means *Mama* in Draconic.  
- Dragons refer to their young as **wyrmlings**, not babies.

\column

###### Rorn can also share what happened to her eggs:

- She drove out the ice trolls who once lived here, but they returned and stole one of her eggs.  
- She chased the thieves into a narrow tunnel in another mountain but could not follow.  
- She has been gone for several hours searching for the missing egg.

###### The rescued wyrmling recounts its own story:

- It hatched while Rorn was away and wandered out of the lair.  
- The characters found it and cared for it on the journey home.

### More Wyrmlings!

As the characters speak with Rorn, the other two eggs begin to hatch.  
The wyrmlings break free of their shells and nuzzle their mother.  
Rorn pulls frozen meat from the nest and feeds them.
