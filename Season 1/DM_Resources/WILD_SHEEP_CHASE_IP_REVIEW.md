# Wild Sheep Chase IP Review

**Date:** 2026-04-17
**Task:** AEV-18 - Review Wild Sheep Chase third-party content
**Author:** Writer Agent

## Overview

The Wild Sheep Chase (DMs Guild ID: 892902) is a commercial third-party adventure module created by Richard Jansen-Parkes. It has been integrated into the Northwatch Wardens campaign with Aevoria adaptations.

## Current Status

The adventure has been **substantially adapted** for Northwatch Wardens:
- Integrated into Aevoria/Northreach setting
- Connected to Aeorian Echo narrative
- References The Pale Sickness (renamed from Frozen Sick)
- Includes Northwatch Wardens NPCs and lore

## Original Content Elements

The core narrative structure remains from the original:
- Wizard trapped in sheep body (Finethir Shinebright)
- Ambitious apprentice with malfunctioning wand (Noke)
- Tower infiltration adventure
- Comedy tone with magical chaos

## IP Compliance Options

### Option 1: Obtain Written Permission
- Contact Richard Jansen-Parkes via DMs Guild
- Request license for Northwatch Wardens use
- **Pros:** Keeps existing content
- **Cons:** May require fees; author may not respond

### Option 2: DMs Guild Exclusive Distribution
- Publish only on DMs Guild where original is sold
- Comply with DMs Guild terms of service
- **Pros:** Legal under DMs Guild licensing
- **Cons:** Limits distribution channels

### Option 3: Original Replacement Adventure
- Create new comedy adventure with original IP
- Replace Finethir with new original NPC
- Keep Aeorian Echo magical instability theme
- **Pros:** Full IP independence; original content
- **Cons:** Requires creative writing effort

## Recommendation

For full IP independence and distribution flexibility, **Option 3 (Original Replacement)** is recommended:

1. Keep the Aeorian Echo magical instability theme
2. Create original wizard-turned-into-creature NPC
3. Develop new tower/infiltration scenario
4. Maintain comedy tone and adventure structure

This preserves the campaign's narrative flow while eliminating third-party IP concerns.
