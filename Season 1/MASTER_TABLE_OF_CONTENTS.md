## Chapter Organization Principles

**Priority Hierarchy:**
1. **Campaign Framework** (Chapters 1-3): Understand campaign first
2. **Session Tools** (Chapter 4): Prep with these resources
3. **Adventures** (Chapter 5): Run individual adventures
4. **Secrets** (Chapter 6): Know what's hidden
5. **References** (Chapter 7): Look up specific details

**Content Placement:**
- **Beginner information:** Early in each section (Chapters 1-3)
- **Detailed reference:** Later in relevant chapters (Chapters 4-6)
- **Quick lookup:** Appendix (Chapter 7)

**Narrative vs. Reference:**
- **Chapters 1-3:** Narrative style (read through for understanding)
- **Chapters 4-6:** Reference style (use for prep and lookup)
- **Chapter 7:** Pure reference (index, lists, tables)

## Print & Organization Tips

**For Physical Campaign:**
- Print Chapters 1-3 as campaign introduction (50-60 pages)
- Print Chapter 4 sections as you prep (reference copies)
- Print Chapter 5 adventures before running them (one per week)
- Keep Chapter 6 digital (DM-only secrets, spoiler risk)

- Keep full PDF searchable
