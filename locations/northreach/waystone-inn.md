## Description

**First impression:** A sprawling two-story inn built around a weathered standing stone in a busy crossroads courtyard — the smell of woodsmoke and mutton stew hits before you even dismount.

**Atmosphere:** The common room is warm and perpetually half-full, filled with the low murmur of travelers trading route warnings over mugs of dark ale. Outside, the Waystone itself stands silent in the courtyard, its carvings worn smooth by decades of weather and the curious fingers of passersby. At night the stone occasionally catches the light in ways that don't quite make sense.

## Notable Features

- **The Waystone** — A pre-inn standing stone covered in carvings no one can fully read. Locals say it hums during storms; it is older than the settlement and marks the crossroads from which the inn takes its name.
- **The Contract Board** — The posted mission board where Wardens pick up assignments; the physical and social heart of guild operations.
- **The Common Room** — Where news, rumors, and travelers converge. The best intelligence in Northreach costs a drink and an open ear.
- **The Library** — A small but growing collection of maps, expedition reports, and historical documents curated by Lorewarden Elric Vael.
- **The Armory** — Practical gear stocked for Warden requisition; not a shop, but party members with standing can draw supplies here.

## Season 1: Northwatch Wardens Establish a Foothold

The Waystone Inn is fully operational as both a travelers' rest and guild headquarters. Brenna Thorne runs tight logistics while Mara Fenwick keeps the civilian side of the inn running smoothly. Elric Vael has been quietly monitoring the standing stone for years and is beginning to document a pattern of increasing resonance he has not yet shared with Brenna. Contracts from the surrounding settlements — wolf attacks, missing persons, trade route trouble — are posting faster than Wardens can clear them, creating a sense of mounting pressure.

## Aeorian Echo Presence

The standing stone is a pre-Fall Aevorian communication beacon, still partially functional. Its carvings are weathered Aevorian script; it contains a dormant magical receiver that resonates with other Aevorian artifacts across the region. The Echo has been slowly reactivating it over the past few years. Players using *detect magic* near the stone notice strengthening auras; high Arcana checks may begin to identify the script. During dramatically charged moments — artifact nearby, high-stakes confrontations — the stone may briefly pulse with cold blue light. A map of Aevorian sites in the region could theoretically be extracted from it with the right magic.

The inn's foundation incorporates Aevorian materials from ruins cleared by the first settlers. Some interior walls contain Aevorian metal and crystal, invisible beneath plaster and timber.

## Adventure Connections

- **Wolves at the Waystone** — The inn is the departure point and return anchor for this opening adventure; the contract board and common room serve as briefing and debrief spaces.
- **Wild Sheep Chase** — Party is dispatched from here to investigate reports of enchanted livestock near Noke's Tower.

## DM Secrets

**The Waystone's True Nature:** The stone was originally an Aevorian facility location marker. When Aevor crashed, it survived as a beacon. The current reactivation of Aevorian technology is making it active again — it is now communicating with other sites, including Salsvault. Elric has recognized the script and secretly documented seven instances of unusual resonance, but has not told Brenna.

**Hidden Spaces:**
- *Brenna's Hidden Office* — Behind a bookshelf in her quarters. Contains encrypted correspondence, maps marking suspected Aevorian sites, a 15-year journal documenting patterns, and contingency evacuation plans.
- *Elric's Research Vault* — Below the library behind a secret door. Holds forbidden Cobalt Soul texts, detailed Aevorian research, a working translation of Aevorian script, and theory documents about "the Echo." He has evidence he arguably should not possess.
- *Mara's Escape Cache* — Behind a false panel in her quarters. Contains emergency funds, false identity papers, weapons, a map of criminal safe houses, and evidence of her past life.
- *The Old Well* — Sealed in the courtyard "for safety." Actually connects to an underground passage that leads toward the Waystone's base, where ancient Aevorian construction survives intact.

**The Previous Owner:** Disappeared mysteriously twenty years ago. Left a journal about "voices from the stone" that Brenna found when she acquired the property. The last entry reads: *"It knows I'm listening."* The previous owner was sensitive to Aevorian magic — the Echo was active then, just quieter. They may have been driven mad or taken, and could potentially be encountered later as a transformed being.

**Current Threats:**
- Corsair operatives have surveilled the inn; at least one attempt has been made to access Elric's library.
- At least one staff member is a spy: a kitchen worker is a Corsair informant and a stable hand reports on arrivals and departures.
- Scrying and divination magic are being focused on the building; Elric's wards are being actively tested.
- Baleth's patron (if in play) receives reports about Warden activities here.
