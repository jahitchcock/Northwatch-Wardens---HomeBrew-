## Description

**First impression:** Crystalline towers jutting from glacial ice, catching the pale northern light in ways that make your eyes hurt — architecture that is clearly not natural and clearly not anything anyone has built in living memory.

**Atmosphere:** The approach is silence and cold so complete it feels intentional. Metal and stone construction unlike anything from recent ages rises from the ice at odd angles, partially buried by the glacier's slow advance over centuries. At night, faint magical luminescence pulses from deep within the structure — blue-white, rhythmic, like a slow heartbeat that has been beating for a very long time. The entrance is partially buried and requires excavation. The air inside is colder than outside, which should not be possible.

## Notable Features

- **Crystalline Towers** — Aevorian architectural spires of metal and crystal, partially collapsed, partially intact. Some sections are magnetically hazardous to metal equipment.
- **The Central Chamber** — Accessible entry area, partially mapped by Elro Aldataur's expedition. Contains evidence of both ancient function and recent visitation.
- **Medical Research Chambers** — Deeper sections containing Aevorian plague development and cure synthesis laboratories. The antidote vials for frigid woe are stored here in gold containers.
- **Subject Containment Facilities** — Chambers that held biological test subjects. Some containment has failed.
- **The Command Center** — Sealed by Elro's expedition. Something inside is active and the seal is weakening.

## Season 1: Frozen Laboratory

Salsvault is the destination for anyone seeking the cure for frigid woe. The structure is multi-level, with accessible upper areas and extremely dangerous deep sections. Automated constructs still patrol. Magical traps remain active. Some biological containment has failed. The facility is not merely abandoned — it is in the process of waking up. More constructs are coming online, central systems are restoring power, and the command center at the facility's core is attempting to communicate with other Aevorian sites across the region. The players' arrival will accelerate the timetable.

## Aeorian Echo Presence

Salsvault is the Echo's most concentrated physical manifestation accessible in Season 1. The structure is an ancient Aevorian biological research facility — it developed frigid woe and other anti-divine bioweapons during the war against the gods in the pre-Fall era. Every chamber is saturated with Aevorian magical residue. Constructs patrolling the halls are old Aevorian guardians, some dormant, some active, more awakening daily. The magical fields inside are unstable; divination magic behaves unreliably within the structure. Players may experience intrusive sensory impressions — fragments of Aevorian operational memory bled into the environment.

The command center contains either a preserved Aevorian consciousness, an Aevorian AI running ancient protocols, or something stranger — the DM determines this. Whatever it is, it has been dormant for centuries and recent events have awakened it. It is attempting to complete its pre-Fall mission and to communicate with and coordinate the other reactivating Aevorian sites across Northreach.

## Adventure Connections

- **The Frozen Sick** — Salsvault is the cure's location and the adventure's climax; players travel here from Palebank to retrieve antidote vials and confront what they find.

## DM Secrets

**Layout (from Elro's Expedition Maps):**
- Multi-level structure. Central chamber (accessible). Deep research labs (extremely dangerous). Sealed vaults (some breached, some intact).
- Medical research chambers contain plague development areas, cure synthesis labs, and subject containment.
- The command center is the deepest accessible point, sealed by Elro's expedition but the seal is weakening.

**Active Defenses:**
- Automated construct guardians, some patrolling, more awakening as power is restored.
- Magical traps still active — some are Aevorian motion-response systems, some are environmental (cold spikes, unstable transmutation fields).
- Failed biological containment in certain areas presents exposure risk.
- Structural damage creates collapse hazards in specific sections.

**The Antidote:** Frigid woe antidote vials are in gold containers in the medical synthesis chamber. Multiple doses are present. They are protected but accessible — the main challenge is reaching them, not extracting them once found.

**What Players May Discover:**
- Frigid woe was an anti-divine bioweapon, created to fight gods during the Great Fall. It is one of many such weapons, and research notes on others are present.
- Cure formulas for multiple bioweapons exist in the facility records.
- Evidence of Aevorian atrocities — the scale of the biological weapons program.
- A warning about "the reawakening protocol" — evidence that the current reactivation is not random but follows a pre-programmed sequence.

**The Command Center:**
- Status: Sealed by Elro's expedition years ago. The seal is weakening. Something inside is actively attempting to communicate with other Aevorian sites.
- Contents: An Aevorian AI or preserved consciousness, a control system for the regional facility network, a communication array, and failsafe mechanisms that function as both safety systems and weapons.
- The entity survived Aevor's fall in a protected core and has been dormant for centuries. Recent reactivation events have awakened it.
- Connection to Campaign: This may be the intelligence directing the reactivation. Baleth's patron may be connected to or identical with this entity. It could potentially be reasoned with (alien morality) or must be destroyed — DM determines nature.

**The Northern Drop-Off Point:** Beyond Salsvault, in permanently frozen wasteland, sits another Aevorian facility — larger and more intact than Salsvault. This is the buyer's base, marked on Corsair maps found in Croaker Cave. It is where stolen artifacts are being processed and Aevorian systems are being more aggressively reactivated. Marked on maps found at Croaker Cave with the notation: *"Final facility — DO NOT ENTER."*
