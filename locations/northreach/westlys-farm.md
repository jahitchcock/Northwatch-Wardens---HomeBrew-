## Description

**First impression:** A lone stone-and-timber farmstead at the edge of open grassland, the nearest tree line uncomfortably close — sheep pens recently reinforced with fresh timber, and no neighbors within an hour's walk.

**Atmosphere:** Wind cuts across the open fields unimpeded. The sheep are restless and the dogs more so, ears up and hackles raised at night even without visible cause. The farmstead is tidy and well-maintained — Westly is a hard worker — but there's a quality to the place, a low-grade unease, as if the ground itself is restless beneath your feet.

## Notable Features

- **The Sheep Pens** — Sturdy wooden enclosures, recently reinforced with extra timber and additional latching. The wolves have been getting in anyway. Tracks around the perimeter show unusual coordination.
- **The Main House** — Single-story stone and timber, functional and spare. Westly lives alone; signs of sleepless nights are evident.
- **The Forest Edge** — The old woods begin about two hundred yards west of the farm. Locals don't go in. The wolves come out of that direction.
- **The Well** — Deep and reliable, and the farm's greatest practical asset. The water has tasted faintly metallic for weeks.
- **Open Grazing Land** — Good pasture compared to closer farms, which is why Westly chose the isolation in the first place.

## Season 1: Ground Zero

Westly has been dealing with wolf attacks on his flock every few nights for weeks. Multiple livestock lost. He's too proud to ask for help but clearly needs it. The attacks are growing more frequent and more purposeful — wolves seem to be targeting the farm specifically, not just opportunistically raiding. Westly himself has been sleeping poorly, plagued by strange dreams he can't quite describe. He's noticed his sheep have been behaving oddly even during the day, long before any wolves appear.

## Aeorian Echo Presence

Westly's Farm sits directly above a sealed Aevorian research facility — a small biological modification station whose automated systems are reactivating and sending signals that attract test subjects. Residual magic leaks upward through the soil, affecting the animals. The wolves are drawn specifically to this location because of those signals. Westly himself is being passively "marked" by the reactivating facility: exposure to the residual magic is causing his strange dreams and subtle biological changes he is unaware of. A *detect magic* check near the farm or its well registers faint, persistent auras of transmutation and conjuration magic with no visible source.

## Adventure Connections

- **Wolves of Welton** — Westly's Farm is the adventure's ground-zero location; investigating the wolf attacks leads here, and the trail into the old woods begins at the farm's western fence line.

## DM Secrets

**The Facility Below:** A small Aevorian research station focused on biological modification sits sealed beneath the farm. Its automated systems have been reactivating, sending signals that attract the experimentally modified wolves. The facility predates the farm by millennia.

**What's Happening to Westly:**
- Prolonged exposure to leaking residual magic is causing recurring strange dreams (Aevorian imagery he can't interpret).
- Subtle biological changes are occurring — he is unaware of them, but a healer examining him closely (DC 14 Medicine or *detect magic*) might notice.
- He is being "marked" by whatever intelligence is reactivating the sites — he could become patient zero for something far worse if the facility fully comes online.

**Potential Developments:**
- Players may detect magic around the farm or near the well.
- The entrance to the facility could be discovered beneath the barn or in the cellar (hidden, requires investigation).
- Westly could be abducted for examination if players fail to act quickly enough.
- The farm site could become a major adventure location for a future session focused on the Aevorian underground network.
