## Description

**First impression:** A mountain temple carved directly into the cliff face, partially collapsed, the carved dragon reliefs above the entrance worn but unmistakable — the air at this altitude is thin and the silence is the kind that feels earned.

**Atmosphere:** The approach is treacherous: narrow alpine passes, loose scree, snow even in late season, and an altitude that makes exertion punishing. The temple itself is cold stone and old memory. Some areas are collapsed rubble; others are mysteriously, inexplicably intact — sealed chambers where the dust has never settled, torches unlit for centuries but their brackets still in place. Deep in the structure, the air carries a faint hum that players may initially attribute to wind.

## Notable Features

- **The Carved Entrance** — Dragon reliefs above the main doors, partly collapsed but still legible. The craftsmanship is extraordinary; this was built for heroes by people who believed in the work.
- **The Main Sanctum** — Central hall with shrines to the Dragonknight order, their history rendered in bas-relief. Partially collapsed ceiling, but structurally safe.
- **The Catacombs** — Tombs of fallen Dragonknights beneath the temple. Each tomb is a ward anchor for the seals below. Some tombs have already been disturbed by treasure hunters.
- **Intact Sealed Chambers** — Sections of the temple that have resisted centuries of decay. Something has preserved them. The preservation is not natural.
- **The Descent** — A path downward, past the catacombs, toward the Aevorian facility sealed beneath the temple. The Dragonknights built over and around it deliberately.

## Season 1: Sealed Evil Stirring

The temple has been abandoned for generations. Weather, time, and occasional treasure hunters have taken their toll on the upper levels, but the seals the Dragonknights wove into the architecture are — or were — holding. Some tombs in the catacombs have been disturbed; each desecrated tomb weakens the wards on the Aevorian facility below. Something beneath the temple is responding to the Echo's reactivation elsewhere in the region. Strange sounds emanate from the catacombs at night. Animals within a mile of the temple behave erratically. The area around the temple shows signs of corruption — discolored stone, unusual frost patterns, plants growing in wrong directions.

## Aeorian Echo Presence

The Echo here is active and escalating. The Aevorian facility sealed beneath the temple is a major research complex focused on draconic subjects — it contains biological weapons specifically designed to target dragons. The Dragonknights did not merely build a memorial; they built a containment structure, weaving magical wards into every tomb and wall to suppress the facility below. Those wards are failing after centuries, and the facility is attempting to break through, sending signals to other Aevorian sites, and corrupting the area around it.

The Dragonknight spirits may be present as guardians — conflicted entities who cannot stop what is coming alone but could guide players who demonstrate genuine purpose. Disrespecting the tombs risks accelerating the breach; treating the dead with honor may earn vital assistance.

## Adventure Connections

- **Temple of the Dragonknights** — This location is the entirety of the adventure; reaching it, navigating the upper temple, descending to the catacombs, and confronting what lies below.

## DM Secrets

**The Historical Truth:**
- The Dragonknights did not merely "fight alongside dragons" — they were a post-Fall military order specifically formed to fight Aevorian threats after the crash. Their "dragon allies" were actual draconic beings who opposed Aevor.
- The "great evil" they sealed is an Aevorian research complex focused on creating biological weapons that target dragons specifically.
- Most Dragonknights died in the final sealing battle. Survivors disbanded and did not pass down full knowledge, allowing legend to replace history.

**The Sealed Facility Below:**
- A major Aevorian research complex, focused on draconic subjects — their physiology, weaknesses, and kill mechanisms.
- Contains biological weapons targeting dragons specifically. If deployed, they could spread to the general population.
- Still active: constructs patrol inner sections, magical defenses respond to intrusion, research data is accessible to Aevorian readers.

**The Ward Architecture:**
- The temple structure was designed to contain, not merely commemorate.
- Every Dragonknight tomb is a ward anchor. Desecration weakens seals.
- Some tombs already disturbed by treasure hunters — the wards there are compromised.
- The Dragonknight spirits are aware of the threat and attempting to protect the seals. They respond to those who show respect.

**What's Stirring:**
- The Aevorian facility is responding to Echo activity elsewhere in the region — the reactivation sequence includes this site.
- It is attempting to break through the seals, sending signals to connect with the regional Aevorian network.
- The area corruption (discolored stone, abnormal frost patterns) is the facility's magical leakage as the wards thin.

**The Stakes:**
- The dragon-killing bioweapons in this facility represent a catastrophic threat. If reactivated and deployed, they threaten not only draconic allies but could spread to humanoid populations.
- Sealing the facility properly (as the Dragonknights intended) is likely more feasible than destroying it outright.
- The Dragonknight ghosts could become crucial allies for players who approach with respect — they know the sealing methods and can guide the party through the descent.
