## Description

**First impression:** A cluster of squat stone longhouses hunkered against a grey sea, the docks reeking of fish and brine, the wind cutting through every gap in your cloak — the last warm lights before Eiselcross.

**Atmosphere:** Palebank smells of salt, fish oil, and wood tar. The sound is constant wind and the creak of dock lines. Light is scarce in winter: grey days, early dark, firelight through oiled-skin windows. The cold here is not dramatic — it is simply the permanent condition. Residents move with a purposeful economy, wasting nothing, saying little to strangers until trust is established.

## Notable Features

- **The Meeting Hall** — Community gathering space and Elro Aldataur's residence. Large hearth, community records, emergency supplies. Site of town meetings, celebrations, and crisis management.
- **The Docks** — Stone and timber construction, rebuilt after every major storm. Dawn departures, evening returns, constant maintenance. Access point for Eiselcross expeditions.
- **Pelc's Curiosities** — Run by Verla Pelc (elf, reclusive). Deals in artifacts from Eiselcross expeditions; opens irregularly. Knowledgeable but antisocial. Currently shuttered.
- **Urgon Wenth's Cabin** — Recently vacated; the owner died under mysterious circumstances. Contains explorer's equipment and personal effects. Scene of significant events.
- **Tulgi Lutan's Cabin** — Home of a fiercely independent dwarf trapper who avoids community events. Shows signs of recent illness.

## Season 1: The Frozen Sick

A strange illness — frigid woe — has killed at least one resident and fear of contagion is spreading. Others are showing symptoms. The community is tightly wound between its ethic of mutual aid (no one gets left behind) and the terrifying possibility that close contact is making things worse. Elro Aldataur is holding things together through force of personal authority, but he is frightened. The community is looking to outsiders for help — specifically, for anyone willing to trace the illness to its source and find a cure. The cure, Elro suspects, lies north — past where most sane people go.

## Aeorian Echo Presence

Palebank is a collection point for Aevorian artifacts retrieved from Eiselcross expeditions, and someone has been systematically stealing the most significant pieces — specifically biological research materials and blue crystal structures. The Echo's presence here is felt through its agents: the Blackwake Corsairs are using Palebank as a staging ground, and frigid woe itself is an Aevorian bioweapon reactivated from Salsvault. The illness in the village is the Echo made physical — a preview of what full Aevorian reactivation could mean. Verla Pelc was investigating the artifact-theft pattern before she was infected.

## Adventure Connections

- **The Frozen Sick** — Palebank is the adventure's base of operations; the illness, Urgon's death, the Corsair conspiracy threads, and the push north to Salsvault all originate here.

## DM Secrets

**The Far North Connection — Organized Theft:** Someone is specifically targeting Far North relics using Palebank as a collection point. Multiple thefts have occurred beyond Urgon's items; the pattern shows prioritization of biological research materials and intact blue crystal structures. The operation has insider knowledge — there is a Frostwatch informant (one militia member, identity DM's choice, currently sick and possibly dying).

**Pelc's Curiosities — Hidden Research:** Before being infected, Verla Pelc was investigating the artifact theft pattern and noticed the same buyer operating through multiple fronts. She decoded some Aevorian text fragments from artifacts and was preparing a report. Hidden behind a false panel in her shop (DC 15 Investigation): a journal, a list of artifact sales with notes, decoded Aevorian text fragments, a map with suspected crash sites marked, and an unsent letter to a northern scholar. The decoded text warns about biological weapons, references "reawakening" procedures, and mentions "the speaking ice."

**Urgon Wenth's Cabin:** Under a floorboard (DC 13 Investigation): a journal detailing his Salsvault expedition, a map of Salsvault with annotations, rough sketches of Aevorian symbols, and a note reading *"Blue ones are important — but dangerous."* His final journal entry: *"Felt watched the whole time."*

**Tulgi Lutan's Cabin:** Hidden in her bedroll: Corsair instructions (coded), a list of acquisition priorities, payment records, and a contact schedule for her sister Hulil. Her instructions specify: steal biological research materials first; intact blue crystal structures are highest priority; report unusual activity immediately; drop-off location is Croaker Cave.

**Elro Aldataur's Guilt:** Elro leads a Salsvault expedition years ago. Sealed in a box in the Meeting Hall: a detailed partial map of Salsvault, descriptions of specific dangers, notes on which traps killed which team members, and a personal journal about his guilt over surviving. He knows the exact route, the location of medical research chambers, the trigger mechanisms for major traps, and that the facility showed signs of active intelligence even then. He can brief the party and his maps are invaluable — he may offer to accompany them despite his age.

**The Frostwatch Informant:** One militia member was recruited for money and didn't fully understand the implications. Now sick (possibly dying) and potentially regretful. Could confess to players or reveal information to save others. Evidence in their quarters if players search.
