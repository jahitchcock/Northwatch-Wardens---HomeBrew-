## Description

**First impression:** A three-story stone tower on a cleared hill visible from miles away, no obvious door, stained-glass windows showing scenes that don't quite match the season outside, and a rotating domed roof that turns even when there's no wind.

**Atmosphere:** The approach to the tower is marked by warning signs in at least three languages, several of which are clearly handwritten additions to older ones. The cleared ground around the tower is unnaturally clean — no weeds, no debris, as though the earth itself has been persuaded to behave. From inside the tower, even on still nights, there is the sound of active work: bubbling, thumping, the occasional sharp crack of something going unexpectedly right or wrong. The tower smells faintly of ozone.

## Notable Features

- **The Hidden Door** — The tower's entrance is invisible to casual inspection and requires either knowing where to look or being invited. Finethir opens it himself for visitors he deems worth the trouble.
- **The Stained Glass Windows** — Show scenes that do not correspond to the current season, time of day, or weather. Some windows appear to show locations elsewhere in Northreach.
- **The Rotating Observatory** — The domed roof spins on no visible mechanism and appears to track things other than stars.
- **The Warning Signs** — A genuinely helpful collection of specific cautions ("Do not touch anything that glows blue," "Livestock entering eastern quadrant will be returned in altered state," "The door knows").
- **The Working Laboratory (Second Floor)** — Active experiments, a reference library, and scrying equipment. Not accessible without invitation.

## Season 1: Eccentric Neighbor

Finethir Shinebright is well-known in the region by reputation and mostly left alone by preference. Local stories about the tower include the week it rained fish, the incident with the animated furniture, the bandit that got polymorphed into a sheep, and various unexplained lights during storms. Travelers are warned to stay on the road and not touch anything that glows. Shinebright occasionally emerges for supplies at the Waystone Inn, where he is treated with the particular wary courtesy reserved for people who are both clearly intelligent and clearly dangerous. He is, currently, conducting an experiment that involves sheep.

## Aeorian Echo Presence

The tower is one of the best-informed locations in Northreach regarding the Echo. Shinebright is a dedicated Aevorian researcher who is fully aware of the reactivation pattern, has identified the entity he believes is behind it, knows the locations of major Aevorian facilities in the region, and has calculated probability of regional catastrophe if nothing intervenes. He uses the observatory to monitor Aevorian sites and the laboratory's scrying equipment to watch for activation events. The "accidents" at the tower are frequently field tests — the Wild Sheep Chase is a deliberate study of Aevorian bio-transmutation magic to understand transformation mechanics and develop counter-spells. His basement contains an extensive Aevorian research archive, captured and contained Aevorian artifacts, biological samples, and countermeasure prototypes that no one else in the region knows exist.

## Adventure Connections

- **Wild Sheep Chase** — The tower is the adventure's central location; Shinebright's sheep have escaped (or been released) in transformed states and the party is dispatched to recover them, ultimately meeting the wizard.
- **Wolves at the Waystone** — Shinebright's tower is visible from the Waystone road; he may be an early NPC contact for information about unusual magical activity in the region.

## DM Secrets

**Shinebright's True Purpose:**
- He is not an eccentric hobbyist. He is a dedicated Aevorian researcher building countermeasures to Aevorian threats before they fully manifest.
- "Accidents" are often field tests providing genuine research data.
- He knows about the Echo and has been preparing for it for years. He is deeply alarmed by the current acceleration of the reactivation sequence.
- The Wild Sheep Chase is not an accident — it is a deliberate test of Aevorian bio-transmutation mechanics, studying transformation to develop anti-transformation counterspells.

**Tower Layout (Actual):**
- *Ground Floor:* Appears to be living space. Actually conceals defenses that recognize and respond to threats; the furniture can animate.
- *Second Floor:* Working laboratory, active experiments, reference library, and scrying equipment watching Aevorian sites.
- *Third Floor:* Observatory (astronomy and divination), long-range detection apparatus, communication equipment, and a ritual space for major magic.
- *Basement (Unknown to Most):* Extensive Aevorian research archive, captured and contained Aevorian artifacts, biological samples, and countermeasure prototypes.

**What Shinebright Knows:**
- Full understanding of the reactivation pattern and which sites are activating in which order.
- A theory about the specific entity (or entities) driving reactivation.
- Knowledge of major facility locations including the command center beyond Salsvault.
- Predictions about the next activation events and approximate timeline.
- Fundamental mechanics of Aevorian magic, its weaknesses, and how to work against it.
- Translation of Aevorian texts (he is one of very few people in the region who can read them).

**Potential Alliance:**
- If the players demonstrate competence, show genuine understanding of the danger, and prove they are not simply looting or glory-seeking, Shinebright will share his research, provide magical support, and offer countermeasure equipment.
- The Wild Sheep Chase is effectively his audition for the players — he engineered the situation to see how they handle unexpected magical complications.
- He needs: specific components from Aevorian sites, data from active facilities, and capable adventurers willing to act as field agents. He can provide: Aevorian text translation, facility maps, protective magic against Aevorian effects, and theoretical solutions to the final threat.
