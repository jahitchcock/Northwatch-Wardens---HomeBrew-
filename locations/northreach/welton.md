## Description

**First impression:** A tidy farming village of stone-and-timber buildings arranged around a central square, smelling of fresh-turned earth and woodsmoke — welcoming at a glance, but the conversations stop a beat too long when strangers arrive.

**Atmosphere:** The clatter of the Old Mill's wheel carries through the whole village day and night. Market days bring real warmth and noise, but in the evenings there's a watchfulness to the place — animals penned early, shutters latched before full dark, conversations about the wolves dropping to hushed tones. The community is tight-knit in the way frontier people get when they've had to survive together.

## Notable Features

- **The Shepherd's Crook Inn** — Social heart of the village run by Darvin Crook (human, third generation). Warm, loud, and serving mutton stew and local ale; the place where you hear the truth if you buy enough rounds.
- **Village Square** — Market every ten days, community well, message board, and the stocks (rarely used). Site of town meetings and seasonal celebrations.
- **The Old Mill** — Grain processing run by Millwright Torrhen (dwarf). Its wheel can be heard throughout the village and is a reliable indicator that things are normal.
- **Council Hall** — Where the five-member elected council meets. Represents farming, herding, trade, crafts, and general community interests.
- **Communal Grazing Lands** — Pastures to the south, recently patrolled more heavily due to wolf activity.

## Season 1: Wolves at the Gate

Wolf attacks have put Welton on edge. Livestock losses are mounting and patrols around outlying farms have been increased, but the wolves' behavior is troublingly organized — too coordinated for natural predators. The community is divided between those demanding a hunting party and those who fear something more sinister is driving the attacks. Superstitions about "the old woods" north of the village are being invoked more often. The Council is looking for outside help while trying to prevent panic.

## Aeorian Echo Presence

A minor Aevorian crash site lies in the old woods north of Welton — the source of the regional magical disturbances affecting local wildlife. Residual magic leaks through the soil and into animals nearby. The wolves are not merely aggressive; they are Aevorian experimental subjects being directed through biological modification as a trial run for a larger operation. Local superstitions about the old woods are genuine protective folk memory: the cairns marking those woods correspond to real dangers.

## DM Secrets

**The Wolves' True Nature:** The wolves are Aevorian experimental subjects being controlled through biological modification via an Aevorian device. Someone visits their lair regularly and uses the device to issue commands. This is a deliberate trial run testing how much intelligence can be granted to animals, and assessing Welton's defensive response. The goal is evaluating the settlement for future targeting.

**Why Welton:** Welton is close to a minor Aevorian crash site, isolated enough for testing, large enough to provide useful behavioral data, and small enough to be manageable.

**The Old Woods North of Welton:** A partially buried Aevorian structure sits here — biological research materials (degraded), malfunctioning but active security systems, and evidence of recent visits by an unknown person. The automated systems attack intruders; residual magic causes hallucinations; biological materials could be infectious; the structure is structurally unstable. This is where wolf modifications originate — the "controller" has established a base here.

**The Shepherd's Crook and Darvin Crook:** Darvin is an unwitting information broker. He sells village news to a southern contact (unknown agent — could be Cobalt Soul, government, or Corsair-affiliated) who pays for regular reports about unusual occurrences. Darvin doesn't realize he is part of a larger intelligence network. Encoded messages may be found; players could observe a meeting with his contact or recruit him as an informant.
