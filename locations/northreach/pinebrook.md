## Description

**First impression:** A village that smells of pine resin, horse sweat, and money — more wagons than houses, it seems, and every conversation sounds like a negotiation.

**Atmosphere:** Pinebrook is perpetually busy in the way that commercial towns are: the clatter of the market square, wagon wheels on packed earth, the rhythmic clang of the smithy repairing axles. The Pine Lodge offers warm rooms and better food than you'd expect this far north, but the price is steep and the walls are thin. Everyone here is watching everyone else — politely, professionally, and with purpose.

## Notable Features

- **Market Square** — The largest market in Northreach, open daily (weather permitting), with permanent stalls, temporary vendor spaces, and an auction block. Peak trade every five days when caravan schedules align.
- **Grimley's Trading Post** — Run by Torven Grimley (human, third generation). Known for fair prices, honest weights, and an exceptional memory for faces. The main commercial building in town.
- **The Pine Lodge** — Halfling proprietress Merryn Pine runs an expensive but genuinely secure inn with private meeting rooms favored by traveling merchants.
- **Warehouses** — Several secure storage buildings for goods in transit; competition for space has recently intensified.
- **The Armed Watch** — Pinebrook's Merchant's Council maintains a paid guard to protect trade goods; they are professional but prioritize commerce over heroics.

## Season 1: Commerce Under Pressure

Trade continues normally but with increased caution. Rumors of danger on the routes to Welton and northeast toward Palebank have pushed prices up on several goods. Some merchants are rerouting or delaying shipments. Competition for warehouse space has intensified as traders hold stock rather than ship it north. On the surface, Pinebrook is prospering; underneath, the Merchant's Council is nervous and certain goods are moving through channels that don't appear in the public ledgers.

## Aeorian Echo Presence

No overt Echo presence is apparent in Pinebrook itself. However, the market square contains active dead drop points used by Corsair operatives to exchange coded information and route Aevorian artifacts through legitimate trade channels. The Echo's influence here is indirect: the conspiracy running through Pinebrook is feeding stolen Aevorian materials northward, accelerating the reactivation sequence. An abandoned logging camp north of town sits atop an Aevorian structure entrance — workers found something in the earth years ago and the company quietly abandoned the site.

## DM Secrets

**Grimley's Trading Post — Hidden Business:** Torven Grimley is a fence for the Blackwake Corsairs, handling stolen Aevorian artifacts and using his legitimate trading operation as cover. Artifacts are mixed with genuine antiques; "buyers" are fellow conspirators; records are coded with fake and real transactions interleaved. He maintains a hidden ledger with real transactions, a secret storage space with current inventory, encoded Corsair correspondence, and a map showing smuggling routes. He knows the identities of some conspirators and the route to a northern drop-off point.

**The Pine Lodge — Merryn Pine:** She reports on guests and her private meeting rooms have listening arrangements. She sells intelligence to the highest bidder. She knows who is moving what goods and where, has overheard conversations about Aevorian artifacts, and knows the identities of multiple conspirators. She is neutral but morally flexible — she could be persuaded, threatened, or recruited depending on player approach.

**The Market Square — Dead Drops:** Contains multiple active dead drop locations used by Corsair operatives: a hollow post in a permanent stall, the base of the market fountain, a specific barrel behind a warehouse, and a mark on the well cover indicating messages. Players who know what to look for can intercept encoded messages and Aevorian artifacts in transit.

**The Abandoned Logging Camp (North of Pinebrook):** Workers found an entrance to an Aevorian structure years ago and experienced strange effects. The company quietly shut down and discouraged the area. The Corsairs now use it as a waystation — items are stored here temporarily before moving north. The entrance to the Aevorian structure is covered by a collapsed building. Guarded by hired muscle; contains stored artifacts and evidence of the larger operation.
