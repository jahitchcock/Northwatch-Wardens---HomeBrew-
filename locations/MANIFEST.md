# Locations Manifest

Regions of Aevoria, ordered by campaign relevance. Northreach is home. The Far North holds the mystery source.

## Active Campaign Regions

- [Northreach](northreach) — Home region. Waystone Inn, Welton, Pinebrook, Palebank Village
- [The Far North](the-far-north) — Wilderness beyond Northreach. Salsvault and ancient Aevorian ruins
- [Emberlands](emberlands) — Ashlands region, Red Covenant territory, volcanic frontier
- [Verdant Marches](verdant-marches) — Agricultural lowlands south of Northreach
- [Nullwood Expanse](nullwood-expanse) — Ancient forest, Silent Court territory
- [Shattered Coast](shattered-coast) — Maritime region, Tide Callers' Guild, island chains

## Distant Regions

- [Stonebound Depths](stonebound-depths) — Deep mountain territory, Stonebound Assembly dwarves
- [Sunken Dominion](sunken-dominion) — Former coastal empire, underwater ruins
- [Solaris Dominion](solaris-dominion) — Southern empire, expansionist, Divinities' Beacon influence
- [Vharoxis](vharoxis) — City-state, Unmasked Parliament, complex political landscape
