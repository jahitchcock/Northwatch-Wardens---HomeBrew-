# Welcome to Aevoria — DM Dashboard

## The Northwatch Wardens

The **Northwatch Wardens** are a newly chartered adventurers' guild operating out of the **Waystone Inn** on the cold frontier of **Northreach**, a hard-edged region of the world of **Aevoria**. The players are the guild's first field unit — untested, underfunded, and operating in a region where danger is real and trust is earned slowly.

Tone is grounded low-magic frontier. Magic exists but it unsettles people. Resources are limited. NPCs remember what the party did last season. The wilderness is indifferent, and the settlements are only barely safer.

Adventures are **order-independent** — run them in any sequence. Each one plants a subtle clue that feeds the campaign's central mystery. Players may notice a pattern emerging; they shouldn't know what it means yet.

---

## The Aeorian Echo (DM Eyes Only)

An ancient arcane disturbance is spreading outward from **Salsvault**, ruins of the lost civilization of Aevor buried in the Northreach mountains. The players do not know this yet. Each adventure exposes one symptom without naming the source.

| Symptom | Adventure | Manifestation |
|---|---|---|
| Animal intelligence | Wolves of Welton | Welton wolves coordinating, herding livestock deliberately |
| Magical disease | Frozen Sick | Frigid Woe afflicting Palebank villagers near the ruins |
| Reality distortion | Wild Sheep Chase | Noke's wand warping local space around the tower |
| Construct activation | Salsvault | Dormant Aeorian constructs waking inside the ruins |

The Echo is not a villain with a plan — it is a residual effect, like radiation from a buried source. It is growing. The Lorewarden suspects. The Covenant knows and is watching.

## Key Guild NPCs

- **Marshal Brenna Thorne** — Field commander. Assigns contracts, runs threat assessments. Direct, pragmatic, protective of her people.
- **Steward Mara Fenwick** — Quartermaster. Controls guild coin, equipment, and favors owed. Knows everyone's business.
- **Lorewarden Elric Vael** — Arcane advisor. Handles research, artifacts, and inter-faction correspondence. Knows more than he says, and says less than he knows.

## Factions at a Glance

- **Northwatch Wardens** — The party's guild. Neutral frontier organization, recently chartered, operating on thin resources and reputation.
- **Solace Trade Consortium** — Merchant interests. Friendly on the surface, profit-motivated underneath. Have agents in most settlements.
- **Covenant of the Silent Vigil** — Ancient order that monitors Aeorian remnants. Aware of Salsvault. Their silence on the Echo is deliberate.
- **Archivists of Aevor** — Scholars hunting Aeorian artifacts. Academically motivated but willing to bend ethics for a good find.
- **Freelance Brotherhood** — Rival contractors. Compete for the same guild contracts. Not evil, just competition — and sometimes useful allies.

Full faction dynamics, relationships, and hidden agendas: [factions/faction-dynamics.md](../factions/faction-dynamics.md)

## Using This Dashboard

This is a quick-reference for features used at the table — not a tutorial.

### Navigation

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Tabs** (Adventures / NPCs / Locations / GM Lore) | Switch between the four main content sections |
| **Season filter** | Scopes the Adventures and NPCs lists to a specific season's subdirectory |
| **Search bar** | Full-text search across all markdown files in the campaign directory |
| **File tree** | Click a folder to expand it; click a file to preview it in the main pane |

### Tools Menu

| Tool | Use at table |
|---|---|
| **Random Encounter** | Pick a CR, get a formatted monster stat block pulled from the 5e SRD |
| **Treasure Hoard** | Generate loot by party level — coins, gems, magic items |
| **World Tables** | Roll on custom tables stored in the `tables/` directory |
| **Seasonal Calendar** | Browse Aevorian months, holidays, and weather patterns by season |
| **5etools links** | Open spells, conditions, and rules in a popup overlay without leaving the doc |

### Terminal

The built-in terminal opens a PowerShell session in the campaign root directory. Use it for builds (`./build.sh`), git operations, and file management. The **⌨ Path** button sends the current file's path to the terminal at the cursor position. **Ctrl+V** pastes from the system clipboard.

### Cross-Reference Links

Blue dotted links throughout NPC and location documents open popup previews without replacing your current document. NPC table rows are clickable — click any row for an inline stat popup with ability scores, traits, and DM notes.
