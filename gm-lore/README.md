# GM Lore

This directory is the **index of all DM-facing lore** in the Northwatch Wardens campaign. Content here contains spoilers, secrets, and campaign truths that should **not be shared with players**.

> ⚠️ **SPOILER WARNING:** Everything linked from this index contains plot reveals, NPC secrets, and hidden campaign information.

---

## What Belongs Here?

GM-only content includes:
- NPC secret motivations and hidden backgrounds
- The full truth behind the Aeorian Echo
- Campaign arc spoilers and revelation sequences
- Adventure prep notes not meant for player eyes
- Faction hidden agendas
