# Book Two — *The Dragon-Sworn*
### Part Two: The War — Beat Sheet
*Part of the series* The Old Songs of Aevoria

---

## Chapter 10: The Spark of War

**Opening image/hook:** Valdris stands before the council, his voice ringing with conviction as he declares war on the eastern warlords.

**Key beats:**
- Valdris reveals his plan to use deeper bonds on captive dragons to secure victory.
- Rynn is assigned as a scout, her connection to the melody growing stronger.
- Corwin watches in silence, sensing the weight of what's coming.

**Character moments:**
- **Valdris:** Sees war as a means to consolidate power and control the Pact.
- **Rynn:** Feels drawn to the mission but senses something is wrong.
- **Corwin:** Remembers the cost of past wars, his unease deepening.

**Dialogue notes:**
- Valdris: "The dragons are not our enemies. They are our tools."
- Rynn: "What if they're not?"

**Closing beat:** The council approves the declaration of war. Valdris turns to Corwin with a smirk, implying he knows more than he should.

**Pacing:** Tension 3 (rising). Rhythm shifts from tense deliberation to charged anticipation.
**Thematic thread:** Hubris as a weapon — war is declared not for survival but for control.

**Character moments:**
