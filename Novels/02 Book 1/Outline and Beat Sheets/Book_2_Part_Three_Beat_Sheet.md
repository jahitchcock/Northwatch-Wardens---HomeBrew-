# Book Two — *The Dragon-Sworn*
### Part Three: The Breaking — Beat Sheet
*Part of the series* The Old Songs of Aevoria

---

## Chapter 20: The Song Unbound

**Opening image/hook:** Corwin finds Rynn in a ruined village, her eyes hollow, the melody humming faintly from her lips.

**Key beats:**
- Corwin confronts Valdris's forces to rescue Rynn.
- Rynn is half-mad, her voice echoing with the corrupted song.
- She recognises Corwin but cannot remember who he is.

**Character moments:**
- **Corwin:** Grief-stricken and desperate, sees the cost of his refusal.
- **Rynn:** Changed by Valdris's bonds, struggling between past and present.
- **Valdris:** Smirks as he watches Corwin fail, knowing he has won.

**Dialogue notes:**
- Rynn: "I remember... but I do not know what it means."
- Corwin: "You carry a song that was never yours to sing."

**Closing beat:** Corwin carries an unconscious Rynn away from the battlefield as the sky darkens.

**Pacing:** Tension 5 (rising dread). Rhythm: staccato, fast-paced action intercut with emotional beats.
**Thematic thread:** The cost of hubris and the corruption of sacred things.
