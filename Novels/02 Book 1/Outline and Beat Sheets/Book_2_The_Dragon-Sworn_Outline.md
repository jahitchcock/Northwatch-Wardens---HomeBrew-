# Book 2: The Dragon-Sworn

## Series Context
See master reference and series outline for context.

- **Setting:** The Age of Dragons — an era when dragons filled the sky, wild and unbonded, before the first mortal-dragon pact.
- **Frame:** Elder-Elowen narrates, Corwin having returned to her in the grove late in her life to confess this story — the oldest thing he carries.
- **Reader's experience:** Believes this is a second, unrelated tragic love lost to war. Attentive readers feel resonance — the song recurs.
- **Character lineup:** Corwin walks among the dragon-sworn as the one man who cannot bind. Wren returns as Rynn. Valdris architects the Pact. Ensemble: Kazir, Sereveth, Aerim.

## Overview

An ensemble of mismatched companions — among them a brilliant, bitter figure tempted to turn sacred pact into weapon — bind themselves to dragons for the first time, blessed by the gods. Corwin walks among them as the one man who cannot bind a dragon, an outsider who sees the hubris forming before anyone else will admit it. The war is glorious, then it is grief: the first pact is broken, and that breaking is why dragons recede into legend.

Wren returns in Rynn's face — a young wayfinder carrying the old melody — tangled in the war, and is taken by the very pact Corwin warned against.

## Key Characters
- **Corwin** — the Unending. Walks among the dragon-sworn as the one man who cannot bind.
- **Valdris** — brilliant, bitter human scholar-warrior. Architects the Dragon Pact.
- **Rynn** — young wayfinder from the coastal lowlands. Wren's new face. Carries the melody.
- **Kazir** — dwarven forge-master of Tharundor. Bonds deep-dragon Thurum.
- **Sereveth** — elven queen of the eastern sky-reaches. Bonds ice-dragon Nyraeth.
- **High Priest Aerim** — Radiant Queen's voice. Brokers the Pact.
- **Vorath** — first bonded dragon (bronze). Driven mad by Valdris's deeper bond.

## Major Themes & Arcs
- **Hubris and its cost:** The Pact is beautiful and right in its pure form. Valdris's corruption of it destroys everything.
- **The song recurs:** Rynn shares a soul-thread with Wren. The melody surfaces unbidden.
- **Corwin's weariness:** He has seen this pattern before. His tragedy is knowing the shape of disaster.

## Plot Summary

### Part 1 — The Gathering (Chapters 1-9)
Frame opens: elder-Elowen, Corwin returns, begins telling. The Age of Dragons is established — wild dragons, unbound. A council at Skyreach fortress. Valdris proposes the Dragon Pact. Corwin watches, asks one quiet question. Rynn arrives — young wayfinder, carries the melody. Corwin feels the recognition. First bondings succeed. Corwin refuses to bind. Rynn seeks him out. Valdris experiments with deeper bonds. Vorath breaks, driven mad. Council fractures. War is coming.

### Part 2 — The War (Chapters 10-19)
War against the eastern warlords. Valdris becomes war-leader, increasingly ruthless. The deeper bonding used on captive dragons. Rynn serves as scout. Corwin and Rynn grow closer. Kazir publicly opposes Valdris. The war turns. Rynn is captured. Corwin must choose: break his refusal and bind, or lose her again.

### Part 3 — The Breaking (Chapters 20-30)
The Pact collapses. Corwin finds Rynn, but she is changed. Dragons grow restless. Valdris attempts mass binding and the backlash destroys Skyreach. Sereveth dies with Nyraeth. Kazir survives, broken. Corwin and Rynn have their last moments — she remembers the melody but does not know why. She dies. The remaining dragons withdraw from mortal lands. Corwin walks east. Frame returns: elder-Elowen, the fire, the telling done.

## Full Chapter Outline

## Part 1: The Gathering

### Chapter 1: The Elder's Return
Elder-Elowen welcomes Corwin back to the Memory Grove, where he begins recounting the story of the Age of Dragons — the oldest thing he carries.

### Chapter 2: The Walk East
Corwin recalls his journey eastward, drawn by a summons to the fortress of Skyreach where a council is gathering to address the dragon question.

### Chapter 3: The Skyreach Council
The council gathers at Skyreach — elven queen Sereveth, dwarven forge-master Kazir, human chieftains, coastal peoples. Valdris presents his proposal: the first mortal-dragon Pact.

### Chapter 4: The Quiet Question
Corwin asks one question that cuts to the heart of Valdris's plan: "What binds a dragon to a mortal, and what does a broken binding leave behind?" The question lingers.

### Chapter 5: The Arrival of Rynn
Rynn arrives at Skyreach — a young wayfinder from the coastal lowlands, carrying an ancient melody she cannot place. Corwin feels the recognition he has learned to dread.

### Chapter 6: The First Bondings
The first dragon-mortal bonds succeed: Valdris bonds Vorath (bronze), Sereveth bonds Nyraeth (ice-blue), Kazir bonds Thurum (stone). The world changes.

### Chapter 7: The Unbound One
Corwin refuses to bind a dragon, drawing suspicion from Valdris. Why would anyone refuse such power? Rynn seeks Corwin out, drawn by an inexplicable connection.

### Chapter 8: The Madness of Vorath
Valdris experiments with a deeper bond on Vorath, pushing beyond the original rite. Vorath goes mad with pain, crashes into the valley. The fracture begins.

### Chapter 9: The Fractured Council
The council fractures. Kazir confronts Corwin: "You knew." Corwin: "I have seen it before." The war is coming.

---

## Part 2: The War

### Chapter 10: The Spark of War
Valdris declares war on the eastern warlords who fear the bonded riders. The deeper bonds are used on captive dragons.

### Chapter 11: The Battle at Ironspire
The first great battle. The bonded riders are devastating — dragons and mortals fighting as one. But the cost is visible to those who watch closely.

### Chapter 12: The Forge of Power
Kazir's forge at Skyreach becomes a place of resistance. He argues that the Pact has been corrupted. Corwin agrees but is powerless.

### Chapter 13: The Song of Rynn
Rynn's melody begins to surface unbidden. She asks Corwin why she carries a tune she has never learned. He does not answer.

### Chapter 14: The Captive Dragons
Valdris captures wild dragons and subjects them to the deeper bonding. The corruption escalates. The bonded dragons grow restless.

### Chapter 15: The Choice of Corwin
Corwin must choose between protecting Rynn and staying unbonded. Rynn is captured during a raid.

### Chapter 16: The Fall of Aerim
The High Priest Aerim is killed — whether by Valdris or by a dragon gone mad is unclear. His death removes the last moral authority.

### Chapter 17: The Siege of Skyreach
Skyreach falls under siege. Valdris turns the Pact into a weapon of conquest, not defense.

### Chapter 18: The Captured Song
Rynn is held captive, her melody twisted by exposure to the deeper bonds. Corwin prepares to act.

### Chapter 19: The Breaking Point
Corwin confronts Valdris. The confrontation sets the stage for the final collapse.

---

## Part 3: The Breaking

### Chapter 20: The Song Unbound
Corwin rescues Rynn, but she is changed — touched by Valdris's experiments, carrying the corruption of the deeper bonds.

### Chapter 21: The Dragons' Restlessness
The bonded dragons grow restless and wild. The Pact is fraying. Kazir warns that the end is near.

### Chapter 22: The Mass Bonding
Valdris attempts a mass binding of all remaining dragons, believing he can control them through sheer force of will.

### Chapter 23: The Destruction of Skyreach
The backlash from the mass binding destroys Skyreach. Sereveth dies with Nyraeth, their bond breaking in fire.

### Chapter 24: The Death of Sereveth
The elven queen's sacrifice is complete. Kazir survives but is broken, his faith in the Pact shattered.

### Chapter 25: The Fall of Rynn
Rynn is consumed by the corruption. In her last moments, she remembers the melody but does not know why. Corwin holds her.

### Chapter 26: The Dragons Withdraw
The surviving dragons withdraw from mortal lands, retreating into myth and legend. The Pact is dead.

### Chapter 27: The Walk East Again
Corwin walks east, carrying the burden of what was lost. The war is over. Nothing is left.

### Chapter 28: The Elder's Memory
The frame returns: elder-Elowen reflects on Corwin's telling. She recognizes the echoes of her own past in his story.

### Chapter 29: The Melody Remains
The melody carried by Rynn lingers in the world — a thread that will surface again, on other lips, in other ages.

### Chapter 30: The End of the Song
Elder-Elowen returns to the grove. The story is complete. Corwin has walked east. But the song is not over — it never is.
