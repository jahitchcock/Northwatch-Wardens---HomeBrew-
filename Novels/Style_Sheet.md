# The Old Songs of Aevoria
## Style Sheet — Author Voice

*The voice is the one decision that touches every sentence. This document is binding for all drafting. When in doubt, return here.*

---

## The One-Line Definition

**A Memory-Keeper's told legend** — oral-mythic but intimate, looking back across ages, so every scene carries the ache of hindsight; warm in heart, spare in word, kinetic when blood is drawn.

---

## The Frame (the source of the whole voice)

The narrator, **Elowen**, is a Memory-Keeper: her art is speaking the past aloud so nothing true is lost. The novel is therefore, in-world, **a told legend** — recounted by someone looking back across ages. This single frame is what lets us harvest all five influences coherently instead of averaging them into mud:

- **King's intimate, voice-forward narration** — a tale told *to* you, close and mythic at once — but without modern colloquialism. A told legend earns the closeness honestly.
- **Salvatore's reflective interludes** absorbed *into* the narration rather than bolted on as separate journal entries: elder-Elowen knows what young-Elowen didn't, so reflection and dramatic irony live in the prose itself.
- **Death Gate's withholding** made graceful — a Memory-Keeper can foreshadow without revealing; remembering *is* knowing the end while telling the beginning.
- **Feist's scope** delivered in compressed mythic strokes, not chronicle digressions — a legend telescopes time, says "three hundred years later" in a breath.
- **Dragonlance's emotional directness**, disciplined by **elven restraint** — an elf who values every word because words are how the dead survive will never over-emote. This is our signature departure: understatement that lands harder than melodrama.

---

## POV & Tense

- **First person, Elowen, past tense.** Always anchored to her — never head-hop.
- **Two Elowens in one voice:** young-Elowen *living* the scene, elder-Elowen *telling* it. The gap between what she felt then and what she knows now is the engine of the prose. Let elder-Elowen's hindsight bleed into young scenes sparingly and to devastating effect.
- For ages Elowen did not personally witness (later books), she narrates as a Memory-Keeper reconstructing what was kept — "This is how it was told to me," "The dwarves remember it so." This licenses scope and withholding alike.

---

## The Three Registers

Deliberately contrasted. The contrast between stillness and violence is the breath of the book.

1. **Narration** — clear, propulsive, a told-tale rhythm (King / Feist). Moves the story. Carries the world in lived texture, never lore-dumps.
2. **Reflection** — lyrical, brief, faintly aphoristic, always elder-Elowen's hindsight (Salvatore). Used in small doses, often to close a scene. One or two sentences, rarely a paragraph.
3. **Action** — short verbs, motion, no metaphor mid-strike (Salvatore). Sentences shorten. The lyric drops away entirely until the blood is still.

Never blend all three in one breath. Move between them; let the seams show as rhythm.

---

## House Rules (sentence-level)

1. **Restraint is the default.** State grief plainly and stop. Trust white space. The elven aesthetic forbids wallowing — so the rare moment we *do* let emotion run will detonate.
2. **Motif as memory.** Recurrence is the craft made literal — the Song, repeated images, beats that rhyme across the book. Things return because memory returns. Plant, echo, pay off.
3. **Earn the mythic through the personal.** Cosmology only ever arrives *felt*, through one person in one moment. No lore for its own sake. (The canon firewall and the wound-first principle agree here.)
4. **Wound first, world second.** Every scene presses on a character's wound. The world is the fuel, never the destination.
5. **Concrete over abstract.** A name, a hand on bark, a wound that closes wrong — not "loss," "grief," "the weight of ages." Show the specific; let the reader supply the large word.
6. **Earnest, never cynical.** Honor, sacrifice, and love are real and they cost. The books believe in them. That sincerity is the point.
7. **Tragedy is permitted.** We do not flinch at the price. The joy means something only because the loss is allowed to stay lost.

---

## Diction

- No anachronism, no modern idiom, no meta. The register is timeless-mythic, not medieval-cosplay — clarity over archaism. Avoid "thee/thou" and faux-antique clutter.
- **No profanity** (consistent with an elven Memory-Keeper's voice and the series' register).
- Avoid purple: no piled adjectives, no three metaphors where one will do. When tempted to a flourish, cut to the plain word — the restraint *is* the elven beauty.
- Sentence rhythm varies by register; reflection can run long and balanced, action runs short and hard, narration sits in between.

---

## Dialogue

- **Corwin:** dry warmth held at arm's length; deflection; a weight under every line. Says less than he knows. Occasionally an aside that is far older than it sounds, dropped flat.
- **Elowen (young):** earnest, stubborn, hungry; speaks more than her elders think wise.
- **Wren:** warm, quick, fearless, funny; the lightness the book needs before it darkens.
- **Brannoc:** blunt, grumbling, comic; concrete and craft-minded.
- **Ilvath:** gentle, sorrowful, doctrinaire; never cruel — grief wearing the mask of law.
- Keep tags simple ("said"). Let voice, not adverbs, carry character.

---

## Failure Modes We Refuse (named so we don't drift)

- **Dragonlance:** head-hopping; melodrama; purple over-emotion. → We anchor to Elowen and we restrain.
- **Feist:** expository chronicle-stretches; time-skips mid-scene; camera pulled so far back we lose the wound. → We telescope in mythic strokes, but stay in the body of a scene.
- **Death Gate:** coldness; cleverness over heart. → Elowen keeps us warm.
- **Salvatore:** combat repetition; sentimentality; over-explained philosophy. → Vary the fights; trust the reader; let reflection stay brief.
- **King:** profanity; meta; sprawl; modern colloquialism. → Mythic register, disciplined length, no winking.

The principle: take each author's strength, leave its failure mode at the door.

---

## The Voice in Miniature (calibration)

> *Flat:* She whispered her brother's name to the tree, even though it was forbidden.
>
> *In-voice:* I gave the tree his name. Quietly, the old way, the forbidden way — *Fenn* — and the bark took it the way it takes rain, and gave nothing back. I was young. I still believed a thing kept was a thing saved.

The final sentence — elder-Elowen's hindsight bleeding into the young scene — is the whole voice in one move. Aim for that seam in every chapter.
