# Antlers of the First Fallen

*Wondrous item (antlers), uncommon*

A large set of antlers belonging to the awakened fawn's father, a direwolf killed by the ambient Echo spread in Northreach. The antlers carry a faint geometric frost patterning in the marrow — crystalline and inward-growing, like frost that never melts. When shown to those who can sense such things, they emit a subtle draconic aura.

John the Paladin has incorporated these antlers into his partial-plate armor, wearing them as both trophy and burden. He does not yet know their full significance.

---

## Properties

**Sentience Resonance.** You have advantage on Charisma checks directed at awakened, sapient, or fey creatures. Such beings sense something honest and true in what you carry, as though the antlers bear witness to a grief they understand.

**Echo-Burden Healing (1/Long Rest).** When you use Lay on Hands or cast a healing spell on a creature that has been harmed specifically by the Echo (ambient or directed), the creature regains the maximum possible hit points from that healing.

**Draconic Significance.** The antlers show faint geometric crystalline patterning under *Detect Magic*. A successful DC 14 Arcana or Nature check reveals they are saturated with ambient Echo resonance and carry a subtle draconic aura — though they are not themselves dragon remains.

**Symbolic Weight.** While the antlers are incorporated into your armor, you cannot benefit from another set of shoulder/helm-mounted magical equipment.

## History

The antlers were given to the party by an awakened fawn encountered on the road to Pinebrook. The fawn had eaten grass near geometric frost patterns and, as a result, gained sapience and the ability to speak Common. She was terrified and newly conscious. The party helped her, and in gratitude — or grief — she gave away the most valuable thing she carried: her father's antlers.

The father was the earliest documented casualty of the ambient Echo spread in Northreach, possibly predating the wolf awakening. The bone marrow shows faint geometric crystalline patterning, like frost that grew inward. If shown the antlers, Elric will recognize their significance.

{{note
**DM Note:** The Antlers of the First Fallen are intentionally light on raw mechanics. Their power is narrative and symbolic. The advantage on Charisma checks should not apply to mundane humanoids — reserve it for awakened beasts, fey, dragons, and other creatures who would sense the Echo-weight carried in the armor. The maximum healing ability is a "secret" power that may trigger automatically when John heals an Echo victim, or it may require the DM to announce it. The antlers may become attuned to other properties as the campaign progresses.
}}
