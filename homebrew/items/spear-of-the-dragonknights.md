# Spear of the Dragonknights

*Weapon (spear), uncommon*

Etched along the haft is a serpent coiled around a blade — the emblem of the Dragonknight order. The weapon feels unnaturally balanced, as if made for someone who spent decades learning the weight of a spear.

---

## Properties

**+1 Spear.** You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

**Thrown (range 20/60 ft.)** The spear can be thrown and returns to your hand immediately after it hits or misses a target.

**Dragonknight's Will.** When you score a critical hit with this spear, the target must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

---

## History

Found in the armory of the Temple of the Dragonknights. The Dragonknights were an order of warriors who predated the Calamity, sworn to keep ancient things sealed beneath the earth. This spear is one of their standard-issue weapons — functional, unadorned, made to last.

{{note
**DM Note:** This is an intentionally modest item — a +1 spear with a ribbon ability. It signals that the Dragonknights were soldiers, not archmages. If a player bonds with it narratively, consider upgrading to +2 after a significant Dragonknight-related story beat.
}}
