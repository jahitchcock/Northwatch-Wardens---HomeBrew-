# Armor of the First Fallen

*Plate armor, very rare (requires attunement)*

This suit of ancient plate armor bears the insignia of the Aeorian sentinel corps — a stylized eye overlaid on a cracked crown. The metal has an unnatural warmth to it and faintly resonates when Aeorian ruins are nearby. The first soldiers who wore it were said to never return from the Far North.

---

## Properties

**AC** 18 · **Weight** 65 lb. · **Stealth** Disadvantage

**Aeorian Resonance.** While wearing this armor, you have advantage on Intelligence (Arcana) checks to identify Aeorian artifacts or magic.

**Echo Ward.** When you would take damage from a spell or magical effect, you can use your reaction to reduce that damage by 1d10 + your proficiency bonus. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, regaining all uses on a long rest.

**Sentinel's Awareness.** You cannot be surprised while you are conscious, and you have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks.

**Antimagic Susceptibility.** The armor is incapacitated while in the area of an *antimagic field*. If targeted by *dispel magic*, it must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against the caster's spell save DC or fall unconscious for 1 minute.

**False Appearance.** While motionless, the armor is indistinguishable from a normal suit of decorated plate armor.

---

## Attunement

To attune to this armor, you must spend a short rest wearing it within sight of an Aeorian ruin or artifact. The armor bonds with the first creature that completes this attunement and cannot be attuned to another until the current bond is severed (willing release or death of the attuned).

---

## History

Recovered from Salsvault during the party's investigation of the Pale Sickness. The armor predates the Calamity and shows no signs of mundane wear. Analysis by Lorewarden Elric Vael suggests the metal was treated with crystallized Echo resonance — a process that should be impossible given the magic required.

{{note
**DM Note:** This armor is intentionally powerful for its rarity tier. It is designed as a landmark reward for the Pale Sickness adventure. The Antimagic Susceptibility is a deliberate vulnerability to balance its Echo Ward ability. The armor will become narratively significant in Season 2 when Aeorian sentinels appear.
}}

## Variant: John's Armor

If a specific player character named John (or similar) bonds with this armor, the DM may rename it **John's Armor of the First Fallen** and add the following personalized property:

**Warden's Bond.** Once per long rest, when a creature you can see within 60 feet would drop to 0 hit points, you can use your reaction to have them drop to 1 hit point instead.
