# Glide

*Passive Movement — Hatchling Stage*

Falcor's wings are real, and he is very aware of this. They are also not yet strong enough to sustain flight. What he can do — and does with great flair — is leap from an elevated position and ride the air in a controlled descent or horizontal carry. He has strong opinions about which perches are worth jumping from.

## Mechanics

**Glide Speed:** 20 ft. per round (replaces normal movement for that round).

**Activation:** Falcor must begin his glide from a position **at least 5 feet above the ground** (a table, a shoulder, a fence post, a convenient enemy's head). He cannot initiate a glide from ground level.

**Direction:** Movement is in a **straight line only**. He cannot turn, bank, or curve mid-glide.

**Altitude:** Falcor **cannot gain altitude** during a glide. He moves horizontally and descends gradually. For every 10 feet of horizontal distance traveled, he descends approximately 5 feet. If he runs out of altitude before completing the full 20 feet, he lands early (no damage from landing).

**Hovering:** Not possible at this stage. If Falcor's forward movement stops for any reason during a glide (e.g., he enters difficult terrain, is grappled, or strikes an obstacle), he lands immediately.

**Vertical Drop:** If Falcor simply drops from height without gliding (no horizontal intent), he falls normally. The Glide ability only triggers when he intentionally launches outward, not straight down.

**Opportunity Attacks:** Gliding out of a creature's reach provokes opportunity attacks as normal. Falcor's small size and erratic wingbeats impose no special advantage or disadvantage on these attacks.

**Carry Capacity:** Falcor cannot carry passengers or significant loads while gliding. He can carry objects up to 1 lb. (roughly a small scroll, a coin, a button he found on the floor).

**Conditions Summary**

| Condition | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Minimum launch height | 5 ft. above ground |
| Movement type | Straight line, horizontal |
| Altitude change | Descent only (~5 ft. per 10 ft. traveled) |
| Max glide distance | 20 ft. per round |
| Can hover? | No |
| Can gain altitude? | No |

**Upgrade Path — Full Flight.** When Perkia reaches **Ranger level 6**, Falcor's wings develop enough for sustained flight. His glide is replaced by a **fly speed of 30 ft.** He will be able to hover, change direction freely, and gain altitude. He will also be insufferably smug about it for the remainder of the session.

At **Ranger levels 3–5**, only the Glide ability is available. Do not award full fly speed early, even if narratively tempting.
