# Bonded Companion

*Passive — Ranger Companion Bond*

Falcor is Perkia Fali's bonded beast companion, granted through the **Beastmaster** Ranger archetype (PHB p. 93). Their bond runs deeper than trainer and animal — Falcor understands Perkia's intent through posture, scent, and years of proximity. He does not simply obey commands; he reads the room.

## Mechanics

**Action Economy.** Falcor acts on Perkia's turn, not on a separate initiative count. He does not roll initiative independently.

**Default Behavior — Dodge.** If Perkia does not use a bonus action to direct him, Falcor takes the **Dodge** action on her turn. He is alert, watchful, and instinctively stays light on his feet when no clear threat presents itself.

**Directing Falcor.** Perkia can use her **bonus action** on her turn to command Falcor to take one of the following actions: Attack, Dash, Disengage, Help, or Hide. He uses Perkia's proficiency bonus (+2) for applicable rolls.

**Attacks.** When directed to Attack, Falcor makes one **Bite** attack:
- **To Hit:** +6 (DEX +2, proficiency +2, homebrew bond +2)
- **Damage:** 1d6 + 2 piercing
- **Range:** 5 ft. (melee)

**Shared Senses.** Falcor communicates simple impressions — hunger, wariness, curiosity, pride — through the bond. He cannot relay tactical information, but Perkia always knows roughly where he is and whether he is distressed.

**If Perkia Is Incapacitated.** Falcor acts on his own initiative at the start of the round, defaulting to self-preservation (Dash or Disengage toward safety). He will not abandon Perkia voluntarily.

**Scaling.** Falcor's statistics and available actions scale with Perkia's ranger level. As she advances, his capabilities grow — additional attacks, improved defenses, and eventually full flight (see *Glide* ability).

> *Falcor does not think of himself as a pet. He thinks of himself as a colleague. A small, silver, occasionally distracted colleague who is very interested in that copper button on your belt pouch.*
