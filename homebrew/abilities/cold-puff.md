# Cold Puff

*Active Ability — Breath Weapon (Hatchling Stage)*

Silver dragons are born with the essence of winter in their lungs. Falcor has not yet mastered this gift — what he produces is less a breath weapon and more an enthusiastic exhalation. The frost is real. The damage is modest. His satisfaction is immeasurable.

## Mechanics

**Uses:** 1 / Day. Recharges on a long rest.

**Action Cost:** Action (Perkia must use her bonus action to direct Falcor, or he may use it on his own turn if acting independently).

**Range:** Self (5-foot cone, originating from Falcor).

**Area:** 5-foot cone. At Tiny size, this cone is narrow — roughly the width of a dinner plate at its widest point.

**Effect:** Each creature in the area is dusted with a light coating of frost.
- **Damage:** 1 cold damage (no saving throw — the damage is negligible enough that a save is not warranted at this stage).
- **Visual:** A thin rime of ice crystals forms on the target's clothing, skin, or armor for 1 round before melting. In cold environments, the effect lingers slightly longer.
- **No Concentration. No concentration required.**

**Tactical Notes.** Cold Puff deals 1 cold damage — not nothing, but close. Its primary uses are:
- Finishing a 1 HP enemy (he will absolutely feel that this was his kill).
- Cooling a hot drink at the table.
- Announcing his presence to someone who has not noticed him yet.
- Expressing displeasure with a creature that touched Perkia's gear without asking.

**Upgrade Path.** As Falcor matures through the **Fledgling** and **Juvenile** stages, Cold Puff will evolve into a proper **Cold Breath** weapon: expanded cone, dice-based damage, and a Constitution saving throw for half. At the Hatchling stage, he is still figuring out how his lungs work.

> *He holds the pose for a full two seconds after the frost lands — chin up, one foreclaw slightly raised — waiting to see if anyone noticed. They always notice. He always looks proud.*
