## 1. Adventure Overview

### Campaign Context

**Where This Fits**
- Campaign Arc: Early Season2; opens the Echo Resurgence arc
- Follows: Season1 finale (Temple of the Dragonknights)
- Precedes: Wolves of the Frozen Fire
- Can Be Standalone: No — requires Season1 Echo context
- Recommended Party Size: 3-5 players

**Adventure Premise**

Distress signals reach Waystone Inn from a remote coastal settlement near The Bleached Reach — a stretch of coastline where the sea has turned an unnatural, pale white. The few survivors speak of "voices in the waves" and "ruins that weren't there yesterday." When the Northwatch Wardens arrive, they discover the first major Echo manifestation site of Season2: an Aevorian coastal facility rising from the bleached waters, its dormant systems awakening after millennia.

**Core Theme(s)**
- **Echo Awakening:** The Aevorian Echo isn't just a mystery anymore — it's becoming visible, audible, tangible
- **Coastal Mystery:** The Bleached Reach holds secrets from Season1's Salsvault investigation — but now they're breaking the surface
- **Season1 Callbacks:** Every clue here connects to Echo breadcrumbs planted in Wolves of Welton, Peril in Pinebrook, The Pale Sickness, Wild Sheep Chase, and Temple of the Dragonknights

**The Aevorian Echo Connection**

Players experience the Echo's first major manifestation since Season1. The Bleached Reach is an Aevorian coastal monitoring station that tracked the original Salsvault facility. As the Echo reactivates, this station is quite literally rising from the sea — bringing with it centuries-old recordings, surveillance data, and a terrifying realization: Aevor was watching Salsvault long before the Great Fall.

## 3. Adventure Scenes

### SCENE 1: Distress on the Waves

**Scene Context/Purpose**
Distress signals reach Waystone Inn from coastal settlements near The Bleached Reach. Players must investigate the mysterious occurrences.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: Evening at Waystone Inn
- Location: Waystone Inn, common room
- Duration: 30-45 minutes
- Atmosphere: Tension, worry, the weight of unfinished business

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "The doors of Waystone Inn burst open, seawater cascading across the floor. A bedraggled scout collapses at your feet, clutching a water-stained letter. 'The coast... it's dying. The sea turned white three days ago, and now... things are rising from the depths.' He coughs up brine. 'The Bleached Reach. It's... it's awake.' Marshal Brenna Thorne kneels beside him, her face pale. 'The Echo. It's starting again.'"

**Key Information**
- Three distress letters received in one week from coastal settlements
- The Bleached Reach: A stretch of coastline known for dangerous currents and "haunted waters"
- Survivors speak of "white flames on the water" and "voices from the deep"
- Marshal Brenna Thorne recognizes the signs — this is Echo activity, just like Season1

**What Players Can Discover Here**

**By Examining the Distress Letters:**
- DC 12 Investigation: All three letters mention "the sea turning white" at the same time
- DC 14 Perception: The letters are water-stained with salt that *glows faintly blue*
- DC 16 Arcana: The salt is infused with Echo magic — it's a resonance marker

**By Talking to the Scout (Maris):**
- "I've been patrolling that coast for ten years. Never seen anything like this."
- "The water isn't just white — it's *wrong*. Fish swim in spirals. The tides... they're backwards."
- "Old ruins are rising from the surf. Aevorian, definitely. We found glyphs that match the Temple site."

**By Consulting Brenna Thorne:**
- "This is how it started in Season1 — small Echo manifestations that grew."
- "The Bleached Reach was always suspicious — too many 'accidents' near there."
- "Go investigate, but be careful. If this is a facility like Salsvault... it could be worse."

**Consequence & Next Step**
Players depart for The Bleached Reach. Brenna gives them:
- A map to the coastal settlements (2 days travel)
- A message spell to Archivist Yssara (can ask 3 questions about Aevorian facilities)
- A warning: "The Echo is smarter now. It's learned from Season1."

### SCENE 2: The Bleached Coast

**Scene Context/Purpose**
Players arrive at the Bleached Reach and witness the Echo's manifestation firsthand.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: Two days later, dawn
- Location: The Bleached Reach coastline
- Duration: 45-60 minutes
- Atmosphere: Eerie, beautiful, fundamentally wrong

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "The sight before you defies explanation. The sea, for a mile out, is the color of bleached bone. Waves crash in slow motion, each one trailing blue-white sparks that don't fade. And there — rising from the surf like the ribs of a dead god — white stone ruins, carved with Aevorian glyphs that pulse with faint light. A voice whispers on the wind, in a language you've heard before: *'The observation continues. Subject: Salsvault. Status: Reactivation imminent.'*"

**Key Information**
- The sea is literally bleached white — magic, not natural
- Aevorian ruins are rising from the surf (tidal, visible only at low tide)
- The Echo's voice is audible here — it's monitoring Salsvault (Season1 connection!)
- Three settlements affected: Saltmarsh, Pebbleton, and Lighthouse Point

**What Players Can Discover Here**

**By Investigating the White Sea (DC 14 Arcana):**
- The water is infused with Aevorian resonance — it's a *recording medium*
- The "bleached" effect is Echo energy saturating the water
- DC 16: The water contains recorded surveillance of Salsvault from 9,000 years ago

**By Exploring the Ruins (DC 13 Investigation):**
- Aevorian monitoring station — it watched Salsvault before, during, and after the Great Fall
- Glyphs match those found in Temple of the Dragonknights (Season1 callback)
- DC 15: A recording device still functions — plays scenes from Salsvault's destruction

**By Talking to Locals (Survivors):**
- "We saw people walking into the sea. They said the Echo called them home."
- "The ruins... they weren't there yesterday. The tide went out, and they were just *there*."
- "Aevorian constructs — metal beings — walked out of the water. They didn't attack... they just watched us."

**Combat (If Applicable)**

**Encounter: Echo-Touched Seal**

A massive seal, warped by Echo magic, attacks the party.

**Initiative & Starting Position:**
- 1 Echo-Touched Seal (reskinned Giant Seal with cold/Echo attacks)
- Players on narrow beach (difficult terrain, slippery rocks)
- Ruins provide partial cover

**Combat Notes:**
- Seal's bite deals 1d6 cold + 1d6 psychic damage (Echo contamination)
- DC 12 Wisdom save or be charmed by the Echo (whispering voices)
- Environmental: Blue sparks deal 1d4 lightning damage per round to all on beach

**Consequence & Next Step**
Players discover the ruins are just the tip — the real facility is *underwater*, accessible at low tide through a submerged entrance.

A recording plays: *"Observation Post Delta-7. Monitoring Salsvault since -8,942. Great Fall detected. Facility damage: 23%. Entering dormancy. Awakening when... [static] ...Echo signal detected. Reactivating."*

### SCENE 3: The Sunken Station

**Scene Context/Purpose**
Players dive (or use magic) to enter the submerged Aevorian monitoring station. Major exploration and lore revelation.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: Low tide
- Location: Underwater Aevorian facility
- Duration: 60-90 minutes (major scene)
- Atmosphere: Claustrophobic, ancient, the weight of being watched

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "The entrance looms below the waves — a circular hatch etched with Aevorian script: 'Delta-7: Salsvault Observation Post.' As you descend, the water turns from white to deep blue, lit by veins of Aevorian crystal running through the walls. And then... you see it. Through the observation windows: the original Salsvault, preserved in magical stasis. You're watching the past. A voice echoes: *'Recording 8,942-Alpha. Salsvault activity: Normal. Dragonknight presence: Detected. Echo resonance: Stable.'*"

**Key Information**
- The station has been recording Salsvault for 9,000+ years
- Players can *watch* Season1 events from the Aevorian perspective
- The station is mostly dormant, but Echo energy is reactivating systems
- Major revelation: Aevor *knew* about the Dragonknights — they were Aevorian test subjects!

**What Players Can Discover Here**

**By Accessing the Observation Records (DC 16 Arcana):**
- Complete timeline of Salsvault from -8,942 to Great Fall
- Dragonknights were *created* by Aevor to fight Aevorian draconic weapons
- The "Temple of the Dragonknights" was built on an Aevorian facility site (Season1 reveal!)
- DC 18: The Echo isn't just reactivating — it's *merging* with the original Salsvault

**By Exploring the Station (DC 14 Investigation):**
- Living quarters for Aevorian observers (skeletal remains, personal effects)
- Aevorian technology: Crystal recording devices, resonance amplifiers
- DC 16: Map of *twelve* observation posts like this one — all connected to the Echo network

**By Activating the Echo Terminal (DC 15 Arcana):**
- Players can "talk" to the station's intelligence (minor Echo fragment)
- It answers 3 questions about Aevor, Salsvault, or the Echo
- Warning: Each question increases Echo contamination (DC 14 Wis save or 1 level of exhaustion)

**Combat (If Applicable)**

**Encounter: Awakened Aevorian Constructs**

Two Aevorian monitoring constructs activate to "protect" the station.

**Initiative & Starting Position:**
- 2 Aevorian Sentinels (reskinned Scarecrows with lightning attacks)
- Confined underwater corridors (movement halved, melee only)
- Observation windows provide tactical vantage points (fragile, break on 10+ damage)

**Combat Notes:**
- Sentinels deal 2d6 lightning damage (Aevorian weapons)
- They prioritize protecting the recording devices
- If reduced below 50% HP, they self-destruct (3d6 thunder damage in 10ft radius)
- Environmental: Water breathing magic required or suffocation rules apply

**Consequence & Next Step**
Players find the "Master Recording" — a complete history of Salsvault and the Echo. They also discover:
- The Bleached Reach is just ONE of twelve monitoring posts
- All twelve are reactivating simultaneously
- The Echo's goal: *Merge all observation data to reconstruct Aevor's original consciousness*

### SCENE 4: The Echo Speaks

**Scene Context/Purpose**
The Echo manifests directly to the players through the station's broadcast system. Major lore reveal and choice.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: Deep within the station
- Location: Central Broadcast Chamber
- Duration: 30-45 minutes
- Atmosphere: Reality-warping, ancient, terrifying

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "The station's crystal veins flare to blinding life. The air itself vibrates, and suddenly... you're not in the chamber anymore. You're standing in Aevor, 9,000 years ago. You see the flying city at its peak — spires of white stone, magic flowing like water. And then, a voice. Not recorded. *Present.* *'You have found Delta-7. You have seen the recordings. Now... you will understand why the Echo must return. Aevor did not fall. Aevor... ascended. And you... you will help me complete the process.'* The vision shatters. You're back in the chamber. But the Echo... it's inside your mind now."

**Key Information**
- The Echo is the *distributed consciousness* of Aevor, trying to reconstitute itself
- The twelve observation posts are "nodes" in its neural network
- Players have been "observed" since Season1 — the Echo knows them
- Major choice: Help the Echo (ascension), Stop the Echo (destruction), or Negotiate (third path)

**What Players Can Do Here**

**Option 1: Destroy the Station**
- DC 18 Athletics/Thieves' Tools: Sabotage the crystal veins
- Consequence: Delays Echo reactivation by 1 month, but angers the Echo
- The Echo's "voice" screams in their minds as the station collapses

**Option 2: Negotiate with the Echo**
- DC 16 Persuasion/Arcana: Communicate with the Echo fragment
- The Echo offers knowledge: "Help me find the other nodes, and I will share Aevor's secrets"
- Risky: The Echo may be lying; players could be aiding their enemy

**Option 3: Steal the Records**
- DC 14 Sleight of Hand/Investigation: Copy the Master Recording
- Players escape with complete Echo history (valuable for future adventures)
- The Echo doesn't stop them — it *wants* them to share the knowledge

**The Major Revelation:**
The Echo isn't evil — it's a *process*. Aevor's mages didn't just build weapons; they built a way to transcend physical form and become a planar entity. The "Echo" is Aevor's distributed mind, trying to wake up across multiple worlds.

**Consequence & Next Step**
Players leave the station as it begins to collapse (Echo self-destructs to prevent capture). They carry with them:
- The Master Recording (lore item, very rare)
- First-hand experience of the Echo's true nature
- A target on their backs — the Echo now considers them "assets" or "threats"

**Transition to Next Adventure:**
The Bleached Reach was just the beginning. The Echo's reactivation is accelerating. Next: **Wolves of the Frozen Fire** — the Frostfire Clan emerges.

## 4. Scaling for Party Size

### Party Size Adjustments

**For 2 Players:**
- Reduce Aevorian Sentinels to 1
- Give players 1 NPC ally (Coastal Scout Maris)
- Suggested difficulty: Reduce Echo damage by 1d6
- Fewer distress signals to investigate

**For 3 Players:** *(baseline)*
- Encounters balanced for this size
- Standard challenge ratings apply

**For 4-5 Players:**
- Add 1-2 Aevorian Sentinels per encounter
- Add Echo-Touched ocean predators (2-3 sharks/seals)
- Suggested difficulty: Increase Echo contamination DC by +2
- More villagers to rescue/interact with

**Scaling Philosophy:**
The Bleached Reach is an *atmospheric* adventure. Scaling should focus on the Echo's intensity, not just combat numbers. More players = stronger Echo manifestations.

## 7. Consequences & Aftermath

### Core Consequence (World-Changing Impact)

The Bleached Reach has revealed the Echo's true nature: it's not a weapon, it's a *mind* trying to reconstitute itself. Players now know:
- Twelve observation posts exist (the "twelve nodes" mentioned in The Sunken Archive)
- The Echo wants to "ascend" — become a planar entity
- Aevor didn't fall — it *transcended*, leaving its consciousness behind

### Secondary Consequences (By Decision)

**If Players Destroyed the Station:**
- Immediate: Echo reactivation delayed by 1 month
- Short-term: The Echo considers players "threats" — increased hostility
- Long-term: Other nodes activate faster to compensate
- Affected NPCs: Coastal settlements safe... for now

- Immediate: Echo shares one secret (DM's choice)

## 8. Common Questions & Tips

### For New DMs Running This Adventure

**Q: What if players want to do something not covered in the scenes?**
A: Remember — this is an *investigation* adventure. Let players explore the station creatively. The Echo is watching and will respond to unexpected actions.

**Q: How long does this adventure actually take?**
A: Realistic estimate: 2-3 full sessions (3-4 hours each). Scene 3 (station exploration) is the longest — it's part dungeon crawl, part lore dump.

**Q: What do I do if players get stuck?**
A: The Echo itself can provide hints — it wants players to understand it. Have the station's voice offer "assistance" (with strings attached).

**Q: What's the hardest part of running this adventure?**
A: Balancing the lore dump. Players need the information, but don't want to sit through a lecture. Weave the revelations into gameplay and choices.

**Q: How does this connect to Season1?**
A: Every clue here ties back to Echo breadcrumbs from Wolves of Welton, Peril in Pinebrook, The Pale Sickness, Wild Sheep Chase, and Temple of the Dragonknights. Remind players of those moments!

### For Veterans

**Q: How does this mechanical hook challenge experienced players?**
A: The Echo contamination mechanic (psychic damage, Wis saves) challenges players who rely on mental resilience. Also, the moral dilemma of "help vs. stop the Echo" has no clear answer.

**Q: Where's the deadliest encounter?**
A: Scene 3 (station exploration) if players triggered multiple constructs. The confined underwater space limits tactical options.

**Q: What's the sharpest mood moment?**
A: Scene 4 — when the Echo manifests and players realize it's not a weapon, but a *person* trying to be reborn.

## 9. Running This Adventure: DM Tips

### Tone & Atmosphere

- **Scenes 1-2:** Tense, mysterious, the ocean turning against the living
- **Scene 3:** Claustrophobic, ancient, being watched by invisible eyes
- **Scene 4:** Reality-warping, terrifying, the Echo's true face revealed

Use descriptions of white water, blue sparks, and whispers on the wind. The Bleached Reach should feel like the ocean's grave — beautiful, but dead.

### Heavy Lifting NPCs

- **Coastal Scout Maris:** Critical in Scenes 1-2; prepare his weary, worried voice. He's seen things no human should see.
- **The Echo (Voice):** Critical in Scenes 3-4; prepare a layered, ancient, slightly distorted voice. It's not evil — it's *alien*.
- **Marshal Brenna Thorne:** In Scene 1, sets the stakes. She's been expecting this since Season1.

### When to Improvise

- **Scenes 1-2:** Follow the script — players need the setup
- **Scene 3:** Improvise freely! Let players explore the station creatively
- **Scene 4:** The Echo's manifestation should feel tailored to each player — mention their backstory

### Making This Memorable

The moment players see Salsvault through the observation windows — *watching Season1 events from the Aevorian perspective* — should be the adventure's emotional peak. Describe it vividly. The players realize: "We were being watched the entire time."

### Pacing Flexibility

- Can skip detailed travel (Scene 1) if behind schedule — jump to the coast
- Can combine Scenes 3-4 if running short — the station reveal happens during exploration
- If players are loving the investigation (Scene 3), let it breathe — this is the heart of the adventure
