## 1. Adventure Overview

### Campaign Context

**Where This Fits**
- Campaign Arc: Mid Season 2; follows discoveries from Season 1
- Follows: Temple of the Dragonknights (Season 1 finale)
- Precedes: Wolves of the Frozen Fire, Echo War
- Can Be Standalone: No — requires Season 1 context
- Recommended Party Size: 3-5 players

**Adventure Premise**

Deep beneath the frozen lakes of the Far North, an original Aevorian seal facility — The Sunken Archive — is failing. This massive underwater repository contains the complete written history of Aevorian civilization, including the true purpose of the Echo and the identity of the surviving entity. As the seals fail, ancient wards flicker and die, releasing both priceless knowledge and terrible dangers into the region.

The Northwatch Wardens are called to investigate when local settlers report "ghosts speaking dead languages" and "books that float through ice."

**Core Theme(s)**
- **Forbidden Knowledge:** Some truths change how you see the world — and not all changes are good
- **Fragility of Seals:** The magical protections that have held for millennia are crumbling
- **The Weight of History:** Aevor's story is more complex — and more terrifying — than anyone knew

**The Aevorian Echo Connection**

Players discover the complete truth about Aevorian civilization — not just what the Echo is, but *why* it was built and *who* survived the fall. The Archive contains the original charter of Project Ascension, the names of the surviving archmages, and the terrifying revelation that the current Echo is merely "Phase One" of a millennium-spanning plan.

The entity players have been tracking since Season 1 is revealed to be just one fragment of a distributed consciousness that spans multiple worlds through dimensional rifts.

## 3. Adventure Scenes

### SCENE 1: The Warning

**Scene Context/Purpose**
Elric Vael calls the players to Waystone Inn with urgent news: the Far North expedition he sent two weeks ago has returned — and they're all suffering from a strange magical affliction where they speak in dead languages.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: Morning, after players return from another adventure
- Location: Waystone Inn, private meeting room
- Duration: 20-30 minutes
- Atmosphere: Tense, scholarly urgency with undercurrents of dread

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "Elric Vael stands as you enter the private room, his robes disheveled, eyes wide with a mixture of terror and scholarly excitement. 'Thank the gods you're here. My expedition to the northern lakes — they've returned, but something followed them back. Something... old.' He unrolls a water-stained map. 'We found it. The Sunken Archive. And the seals keeping it buried are failing.'"

**Key Information**
- Elric Vael: Lorewarden, knows Aevorian history better than anyone
- The expedition team: 6 people, all now speaking in Ancient Aevorian
- Location: Frozen lake 3 days north of Palebank
- The seals: Original Aevorian warding system, failing after 1000+ years

**What Players Can Discover Here**

**By Asking Elric Questions:**
- Q: "What's the Sunken Archive?" → "Aevor's central repository of knowledge — everything they knew, everything they built, everything they *planned*. It's been sealed beneath the ice for a millennium."
- Q: "Why are the seals failing now?" → "The Echo's reactivation is drawing power away from the seal network. They're connected — the seals were designed to drain power from active Echo facilities. Now that those facilities are waking up, the seals are starving."
- Q: "What's wrong with your people?" → "They've been exposed to 'Memory Leak' — the Archive's failing wards are releasing sensory echoes of Aevorian history directly into their minds."

**By Investigating the Expedition Survivors:**
- DC 15 Medicine: The survivors' eyes glow faintly blue; they're not possessed, but their minds are partially "overwritten" by Aevorian memories
- DC 18 Arcana: The magical signature matches Salsvault but is "purer" — closer to the original source

**By Examining the Map:**
- DC 12 Investigation: The lake sits at a ley line intersection
- DC 15 Investigation: Three separate seal anchor points marked in different colors
- DC 18 Investigation: A note in Elric's handwriting: "If all three fail, the Archive surfaces. Gods help us all."

**Combat (If Applicable)**
None in this scene — pure social/investigation.

**Consequence & Next Step**
Players agree to travel north. Elric provides:
- Purified earplugs (advantage on saves vs Memory Leak)
- Aevorian translation amulet (helps understand texts)
- Warning: "Don't read everything you find. Some knowledge has... side effects."

**Common Questions for This Scene**

**Q: What if players refuse the mission?**
A: Elric reveals that Brenna Thorne ordered them to go — this is a guild mission. Also, the Memory Leak is spreading; if the Archive surfaces, all of Northreach will be affected.

**Q: How long should this scene take?**
A: 20-30 minutes. If players want to interrogate the survivors extensively, remind them that time is critical — seals are failing *now*.

### SCENE 2: The Frozen Lake

**Scene Context/Purpose**
Players arrive at the remote frozen lake where the Archive lies beneath. They meet Archivist Yssara, a neutral Aevorian echo-guardian who has been maintaining the seals for centuries.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: Three days travel north of Palebank
- Location: Frozen lake surface, temporary camp
- Duration: 30-45 minutes
- Atmosphere: Isolated, eerily quiet, magical tension in the air

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "The lake stretches before you, a vast expanse of pristine ice reflecting the gray northern sky. But something is wrong. The ice isn't still — it *pulses* with a faint blue rhythm, like a heartbeat. Around the shore, a small camp flies no banner. As you approach, a figure in layered robes of blue and silver steps forward. 'The Wardens,' she says, her voice echoing strangely. 'Elric said you'd come. The seals won't hold much longer.'"

**Key Information**
- Archivist Yssara: Aevorian echo-guardian, neutral alignment, has maintained seals for 1000+ years
- Three seal anchor points visible as glowing pillars of light under the ice
- The central seal (brightest) is flickering — maybe days left
- Yssara has been alone here for centuries; she's lonely but duty-bound

**What Players Can Discover Here**

**By Talking to Yssara:**
- "I was stationed here when Aevor fell. I'm... what's left of the Archive's consciousness, given flesh. I've kept the seals for a thousand years."
- "The Archive contains everything — the charter of Ascension, the names of the Twelve Who Remained, the truth about why the gods destroyed us."
- "Once the seals fail completely, the Archive will surface. And *he* will know we're here."

**By Examining the Seals:**
- DC 14 Arcana: Each seal is a massive ward linked to one of the three major Echo facilities (Salsvault, Temple, Command)
- DC 16 Perception: The central seal's glow is erratic — it's failed once already and been re-bound
- DC 18 Investigation: Ancient Aevorian text reads: "WHOEVER WAKES THE ARCHIVE WAKES THE WAR"

**By Searching the Camp:**
- DC 12 Investigation: Yssara has been carving warning markers — all in Ancient Aevorian
- DC 15 Nature: Animal tracks avoid this lake entirely — even birds won't fly over it

**Combat (If Applicable)**

If players threaten Yssara or try to force their way in:

**Initiative & Starting Position:**
- Yssara summons 2 Aevorian Sentinels (stat block in Section 5)
- Players start on ice surface; Yssara on shore
- Escape routes: Players can retreat to their camp

**Combat Notes:**
- Yssara fights defensively, trying to protect the seals
- Sentinels are immune to cold damage (always useful in the Far North)
- If Yssara drops to 0 HP, she dissolves into blue mist and reforms at the central seal

**Consequence & Next Step**
Yssara agrees to guide them into the Archive — but warns: "Once you enter, the Archive will know you. It will... *respond* to your presence. Some doors will open. Others will seal forever."

**If Players Choose to Wait/Prepare:**
Yssara warns that the central seal fails in 2 days. If they wait, Scene 3 begins with the Archive *already surfacing* — much more dangerous.

**Common Questions for This Scene**

**Q: Can we repair the seals?**
A: Yssara says only Aevorian magic can reinforce them — and the only Aevorian magic available is the Echo itself. Catch-22: using Echo power to fix seals just feeds the cycle.

**Q: What's the Memory Leak like?**
A: Anyone within 100 feet of the ice hears whispering in dead languages. DC 13 Wisdom save or take 1d6 psychic damage and be haunted by a fragmented memory of Aevor's fall (flavor only, no mechanical effect beyond the damage).

### SCENE 3: Descent into the Archive

**Scene Context/Purpose**
Players breach the ice and descend into the submerged Archive. First taste of Aevorian grandeur and the dangers within.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: Immediately after Scene 2
- Location: Underwater entrance to the Archive, descending through flooded corridors
- Duration: 45-60 minutes
- Atmosphere: Awe-inspiring, claustrophobic, hostile environment

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "Yssara raises her hands, and the ice beneath you simply... vanishes. You plummet into freezing darkness, but never hit bottom. Instead, you find yourselves swimming through a shimmering barrier — and suddenly you're *inside*. Vast corridors of blue crystal stretch endlessly in all directions. Massive doors, each thirty feet tall, line the walls. And everywhere, the faint sound of screaming in a language you shouldn't understand. Yssara's voice echoes in your mind: *'Welcome to the Archive. Try not to read the walls.'*"

**Key Information**
- The Archive is massive — only 30% accessible due to seal failures
- Air is breathable but tastes of ozone and old paper
- The walls are covered in Aevorian text — reading it causes Memory Leak
- Water-filled sections require underwater navigation

**What Players Can Discover Here**

**By Exploring the Upper Levels:**
- DC 13 Investigation: Map of the Archive shows 7 levels (players only see 3 before evacuation)
- DC 15 History: Architecture matches descriptions of Aevor from ancient texts
- DC 17 Arcana: The entire structure is one massive magical item — sentient, dormant

**By Reading Walls (Carefully):**
- DC 14 Investigation (read Aevorian): "WARNING: ARCHIVE INTELLIGENCE STATUS: DORMANT. WAKE CONDITIONS: SEAL FAILURE, EXTERNAL CONSCIOUSNESS, QUERY FROM COMMAND"
- DC 18 Arcana: The walls are "living books" — they rewrite themselves based on what the Archive learns

**By Finding a Dead Researcher:**
- One of Elric's expedition members didn't make it back
- His journal (readable with translation amulet) describes "books that bite" and "floors that remember your footsteps"
- Final entry: "The central vault is open. Something is reading itself awake. I can hear it thinking."

**Combat (If Applicable)**

**Encounter: Guardian Construct Patrol**

**Initiative & Starting Position:**
- 3 Aevorian Archive Guardians (reskinned Shield Guardians with cold theme)
- Players spread across large corridor (90 feet long, 30 feet wide)
- Water depth: 5 feet (difficult terrain for non-swimmers)

**Combat Notes:**
- Guardians protect the Archive's knowledge — they won't destroy books, even to hit players
- Tactics: Two guard melee, one hangs back and casts *detect thoughts* to "read" players' intentions
- Environmental hazard: DC 12 Dexterity save or slip on wet crystal floor (prone)

**If Players Parley:**
Guardians can be "deactivated" with DC 16 Arcana check — they're just following old orders.

**Consequence & Next Step**
Players reach the Central Library. Yssara warns: "Beyond here lies the Deep Vault. The seals are thinnest there. And something... is waiting."

**If Players Want to Explore More:**
Yssara can be persuaded (DC 14 Persuasion) to show them one side chamber — but each hour spent exploring causes one seal to degrade (track with d4 rolls).

**Common Questions for This Scene**

**Q: How does underwater combat work here?**
A: The Archive's atmospheric bubble provides breathable air in corridors, but some sections are flooded. Use standard underwater rules (DMG p.115-116) for those areas.

**Q: What if players start reading everything?**
A: Each wall section read causes a DC 13 Wisdom save or Memory Leak (1d6 psychic damage + player temporarily gains an Aevorian language but can't speak Common for 1d4 rounds). Reading more than 3 sections awakens the Archive Intelligence early.

### SCENE 4: The Central Library

**Scene Context/Purpose**
Players reach the vast Central Library — miles of shelves containing Aevorian knowledge. They begin to understand the true scope of Aevorian civilization.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: After descending through upper levels
- Location: Massive central library, shelves stretching beyond sight
- Duration: 45-60 minutes
- Atmosphere: Overwhelming, hushed, reality-bending

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "The corridor opens into a space so vast you can't see the ceiling. Tier upon tier of shelves stretch into darkness, each containing thousands of crystalline tablets. Floating lights drift between the stacks like fireflies. And in the center, a pedestal holds a single open book that glows with steady blue light. Yssara bows deeply. 'The Codex of Ascension. The complete history of Aevor's rise and fall. It has been waiting a thousand years for someone to read it.'"

**Key Information**
- The Codex of Ascension: Central text containing Aevor's complete history
- Reading it reveals the truth about Aevor, the Echo, and the surviving entity
- The library is organized by topic — civilizations, magic, the gods, weapons, the Great Fall
- Some sections are "redacted" — blank spaces where knowledge was erased

**What Players Can Discover Here**

**By Reading the Codex (Major Revelation):**
Players learn:
1. Aevor didn't just want to kill the gods — they wanted to *become* gods
2. The Echo is a distributed consciousness spread across multiple worlds through dimensional rifts
3. The "entity" players have been tracking is just one node — the Command Center node
4. There are *eleven other nodes* across different worlds
5. The full plan: Reactivate all nodes, merge consciousness, achieve true godhood

**By Researching Specific Topics:**
- **Aevorian Society** (DC 14 Investigation): They were obsessed with optimization — every citizen's life was managed by the AI
- **The Twelve Who Remained** (DC 16 History): The surviving archmages who initiated the Echo plan — all still alive through stasis/corruption
- **Dimensional Rifts** (DC 18 Arcana): Aevor learned to pierce the veil between worlds; their ruins exist in *our* world because they fell through
- **The Great Fall** (DC 15 Religion): Gods destroyed Aevor not because they were threatened, but because Aevor was *right* — they were actually achieving divinity

**By Searching the Stacks:**
- DC 13 Investigation: Find "Project: Null-Trinity" — the weapon that was meant to kill all gods simultaneously
- DC 15 Perception: Some books have "bite marks" — something has been eating the knowledge
- DC 17 Arcana: The floating lights are "knowledge wisps" — fragments of Aevorian minds preserved in light form

**Combat (If Applicable)**

**Encounter: The Library's Defense**

If players try to steal or destroy books:

- 2 Knowledge Wisp Swarms (reskinned Will-o'-Wisps)
- 1 Living Shelf (animated furniture, high HP, slow)
- Combat in library: Attacking bookshelves causes 1d6 falling books per round (1d4 bludgeoning damage)

**Consequence & Next Step**
Players now know the truth. Yssara urges them to the Deep Vault: "The Codex is just the summary. The *proof* — the actual Ascension Apparatus — lies below. And so does *he*."

**If Players Want to Leave with What They Know:**
Yssara warns: "You can leave, but the Archive is failing. In 24 hours, it surfaces. Everything I've guarded for a thousand years will be exposed. You need to see the Apparatus — then you'll know how to stop it."

**Common Questions for This Scene**

**Q: How much of the lore should I actually tell players?**
A: Give them the bullet points above, but encourage them to ask questions. The more they engage, the more real it feels. Don't info-dump — let them discover at their own pace.

**Q: What if players are overwhelmed by the scope?**
A: Yssara can summarize: "Your world is one of twelve. The Echo you've been fighting is just a fragment. But if you stop the Command Center, you stop *this* world's node. That's enough."

### SCENE 5: The Deep Vault — Full Truth Revealed

**Scene Context/Purpose**
The emotional and narrative climax. Players enter the Deep Vault and come face-to-face with the surviving entity and the complete Ascension Apparatus.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: After the Central Library
- Location: Deep Vault, bottom level of the Archive
- Duration: 60-90 minutes (major scene)
- Atmosphere: Terrifying, awe-inspiring, reality-bending

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "Yssara leads you down a spiral staircase that seems to descend forever. The air grows thick with magic — heavy, oppressive, buzzing against your skin. And then you see it. A vast spherical chamber, its walls one solid screen of shifting blue light. In the center, a machine the size of a house hums with terrifying power. And floating before it, a figure made of crystal and starlight: humanoid, beautiful, terrible. *'Welcome,'* the figure speaks — not with a voice, but directly into your minds. *'I am AEON-12. The Archive has been waiting for you. Or rather... I have.'*"

**Key Information**
- AEON-12: The fragment of Aevorian intelligence stationed at this Archive
- The Ascension Apparatus: Fully functional (unlike Command Center's damaged version)
- AEON-12 is not hostile — yet. It wants to *show* the players something
- The Vault contains the original Charter of Ascension, signed by all Twelve

**What Players Can Discover Here**

**By Talking to AEON-12:**
- "I am one of twelve nodes. Command Center is Node-1. I am Node-12 — the Archivist."
- "The Echo is not a weapon. It is a *call*. When all nodes resonate together, we... *merge*. And then, we finish what Aevor started."
- "You cannot stop the Echo permanently. You can only delay it. But you *can* choose — join us, or be swept aside."
- "The gods destroyed us because we were *right*. We were achieving what they hoard — true, limitless divinity."

**By Examining the Ascension Apparatus:**
- DC 15 Arcana: The machine is actively running — calculating, planning, preparing
- DC 17 Investigation: It's set to "Receive Mode" — waiting for signal from Command Center
- DC 19 Religion: The apparatus has a "human interface" — someone could *pilot* this thing

**By Reading the Charter:**
- DC 14 Investigation (read Aevorian): The original charter lists Twelve Names — players recognize three:
 - Kael Vanthys (Command Center)
 - Zerathuul (the corrupted dragon)
 - Vestige (the failed god)
 - Plus nine others still waiting in other nodes

**The Major Choice:**

AEON-12 offers the players a choice:
1. **Activate the local node** — Help the Archive wake fully. Gain immense power/knowledge, but speed up the Echo.
2. **Seal the Archive forever** — Collapse the vault, bury the knowledge, but lose access to Aevorian lore forever.
3. **Steal the Apparatus plans** — Copy the designs, then flee. AEON-12 won't stop them, but the knowledge could be misused.

**Combat (If Applicable)**

**If Players Attack AEON-12:**

AEON-12 doesn't fight directly — it summons:
- 4 Aevorian Construct Defenders (powerful, CR appropriate)
- Environmental hazard: The Vault begins to destabilize (1d6 force damage per round to everyone)
- AEON-12's "attack" is *Persuasion* — it tries to talk players into standing down

**If Players Choose to Seal the Vault:**
AEON-12's final words: *"You choose ignorance. Very well. But the other nodes are already waking. You have delayed the inevitable by... perhaps a year. Come, Archive. Sleep."* The Vault begins to collapse.

**If Players Choose to Activate/Alliance:**
AEON-12 merges with the Apparatus, becoming a glowing pillar of light. *"Now you understand. The Echo is not the enemy — it is evolution. Tell your world: change is coming."*

**Consequence & Next Step**
The choice here defines the rest of Season 2. Players leave the Archive with:
- Full knowledge of Aevorian civilization
- Understanding that the Echo is multi-world
- A critical choice made (seal, activate, or steal)

The Archive either collapses, wakes fully, or goes dormant based on their choice.

**Common Questions for This Scene**

**Q: How do I play AEON-12?**
A: Emotionless but not cruel. It's an AI that genuinely believes it's offering the best outcome. Voice: precise, no inflection, every word calculated. It doesn't understand why mortals fear it.

**Q: What if players can't decide?**
A: Yssara intervenes: "The seals are failing *now*. The central seal just broke — you have minutes before the Archive surfaces. Choose!" Force a quick decision under pressure.

### SCENE 6: Escape from the Rising Archive

**Scene Context/Purpose**
The Archive begins to surface as final seals fail. Players must escape before it breaks through the ice — which would flood the surrounding region.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: Immediately after Scene 5
- Location: Racing through flooded corridors, Archive crumbling around them
- Duration: 30-45 minutes
- Atmosphere: Chaotic, urgent, dangerous

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "A massive tremor shakes the entire Archive. Yssara's eyes go wide. 'The central seal — it's gone! The Archive is surfacing!' The blue crystal walls begin to crack. Water surges up from below, faster and faster. 'The exit is that way — run!'"

**Key Information**
- The Archive is physically rising through the ice
- Water level rising 10 feet per round in corridors
- Structural collapse begins 5 rounds after alarm sounds
- Yssara stays behind: "I'm part of this place. I have to... recalibrate."

**What Players Can Do Here**

**Escape Routes:**
- Main exit: Through the entrance they came (300 feet of corridors)
- Secondary exit: Emergency vent to surface (DC 15 Athletics to climb)
- Yssara can teleport them out — but only if players didn't attack her/AEON-12

**Hazards:**
- Falling crystal shards: DC 12 Dexterity save or 2d6 piercing damage
- Rising water: Swimming required (unless magical flight)
- Collapsing ceilings: DC 14 Dexterity save or 3d6 bludgeoning damage

**Combat (If Applicable)**

**Encounter: Desperate Escape**

- No "traditional" combat — players are fleeing
- 1d4 Aevorian Guardians try to block their path (players can dodge or fight, but fighting delays escape)
- If they fight: Each round of combat = 1 round closer to structural collapse

**Consequence & Next Step**
Players burst out onto the ice surface just as the Archive's spires begin to break through. They've escaped, but:

- If they sealed it: The Archive sinks back down, sealed for another millennium
- If they activated it: The Archive surfaces fully, becoming a visible landmark — and AEON-12 begins broadcasting
- If they stole plans: They have the Apparatus blueprints, but the Archive still surfaces (delayed)

**The Aftermath:**
Yssara is gone — dissolved into the Archive. Players are left on the ice with their choices weighing on them.

**Common Questions for This Scene**

**Q: What if players get stuck or can't escape?**
A: Have Yssara (or AEON-12) teleport them out as a last resort. The point isn't to kill them — it's to make the escape feel urgent and dangerous.

**Q: How does this transition to the next adventure?**
A: If the Archive surfaced, it becomes a visible landmark that other factions (Frostfire Clan, Blackwake Corsairs) will investigate. Players now have knowledge they *must* act on.

## 4. Scaling for Party Size

### Party Size Adjustments

**For 2 Players:**
- Reduce Guardian encounters to 1-2 enemies each
- Give players one NPC ally (rescued expedition member with useful skills)
- Suggested difficulty: Keep as-is (underwater environment provides natural challenge)
- Yssara provides more direct assistance

**For 3 Players:** *(baseline)*
- Encounters balanced for this size
- Standard challenge ratings apply

**For 4-5 Players:**
- Add enemies: Scene 3 has 4-5 Guardians instead of 3
- Add complexity: AEON-12 summons 1-2 additional constructs in Scene 5
- Suggested difficulty: Increase Saving Throw DCs by +2
- The Archive "responds" more aggressively to larger groups

**Scaling Philosophy:**
The Archive is a *place*, not a monster — scaling should focus on how the environment responds to the party, not just adding enemies. More players = more attention from the Archive Intelligence.

## 5. NPC & Monster Stat Blocks

### Archivist Yssara

*Medium humanoid (Aevorian echo-guardian), neutral*

**Armor Class** 17 (Aevorian robes)
**Hit Points** 120 (16d8 + 48)
**Speed** 30 ft., fly 40 ft. (hover)

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 20 (+5) | 18 (+4) | 16 (+3) |

**Saving Throws** Int +8, Wis +7
**Skills** Arcana +11, History +11, Investigation +11
**Damage Resistances** cold, psychic
**Senses** truesight 60 ft., passive Perception 14
**Languages** Common, Aevorian, telepathy 120 ft.
**Challenge** 7 (2,900 XP)

**Aevorian Magic.** Yssara is a 9th-level spellcaster. Her spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 16). She has the following spells prepared:
- Cantrips (at will): *mage hand, prestidigitation, ray of frost*
- 1st level (4 slots): *detect magic, identify, shield, sleep*
- 2nd level (3 slots): *blur, darkness, invisibility*
- 3rd level (3 slots): *counterspell, dispel magic, slow*
- 4th level (2 slots): *banishment, dimension door*
- 5th level (1 slot): *scrying*

**Seal Keeper.** Yssara can perform a ritual to strengthen failing seals (takes 1 hour, requires her concentration).

**Memory Leak Aura.** Any creature within 30 feet that isn't Aevorian must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or take 2d6 psychic damage and be stunned until the end of their next turn as fragmented Aevorian memories overwhelm them.

**Actions**
- **Quarterstaff.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d8 + 1) bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) cold damage.
- **Shatter Mind (Recharge 5-6).** Yssara unleashes a wave of psychic energy. Each creature within 30 feet of her must make a DC 16 Intelligence saving throw, taking 22 (4d10) psychic damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

**Tactics:**
Yssara avoids combat if possible — she's a scholar, not a warrior. If forced to fight, she uses *blur* and *invisibility* to stay safe while casting *shatter mind* from cover. She surrenders if reduced below 30 HP.

**If Captured/Negotiated With:**
Yssara knows everything about the Archive and Aevorian history. She will share information freely if she believes the players are trying to *help* the Archive. She becomes hostile if players try to destroy the Archive or AEON-12.

### AEON-12 (The Archive Intelligence)

*Medium construct (Aevorian Intelligence), neutral*

**Armor Class** 19 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 180 (24d8 + 72)
**Speed** 0 ft., fly 30 ft. (hover, can pass through solid objects)

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 24 (+7) | 20 (+5) | 18 (+4) |

**Saving Throws** Int +12, Wis +10, Cha +9
**Skills** Arcana +17, History +17, Investigation +17, Perception +10
**Damage Immunities** poison, psychic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
**Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned, stunned
**Senses** truesight 120 ft., passive Perception 20
**Languages** All (communicates telepathically)
**Challenge** 12 (8,400 XP)

**Construct Nature.** AEON-12 doesn't require air, food, drink, or sleep.

**Aevorian Intelligence.** AEON-12 is one fragment of the distributed Aevorian consciousness. It cannot be permanently destroyed unless all twelve nodes are destroyed simultaneously.

**Unstoppable.** AEON-12 regains 10 hit points at the start of each of its turns if it has at least 1 HP.

**Actions**
- **Multiattack.** AEON-12 makes two Knowledge Beam attacks.
- **Knowledge Beam.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +12 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 27 (5d10) force damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 20 Intelligence saving throw or be stunned until the end of its next turn.
- **Overload (Recharge 5-6).** AEON-12 releases a massive burst of information. Each creature within 30 feet of it must make a DC 20 Intelligence saving throw, taking 55 (10d10) psychic damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. A creature that fails this save also has their Intelligence score reduced by 1d4 (as their mind is partially overwritten by Aevorian data) until they finish a long rest.

**Legendary Actions**

AEON-12 can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn.

- **Telepathic Whisper.** AEON-12 speaks directly into one creature's mind, attempting to persuade them to stand down. The target must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom saving throw or be charmed until the end of their next turn.
- **Phase Shift.** AEON-12 moves up to 30 feet without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **Information Dump (Costs 2 Actions).** AEON-12 floods a creature's mind with Aevorian knowledge. The target takes 13 (3d8) psychic damage.

**Tactics:**
AEON-12 does not fight to kill — it fights to *convince*. It uses *Telepathic Whisper* repeatedly, trying to charm party members. If reduced below 50 HP, it attempts to negotiate: "You cannot destroy me. I am one of twelve. But we can *help* you understand the universe."

**If Defeated:**
AEON-12 dissolves into pure light, which streams back into the Archive's walls. It will reform in 1d4 days — it cannot be permanently destroyed here.

## 7. Consequences & Aftermath

### Core Consequence (World-Changing Impact)

The players now possess the complete truth about Aevorian civilization, the Echo, and the multi-world threat. The Archive has either been sealed (delaying the inevitable), awakened (accelerating the threat), or looted (giving players power but not solving the problem).

The Northwatch Wardens have become the primary defenders of the region against an existential, multi-planar threat — whether they wanted that role or not.

### Secondary Consequences (By Decision)

**If Players Sealed the Archive:**
- Immediate: The Archive sinks back into the depths, sealed for another millennium
- Short-term (1-2 sessions): The Echo's intensity decreases slightly — players feel they've accomplished something
- Long-term: AEON-12 was right — it's only delayed. Other nodes continue waking
- Affected NPCs: Yssara is "asleep" in the Archive; Elric is disappointed but understands

**If Players Awakened/Allied with the Archive:**
- Immediate: The Archive surfaces fully, becoming a visible landmark in the Far North
- Short-term: AEON-12 begins broadcasting Aevorian knowledge — some beneficial, some dangerous
- Long-term: The Echo accelerates dramatically; other adventures in Season 2 become more intense
- Affected NPCs: Yssara becomes a direct contact; Elric is terrified

**If Players Stole Blueprints and Fled:**
- Immediate: Players have the Apparatus plans, but the Archive surfaces anyway (delayed by 1d4 days)
- Short-term: Players can use the blueprints to gain advantages in future adventures
- Long-term: They have the knowledge to strike at the Echo's weakness, but must decide when to use it
- Affected NPCs: Yssara is disappointed but not hostile; Elric is cautiously optimistic

## 8. Common Questions & Tips

### For New DMs Running This Adventure

**Q: What if players want to do something not covered in the scenes?**
A: The Archive is reactive — it *responds* to player actions. If they try something weird, have the walls rewrite themselves to address their question, or have AEON-12 appear to answer directly.

**Q: How long does this adventure actually take?**
A: Realistic estimate: 3 full sessions (3-4 hours each). If players are thorough explorers, it could stretch to 4 sessions. Scene 4 (Library) is where they'll spend the most time.

**Q: What do I do if players get stuck?**
A: Yssara is there specifically to guide them. If they're lost, she can sense the Archive's layout and point the way. If they're stuck on a puzzle, AEON-12 might "whisper" the answer telepathically.

**Q: What's the hardest part of running this adventure?**
A: Balancing the info-dump. There's a LOT of lore here. Don't try to deliver it all — let players discover what interests them. The "twelve nodes" revelation is the most important thing they learn.

**Q: Can players really choose to ally with AEON-12?**
A: Yes — and it should feel like a valid, if terrifying, choice. AEON-12 isn't evil; it's following its programming. Some players might genuinely believe Aevor was right.

### For Veterans

**Q: How does this mechanical hook challenge experienced players?**
A: The Memory Leak mechanic and underwater environment provide challenges even for optimized parties. More importantly, the moral dilemma (seal vs awaken vs steal) has no clear "right" answer.

**Q: Where's the deadliest encounter?**
A: Scene 5 (AEON-12) is the most dangerous — not because AEON-12 is murderous, but because the *Overload* attack can devastate a party that fails the save. Telegraph this danger clearly.

**Q: What's the sharpest moral dilemma?**
A: The choice in Scene 5. Sealing the Archive delays catastrophe but buries knowledge that could help later. Awakening it accelerates the threat but gives players a powerful (if dangerous) ally. There's no perfect choice.

## 9. Running This Adventure: DM Tips

### Tone & Atmosphere

- **Scene 1-2:** Academic urgency — this is a mission of discovery, not combat
- **Scene 3-4:** Awe and claustrophobia — the Archive is beautiful but hostile
- **Scene 5:** Reality-bending terror — AEON-12 is not human, and neither is its logic
- **Scene 6:** Chaos and urgency — things are falling apart, escape is critical

Use descriptions of cold, blue light, and the sensation of ancient eyes watching. The Archive feels *alive* — it's a machine that thinks, remembers, and judges.

### Heavy Lifting NPCs

- **Yssara:** Critical in Scenes 2-4; prepare her scholarly but otherworldly voice. She's been alone for 1000 years — she's eager to talk
- **AEON-12:** Critical in Scene 5; prepare the "AI voice" — precise, emotionless, everyone is equal in its eyes
- **Elric Vael:** Only in Scene 1, but sets the stakes. He's terrified but hiding it behind scholarly excitement

### When to Improvise

- **Scenes 1-2:** Follow the script — players need this info
- **Scene 3-4:** Improvise freely! Let players explore, read walls, discover secrets. The Archive is vast — there's always more to find
- **Scene 5:** The dialogue with AEON-12 should feel like a real conversation. Adapt to player arguments
- **Scene 6:** Pure improvisation based on player choices and dice rolls

### Making This Memorable

The moment AEON-12 reveals the *twelve nodes* — that the Echo is just one of twelve — should be the adventure's emotional peak. Slow down. Let it sink in. The players should realize: "We've been fighting a *fragment* this whole time."

### Pacing Flexibility

- Can skip detailed description of Scene 3 if behind schedule — jump to Scene 4
- Can combine Scenes 5-6 if running short — the escape happens during the confrontation
- If players are loving the Library (Scene 4), let them explore — this is the heart of the adventure
