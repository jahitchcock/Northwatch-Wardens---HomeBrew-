## 1. Adventure Overview

### Campaign Context

**Where This Fits**
- Campaign Arc: Early-Mid Season 2; follows The Sunken Archive revelations
- Follows: The Sunken Archive
- Precedes: Echo War
- Can Be Standalone: No — requires understanding of Frostfire Clan origins
- Recommended Party Size: 3-5 players

**Adventure Premise**

The Frostfire Clan — a breakaway faction of Dragonknights who vanished during the Great Fall — emerges from the northern wastes with a terrifying new power: "frostfire," a cold-burning blue flame tied to the Echo's draconic weapons research. They begin raiding settlements, demanding "the return of what was stolen" (referencing the Temple of the Dragonknights, Season 1).

The Northwatch Wardens are caught between defending settlements and uncovering the truth: the Frostfire Clan isn't just raiding — they're gathering components for a weapon that could destroy the Echo's enemies... including the Wardens themselves.

**Core Theme(s)**
- **Faction vs. Frontier:** The Wardens must protect civilians while navigating complex faction politics
- **Echo mutations:** The Frostfire Clan's power comes from Echo draconic research — they're both victims and villains
- **Season 1 Callbacks:** The Temple of the Dragonknights wasn't destroyed — its former members survived and evolved

**The Aevorian Echo Connection**

Players discover that the Frostfire Clan's "frostfire" power is a direct result of the Temple of the Dragonknights' draconic weapon experiments (from Season 1). The Clan was subjected to Aevorian experimentation centuries ago, and the Echo's reactivation has *awakened* their latent draconic modifications.

Warlord Varyx Frostfire believes the Echo is "the old gods returning" — and he intends to control it, not be destroyed by it.

## 3. Adventure Scenes

### SCENE 1: The Raid on Pinebrook

**Scene Context/Purpose**
Frostfire Clan launches their first major raid on a Warden-protected settlement. Players must defend civilians and gather intelligence.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: Night, during a blizzard
- Location: Pinebrook (from Season 1's "Peril in Pinebrook")
- Duration: 45-60 minutes
- Atmosphere: Chaos, snow, blue flames dancing in the dark

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "The warning bells of Pinebrook shatter the night air. You rush from the inn to see buildings burning — but the flames are wrong. They're blue, dancing with cold light that casts no warmth. Shadows move through the snow — tall figures wreathed in frostfire, moving with impossible grace. A voice booms over the chaos: *'The Dragonknights' betrayal ends tonight! Return what you stole, or burn in the old fire!'*"

**Key Information**
- Frostfire warriors: 8-10 raiders, use frostfire weapons
- They're targeting the town hall — specifically the basement (where Season 1 storage was)
- Civilians panicking: 30-40 people in danger
- The raid is coordinated, not a mindless attack

**What Players Can Discover Here**

**By Fighting Frostfire Warriors:**
- DC 12 Investigation: Their armor bears the symbol of the Dragonknights — but corrupted, with blue flames
- DC 14 Medicine: Wounded raiders have draconic features (scales on neck, slit pupils)
- DC 16 Arcana: The frostfire is Aevorian in origin — it feels like "Echo magic"

**By Searching the Town Hall After:**
- DC 13 Investigation: They were looking for "The Dragon Scrolls" (Season 1 item)
- DC 15 Perception: Raiders left behind a manifesto — "The Frostfire Manifesto"
- DC 18 Investigation: A hidden compartment was forced open; something was taken

**By Talking to Survivors:**
- "They didn't kill anyone — they just burned buildings and searched"
- "Their leader had dragon wings... but made of ice and fire"
- "They shouted something about 'the Temple' and 'stolen birthright'"

**Combat (If Applicable)**

**Encounter: Frostfire Raid Party**

**Initiative & Starting Position:**
- 6 Frostfire Warriors (reskinned Veterans with cold fire flavor)
- 1 Frostfire Shaman (reskinned Cult Fanatic with ice/fire spells)
- Players start in town center; raiders spread across 200-foot area
- Civilians: 30 NPCs need protection (optional objective)

**Combat Notes:**
- Frostfire warriors deal 1d6 cold damage + 1d6 fire damage (blue flames that freeze)
- Shaman casts *darkness* and *hold person* to control the battlefield
- If reduced below 50% HP, raiders retreat — they've found what they came for
- Environmental hazard: Blizzard reduces visibility (DC 12 Perception to see beyond 60 feet)

**Consequence & Next Step**
Raiders escape with "The Dragon Scrolls" (retrieved from Pinebrook's basement). Players find the Frostfire Manifesto, which reveals:
- The Frostfire Clan was once Dragonknights
- They were subjected to Aevorian experiments at the Temple site
- They want their "birthright" — the draconic power Aevor promised them
- Their stronghold: "The Ember Crag" in the Far North

**If Players Chase:**
The raiders have a head start and mounted frost-wyverns. Players can track them (DC 14 Survival) but can't catch them in time.

**Common Questions for This Scene**

**Q: What if players focus on saving civilians instead of fighting?**
A: That's valid! Reduce combat to 3 warriors (distraction force) and have the rest escape with the scrolls. Players succeed at protection but "fail" at stopping the theft.

**Q: How does frostfire work mechanically?**
A: It deals cold + fire damage simultaneously. Resistance to one doesn't apply to the other. It also glows blue, providing bright light in a 20-foot radius.

### SCENE 2: The Frostfire Manifesto

**Scene Context/Purpose**
Players investigate the Manifesto and learn about the Frostfire Clan's origins and demands.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: The morning after the raid
- Location: Waystone Inn, private meeting room
- Duration: 30-45 minutes
- Atmosphere: Tense, analytical, the weight of history

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "Brenna Thorne unrolls a blood-stained parchment — the Frostfire Manifesto, left behind in the raid. Her face is pale. 'I hoped this day wouldn't come. The Frostfire Clan... they were the Dragonknights who refused to surrender after the Great Fall. Aevor changed them. And now the Echo is waking them up.' She looks at you, grim. 'We need to understand what they've become. Before more towns burn.'"

**Key Information**
- Marshal Brenna Thorne: Knows about Frostfire Clan, hoped they were gone forever
- The Manifesto: Written by Warlord Varyx, demands "return of the Temple and all Aevorian artifacts"
- Frostfire Clan: ~200 members, fortified at Ember Crag
- They believe the Wardens "stole" the Temple site (from Season 1)

**What Players Can Discover Here**

**By Reading the Manifesto (DC 14 Intelligence/Aevorian):**
- "We were the first. The Temple was ours before the Aevorians came."
- "They made us into weapons — draconic flesh bonded to cold fire."
- "The Echo wakes. The old power returns. Return what you stole, or we take it."
- "Varyx Frostfire, Last of the True Dragonknights"

**By Talking to Brenna:**
- "I've known about them for years. They've been watching from the north."
- "The Temple of the Dragonknights mission in Season 1... that was just the surface. The Clan *lived* there after the Great Fall."
- "Varyx was a test subject — Aevor wanted to create dragon-hybrid soldiers. The Echo is finishing what Aevor started."
- "If you go north, take this" — she provides *Ring of Resistance (Cold)* for one player

**By Researching Dragonknight History (DC 16 History):**
- The Dragonknights were founded to fight Aevor's draconic weapons
- During the Great Fall, some were captured by falling Aevorian facilities
- They were subjected to "Project: Frostfire" — draconic enhancement experiments
- They survived for centuries in the Far North, changed by the experiments

**If Players Ask Brenna: "Why didn't you tell us?"**
She responds: "Because I hoped they'd stay buried. Some secrets... some truths are better left in the ice. But the Echo is waking *everything* up."

**Consequence & Next Step**
Players decide to travel north to Ember Crag. Brenna provides:
- A map to the Frostfire Stronghold (3 days travel through tundra)
- A warning: "Varyx isn't evil — he's been twisted by centuries of Aevorian modification. Don't judge him too quickly."
- A contact: "If you reach the Ember Crag and need help, look for Kaelith. She... she was one of us once."

**If Players Want to Wait/Prepare:**
Brenna warns: "Every day you wait, they grow stronger. The Echo is feeding their power. In a week, they'll be unstoppable."

**Common Questions for This Scene**

**Q: Can players negotiate with Frostfire Clan without going north?**
A: Messengers have been sent (Manifesto is their opening move). Varyx won't negotiate remotely — he wants to look Warden representatives in the eye.

**Q: What if players want to attack immediately with an army?**
A: Brenna shuts this down: "We don't have the numbers. The Wardens are 40 strong; they're 200. And they're *enhanced*. This needs to be surgical, not a war."

### SCENE 3: The Tundra Approach

**Scene Context/Purpose**
Players travel through the northern tundra, tracking the Frostfire raiders and learning about the land.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: 3 days of travel north
- Location: Frozen tundra, approaching the Far North
- Duration: 30-45 minutes (can be compressed if players are eager)
- Atmosphere: Isolation, bitter cold, signs of Frostfire presence everywhere

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "Three days of trudging through chest-deep snow has brought you to the edge of the known world. The air itself feels sharp here — each breath cuts your lungs. But you're not alone. Blue frostfire markers line the path, like breadcrumbs left by a guide. Or warnings. Ahead, a massive spire of black ice rises from the tundra — the Ember Crag. And something is watching you from the snow."

**Key Information**
- Travel: 3 days through harsh tundra (DC 12 Con save each day or 1 level of exhaustion)
- Frostfire markers: Guide players to the stronghold (or track them)
- The Ember Crag: Visible for the last day of travel — massive, imposing
- "Something watching": Echo-Weavers (scouts) are tracking the players

**What Players Can Discover Here**

**By Tracking the Raiders (DC 14 Survival):**
- They're mounted on "frost-wyverns" — ice-blue drakes
- They're carrying the Dragon Scrolls (stolen from Pinebrook)
- They move fast — players can't catch them on foot

**By Investigating Frostfire Markers:**
- DC 13 Arcana: Each marker is an Aevorian waypoint — part of the facility network
- DC 15 Investigation: They're counting down miles to the Ember Crag
- DC 17 Perception: Some markers have "messages" frozen inside — warnings to outsiders

**By Spotting the Watchers:**
- DC 12 Perception: 2 Echo-Weavers (reskinned scouts) are observing from a ridge
- If spotted, they don't attack — they flee toward the Ember Crag
- If caught/parleyed with: "The Warlord expects you. Come to the Crag. He will... *speak* to you."

**Combat (If Applicable)**

**Encounter: Frost-Wyvern Ambush**

If players are reckless or the Echo-Weavers alert the mounted patrol:

**Initiative & Starting Position:**
- 2 Frost-Wyverns (reskinned Wyverns with cold/fire breath)
- 2 Frostfire Riders (reskinned Veterans)
- Players traveling in open tundra (difficult terrain, no cover)

**Combat Notes:**
- Wyverns breathe "frostfire" — 15-foot cone, 3d6 cold + 3d6 fire damage (half on save)
- Riders dismount to fight, use hit-and-run tactics
- If reduced below 50% HP, they retreat toward Ember Crag
- Environmental: DC 10 Dex save or slip on ice (prone)

**Consequence & Next Step**
Players reach the base of the Ember Crag. The Echo-Weavers have alerted the clan — they're expected.

A figure approaches: Kaelith, the Echo-Weaver (heavily modified Dragonknight, wears tattered robes that shift between ice and fire).

"You've come. Varyx is... eager to meet you. But be warned — the Echo is singing here. Loudly. Can you hear it?"

**If Players Want to Attack Immediately:**
Kaelith holds up a hand: "We don't fight guests. Yet. Enter the Crag, or leave the tundra. Choose."

**Common Questions for This Scene**

**Q: How dangerous is the tundra travel?**
A: Not very — it's flavor. Only roll saves if players are unprepared (no cold weather gear). The real danger is the Frost-Wyvern patrol.

**Q: Who is Kaelith?**
A: She was a Dragonknight scout who was captured with Varyx. Now she's an "Echo-Weaver" — her mind is partially linked to the Echo network. She's the clan's voice and spy.

### SCENE 4: The Ember Crag — Warlord Varyx

**Scene Context/Purpose**
Players enter the Frostfire stronghold and confront Warlord Varyx Frostfire. Major social encounter with possible combat.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: Upon reaching the Ember Crag
- Location: Great Hall of the Ember Crag (massive cavern carved from black ice)
- Duration: 60-90 minutes (major scene)
- Atmosphere: Oppressive, ancient, the weight of centuries of bitterness

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "The Ember Crag is a fortress of black ice, carved from a massive glacier. Inside, the cavern ceiling vanishes into darkness, lit only by veins of frostfire running through the walls. And there, on a throne of dragon bone and Aevorian metal, sits Warlord Varyx Frostfire. He's... not what you expected. Yes, he has draconic features — scales, wings, glowing eyes. But he looks tired. Ancient. He gestures, and the frostfire dims. *'Dragonknights. Wardens. The names change, but the betrayal remains the same. You stole our Temple. You stole our birthright. Now... you'll watch us finish what Aevor started.'*"

**Key Information**
- Warlord Varyx Frostfire: Ancient draconic-human hybrid, former Dragonknight
- He's been alive since the Great Fall (1000+ years)
- The "birthright": Aevorian draconic enhancement — he's more dragon than human now
- The Temple of the Dragonknights: Frostfire Clan lived there after the Great Fall, until Season 1
- He wants the Temple *and* all Aevorian artifacts the Wardens have found

**What Players Can Discover Here**

**By Talking to Varyx:**
- "We were the first test subjects. Aevor wanted dragon-hybrids to fight their draconic weapons."
- "The Temple... it was our home after the Great Fall. We guarded it. Kept the weapons sealed."
- "Then you came. The Wardens. You 'investigated' our home, broke our seals, woke the Echo."
- "Now the Echo is *finishing* us. Making us into what Aevor always wanted. And you want to stop it?"

**By Observing the Clan (DC 14 Perception):**
- ~200 clan members, all show signs of draconic modification (scales, wings, glowing eyes)
- They're not "evil" — they're a community, living in the ice, trying to survive
- Children: Young frostfire hybrids, born with scales and wings
- The Echo is affecting them MORE than anyone — they're "antennas" for Echo power

**By Exploring the Great Hall (DC 16 Investigation):**
- Aevorian technology embedded in the walls — this isn't just a cave, it's a *facility*
- Murals show the history: Dragonknights → Capture → Experiments → Frostfire Clan
- The "Frostfire Weapon": A massive device in the center of the hall, currently dormant

**The Major Choice:**

Varyx offers the players a deal:

1. **Join him:** Help him activate the Frostfire Weapon. He'll share the power — players get draconic enhancements.
2. **Leave in peace:** He won't raid again if the Wardens give him the Temple site and all Aevorian artifacts.
3. **Fight:** He'll defend the Ember Crag to the death. But his people... his *children*... they'll suffer.

**If Players Mention Brenna Thorne:**
Varyx laughs bitterly: "Brenna... yes, she knows. She's known for years. She's a *coward* — knew we were here and did nothing. Just like the Dragonknights."

**Combat (If Applicable)**

**If Players Attack Varyx:**

**Initiative & Starting Position:**
- Warlord Varyx (reskinned Young Red Dragon with cold/fire attacks)
- 4 Frostfire Guards (reskinned Veterans)
- 2 Echo-Weavers (caster support)
- Massive cavern, 100x100 feet, pillars of black ice provide cover
- The Frostfire Weapon is in the center — Varyx will activate it if reduced below 100 HP

**Combat Notes:**
- Varyx's breath weapon: "Frostfire" — 30-foot cone, 8d6 cold + 8d6 fire (half on save)
- Guards fight to protect Varyx and the Weapon — they're fanatically loyal
- Echo-Weavers cast *wall of fire* and *darkness* to disrupt players
- Environmental: Frostfire veins deal 1d6 fire + 1d6 cold damage to anyone adjacent

**If Players Negotiate Successfully:**
Varyx stands down — but only if they agree to *one* of his demands. He's not unreasonable, just bitter and desperate.

**Consequence & Next Step**
Players' choice determines the next scene:

- **If they join/negotiate:** Move to Scene 5a (Witnessing the Weapon)
- **If they fight and win:** Move to Scene 5b (Sabotaging the Weapon)
- **If they fight and lose / surrender:** Move to Scene 5c (Captured — the Weapon activates)

**Common Questions for This Scene**

**Q: How should I play Varyx?**
A: He's not a mustache-twirling villain. He's a 1000-year-old victim of Aevorian experiments who's trying to protect his people. Voice: gravelly, tired, occasional flashes of ancient draconic pride. He's *bitter*, not evil.

**Q: What if players want to "cure" the Frostfire Clan?**
A: Varyx responds: "Cure? This isn't a *disease*. This is what we *are*. The Echo didn't break us — it *completed* us. Or haven't you read the Archive?" (Reference to .)

### SCENE 5a: The Frostfire Weapon (Players Negotiated/Joined)

**Scene Context/Purpose**
Varyx shows the players the Frostfire Weapon — an Aevorian draconic enhancement device. Players witness its activation.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: After negotiation succeeds
- Location: The Frostfire Weapon chamber, center of Ember Crag
- Duration: 45-60 minutes
- Atmosphere: Terrifying, awe-inspiring, power leaking into reality

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "Varyx leads you to the center of the hall, to the massive device you've been eyeing all along. It's a ring of Aevorian metal, thirty feet across, etched with draconic script. Frostfire dances between the runes. 'The Draconic Amplifier,' Varyx says, reverence in his voice. 'Aevor built it to enhance dragon-hybrids. To create the ultimate soldiers.' He places his hand on the device. 'And now... the Echo wakes it up.' The ring *roars* to life, blue flames erupting upward. Varyx's form *changes* — growing larger, more draconic, more *terrible*."

**Key Information**
- The Frostfire Weapon: Aevorian "Draconic Amplifier" — enhances draconic power
- Currently at 30% power (Echo reactivation is fueling it)
- At 100%: Could enhance ALL draconic creatures in Northreach
- Varyx wants to use it on his clan — make them strong enough to survive the Echo

**What Players Can Discover Here**

**By Examining the Weapon (DC 16 Arcana):**
- It's broadcasting a signal — connected to the Temple site and other facilities
- It's drawing power from the Echo network (the "twelve nodes")
- At full power, it could *awaken* every draconic creature in range
- The signal is *also* waking up something else — the "draconic weapons" Aevor built

**By Talking to Varyx (After Transformation):**
- "You see? The power... it's magnificent. My people... they can be *protected*."
- "The Echo isn't our enemy. It's finishing what Aevor started. Making us into what we were *meant* to be."
- "But there's a cost. The Amplifier... it's also waking the *weapons*. The things Aevor built to *kill* dragons. They're still out there. Dormant. Waiting."

**The Dilemma:**

Players now know:
- The Frostfire Weapon can protect the clan from the Echo
- But it's also waking up Aevor's *anti-dragon weapons*
- If they help Varyx, they're accelerating the Echo
- If they stop him, the clan is defenseless against the coming storm

**Consequence & Next Step**
Players must choose: Help Varyx complete the Weapon (allies with Frostfire Clan, but accelerates Echo), or sabotage it (saves the region from draconic awakening, but dooms the clan).

**If Players Choose to Help:**
Varyx invites them to witness the activation. Move to Scene 6a (The Awakening).

**If Players Choose to Sabotage:**
They need to disable the Weapon without being caught. Move to Scene 6b (Sabotage).

**Common Questions for This Scene**

**Q: What are the "anti-dragon weapons"?**
A: The weapons stored at the Temple site (from Season 1) — Wyrmscourge Missiles, Domination Protocols, etc. The Frostfire Weapon is waking them up. If they activate, *all* dragons and dragonborn in Northreach are in danger.

### SCENE 5b: Sabotaging the Weapon (Players Fought and Won)

**Scene Context/Purpose**
Players have defeated Varyx (or he's been neutralized) and must sabotage the Frostfire Weapon before it fully activates.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: After combat victory
- Location: The Frostfire Weapon chamber
- Duration: 30-45 minutes
- Atmosphere: Urgent, dangerous, the device is unstable

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "Varyx lies defeated, his form shrinking back to near-human. But the Weapon... it's still active. The ring pulses with blinding blue light, and a low hum vibrates through the ice beneath your feet. Kaelith rushes in, eyes wide. 'Don't just stand there! The Amplifier is drawing power from the Echo — if it reaches 100%, it'll broadcast the signal across the entire region! Every draconic creature will... *change*.'"

**Key Information**
- The Weapon is at 60% power and rising (1% per round)
- Destroying it: Requires DC 18 Thieves' Tools or DC 16 Strength (check each round)
- Sabotaging it: DC 16 Arcana to safely disable (slower but safer)
- The broadcast signal: Once at 100%, it can't be stopped

**What Players Can Do Here**

**Option 1: Destroy It (Fast, Dangerous)**
- DC 18 Strength (Athletics): Smash the control crystal (1d6 bludgeoning damage per attempt)
- DC 18 Thieves' Tools: Disable the power core (risk of explosion on failure)
- Failure by 5+: The device surges, dealing 4d6 lightning damage to everyone in 30 feet

**Option 2: Sabotage It (Slow, Safe)**
- DC 16 Arcana: Rewrite the Aevorian code to shut it down safely
- DC 14 Investigation: Find the manual override (hidden panel)
- Success: Device shuts down safely, can be studied later
- Failure: Device surges to 80% power — speed up!

**Option 3: Steal the Plans**
- DC 15 Investigation: Copy the blueprints before disabling
- The plans show how Aevor enhanced draconic beings
- Brenna will want these — they could help understand the Echo's draconic effects

**Combat (If Applicable)**

**Encounter: Desperate Defense**

If clan members find the players sabotaging their only hope:

- 6 Frostfire Warriors (fanatically defending the Weapon)
- 2 Echo-Weavers (trying to complete the activation ritual)
- Environmental: Device emits random surges (1d6 lightning damage per round to all in chamber)

**Consequence & Next Step**
- **If Destroyed/Sabotaged:** The Weapon shuts down. The clan is devastated. Varyx, if alive, surrenders. Move to Aftermath.
- **If Players Wait Too Long (100%):** The signal broadcasts. Move to Scene 6a (The Awakening).
- **If They Steal Plans and Flee:** They escape with valuable intel, but the Weapon activates later (worse outcome).

**Common Questions for This Scene**

**Q: What happens if the Weapon reaches 100%?**
A: The signal broadcasts. Every draconic creature in Northreach (dragons, dragonborn, Frostfire clan) gets an immediate "amplification" — they become more powerful, but also more controlled by the Echo.

### SCENE 6a: The Awakening (Weapon Activates)

**Scene Context/Purpose**
The Frostfire Weapon reaches full power. The Echo's draconic signal broadcasts across Northreach. Epic climax.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: After Weapon activation
- Location: Ember Crag, then vision of Northreach
- Duration: 60-90 minutes
- Atmosphere: Reality-warping, draconic power unleashed

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "The ring *screams*. Not a sound — a *frequency*. You feel it in your bones, your blood, your very *being*. And then, visions flood your mind: Dragons across Northreach, waking up. Changing. Growing larger, more terrible, more *aware*. Varyx throws his head back and roars — a sound that's half human, half dragon. *'Yes! The old power returns! The Echo fulfills its promise!'* But then... something else. A cold, mechanical voice echoes in your mind: *'Draconic test subjects: Enhanced. Commencing Phase Two: Weapon Deployment.'* Varyx's eyes go wide. *'No... that's not... that wasn't supposed to happen.'*"

**Key Information**
- The Weapon worked — but not how Varyx expected
- It enhanced the clan, yes — but it also *woke up* Aevor's anti-dragon weapons
- The "Phase Two" voice: The Echo's distributed intelligence (from )
- Across Northreach: Dragons are waking up, changing, and *Aevor's weapons are targeting them*

**The Horrifying Realization:**

The Frostfire Weapon didn't just empower draconic beings — it made them *targets*. Aevor's dormant anti-dragon weapons (Wyrmscourge Missiles, Domination Protocols) are now active and hunting.

**What Players Can Do Here**

**Fight the Echo's Response:**
- Aevorian anti-dragon constructs emerge from the ice (4-6 constructs, CR appropriate)
- Varyx, if alive, fights *with* the players — "I didn't sign up for *this*!"
- The Weapon is overloading — it needs to be destroyed *now*

**Try to Reverse It:**
- DC 18 Arcana: Overload the device to broadcast a "cease" signal
- DC 16 Persuasion: Convince Varyx to help reverse it (he knows the code)
- Success: Device explodes safely, signal stops (but the weapons are *still* awake)

**Save the Clan:**
- The children — young frostfire hybrids — are terrified, changing rapidly
- DC 14 Medicine: Stabilize them, prevent complete draconic transformation
- If saved: They become "enhanced" but retain their humanity
- If failed: They become mindless draconic beasts (future villains?)

**Consequence & Next Step**
The Weapon is destroyed or overloaded. The signal is stopped — but the damage is done. Aevor's anti-dragon weapons are awake across Northreach.

Varyx, if alive: "You were right. The Echo... it's not our friend. It's using us. All of us." He dissolves into frostfire and vanishes — teleported away by the Echo.

The players have:
- Saved the region from immediate draconic awakening
- But awakened Aevor's anti-dragon weapons (major Season 2 plot point)
- The Frostfire Clan scatters — some join the Wardens, others go into hiding

**If Players Die/Retreat:**
The Weapon activates fully. The Echo's draconic phase begins. This leads directly into Echo War — the climactic battle.

**Common Questions for This Scene**

**Q: Is this the climax of Season 2?**
A: No — this is the *middle* climax. The real Season 2 finale is Echo War, where ALL the threads converge. This sets up that final battle.

**Q: Can players actually "win" here?**
A: Define "win." They can stop the Weapon, but they can't undo the fact that Aevor's weapons are now awake. The best outcome is: Weapon destroyed, clan scattered, players have critical intel for Echo War.

### SCENE 6b: The Aftermath (Weapon Sabotaged)

**Scene Context/Purpose**
Players successfully sabotaged the Frostfire Weapon. They must now deal with the consequences and the Frostfire Clan's future.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: After the Weapon is disabled
- Location: Ember Crag, morning after
- Duration: 30-45 minutes
- Atmosphere: Somber, defeat, an uncertain future

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "The Ember Crag is silent. The Weapon lies dead, its blue flames extinguished. The Frostfire Clan gathers in the great hall, looking at their hands, their scales, their wings... and realizing they're still *them*. Varyx stands before you, smaller now, more human. 'You did it. You saved us from... whatever the Echo had planned.' He looks at his people. 'But now we're just... us. No enhancement. No protection. Just a clan of broken Dragonknights.' He meets your eyes. 'Thank you. And curse you. Because now we have to figure out who we are... without the power.'"

**Key Information**
- The Weapon is destroyed — clan is safe from Echo enhancement
- But they're vulnerable — no protection from the coming Echo storms
- Varyx is... grateful? Angry? A complex mix of emotions
- The clan's future: They need a new purpose (player decision)

**What Players Can Do Here**

**Option 1: Offer Them Protection**
- The Wardens can recruit the Frostfire Clan as allies
- Varyx is hesitant: "You? After what we've been through?"
- DC 16 Persuasion: Convince him that unity is the only path forward
- Success: Clan becomes Warden allies (50+ enhanced soldiers for Echo War)

**Option 2: Help Them Find a New Home**
- The clan can't stay at Ember Crag — it's too exposed
- Players can help them relocate to a safer area
- Brenna can authorize a new settlement (diplomatic success)
- Outcome: Clan becomes a peaceful neighbor, no longer a threat

**Option 3: Disband Them**
- The clan is too dangerous to exist as a unified force
- Varyx accepts this: "Maybe you're right. We've been a weapon too long."
- The clan scatters — some join the Wardens, others vanish into the wild
- Outcome: No Frostfire Clan in Echo War (easier, but less allies)

**Consequence & Next Step**
Players leave the Ember Crag. The Frostfire Clan's fate is in their hands.

Varyx's final words: "The Echo is still out there. And now... we're just ordinary people again. Thank you for that. Or maybe... curse you. Only time will tell."

**Transition to Echo War:**
Regardless of the choice, Aevor's anti-dragon weapons are still awake. The Frostfire Clan arc concludes, but the larger threat remains.

**If Players Killed Varyx:**
The clan is leaderless and falls into chaos. They scatter violently — some become bandits, others seek revenge. A darker outcome, but valid.

**Common Questions for This Scene**

**Q: Should I make this feel like a "win"?**
A: It should feel *bittersweet*. They stopped the Weapon, but at a cost. The clan is safe, but they're also vulnerable. There's no perfect outcome here — that's the point.

**Q: How does this connect to Echo War?**
A: The anti-dragon weapons that woke up here are now active across Northreach. Echo War brings ALL the threads together — this is one piece of the puzzle.

- **Frostfire Strike.** Varyx makes one Frostfire Greatsword attack.
- **Draconic Roar (Costs 2 Actions).** Each creature of Varyx's choice within 30 feet must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened until the end of their next turn.
- **Phase Shift.** Varyx moves up to 20 feet without provoking opportunity attacks.

**Tactics:**
Varyx fights to protect his clan and the Weapon. He's not a mindless berserker — he uses tactics, terrain, and his people as force multipliers. If reduced below 50 HP, he attempts to parley or flee to protect the clan's future.

**If Defeated:**
Varyx surrenders if his clan is threatened: "Spare my people, and I yield." If the players kill him, the clan scatters and becomes a future threat.

## 8. Common Questions & Tips

### For New DMs Running This Adventure

**Q: What if players want to do something not covered in the scenes?**
A: Remember — the Frostfire Clan is a *community*. If players want to talk to clan members, let them. If they want to explore the Ember Crag, let them. The clan has 200 people — there are stories everywhere.

**Q: How long does this adventure actually take?**
A: Realistic estimate: 3 full sessions (3-4 hours each). Scene 4 (Varyx confrontation) is the longest — it's part social, part combat, part moral dilemma.

**Q: What do I do if players get stuck?**
A: Kaelith is the key. If players are lost or don't know what to do, have her approach them with information or a suggestion. She's the clan's voice — use her to guide players.

**Q: What's the hardest part of running this adventure?**
A: Balancing the moral complexity. Varyx isn't a villain — he's a victim trying to protect his people. Make sure players feel that. There's no "good" vs "evil" here — just different perspectives.

**Q: Can players really negotiate with Varyx?**
A: Absolutely. In fact, it's the *better* path — it gives them a powerful ally for Echo War. Combat should be a last resort, not the first option.

### For Veterans

**Q: How does this mechanical hook challenge experienced players?**
A: The frostfire mechanic (cold + fire simultaneously) challenges players who rely on resistance/immunity. Also, the moral dilemma has no clear "right" answer — veterans will appreciate the nuance.

**Q: Where's the deadliest encounter?**
A: Scene 6a (The Awakening) if the Weapon activates fully. Varyx + the Echo constructs + the environment create a multi-threat battlefield. Telegraph the danger clearly.

**Q: What's the sharpest moral dilemma?**
A: Scene 4 — Varyx's offer. Help him (allies + power, but accelerates Echo) vs. Stop him (saves region, but dooms the clan). There's no perfect choice.

## 9. Running This Adventure: DM Tips

### Tone & Atmosphere

- **Scenes 1-2:** Urgent, chaotic — raids and investigation
- **Scenes 3-4:** Isolated, ancient, the weight of history
- **Scene 5-6:** Powerful, reality-warping, the Echo's influence
- **Aftermath:** Bittersweet, complex, no easy answers

Use descriptions of cold, blue fire that doesn't warm, and the sensation of ancient eyes watching. The Frostfire Clan has been waiting 1000 years — their patience is both their strength and their curse.

### Heavy Lifting NPCs

- **Warlord Varyx:** Critical in Scenes 4, 5, 6; prepare his bitter, ancient voice. He's not evil — he's *tired*.
- **Echo-Weaver Kaelith:** Critical in Scenes 3, 4, 5; prepare her telepathic, layered voice. She's the clan's conscience.
- **Marshal Brenna Thorne:** In Scenes 2, sets the stakes. She's known about the clan for years — that revelation should feel heavy.

### When to Improvise

- **Scenes 1-2:** Follow the script — players need this info
- **Scene 3:** Improvise freely! Let players explore the tundra, meet clan members, discover stories
- **Scene 4:** The negotiation with Varyx should feel like a real conversation. Adapt to player arguments
- **Scene 5-6:** The Weapon activation is scripted but adaptive — respond to player choices

### Making This Memorable

The moment Varyx transforms in Scene 5a — growing larger, more draconic, more terrible — should be the adventure's visual peak. Describe it vividly. The players should realize: "He's becoming what Aevor *made* him."

### Pacing Flexibility

- Can skip detailed tundra travel (Scene 3) if behind schedule — jump to Ember Crag
- Can combine Scenes 5-6 if running short — the Weapon's activation happens during confrontation
- If players are loving the negotiation (Scene 4), let it breathe — this is the heart of the adventure
