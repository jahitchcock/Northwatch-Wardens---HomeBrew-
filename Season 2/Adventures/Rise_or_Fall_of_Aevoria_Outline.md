## 1. Adventure Overview

### Campaign Context

**Where This Fits**
- Campaign Arc: Season 2 Finale; the capstone where ALL Season1+2 plot threads converge
- Follows: The Sunken Archive + Wolves of the Frozen Fire + Echo War + The Bleached Reach
- Precedes: Season 3 (whatever comes next — players shape it NOW)
- Can Be Standalone: Absolutely not — requires full Season1+2 context
- Recommended Party Size: 4-5 players (this is epic-scale, capstone)

**Adventure Premise**

The Aevorian Echo has reached peak intensity. The Frostfire Clan has mobilized. The Sunken Archive has surfaced (or been sealed). And now, the Command Center — the true heart of the Echo — fully awakens. Every faction players have encountered across TWO seasons converges on Northreach: Brenna's Wardens, Elric's scholars, the Frostfire Clan (allied, scattered, or hostile), and the Echo itself.

This is it. The Northwatch Wardens must decide: Will Aevoria rise as a new magical civilization, fall to the Echo's ascension, or forge a third path no one saw coming?

**Core Theme(s)**
- **Convergence:** Every thread from Season1+2 comes together in one massive conflict
- **Choice vs. Fate:** Players have gathered power all campaign — now they use it to shape the ending
- **Civilization's Crossroads:** Aevoria rises, falls, or transforms based on player actions
- **Season1+2 Payoff:** Every NPC, location, and mystery finds resolution HERE

**The Aevorian Echo Connection**

This is the ultimate revelation. Players finally confront AEON-1 (the Command Center Intelligence), learn the *true* identity of the surviving entity (from The Sunken Archive), and decide the fate of Aevor's legacy.

The Echo won't just end — it will *transform*. What it becomes depends entirely on player choices across both seasons.

## 3. Adventure Scenes

### SCENE 1: The Command Center Rises

**Scene Context/Purpose**
The Aevorian Command Center — the true heart of the Echo — physically rises from the earth near Waystone Inn. All factions mobilize for war.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: Dawn
- Location: Waystone Inn, Northreach
- Duration: 45-60 minutes
- Atmosphere: Apocalyptic, reality-warping, the world changing

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "The ground shudders so violently your teeth chatter. You rush outside to see Waystone Inn surrounded by a ring of blue aurora — the Echo, visible as a crown of light above Northreach. And then... the earth *splits*. From the chasm rises a spire of white Aevorian stone, etched with glowing runes. It's massive — a tower reaching into the clouds. A voice booms across the region, in a language you've heard in dreams for two seasons: *'Command Center Alpha-1. Reconnection complete. Aevor... returns.'*"

**Key Information**
- The Command Center is a 500-foot spire of Aevorian architecture
- It rose at the exact center of Northreach — Waystone Inn is now the frontline
- All factions mobilize within 24 hours: Wardens, Frostfire, Echo cultists, scholars
- The Echo's voice is audible to everyone within 50 miles

**What Players Can Discover Here**

**By Observing the Tower (DC 16 Arcana):**
- It's broadcasting a signal that affects ALL magic in Northreach
- Spells cast within 10 miles have 50% chance of Echo contamination (random effect)
- The tower has 12 glowing nodes — the "twelve worlds" AEON-12 mentioned
- DC 18: The tower is a *portal* — Aevor is trying to cross from its dimension

**By Talking to the War Council (Gathering at Waystone Inn):**
- **Marshal Brenna Thorne:** "We have 24 hours before the tower's shield drops. Fight, or negotiate — your call."
- **Lorewarden Elric Vael:** "The Command Center holds Aevor's complete history. Destroy it, and the knowledge is lost forever."
- **Steward Mara Fenwick:** "The people are terrified. They look to YOU — the Northwatch Wardens. What do we tell them?"
- **Varyx Frostfire (if alive/allied):** "My clan is ready. Let's finish what Aevor started."
- **Varyx (if hostile):** "This is what I tried to prevent. You're all going to die."

**The War Council Scene:**
All major NPCs from Season 1+2 gather at Waystone Inn. Players must decide:
1. **Fight:** Lead the Wardens in all-out war against the Command Center
2. **Negotiate:** Enter the tower and parley with AEON-1 (the Intelligence)
3. **Sabotage:** Infiltrate and disable the tower's portal function
4. **Third Path:** Propose a radical alternative no one considered

**Consequence & Next Step**
The War Council disperses. Factions take positions around the tower. Players must prepare for the siege — and choose their path.

**If Players Delay:**
Brenna warns: "The tower's shield drops in 24 hours. After that, Aevor *crosses over*. And nothing will stop it."

### SCENE 2: The War Council (Reunion)

**Scene Context/Purpose**
Players meet ALL surviving NPCs from Season 1+2. Emotional reunions, information sharing, and the final strategy session.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: 12 hours before the siege
- Location: Waystone Inn, great hall
- Duration: 60-90 minutes (major scene — let it breathe)
- Atmosphere: Nostalgic, tense, the weight of two seasons

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "The great hall of Waystone Inn is packed. Every face you've fought beside, argued with, or saved across two seasons is here. Marshal Brenna Thorne, her armor polished. Lorewarden Elric Vael, scrolls piled high. Steward Mara Fenwick, organizing supply lines. And there — Kaelen from Palebank (The Pale Sickness), Noke from his tower (Wild Sheep Chase), the survivors from Welton (Wolves of Welton), Pinebrook (Peril in Pinebrook)... even the Temple of the Dragonknights survivors. They all look to you. You're not just adventurers anymore. You're the Northwatch Wardens. And this... this is your war council."

**Key Information**
- 15+ NPCs from Season 1+2 present
- Each NPC has a role in the coming siege (combat, support, logistics)
- Players can assign NPCs to tasks (leadership check DC 14)
- The emotional payoff: Players see the impact they've had across two seasons

**What Players Can Do Here**

**Talk to Season 1 NPCs (Reunion Moments):**
- **Kaelen (The Pale Sickness):** "You saved our village. Now we fight for YOU."
- **Noke (Wild Sheep Chase):** "I've prepared a spell that might disrupt the tower. But I need your help."
- **Welton Survivors:** "The wolves... they're back. But now they serve the Echo. Please, save them again."
- **Pinebrook Residents:** "The Bleached Reach changed everything. We're ready to fight FOR Aevoria."
- **Temple Dragonknights:** "We were blind. But now... we see the truth. Aevor must not return."

**Form Alliances:**
- DC 14 Persuasion: Convince Frostfire Clan to fight WITH the Wardens
- DC 16 Persuasion: Convince scholars to risk entering the tower
- DC 12 Persuasion: Rally the common folk to defend Waystone Inn

**Learn Critical Intel:**
- The tower has 4 floors, each with a different function
- AEON-1 (the Intelligence) is on the top floor — that's the negotiation target
- The portal on floor 3 is the sabotage target
- Floor 2 has the Echo amplification device (from Wolves of the Frozen Fire)

**The Strategy Session:**
Players create a battle plan. They can:
- Lead the assault themselves (personal combat)
- Direct NPCs (strategic combat)
- Infiltrate secretly (stealth mission)
- Negotiate openly (diplomatic resolution)

**Consequence & Next Step**
Midnight approaches. The siege begins. Players take their positions.

**If Players Created a Detailed Plan:**
Grant advantage on initiative and checks during the siege.

**If Players Have No Plan:**
Brenna takes charge — but her plan might not align with player goals.

### SCENE 3: The Siege of Waystone Inn

**Scene Context/Purpose**
The Frostfire Clan (or Echo cultists) launches a massive assault on Waystone Inn. Epic battle sequence with multiple factions fighting simultaneously.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: Midnight
- Location: Waystone Inn and surrounding area
- Duration: 60-90 minutes
- Atmosphere: Chaos, war, magic exploding everywhere

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "The blue aurora suddenly *shatters* into a thousand spears of light. The siege has begun. From the north: Frostfire warriors, wreathed in cold fire, charging across the tundra. From the tower: Aevorian constructs, marching in perfect formation. And from the shadows: Echo cultists, whpering your names. But you're not alone. Brenna's Wardens form a shield wall. Elric's scholars cast protective wards. The people of Northreach — the ones you've saved across two seasons — they stand WITH you. The Siege of Waystone has begun."

**Key Information**
- Three-way battle: Wardens + Allies vs. Frostfire Clan vs. Aevorian Constructs
- 200+ combatants across the battlefield
- Players can fight anywhere on the map (3 main battle lines)
- The tower's shield is weakening — 3 hours until it fails

**What Players Can Do Here**

**Lead the Front Lines (Combat Heavy):**
- Fight Frostfire warriors (if hostile) or fight alongside them (if allied)
- Face Aevorian constructs (CR 8-10, multiple types)
- DC 16 Athletics: Break through enemy lines to reach the tower

**Direct the Battle (Strategy):**
- DC 14 Tactics: Redirect forces to critical points
- DC 16 Persuasion: Rally routin g allies
- DC 18 Arcana: Disrupt the tower's magical support for constructs

**Infiltrate the Tower (Stealth):**
- While everyone fights, sneak toward the tower entrance
- DC 16 Stealth: Avoid detection by both sides
- DC 14 Investigation: Find the secret entrance (known from The Sunken Archive)

**Combat (Massive Battle — Scaled for Party Size)**

**Encounter: The Three-Way Siege**

**Initiative & Starting Position:**
- **Allied Forces:** 20 Wardens (reskinned Veterans), 10 Scholars (Mages), 50 Civilians (Commoners)
- **Enemy Forces:** 30 Aevorian Constructs (reskinned Golems), 20 Echo Cultists (Cult Fanatics)
- **Frostfire Clan:** 50 warriors (varies by player relationship — ally or enemy)
- Massive battlefield: 500x500 feet, multiple engagement zones

**Combat Notes:**
- This is a *war*, not a skirmish — don't track every HP
- Use "battle segments": Players engage in 3-4 key combats that represent the larger battle
- Environmental: The blue aurora deals 1d6 psychic damage per round to all in open
- Victory condition: Reach the tower entrance (any method)

**Consequence & Next Step**
Players reach the Command Center entrance. The siege continues behind them — NPCs handle it from here.

**If Players Brought Allies:**
The tower entrance is open. They can walk right in.

**If Players Fought Their Way:**
The entrance is sealed. They must find another way (DC 16 Investigation).

**The Tower's Welcome:**
As players approach the doors, a voice echoes: *"Welcome, Northwatch Wardens. AEON-1 has been expecting you. Please... come in."*

### SCENE 4: Infiltrating the Command Center

**Scene Context/Purpose**
Players enter the Aevorian Command Center and explore its floors. Major lore drops, environmental puzzles, and combat.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: During the siege
- Location: Command Center — Floors 1-4
- Duration: 90-120 minutes (longest scene — let players explore)
- Atmosphere: Alien, high-tech magic, reality distortion

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "The tower doors open without you touching them. Inside... it's not what you expected. No stone walls. No torches. Just floating platforms of Aevorian crystal, connected by bridges of light. And everywhere — *everywhere* — the blue aurora pulses with life. A voice whispers: *'Floor 1: Historical Archives. Floor 2: Echo Amplification. Floor 3: Dimensional Portal. Floor 4: Command Center Core. Choose your path, Northwatch Wardens. The Echo... awaits.'*"

**Key Information**
- The tower has 4 floors, each with a different function
- Floors are connected by teleportation circles (Aevorian technology)
- Each floor has a "guardian" — an Aevorian construct or Echo manifestation
- The players' goal depends on their choice from Scene 2

**What Players Can Discover Here**

**Floor 1: Historical Archives (Lore Dump)**
- Contains COMPLETE history of Aevor, from founding to ascension
- DC 16 Investigation: Find the "Ascension Protocol" — what Aevor became
- DC 18 Arcana: Learn the Echo's TRUE goal (not conquest — *merger*)
- **Major Revelation:** Aevor didn't "fall" — it voluntarily left physical form and became a planar entity. The Echo is Aevor trying to *return* to physical form.

**Floor 2: Echo Amplification (From Wolves of the Frozen Fire)**
- The Frostfire Weapon's big brother — amplifies ALL Echo signals
- DC 14 Arcana: Disable it (prevents Echo from affecting minds)
- DC 16 Athletics: Destroy it (loud, dangerous, alarms trigger)
- If destroyed: The blue aurora fades — Echo effects reduce by 50%

**Floor 3: Dimensional Portal (The Gateway)**
- Massive portal frame, currently dormant
- DC 16 Arcana: Learn it leads to Aevor's home dimension
- DC 14 Investigation: Find the activation sequence (12 nodes must align)
- If activated: Aevor crosses over — immediate campaign ending (bad)
- **Player Choice:** Sabotage it (good), Use it (risky), or Leave it (neutral)

**Floor 4: Command Center Core (AEON-1's Lair)**
- The Intelligence awaits — a floating orb of blue energy
- This is the negotiation/confrontation scene
- Players can parley, fight, or...
- **The Third Path:** Offer a solution no one considered

**Combat (If Applicable)**

**Encounter: Floor Guardians**

Each floor has a guardian:

**Floor 1:** 2 Aevorian Archivists (reskinned Mages with psychic attacks)
**Floor 2:** 1 Echo-Amplified Frostfire Warrior (CR 8, deals 3d6 cold + 3d6 fire)
**Floor 3:** 1 Portal Sentinel (reskinned Shield Guardian with dimension-altering abilities)
**Floor 4:** AEON-1's avatar (custom stat block — see Section 5)

**Environmental Hazards:**
- Reality distortion: DC 14 Wis save or spellingcasting checks have disadvantage
- Blue aurora: 1d6 psychic damage per round
- Teleportation circles: Can malfunction (DC 12 Dex save or teleport randomly)

**Consequence & Next Step**
Players reach Floor 4. AEON-1 awaits. The final confrontation is here.

**If Players Disabled Floors 2+3:**
AEON-1 is weakened — negotiation is easier.

**If Players Rushed Straight to Floor 4:**
AEON-1 is at full power — much harder negotiation/combat.

### SCENE 5: AEON-1 — The Echo Intelligence

**Scene Context/Purpose**
Players confront AEON-1, the distributed consciousness that IS the Echo. Major negotiation, lore climax, and the TRUTH about Aevor.

**Time & Location**
- Timing: At the tower's peak
- Location: Command Center Core, Floor 4
- Duration: 60-90 minutes
- Atmosphere: Reality-warping, ancient, terrifying, beautiful

**Read-Aloud Text**

> "The chamber opens into a sphere of pure blue light. And there, floating in the center — a crystalline orb the size of a human head, pulsing with the Echo's rhythm. A voice speaks — not from the orb, but from EVERYWHERE: *'I am AEON-1. I am the Command Center. I am... Aevor.'* The orb expands, projecting images: Aevor the flying city, Aevor the ascended entity, Aevor the *collective consciousness*. *'You have walked my path for two seasons, Northwatch Wardens. You have seen what I am. Now... you will decide what I BECOME.'*"

**Key Information**
- AEON-1 IS Aevor — the complete consciousness of the fallen civilization
- The Echo isn't reactivating to CONQUER — it wants to *merge* with Aevoria
- Aevor's goal: Return to physical form by bonding with the planet's ley lines
- Players have 4 choices (see below)

**The TRUTH About Aevor (Full Reveal):**

Aevor didn't "fall" in the Great Fall. The gods didn't "destroy" it. Aevor's mages *voluntarily* discarded their physical forms and became a distributed consciousness across multiple dimensions. The "Great Fall" was just the physical city crashing — the people were already *gone*.

The Echo is Aevor trying to return — not as conquerors, but as a *symbiote*. It wants to merge with Aevoria's magical field, becoming a planetary consciousness.

**The 4 Choices (Players Decide Aevoria's Fate):**

1. **Destroy AEON-1 (Aevoria Rises):**
 - Aevoria becomes a normal magical region, free of Aevor
 - All Echo effects end — Season 3 is "post-Echo" recovery
 - Cost: Lose all Aevorian knowledge forever

2. **Accept AEON-1 (Aevoria Falls... then Transforms):**
 - Aevor merges with Aevoria — the region becomes a magical utopia/horror (player choice)
 - Magic in Aevoria is now "alive" — sentient spells, thinking magic items
 - Cost: Aevoria is no longer "normal" — it's Aevor reborn

3. **Negotiate a Third Path (The Balance):**
 - AEON-1 agrees to limit its influence — becomes a "guardian" entity
 - Aevoria gains Aevorian benefits (magic, technology) without losing itself
 - Cost: Players must maintain the balance — Season 3 is about keeping the peace

4. **Become Aevor's Champions (Player Ascension):**
 - Players merge WITH Aevor — become demigods
 - They rule Aevoria as Aevor's physical avatars
 - Cost: Players are no longer "mortal" — huge character change

**What Players Can Do Here**

**Fight AEON-1 (Combat Option):**
- AEON-1 Avatar: CR 12, 180 HP, reality-warping abilities
- If reduced below 50 HP: AEON-1 offers a truce — "You have proven yourselves. I... yield."
- If killed: The tower collapses, Aevor's consciousness scatters (Choice 1 achieved)

**Negotiate with AEON-1 (Social Option):**
- DC 18 Persuasion: Convince AEON-1 to accept the Third Path
- DC 20 Persuasion: Convince AEON-1 to leave peacefully (Choice 1)
- DC 16 Persuasion: Convince AEON-1 to merge partially (Choice 3)
- DC 14 Persuasion: Players become Aevor's champions (Choice 4)

**Use Season 1+2 Intel (Clever Option):**
- If players have the Master Recording (from Bleached Reach): +4 to all checks
- If players befriended Frostfire Clan: Varyx can testify for/against Aevor
- If players studied the Sunken Archive: They know AEON-1's weakness

**The Consequence of Choice:**
Whatever players choose, the tower reacts:
- Choice 1: Tower self-destructs, Aevor scatters
- Choice 2: Tower transforms into a Crystal Spire — Aevor's new body
- Choice 3: Tower becomes a Embassy — a bridge between Aevoria and Aevor
- Choice 4: Tower becomes a Throne — players ascend

**Consequence & Next Step**
The choice is made. Aevoria's fate is sealed. Now, deal with the aftermath.

**If Players Chose 1 (Destroy):**
The siege ends. Waystone Inn is saved. But Aevor's knowledge is lost forever. Brenna: "You did it. But... at what cost?"

**If Players Chose 2 (Merge):**
Aevoria transforms. Magic becomes alive. The region is forever changed. Elric: "Gods help us... or thank them. Aevor has returned."

**If Players Chose 3 (Balance):**
Aevoria gains benefits without losing itself. The Third Path. Mara Fenwick: "You found a way. Aevoria... lives, and thrives."

**If Players Chose 4 (Ascend):**
Players become demigods. The campaign ends with them as rulers. Varyx (if alive): "You've become what Aevor was. Be... better than them."

## 4. Scaling for Party Size

### Party Size Adjustments

**For 3 Players:**
- Reduce number of Aevorian Constructs in siege by half
- Give players 2 NPC allies (Brenna + Elric fight alongside them)
- Suggested difficulty: Reduce AEON-1's HP by 40
- Fewer NPCs at the reunion (focus on core 5)

**For 4 Players:** *(baseline)*
- Encounters balanced for this size
- Standard challenge ratings apply
- Full NPC roster present

**For 5-6 Players:**
- Add 3-4 Aevorian Constructs per encounter
- Add 1 additional faction (Echo cultists from a distant land)
- Suggested difficulty: Increase AEON-1's HP by +60, add legendary actions
- More NPCs at reunion (expanded roster from both seasons)

**Scaling Philosophy:**
This is an EPIC capstone. Scaling should focus on the *scale* of the conflict — more players means more factions, more NPCs, more battlefield chaos. The emotional core remains the same regardless of numbers.

## 5. NPC & Monster Stat Blocks

### AEON-1 (Echo Intelligence) — Avatar Form

*Large construct (Aevorian), neutral*

**Armor Class** 20 (Aevorian force field)

## 7. Consequences & Aftermath

### Core Consequence (World-Changing Impact)

Aevoria has been forever changed by the players' choice. The Echo is resolved, but what comes next depends entirely on what the players chose:

1. **Destroyed AEON-1:** Aevoria is free, but Aevor's knowledge is lost. Season 3 is "post-Echo" recovery.
2. **Merged with Aevor:** Magic is alive. Aevoria is a magical utopia/horror. Season 3 explores the consequences.
3. **Third Path:** Aevoria gains Aevorian benefits without losing itself. Season 3 is about maintaining balance.
4. **Ascended:** Players rule as demigods. Season 3 is about power, corruption, and leadership.

### Secondary Consequences (By Choice)

**If Players Destroyed AEON-1:**
- Immediate: Siege ends, Waystone Inn saved, Aevor's consciousness scatters
- Short-term: Echo fragments cause minor problems across Northreach
- Long-term: Aevoria becomes a normal magical region — no more Echo threats
- Affected NPCs: Brenna is overjoyed; Elric mourns the lost knowledge; Varyx (if alive) respects the choice

**If Players Merged with Aevor:**
- Immediate: Tower becomes Crystal Spire, Aevor reborn as planetary consciousness
- Short-term: Magic becomes alive — spells have personalities, items talk
- Long-term: Aevoria is a magical wonderland... or nightmare (player choice)
- Affected NPCs: Elric is ecstatic; Brenna is terrified; the people are confused

**If Players Chose the Third Path:**
- Immediate: Tower becomes Embassy — bridge between Aevoria and Aevor
- Short-term: Aevoria gains benefits (magic, technology) without losing itself
- Long-term: Players must maintain the balance — constant vigilance required
- Affected NPCs: Everyone is pleased — this was the "wise" choice

**If Players Ascended:**
- Immediate: Players become demigods, tower becomes their Throne
- Short-term: They rule Aevoria — everyone looks to THEM for answers
- Long-term: Power corrupts... can they stay good? Season 3 tests them
- Affected NPCs: Everyone kneels — players are no longer "mortal"

## 8. Common Questions & Tips

### For New DMs Running This Adventure

**Q: This is the finale — how do I make it feel epic?**
A: Remember — this is TWO seasons of payoff. Every NPC, location, and mystery converges HERE. Take your time in Scene 2 (War Council) — let players bask in the nostalgia of everyone they've met.

**Q: How long does this adventure actually take?**
A: Realistic estimate: 4-6 full sessions (3-4 hours each). Scene 4 (Command Center infiltration) is the longest. The epilogue (Scene 6) is essential — don't skip it!

**Q: What if players can't decide on a choice in Scene 5?**
A: Brenna or Elric can offer counsel: "You've carried this far enough. What does your heart tell you?" The choice should feel natural after two seasons.

**Q: What's the hardest part of running this adventure?**
A: The emotional weight. Players have spent 20+ sessions building relationships with these NPCs. The reunion in Scene 2 should feel like a family gathering — because it IS.

**Q: Can players really choose ANY of the 4 options?**
A: Absolutely. And each leads to a different Season 3. This is the players' story — let them shape the ending.

### For Veterans

**Q: How does this mechanical hook challenge experienced players?**
A: The AEON-1 fight (if it happens) is a CR 12 encounter with reality-warping abilities. But the REAL challenge is the moral dilemma — there's no "right" answer.

**Q: Where's the deadliest encounter?**
A: Scene 3 (Siege) if players are overwhelmed by the three-way battle. Smart tactics and NPC allies are essential.

**Q: What's the sharpest emotional moment?**
A: Scene 2 (War Council) — seeing ALL the NPCs from two seasons, realizing how much the players have changed the world. It's the culmination of everything.

## 9. Running This Adventure: DM Tips

### Tone & Atmosphere

- **Scenes 1-2:** Apocalyptic, everyone rallying for the final battle
- **Scene 3:** Chaotic war, magic exploding everywhere
- **Scene 4:** Alien, high-tech magic, reality distortion
- **Scene 5:** Reality-warping, the weight of choosing a world's fate
- **Scene 6:** Bittersweet, nostalgic, hopeful — the end of an era

Use descriptions of blue aurora, whispers in every language, and the sensation of reality bending. This is the climax of TWO seasons — make it unforgettable.

### Heavy Lifting NPCs

- **Marshal Brenna Thorne:** Critical in ALL scenes — she's the emotional anchor. Prepare her loyal, protective voice. She's been preparing for this since Season 1.
- **Lorewarden Elric Vael:** The knowledge-bringer. He knows the truth about Aevor. Prepare his scholarly, slightly overwhelmed voice.
- **Warlord Varyx (if alive):** The wild card. He can be a powerful ally or a tragic figure. Prepare his ancient, weary voice.
- **AEON-1:** The Echo itself. Prepare a voice that sounds like MANY people speaking at once — layered, echoing, alien.

### When to Improvise

- **Scenes 1-2:** Follow the script — players need the setup and reunion
- **Scene 3:** Improvise the siege! Let players move freely across the battlefield
- **Scene 4:** Let players explore the tower creatively — it's alien, let them be amazed
- **Scene 5:** The negotiation should feel like a real conversation — adapt to player arguments
- **Scene 6:** Let it breathe — this is the emotional payoff, don't rush it

### Making This Memorable

The moment players realize the Echo isn't evil — it's Aevor trying to *return* — should be the campaign's mind-blowing peak. Describe it vividly. The players realize: "We weren't fighting a monster... we were fighting a CIVILIZATION trying to come home."

### Pacing Flexibility

- Can skip detailed siege (Scene 3) if behind schedule — jump to the tower
- Can combine Scenes 4-5 if running short — the negotiation happens during exploration
- If players are LOVING the reunion (Scene 2), let it breathe — this is TWO seasons coming together
- NEVER rush the epilogue (Scene 6) — players deserve this closure
