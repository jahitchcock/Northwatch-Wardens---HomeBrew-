## Summary

The Red Covenant is a mutual aid network that emerged from the volcanic devastation of the Emberlands, where entire communities were displaced and traditional social structures collapsed. Born from catastrophe, the Covenant exists to ensure that no disaster leaves survivors entirely alone — they mobilize food, shelter, and labor in response to crisis, help displaced people rebuild, and maintain a dispersed network of aid nodes across regions affected by ongoing instability. They welcome anyone willing to help others regardless of background, and their leadership rotates based on current need rather than formal hierarchy. The Covenant is not powerful in a martial or political sense, but they are present everywhere people are suffering — and they remember who helped and who didn't.

## Leadership

Leadership is fluid and rotates with crisis response, but recognized figures include:

- **Ember Mother Kasi Firewarden** (human) — The Covenant's most prominent coordinator for Emberlands relief; commands significant respect and moral authority within the network.
- **Wanderer Torin Ashwalker** (tiefling) — Mobile aid coordinator who operates across multiple regions simultaneously; the Covenant's face in areas far from the Emberlands.

## Goals

1. Sustain active aid operations in Northreach and the Emberlands through the current season — increasing frontier instability is generating the exact kind of displacement and isolated suffering the Covenant exists to address.
2. Build enough institutional credibility across Aevoria that the Covenant can operate freely in any region without having to negotiate political access during a crisis.

## Relationship to the Party

The Covenant views the Northwatch Wardens as natural allies. Frontier protection guilds and mutual aid networks serve overlapping communities, and Warden reputation for protecting the innocent translates directly into Covenant trust. The party will encounter Covenant aid workers in communities affected by the campaign's events — they provide information, logistical support, and genuine warmth toward Wardens who show up to help. If the party ever needs community intelligence, safe houses, or local contacts in a settlement they haven't visited, the Covenant can often help.

## Season 1: First Responders

The Covenant appears wherever the campaign's events generate civilian suffering — settlements affected by monster activity, communities dealing with unexplained illness, refugee groups on the frontier roads. They are not combatants and will not directly engage threats, but they will be at the scene before and after the Wardens. Use them to show the human cost of the campaign's events and to reward Wardens who take time to help communities beyond what a contract requires. Torin Ashwalker may be operating in Northreach by mid-Season 1 if disruptions in the region generate enough need.

## Key Locations

- **Emberlands Relief Camp (primary base)** — The Covenant's most organized operational hub, managing ongoing displacement from volcanic instability.
- **Mobile Aid Posts (frontier roads)** — Temporary waypoints that follow disaster zones; marked with red cloth so survivors know to approach.
- **Community Sanctuaries (scattered settlements)** — Homes and community spaces in stable areas that Covenant members have designated as informal safe houses for displaced individuals.

## Secrets (DM Only)

- Ember Mother Kasi Firewarden has been quietly collecting reports from Covenant aid workers about a pattern of unusual military-style organization in the groups causing displacement in the Emberlands. The volcanic instability isn't purely natural — someone has been deliberately destabilizing communities to drive population movements in specific directions. Kasi doesn't know why, but she has enough data to suspect it, and she is looking for someone trustworthy enough to hand the reports to.
- Wanderer Torin Ashwalker carries a secondary identity as a former Myriad criminal organization operative who fled after a crisis of conscience. He uses his knowledge of criminal logistics to run the Covenant's aid supply chains with extraordinary efficiency and discretion. His past means he recognizes criminal operation signatures in the frontier disruptions — including the Blackwake Corsairs' activities — and he has been trying to figure out how to raise the alarm without revealing how he knows what he knows.
