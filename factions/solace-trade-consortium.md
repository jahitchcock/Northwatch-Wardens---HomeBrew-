## Summary

The Solace Trade Consortium is the dominant merchant guild in southern Aevoria, controlling much of the legitimate trade flowing between major settlements from their regulated hub at Solace Nexus. They employ spellcasters for magical logistics, maintain trade outposts across the realm, and hold political influence with rulers in multiple regions. Wealthy, connected, and thoroughly pragmatic, the Consortium follows money rather than morality — making them powerful potential allies, convenient employers, or quiet obstacles depending on which way the winds of profit blow.

## Leadership

- **Guildmaster Torvin Goldscale** (dragonborn) — Head of the ruling council; sets strategic direction and brokers high-level political agreements, prioritizing the Consortium's long-term market dominance.
- **Trade Prince Kallas Vermeer** (human) — Controls all southern trade routes; personally negotiates contracts with frontier settlements and handles arrangements in Northreach's moderate-presence zone.

## Goals

1. Expand trade infrastructure into Northreach's frontier settlements to capture emerging economic activity before competitors establish relationships.
2. Maintain political neutrality between rival powers (Solaris Dominion, Vharoxis, frontier guilds) to preserve the Consortium's freedom to operate in all markets simultaneously.

## Relationship to the Party

The Consortium is aware of the Northwatch Wardens as a reliable frontier guild capable of caravan protection and item retrieval — useful contractors, not rivals. They will hire the party for transport and protection work, offer fair prices at trade posts, and treat guild members with professional courtesy. The relationship sours if the party disrupts trade operations, sides against Consortium interests in settlement disputes, or develops strong ties to Vharoxis's black markets.

## Season 1: Peripheral but Present

The Consortium is a background power in Season 1 — present in Northreach through trade post staff and the occasional caravan passing through. They are not a primary antagonist or patron but represent the economic reality of the frontier: goods have to come from somewhere, and someone takes a cut. Introduce them through contract board postings for caravan escort work, merchant NPCs at Waystone, and references by Steward Mara Fenwick (who watches their trade patterns closely). They become more relevant if the party investigates the movement of Aevorian artifacts through legitimate channels.

## Key Locations

- **Solace Nexus, Verdant Marches** — Consortium headquarters and regulated spellwork hub; the center of legitimate long-distance trade in southern Aevoria.
- **Trade Post Outposts (Northreach region)** — Small waypoint operations in moderate-presence settlements; offer fair prices, equipment purchase, and contract postings.
- **Divinity's Beacon office** — Significant Consortium presence alongside the Covenant; politically sensitive given the Covenant's scrutiny of unregulated magic.

## Secrets (DM Only)

- Guildmaster Torvin Goldscale has been quietly tasked by the Consortium's inner council to investigate the source of "irregular biological samples" appearing in Far North smuggling manifests — items that fetch extraordinary prices from an unknown buyer. The Consortium doesn't know what they are dealing with, but they know someone with deep pockets is disrupting their normal artifact trade channels, and they want to control that market.
- Trade Prince Kallas Vermeer has an informal arrangement with Vharoxis intermediaries that technically violates the Consortium's own trading codes — he routes morally questionable contracts through deniable third parties to maintain the guild's public reputation for legitimacy.
