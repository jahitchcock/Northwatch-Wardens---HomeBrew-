## Summary

The Horizon Wayfinders are a small but dedicated society of arctic explorers dedicated to mapping the dangerous Far North. They maintain scattered supply caches across the region, share survival and navigation knowledge among members, and push further into unmapped territory than any other organized group. Their numbers are few and their losses are high, but the maps they produce are unmatched.

## Leadership

- **Pathfinder Orla Duskreach** (human ranger) — The Wayfinders' most experienced active member; coordinates cache placement and approves new expeditions based on safety assessments.

## Goals

1. Complete a reliable map of the Far North's navigable regions before another exploration season ends in preventable deaths from inadequate preparation.
2. Document the increasing anomalies — strange magnetic disturbances, areas of unnatural cold, ruins that were not in last year's surveys — that have been showing up in recent expedition reports.

## Relationship to the Party

The Wayfinders are potential allies and information sources if the campaign takes the party into the Far North. They know the terrain, the dangers, and — crucially — where things have changed recently. They respond positively to explorers who contribute to shared knowledge and respect supply cache protocols.

## Season 1: Distant Expertise

The Wayfinders are primarily relevant as a resource the party hasn't tapped yet. Their recent expedition reports contain observations about anomalous sites that match the Aevorian Echo pattern, but no one has asked the right questions yet.

## Key Locations

- **Far North Supply Caches** — Hidden waypoints stocked for survival; known locations shared only with trusted members and allies.
- **Expedition Staging Camp (Far North edge)** — The Wayfinders' primary base of operations for northern expeditions.

## Secrets (DM Only)

- Pathfinder Orla Duskreach's most recent expedition mapped a structure that should not exist — a partially intact building of non-natural geometry rising from the ice, emitting occasional light pulses in a pattern that her notes describe as "rhythmic, like breathing." She has not published this finding because she is not certain what to do with it. The structure matches Aevorian architectural descriptions in Lorewarden Elric's restricted texts.
