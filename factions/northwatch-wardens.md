## Summary

The Northwatch Wardens are a frontier protection guild operating out of Waystone Inn in Northreach, dedicated to safeguarding remote settlements, investigating arcane and mundane threats, and providing aid to travelers across the wild roads and frozen forests of the frontier. Founded roughly fifteen years ago by Marshal Brenna Thorne, the guild operates on a contract system governed by a Triad Council and a strict six-tenet code of conduct. They are the party's own organization — the lens through which the campaign's threats are engaged, reported, and ultimately confronted.

## Leadership

- **Marshal Brenna Thorne** (human fighter) — Field Commander and tactical leader; drives the guild's investigative posture and holds members to exacting standards of honesty and competence.
- **Steward Mara Fenwick** (dwarf) — Quartermaster and logistics coordinator; controls guild finances, supply chains, and institutional memory of frontier trade relationships.
- **Lorewarden Elric Vael** (half-elf) — Arcane scholar and records-keeper; investigates magical phenomena and ancient civilizations with quiet obsession.

## Goals

1. Keep Northreach's affiliated settlements — Welton, Palebank, Pinebrook — safe through the current season of escalating strange occurrences.
2. Build a coordinated intelligence network capable of identifying and responding to existential threats before they become irreversible crises.

## Relationship to the Party

The party **is** the Northwatch Wardens. Their standing with this faction is defined by how they uphold the Six Tenets, complete contracts, and support fellow members. NPC Wardens view the player characters as fellow members — trust is earned through demonstrated competence and honest reporting, not assumed.

## Season 1: Active and Watchful

The guild is fully operational and the players' home base. The Triad Council assigns contracts from the Waystone Board; each completed job builds reputation and unlocks higher-difficulty assignments. Marshal Thorne is quietly tracking anomalous patterns that she hasn't fully shared with the players. Mara provides logistical support and keeps an ear to underground trade channels. Elric offers research assistance and grows increasingly tense as the investigation deepens. Play the NPC members as a community — loyal but with private burdens that surface under pressure.

## Key Locations

- **Waystone Inn, Northreach** — Guildhall, safehouse, bunk, and mess hall; the social and operational heart of the campaign.
- **Elric's Library (Waystone upper floor)** — Research archive covering frontier anomalies, regional history, and Aevorian scholarship; access is restricted to trusted members.
- **Affiliated Settlements (Welton, Palebank, Pinebrook)** — Communities under Warden protection; each has local contacts and posted contracts.

## Secrets (DM Only)

- **Brenna Thorne** was born Brenna Ashcroft and served as a military intelligence officer in the south; she fled after uncovering deep corruption and changed her name. She still maintains encrypted correspondence with southern intelligence contacts and has been documenting Aevorian magical signatures in the frontier for years — the guild was partially founded *as a response mechanism* for an escalating threat she hasn't named aloud.
- **Mara Fenwick** was a guild accountant for a thieves' organization before fleeing north with embezzled funds, which she laundered through legitimate purchases to help establish the Wardens. She maintains coded contacts with underworld brokers and has been tracking the movement of Aevorian artifacts through black-market channels. Her old organization may still be looking for her.
- **Lorewarden Elric Vael** was expelled from the Cobalt Soul for pursuing forbidden research into Aevorian pre-Fall magical warfare. He came to Northreach because *the evidence is here*. He knows the current anomalies are Aevorian in origin, understands the implications better than anyone in the region, and has forbidden Cobalt Soul texts smuggled in his library. He suspects an entity that survived Aevor's fall is deliberately reactivating ancient systems — and he is terrified to say so before he has proof.
- **Baleth Cindermoon** (Lorewatch warlock) has a patron that is an Aevorian entity that survived the Great Fall in suspended animation — and that patron is the one causing the Aevorian Echo. Baleth is beginning to suspect this but is addicted to the power and bound by pact.
- **Takk Oaksplitter** (Lantern Guard) participated in a mercenary raid on an archaeological site that recovered Aevorian artifacts for an unknown client and resulted in the deaths of scholars. He now recognizes the artifacts surfacing in the campaign and is haunted by a dying researcher's last words.
- Multiple field members (Corel, Galvena, Ariodh, Guz, Shinebright, Father Merriksonn) each have direct prior exposure to Aevorian magic and carry trauma, knowledge, or hidden capabilities that will become critical as the campaign escalates.
