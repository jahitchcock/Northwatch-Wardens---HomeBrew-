## Summary

The Tide Callers' Guild is an informal maritime union linking sailors, fishers, and coastal traders along the Shattered Coast. Membership is claimed rather than conferred, and the network's value lies in shared weather intelligence, current charts, and crew placement rather than formal services. They are the difference between a seaworthy voyage and a desperate one.

## Leadership

No formal leadership. Respected senior sailors in major Shattered Coast ports serve as informal nodes — people others know to ask when they need reliable information or a trustworthy crew.

## Goals

1. Maintain the flow of reliable maritime intelligence across Shattered Coast ports so members can avoid dangerous conditions and identify profitable routes.
2. Keep the coastal trade lanes free from the kind of organized piracy that would force formal external authority onto the Guild's informal networks.

## Relationship to the Party

The Tide Callers have no particular view of the Northwatch Wardens unless the party operates near the Shattered Coast. If the party needs a ship, a crew, or information about coastal activity — including smuggling routes — informal Guild contacts are the fastest path. They respond to demonstrated competence and fair payment.

## Season 1: Background Resource

The Guild is a background asset in Season 1 — relevant if the campaign takes the party toward the coast, potentially significant if the Blackwake Corsairs' smuggling operations draw attention to Shattered Coast ports.

## Key Locations

- **Shattered Coast Port Towns** — The Guild's natural habitat; informal contact points in every significant harbor.
- **Tide Caller Waypoints** — Known meeting spots (usually specific dockside taverns) in major ports where members exchange information.

## Secrets (DM Only)

- Several Tide Caller members have been paid by Blackwake Corsair operatives to look the other way when specific Far North shipments pass through their ports. Most of them don't know what's in the cargo and don't want to. One senior sailor named Brix Orvallen knows more than he should and is quietly terrified of what he saw being loaded — crystalline blue-grey biological samples in sealed containers — and is waiting for someone trustworthy enough to tell.
