## Summary

The Stonebound Assembly is the governing cultural council of the dwarven communities in the deep places beneath Aevoria's mountains. Traditionalist, selective in their dealings with surface peoples, and deeply invested in the sacred resonance of the earth, the Assembly prioritizes preservation of dwarven culture and ancestral craft above all external relationships. They are not hostile — they are simply operating on a timeframe that makes most surface factions seem like seasonal inconveniences. Their geomantic expertise and master craftwork make them invaluable when they choose to engage, but earning that engagement requires patience and demonstrated respect for dwarven protocol.

## Leadership

- **High Anvil Morgran Ironheart** (dwarf) — Speaker for the Assembly's ruling council; sets the terms for all external engagement and personally approves any trade arrangements with non-dwarven parties.
- **Resonant Keeper Dhalia Deepdelver** (dwarf) — Guardian of sacred geomantic sites and keeper of the deep archives; her judgment determines which surface visitors may approach the Assembly's holy places.

## Goals

1. Monitor and respond to the unusual resonance disturbances being felt in the deep stone — the Aevorian Echo registers as a physical wrongness to geomantically sensitive dwarves, and the Assembly wants to understand its source before it gets closer.
2. Maintain the Assembly's independence from surface political entanglements while preserving access to trade relationships that support the deep communities' needs.

## Relationship to the Party

The Stonebound Assembly has limited contact with the Northwatch Wardens at campaign start. They are aware of the guild's existence as a responsible frontier operation — Steward Mara Fenwick has established trade relationships with dwarven intermediaries that create goodwill. Building a direct relationship requires the party to demonstrate respect for dwarven customs, approach through proper channels, and — most importantly — have something worth the Assembly's time. Once trust is established, they are loyal and generous; until then, they are politely unavailable.

## Season 1: Distant Watchers

The Assembly does not appear directly early in play. They are present as reputation — fine Stonebound metalwork shows up in frontier markets, dwarven traders pass through settlements, and Resonant Keeper Deepdelver's name comes up if the party asks about the strange vibrations being felt in the ground near certain Northreach locations. If the party investigates the deep origins of the Aevorian Echo, the trail will eventually lead toward dwarven territory and the Assembly's geological knowledge becomes necessary.

## Key Locations

- **Khardûn-Tharum, Stonebound Depths** — The Assembly's seat of government and cultural heart; the deep-mountain city that represents centuries of unbroken dwarven civilization.
- **Surface Trade Outposts** — Controlled points where dwarven intermediaries manage commerce with surface settlements; the public face of Assembly commerce.
- **Sacred Geomantic Sites** — Deep resonance points within the mountains where the Assembly maintains ancient wards; access is tightly controlled.

## Secrets (DM Only)

- Resonant Keeper Dhalia Deepdelver has been detecting Aevorian resonance signatures in the deep stone for months — the same frequencies that the Assembly's oldest archives describe as preceding the Great Fall. She has not shared the full implications with High Anvil Ironheart yet because she is not certain the Assembly can do anything about it, and she fears the knowledge would cause panic. She is quietly searching the deep archives for anything that describes how the dwarven communities survived the original Aevorian catastrophe.
- Khardûn-Tharum sits above what the dwarves call the "Voiceless Deep" — a sealed section of the mountain where their ancestors reportedly entombed something from the age of Aevor that they could not destroy. The seals are among the Assembly's most guarded secrets, maintained by each Resonant Keeper in succession. Deepdelver is the first Keeper in generations to feel them straining.
