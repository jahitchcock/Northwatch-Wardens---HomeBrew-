## Summary

The Unmasked Parliament is the current power structure of Vharoxis — a city that rejects traditional authority and operates instead on a brutal meritocracy of cunning, force, and adaptability. Parliament membership shifts as fortunes rise and fall, identities are routinely obscured, and the city itself functions as the continent's premier black market and center for dangerous innovation. To outsiders, Vharoxis looks like chaos; to those who understand it, the city runs on its own ruthless logic. The Parliament is simultaneously a city government and a criminal organization, and the distinction between the two is not a distinction Vharoxis recognizes.

## Leadership

Parliament membership changes frequently and identities are often concealed. Known public figures operate under titles rather than names:

- **The Architect** — Controls city development, infrastructure, and building permits; determines which operations get real estate and which get demolished.
- **The Scale** — Manages trade, markets, and taxation; sets the exchange rates that make Vharoxis's black market function with surprising efficiency.
- **The Shadow** — Rumored spymaster whose identity is unknown even to most Parliament members; intelligence gathering and enforcement of the Parliament's decisions against those who violate city protocols.

## Goals

1. Maintain Vharoxis as a sovereign entity free from external authority — Solaris Dominion, the Covenant, the Consortium — while expanding the city's influence through economic leverage and information control.
2. Consolidate control over the Far North artifact trade, which has become the city's most lucrative and least understood emerging market.

## Relationship to the Party

Vharoxis views the Northwatch Wardens with detached pragmatism. The guild is a frontier operation with no authority in the city, and the Parliament has no particular reason to engage with or oppose them — unless the Wardens begin investigating artifact smuggling routes that pass through Vharoxis's markets. At that point, the Parliament sends intermediaries rather than making threats; they prefer to offer deals before escalating to obstruction. The Wardens can operate in Vharoxis if they follow city protocols, but no outside laws protect them there.

## Season 1: Distant and Indirect

The Parliament does not appear directly in early play. Their presence is felt through the Blackwake Corsairs, who are contracted by (and partially dependent on) Vharoxis networks for artifact smuggling. References to "buyers in the south" and "Vharoxis intermediaries" in Corsair information hint at the city's involvement. If the party investigates the conspiracy's supply chain, the trail eventually points toward Vharoxis. Play the Parliament as powerful, amoral, and genuinely interested in their own survival — not evil for its own sake, but willing to tolerate great harm if it's profitable.

## Key Locations

- **Vharoxis City** — The Parliament's domain; every market stall, back alley, and public building is part of their operational territory.
- **The Mask Markets** — The city's central trading district where illegal goods move alongside legal ones without clear distinction.
- **Parliament Hall** — The formal meeting space where power is demonstrated and alliances are negotiated, though real decisions often happen elsewhere.

## Secrets (DM Only)

- The Shadow's identity is a Vharoxis-born information broker named Tessali who has independent connections to both the Blackwake Corsairs and the anonymous buyer driving the Aevorian artifact trade. Tessali does not know the buyer's full identity but has assembled enough pieces to be dangerous — and has been quietly selling fragments of that information to multiple interested parties, including a southern intelligence network.
- The Architect has been hired to construct something in the city's deep underbelly using specifications that match Aevorian design principles. The Architect doesn't recognize what they are building or why the geometry matters. The commission came through a chain of intermediaries that traces back, ultimately, to the same entity driving the Aevorian Echo.
- The Parliament as a whole is being used without knowing it. The artifact trade flowing through Vharoxis is not random commerce — it is a deliberately cultivated supply chain feeding a specific reactivation agenda. If the Parliament ever understood that their city was a cog in something that could threaten its existence, they would become desperate and unpredictable allies.
