# Faction Manifest

## Major Factions

- [The Northwatch Wardens](northwatch-wardens.md) — frontier protection guild, Season 1 core, player faction
- [The Solace Trade Consortium](solace-trade-consortium.md) — merchant guild, economic background power
- [The Covenant of the Silent Vigil](covenant-of-the-silent-vigil.md) — religious order, ally, anti-unaccountable-magic
- [The Freelance Brotherhood](freelance-brotherhood.md) — decentralized adventuring network, neutral competitor
- [The Archivists of Aevor](archivists-of-aevor.md) — scholarly society, Aevorian relic research, Palebank presence
- [The Unmasked Parliament](unmasked-parliament.md) — Vharoxis city authority and criminal organization, complex
- [The Stonebound Assembly](stonebound-assembly.md) — dwarven cultural council, Khardûn-Tharum, isolationist
- [The Silent Court](silent-court.md) — elven guardians of Nullwood Expanse, memory keepers
- [The Red Covenant](red-covenant.md) — mutual aid network, Emberlands survivors, ally

## Minor Factions

- [The Tide Callers' Guild](tide-callers-guild.md) — maritime union, Shattered Coast
- [The Horizon Wayfinders](horizon-wayfinders.md) — arctic explorer society, Far North
- [The Silver Chain](silver-chain.md) — slave liberation underground, unknown alignment
- [The Gleaners](gleaners.md) — rural farmer cooperative, Verdant Marches
