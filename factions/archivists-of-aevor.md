## Summary

The Archivists of Aevor are a secretive scholarly society dedicated to recovering, studying, and cataloging relics from the fallen civilization of Aevor. They operate from scattered libraries and research outposts — most notably the archive at Palebank Village — and are viewed with a mixture of respect for their knowledge and deep suspicion for what that knowledge might unlock. They pay premium prices for authenticated Aevorian artifacts and ask few questions about how those artifacts were acquired. Their research crosses directly into the territory the Covenant of the Silent Vigil considers dangerous, and their activities in Northreach are under closer scrutiny than they realize.

## Leadership

- **Loremaster Kethis Vraal** (elf) — Oversees research at the Palebank Archive; drives the scholarly agenda with obsessive focus and shares findings selectively even with fellow Archivists.
- **Seeker Elara Moonwhisper** (half-elf) — Field operations coordinator; manages artifact procurement contracts and manages relationships with the networks of freelance retrieval agents the Archivists rely on.

## Goals

1. Acquire and document Aevorian artifacts surfacing in the Northreach/Far North region before they are destroyed, lost to criminal networks, or suppressed by the Covenant.
2. Reconstruct enough of Aevor's pre-Fall magical methodology to understand how the civilization fell — and, the inner circle believes, potentially reverse-engineer its most significant techniques.

## Relationship to the Party

The Archivists see the Northwatch Wardens as useful — capable adventurers with access to frontier sites and a reputation for honest reporting. They will hire the party for artifact retrieval contracts, offer above-market prices for finds, and grant limited library access to members who bring them useful material. The relationship is transactional and the Archivists are not forthcoming about what they intend to do with what the party brings them. If the party realizes that some of the artifacts they've retrieved are ending up in dangerous hands, or that the Archivists' research is accelerating something they shouldn't, the relationship will become complicated.

## Season 1: Active in the Background

The Palebank Archive has staff quietly working through a significant recent acquisition of Aevorian materials. Loremaster Vraal is increasingly distracted by something in his research and less communicative than usual. Seeker Moonwhisper is expanding the contracts offered to frontier operatives, seeking "biological samples" and "crystalline-growth specimens" specifically. The Archivists do not understand that they are part of a larger conspiracy — they believe they are doing legitimate scholarship. Use them to deliver partial exposition about Aevor, to put artifacts in the party's path, and to show that knowledge without wisdom is its own kind of danger.

## Key Locations

- **Palebank Archive (Palebank Village)** — Primary research outpost in the region; restricted library access and a small staff of specialists; closest Archivist presence to the campaign's active sites.
- **Far North Research Cache** — Scattered supply and documentation points near suspected Aevorian ruin sites; maintained by field teams.
- **Major City Libraries (regional)** — Secondary Archivist presences in large settlements with significant historical collections; share findings selectively.

## Secrets (DM Only)

- Loremaster Kethis Vraal has been secretly corresponding with an anonymous buyer who has been directing specific artifact acquisition requests — always biological samples, always with blue crystalline growth. Vraal does not know who the buyer is and has been rationalizing the arrangement as "funded patronage." He is beginning to suspect the buyer has knowledge of Aevorian systems that exceeds the Archivists' own — and rather than alarm him, this makes him more eager to cooperate.
- Seeker Elara Moonwhisper independently suspects the artifacts she is acquiring for "the patron" are not going to a collector or museum. She has not yet acted on this suspicion because the funding is genuine and the research it enables is extraordinary. She is the more moral of the two leaders and could become a cautious ally if the party helps her see the full picture without destroying the institution she's dedicated her career to.
- The Palebank Archive contains a partial translation of an Aevorian operational text that describes a "reactivation sequence" for biological laboratories. The text is in restricted access. Lorewarden Elric Vael knows it exists; the Archivists do not know Elric knows.
