# Session Prep Output Format

Six sections, strict word budgets. This is a quick-reference tool — every word must earn its place.

---

## THE SITUATION (50–75 words)

Where the party is right now. What they know. What they don't know yet.

- Be concrete: location, last scene, immediate hook
- Omit backstory and worldbuilding the players won't surface this session

---

## 3 MOST LIKELY THINGS THAT HAPPEN (150–200 words)

Based on the adventure, predict the three most probable player actions. For each:

**[Action the players will probably take]:** How it plays out. One contingency if they go off-script.

---

## KEY NPCs THIS SESSION (100–125 words)

Only NPCs likely to appear. For each:

**Name** — Voice/mannerism in one phrase. What they know. What they want.

---

## ENCOUNTERS READY TO RUN (100–150 words)

Only encounters likely to happen this session. For each:

**Encounter name:** AC X, HP X. Key ability or tactic in one sentence. Scaling: [adjustment for 2–3 players] / [adjustment for 4–5 players].

---

## AEORIAN ECHO MOMENT (25–50 words)

One specific, concrete sensory detail hinting at the spreading phenomenon. Do not explain it.

*Example: "The innkeeper's cat hasn't blinked in three days. Its eyes track movement, but it no longer flinches at sound."*

---

## SESSION END HOOKS (50–75 words)

Two options for ending on a compelling beat regardless of pacing:

- **If the session ran fast:** [cliffhanger or revelation that works if they covered a lot of ground]
- **If the session ran slow:** [hook that rewards deliberate play and sets up next time]
