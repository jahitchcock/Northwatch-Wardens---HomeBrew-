## Tone: Flag These Patterns

### Emotion dictation (in read-aloud text)
- "you feel scared / uneasy / dread / hopeful"
- "you sense something is wrong"
- "you are overcome with"
- "you notice a feeling of"

### Generic fantasy clichÃ©s
- "ancient evil"
- "mystical power" / "mystical energies"
- "dark forces awakening"
- "eldritch energies" / "eldritch power"
- "arcane energy" (when used vaguely)
- "chosen one" / "destiny"

### Overwrought prose
- More than 3 adjectives modifying a single noun
- Sentences with 4+ adverbs

## Tone: What's Correct

- Specific, tactile sensory details ("the mud is ankle-deep, cold, and smells of rot")
- Understated menace ("the silence where birdsong should be")
- Observations without interpretation ("three crows sit on the fence post. None of them have moved.")
- Frontier realism: things are hard, scarce, and earned
