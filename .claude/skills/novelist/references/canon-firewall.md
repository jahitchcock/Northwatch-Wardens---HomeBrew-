# Canon Firewall — Quick Reference

The Old Songs of Aevoria series draws ONLY on native Aevoria deep-time elements. No current-campaign content.

## Allowed (Native Aevoria)

**Pantheon:** Radiant Queen, Stonefather, Verdant Mother, Flame-Mother, Ash-Father

**Magic traditions:** Spoken-memory (Nullwood), resonance/geomancy (dwarves), tide/storm (coastal/sunken peoples), fire-shaping (Emberlands)

**Cultures:** Nullwood elves, Tharundor dwarves, coastal/sunken peoples, Emberlands folk

**Geography (Book One):** Nullwood, Thistlebrook, Karrendeep, Greymarch, Haldren's Ford, The Thinning, The Flats, Stone's Rest, the wild miles, the Hollow Choir

**Geography (future books):** Tharundor (Book Three), sunken coastal kingdoms (Book Four), the whole world before the Sundering (Book Five)

**Threats:** Road-reavers, deep-beasts, territorial predators, ruin-scavengers

## Excluded Entirely

- **Aeor** and anything Aeorian
- **The Calamity**
- **Exandria** (the continent and the setting)
- **The dimensional rift / Echo**
- **Eclipse Day**
- **The cascade**
- **The Heart of Winter**
- **The White Maw**
- **Northwatch Wardens** (the guild, its members, its missions)
- **Any current campaign-era NPC, location, or organization**
- **Divinity's Beacon pantheon** (Pelor, Tyr, Moradin, Selûne, Lathander)

## Subtle / Edge-Case Violations

A passage can violate the firewall without using a proper name. These descriptive references are also excluded:

| Disguised reference | Excluded element | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| "the flying city" / "the city of mages that fell" | Aeor | Hard |
| "the day the sky cracked open" / "when the sky split" | Eclipse Day | Hard |
| "the war of the gods" / "when the gods fought" | The Calamity | Hard |
| "the icy heart beneath the peaks" | Heart of Winter | Hard |
| "the white beast that sleeps in winter" | White Maw | Hard |
| "the rift in the sky" / "the crack between worlds" | The Echo | Hard |
| "the adventurers' guild in the north" | Northwatch Wardens | Hard |
| "the inn at the crossroads" | Waystone Inn | Hard |
| "Pelor's blessing" / "by Tyr's hammer" | Divinity's Beacon pantheon | Hard |
| "I swear by the sun god" | Radiant Queen disguised as Pelor | Hard |

**Violation Severity:**
- **Hard:** Must be replaced before the chapter ships
- **Soft:** Descriptive reference to excluded element (e.g., "that old stone inn" when context implies Waystone Inn) — flag and replace
- **Edge:** Name coincidentally overlaps but has different in-world referent (e.g., "Stone's Rest" is fine — that's novel-specific geography). When in doubt, flag and ask

## Relationship to Campaign Canon

The novels are set in **deep-time / mythic-age Aevoria**, long before the current campaign era. The campaign's world is the *descendant* of the novels' world. When the novels reference something that also exists in the campaign (e.g., the Verdant Mother, the Memory-Keepers), the novel version is the *origin story* — the campaign version is the evolved institution.
