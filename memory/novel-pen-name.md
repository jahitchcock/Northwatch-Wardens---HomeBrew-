The novel series *The Old Songs of Aevoria* is published under the pen name **J. H. Thorne** (keeps the user's real initials, Joshua Hitchcock; "Thorne" echoes the in-world Thornwatch). Use this on all covers, title pages, and series-wide author credits.

Chosen 2026-06-27 while designing the Book One cover (*She Who Would Not Be Silent*). Final cover art and clean plate live in `Novels/01 Book One/Cover/`. Cover typography style: restrained classic engraved serif (Constantia), ivory text with soft shadow.
