## Overview

Deep beneath the northern ice, buried Aeorian ruins at **Salsvault** are leaking residual arcane energy — the *Aeorian Echo*. This energy doesn't announce itself dramatically; it seeps into the land slowly, awakening animals, destabilising old magic, and drawing in those ambitious or desperate enough to exploit it.

Season 1 is structured so that players can experience the adventures in any order. Each adventure plants a distinct clue about the Echo without revealing the full picture. The mystery accumulates across sessions, building toward the revelation that all the strange events in Northreach share a single, ancient source.

The arc has two layers: the **external threat** (the Echo spreading, factions exploiting it) and the **internal question** (will the Wardens hold together under pressure, and what kind of guild do the players want to be?).

## Consequences

### If Players Succeed (Seal or Destroy the Source)

- Northreach stabilises; animal attacks stop, spore sickness ends
- The Wardens gain significant reputation as protectors of the frontier
- Brenna Thorne's secret monitoring of the Echo becomes known; relationship with players shifts
- Salsvault is sealed — but Aeorian ruins exist elsewhere (Season 2 hook)

### If Players Fail or Divert

- The Echo intensifies; more settlements are affected
- The cult (or a successor faction) succeeds in harnessing Echo energy — power at great cost
- Northreach faces an existential threat that external powers (Solace Consortium, Solaris Dominion) begin to notice
- The guild's reputation suffers; recruitment and contracts dry up

## DM Notes

### Pacing Guidance

- **Early sessions:** Keep the Echo in the background — strange but explicable. Don't let players fixate on it before they've bonded with NPCs.
- **Mid-season:** Begin connecting dots. Let Elric Vael offer arcane analysis. Drop the name "Salsvault."
- **Late season:** The Echo should feel urgent. NPCs reference it unprompted. Brenna Thorne is visibly stressed.
- **Season finale:** The reveal should feel earned, not sudden. Players should be able to look back and see that all clues pointed here.

### Adaptations for Variable Attendance

- Each adventure is self-contained; missing a session doesn't break the arc.
- Any adventure can be the "first" clue — the revelation ladder doesn't require a fixed order.
- If a clue-planting adventure is skipped entirely, have Elric Vael summarise the relevant information as "rumours from the north."

### If Players Chase the Mystery Early

- Let them find fragments — spore samples, cult pamphlets, old maps — but keep Salsvault's full truth gated behind the Temple arc.
- Brenna Thorne will deflect direct questions: *"There are a lot of theories about what's happening. Let's focus on what we know."*
