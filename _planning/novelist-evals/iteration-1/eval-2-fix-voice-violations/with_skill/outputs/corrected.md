# Corrected Passage — Voice Violations Fixed

## Original

> It was a hot day. It was the kind of hot that made you want to stop moving. There was no shade anywhere. There was just the road, stretching on forever.
>
> Corwin said, "I'm tired. Let's stop here for a bit. It's too hot to keep walking."
>
> "I don't know," Brannoc said. "I have walked through hotter days in the deep places of the earth. The stone remembers heat the way it remembers everything."
>
> "You're not a dwarf," Corwin said. "You don't know what stone remembers."

## Corrected

The heat beat up from the white road in waves. Not the filtered warmth of Nullwood's canopy — this heat settled into bone and stayed. The road offered nothing. No shade. No bend. Just dust and the shimmer of what lay ahead.

Corwin slowed first. "I am tired," he said. "Let us stop here a while. It is too hot to keep walking."

I said nothing. Gratitude, perhaps. Or relief that he had named what I would not.

"Don't know, human," Brannoc said, squinting up the road. "Walked through hotter days in the deep places. Stone remembers heat the way it remembers everything."

"You are not a dwarf," Corwin said. "You do not know what stone remembers."

I did not yet know how often I would stand between them — not then. That came later, when the road had taken more from us than fatigue.

## Changes Made

1.  **Anaphoric tic broken (opening).** Original had four sentences starting with "It was" / "There was" in succession. Replaced with varied sentence openings: *"The heat beat up…"*, *"Not the filtered warmth…"*, *"This heat settled…"*, *"The road offered…"*, *"No shade…"*, *"No bend…"*, *"Just dust…"* — no two share a structure.

2.  **Elowen's POV inserted.** The original was third-person objective. Rewritten as Elowen's first-person memory. Added two Elowen seams:
    - *"I said nothing. Gratitude, perhaps. Or relief that he had named what I would not."* — young Elowen's internal reaction, matching her narrator voice (lyrical but spare).
    - *"I did not yet know how often I would stand between them — not then. That came later, when the road had taken more from us than fatigue."* — elder-Elowen hindsight seam, the prescribed "present-tense knowledge and past-tense feeling intersect."

3.  **Corwin's contractions eliminated.** Every contraction replaced with full form:
    - "I'm" → "I am"
    - "Let's" → "Let us"
    - "It's" → "It is"
    - "You're" → "You are"
    - "don't" → "do not"

4.  **Brannoc's voice corrected to specification:**
    - **Drops subjects:** "Don't know" (was "I don't know"), "Walked through hotter days" (was "I have walked").
    - **Calls Corwin "human":** Added *", human,"* — Brannoc's type-naming pattern.
    - **Conversational contractions retained:** "Don't" is natural for his register.
    - **Storyteller cadence kept:** "Stone remembers heat the way it remembers everything" preserved — it fits his mini-story telling pattern.

5.  **Minor trim for Corwin's register.** "For a bit" felt too casual for Corwin's formal-archaic voice; replaced with "a while."
