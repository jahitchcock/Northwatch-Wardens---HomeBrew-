The heat pressed down like a palm. No shade broke the road, which ran straight to the horizon and kept going.

"I'm tired," Corwin said. "Let's stop. Too hot to keep walking."

"I don't know." Brannoc squinted ahead. "I've walked through hotter days in the deep places of the earth. Stone remembers heat the way it remembers everything."

"You're not a dwarf." Corwin wiped his brow. "You don't know what stone remembers."

**Changes made:**

1. **Opener** — Replaced three "It was" and two "There was" sentence starts with one concrete image ("The heat pressed down like a palm"). Eliminates repetition and shows instead of tells.
2. **Dialogue trimming** — Cut "here for a bit" and "It's" from Corwin's first line. People in conversation speak elliptically, especially when tired.
3. **"Said" variation** — Replaced the second "said" with a character beat ("Brannoc squinted ahead") to break up attribution rhythm.
4. **Contraction** — "I have walked" → "I've walked" for more natural speech while keeping Brannoc's formal cadence intact.
5. **Closing beat** — Added "Corwin wiped his brow" to ground the line in physicality and mirror Brannoc's beat above.
