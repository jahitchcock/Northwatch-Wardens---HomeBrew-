## Arc Structure

**Arc 1: The Echo Resurgence** (Adventures 1–2)
The Echo stops being a rumor and becomes a presence. Players discover the observation post network, the twelve nodes, and the full history of Aevorian civilization. Tone: atmospheric dread, lore discovery.

**Arc 2: The Frostfire Clan** (Adventure 3)
The Echo has been doing things to *people* for a thousand years. The Frostfire Clan emerges as the campaign's most morally complex faction — victims and threats simultaneously. Tone: cold, isolated, politically heavy.

**Arc 3: The War** (Adventures 4–5)
All threads converge. Faction alliances form or fracture. The Command Center activates. Players decide what happens to Aevor's legacy and Northreach's future. Tone: epic, nostalgic, bittersweet.

---

## Recommended Order

Run in sequence. Adventures 4 and 5 both deal with the Command Center and AEON-1 — **do not run both**. Choose one as your finale:

- **Echo War** — For groups that want a multi-faction political climax with a hard-fought battle and three concrete endings (destroy, control, ascend).
- **Rise or Fall of Aevoria** — For groups that want the full emotional payoff with every Season 1+2 NPC present, four ending choices, and the clearest Season 3 setup.

If running a longer campaign, Echo War can serve as a mid-season climax (the Command Center first activation, partially defeated) with Rise or Fall of Aevoria as the true finale (Command Center fully surfaces).
