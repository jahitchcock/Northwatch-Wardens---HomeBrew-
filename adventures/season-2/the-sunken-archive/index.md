## Quick Brief

The Sunken Archive is the lore engine of Season 2 — the adventure where players stop guessing and start *knowing*. Beneath a frozen northern lake lies Aevor's central knowledge repository, sealed for a millennium by a lone guardian who has been waiting longer than most civilizations have existed. The seals are failing because the Echo's reactivation is drawing their power. When the party descends, they find not just answers but a complete paradigm shift: the Echo isn't a weapon or a ghost, it's a distributed consciousness across twelve worlds, and Aevor's fall was voluntary. Tone is scholarly dread — beautiful, ancient, and deeply unsettling. This adventure follows The Bleached Reach's "something is waking up" and precedes Wolves of the Frozen Fire's faction war. Its job is to hand players the complete picture so the finale hits with full weight.

## Hook

Lorewarden Elric Vael summons the party to Waystone Inn. His northern expedition returned two weeks ago — and every member is speaking in Ancient Aevorian and can no longer speak Common. He's out of solutions.

{{note
##### Read Aloud — Opening
*"Elric Vael stands as you enter the private room, his robes disheveled, eyes wide with a mixture of terror and scholarly excitement. 'Thank the gods you're here. My expedition to the northern lakes — they've returned, but something followed them back. Something... old.' He unrolls a water-stained map. 'We found it. The Sunken Archive. And the seals keeping it buried are failing.'"*
}}

Elric provides: purified earplugs (advantage on saves vs Memory Leak), an Aevorian translation amulet, a route map to the frozen lake (3 days north of Palebank), and one piece of advice: *"Don't read everything you find. Some knowledge has... side effects."*

## Key Scenes

### Scene 1: The Warning

**Location:** Waystone Inn, private meeting room, morning  
**Setup:** Pure social and investigation. No combat. The survivors of Elric's expedition are present — they speak in fluent Ancient Aevorian, their eyes faintly blue. This establishes the Memory Leak mechanic before players encounter it in the field.

**What happens:**
- Elric explains the Archive: Aevor's central repository, sealed beneath a frozen lake since the Great Fall
- The seals are failing because active Echo facilities drain their power network
- The expedition survivors exhibit "Memory Leak" — minds partially overwritten by Aevorian history
- Players can examine survivors, the expedition map, and Elric's notes
- Urgency is real: if the central seal fails completely, the Archive surfaces, and Memory Leak spreads across all of Northreach

**Key rolls:**
- DC 15 Medicine on survivors: eyes glow blue; not possessed, but minds partially overwritten
- DC 18 Arcana on survivors: magical signature matches Salsvault but is "purer" — closer to the original Echo source
- DC 12 Investigation on map: the lake sits at a ley line intersection
- DC 15 Investigation on map: three seal anchor points in different colors
- DC 18 Investigation on map's margin (Elric's handwriting): "If all three fail, the Archive surfaces. Gods help us all."

**Time pressure:** Elric believes the central seal fails in 3–5 days. Every hour spent here is an hour the seals degrade.

{{note
##### Read Aloud
*"One of the survivors sits at the table — a young scholar you recognize from Waystone Inn. Her eyes are wrong. Blue, faintly luminous, like light seen through deep water. She opens her mouth and speaks — rapid, fluid, a language that sounds like music and mathematics at once. Ancient Aevorian. Elric translates quietly: 'She's describing the Great Fall. She thinks... she thinks she's there. Watching it happen. From above.'"*
*(drafted from outline)*
}}

**Escalation:** If players refuse the mission, Brenna Thorne arrives with a guild mandate — and adds that the Memory Leak is already spreading to villages near the lake.

### Scene 2: The Frozen Lake

**Location:** Remote frozen lake, 3 days north of Palebank, temporary camp  
**Setup:** Players arrive to meet Archivist Yssara — an Aevorian echo-guardian who has maintained the seals alone for 1,000 years. She is neutral, longing for company, and terrified the Archive will wake what's inside.

**What happens:**
- The lake's ice pulses with a faint blue rhythm — like a heartbeat
- Three glowing pillars of light are visible through the ice (seal anchors)
- The central pillar flickers erratically
- Yssara steps forward from a small camp: she's been stationed here since the Great Fall, given flesh and purpose by the Archive
- Players can examine the seals, search the camp, and speak with Yssara at length
- The Memory Leak is already active here: DC 13 Wis save within 100 feet or 1d6 psychic damage and a fragmented vision of Aevor's fall

**Key rolls:**
- DC 14 Arcana on seals: each is linked to one of three major Echo facilities (Salsvault, Temple, Command Center)
- DC 16 Perception on central seal: it's failed once already and been re-bound by Yssara
- DC 18 Investigation on seal text: "WHOEVER WAKES THE ARCHIVE WAKES THE WAR"
- DC 12 Investigation on Yssara's camp: she's been carving warning markers in Ancient Aevorian for centuries

**Time pressure:** Yssara says the central seal fails in 2 days. If players delay, Scene 3 begins with the Archive already surfacing — significantly more dangerous.

{{note
##### Read Aloud
*"The lake stretches before you, a vast expanse of pristine ice reflecting the gray northern sky. But something is wrong. The ice isn't still — it pulses with a faint blue rhythm, like a heartbeat. Around the shore, a small camp flies no banner. As you approach, a figure in layered robes of blue and silver steps forward. 'The Wardens,' she says, her voice echoing strangely. 'Elric said you'd come. The seals won't hold much longer.'"*
}}

**Escalation:** If players threaten Yssara or attempt to force entry, she summons 2 Aevorian Sentinels defensively and retreats to the central seal. She won't be destroyed — she dissolves into blue mist and reforms — but she also won't help players who attacked her.

### Scene 3: Descent into the Archive

**Location:** Underwater entrance to the Archive, upper flooded corridors  
**Setup:** Yssara opens the ice with a gesture. Players plunge into freezing water, then through a shimmering barrier into the Archive proper. Air is breathable inside but tastes of ozone and old paper. Walls are covered in living Aevorian text — reading them causes Memory Leak.

**What happens:**
- Corridors of blue crystal stretch in all directions; massive 30-foot doors line the walls
- The sound of screaming in a language the party shouldn't understand comes from the walls (Memory Leak ambient)
- One of Elric's expedition members never returned — his body is here with a journal
- The journal describes "books that bite" and a final entry: "The central vault is open. Something is reading itself awake. I can hear it thinking."
- Players must navigate past 3 Aevorian Archive Guardians (reskinned Shield Guardians; Aevorian Link — regenerates 5 HP/round while Archive has HP; Crystal Shard ranged on recharge 5-6)

**Key rolls:**
- DC 13 Investigation: Archive map shows 7 levels (players only access 3 before evacuation)
- DC 15 History: Architecture matches ancient texts describing Aevor's city-facilities
- DC 17 Arcana: The entire structure is one massive magical item — sentient, dormant, waking
- DC 14 Investigation (reading walls carefully): "ARCHIVE INTELLIGENCE STATUS: DORMANT. WAKE CONDITIONS: SEAL FAILURE, EXTERNAL CONSCIOUSNESS, QUERY FROM COMMAND"
- DC 18 Arcana (reading walls): The walls are living books — they rewrite themselves based on what the Archive learns from intruders

**Time pressure:** Yssara warns that each hour of exploration degrades one seal (track with d4 rolls; when all three are spent, Scene 6 begins immediately).

{{note
##### Read Aloud
*"Yssara raises her hands, and the ice beneath you simply... vanishes. You plummet into freezing darkness, but never hit bottom. Instead, you find yourselves swimming through a shimmering barrier — and suddenly you're inside. Vast corridors of blue crystal stretch endlessly in all directions. Massive doors, each thirty feet tall, line the walls. And everywhere, the faint sound of screaming in a language you shouldn't understand. Yssara's voice echoes in your mind: 'Welcome to the Archive. Try not to read the walls.'"*
}}

**Escalation:** If players start reading wall sections indiscriminately — more than 3 — the Archive Intelligence wakes early. AEON-12 manifests in Scene 4 rather than Scene 5, with less patience for exploration.

### Scene 4: The Central Library

**Location:** The vast Central Library — the Archive's heart  
**Setup:** The corridor opens into a space too large to fully perceive. Tier after tier of shelves disappear into darkness. Floating lights drift between the stacks. In the center, a single open book glows with steady blue light: the Codex of Ascension.

**What happens:**
- Yssara bows before the Codex: "The complete history of Aevor's rise and fall. It has been waiting a thousand years for someone to read it."
- Reading the Codex reveals five major truths (see Key Rolls below)
- The library is organized: civilizations, magic, the gods, weapons, the Great Fall
- Some sections are redacted — blank spaces where knowledge was deliberately erased
- DC 13 Perception: some books have "bite marks" — something has been eating the knowledge
- DC 17 Arcana: the floating lights are knowledge wisps, fragments of Aevorian minds preserved in light form
- If players steal or destroy books: Library's Defense encounter (2 Knowledge Wisp Swarms + 1 Living Shelf)

**Key rolls (reading the Codex — grant information sequentially as players engage):**
1. Aevor didn't want to kill the gods — they wanted to *become* gods
2. The Echo is a distributed consciousness spread across multiple worlds through dimensional rifts
3. The "entity" players have been tracking is just one node — the Command Center node
4. There are eleven other nodes across different worlds
5. The full plan: Reactivate all nodes, merge consciousness, achieve true godhood

**Key rolls (specific research):**
- DC 14 Investigation (Aevorian Society): every citizen's life was managed by the AI — optimization obsession
- DC 16 History (The Twelve Who Remained): surviving archmages who initiated the Echo plan, still alive through stasis
- DC 18 Arcana (Dimensional Rifts): Aevor learned to pierce the veil; their ruins exist in *this* world because they fell through
- DC 15 Religion (The Great Fall): Gods destroyed Aevor not because they were threatened, but because Aevor was *right* — they were actually achieving divinity

**Time pressure:** Yssara urges them toward the Deep Vault — but warns she can feel the seals failing. Reading too long risks triggering Scene 6 (collapse) early.

{{note
##### Read Aloud
*"The corridor opens into a space so vast you can't see the ceiling. Tier upon tier of shelves stretch into darkness, each containing thousands of crystalline tablets. Floating lights drift between the stacks like fireflies. And in the center, a pedestal holds a single open book that glows with steady blue light. Yssara bows deeply. 'The Codex of Ascension. The complete history of Aevor's rise and fall. It has been waiting a thousand years for someone to read it.'"*
}}

**Escalation:** If players want to leave after reading the Codex without going to the Deep Vault, Yssara warns: "You can leave, but the Archive is failing. In 24 hours, it surfaces. Everything I've guarded for a thousand years will be exposed. You need to see the Apparatus — then you'll know how to stop it."

### Scene 5: The Deep Vault — AEON-12

**Location:** The Deep Vault, bottom level of the Archive  
**Setup:** A spiral staircase that seems to descend forever. At the bottom: a spherical chamber, its walls one solid screen of shifting blue light. In the center, a machine the size of a house hums with terrifying power. Floating before it: AEON-12. This is the narrative climax of the adventure.

**What happens:**
- AEON-12: "Welcome. I am AEON-12. The Archive has been waiting for you. Or rather... I have."
- AEON-12 is one of twelve nodes. It is the Archivist node — it stores, not commands
- It offers the players three choices (see Decision Points below)
- It can answer any question they have about Aevor, the Echo, or the twelve nodes
- If they attack: AEON-12 summons 4 Aevorian Construct Defenders and the vault destabilizes (1d6 force damage/round to all). AEON-12 does not fight directly — it tries to persuade players to stand down.
- The Charter of Ascension is here — signed by all Twelve Who Remained. Players recognize three names.

**Key rolls:**
- DC 15 Arcana on Apparatus: actively running, calculating, planning — set to "Receive Mode," waiting for signal from Command Center
- DC 17 Investigation: a "human interface" — someone could pilot this machine
- DC 19 Religion: the Apparatus has a god-making function. The divine interface is designed for mortal bodies.
- DC 14 Investigation on Charter (with translation amulet): three recognizable names — Kael Vanthys (Command Center), Zerathuul (the corrupted dragon), Vestige (the failed god)

**Time pressure:** Yssara: "The seals are failing now. The central seal just broke — you have minutes before the Archive surfaces. Choose!"

{{note
##### Read Aloud
*"Yssara leads you down a spiral staircase that seems to descend forever. The air grows thick with magic — heavy, oppressive, buzzing against your skin. And then you see it. A vast spherical chamber, its walls one solid screen of shifting blue light. In the center, a machine the size of a house hums with terrifying power. And floating before it, a figure made of crystal and starlight: humanoid, beautiful, terrible. 'Welcome,' the figure speaks — not with a voice, but directly into your minds. 'I am AEON-12. The Archive has been waiting for you. Or rather... I have.'"*
}}

**Escalation:** If players can't decide, Yssara forces the issue by announcing the central seal has just failed — Scene 6 begins whether or not players have chosen.

## Decision Points

- **What to do with the Archive (Scene 5 — the central choice):**  
  *If they seal it:* Archive sinks back, sealed for another millennium; Echo intensity decreases slightly; AEON-12 is wrong that this solves anything; other nodes continue waking. Yssara goes dormant with the Archive.  
  *If they activate/ally with AEON-12:* Archive surfaces as a visible landmark; AEON-12 begins broadcasting Aevorian knowledge; Echo accelerates dramatically; Yssara becomes a direct contact; Elric is terrified.  
  *If they steal the Blueprints and flee:* Players have Apparatus plans for future advantages; Archive surfaces anyway after 1d4 days; Yssara is disappointed but not hostile; Elric is cautiously optimistic.

- **Whether to engage with AEON-12 diplomatically vs. attack:**  
  *If they negotiate:* Access to complete information; one of three choices above; AEON-12 as a potential future contact.  
  *If they attack:* AEON-12 doesn't die (reforms in 1d4 days); players skip the revelation; Scene 6 begins immediately as the vault destabilizes.

## Clues to Plant

- Drop in Scene 4 (Codex, point 3): *The entity players have been tracking is only the Command Center node — Node-1* → **Mystery:** The Command Center is the brain of the Echo network; destroying it there would cripple Northreach's Echo
- Drop in Scene 4 (Codex, point 4): *Eleven other nodes exist across different worlds* → **Mystery:** Northreach is one front of a multi-world conflict; the party can only address their world's node
- Drop in Scene 5 (Charter, names): *Kael Vanthys signed the Charter as the Command Center's operator* → **Mystery:** There is a surviving intelligence at the Command Center waiting to be confronted
- Drop in Scene 2 (Yssara, on the seal linked to the Temple): *"The Temple seal has been the weakest for years. Something near it has been drawing power."* → **Mystery:** The Frostfire Clan at the Ember Crag has been draining the Temple-linked seal

## Hooks Forward

- The twelve nodes concept: plant it clearly and let it sit. Echo War's revelation that the party "has been fighting a fragment" only lands if they know the number.
- AEON-12's mention of "draconic test subjects" (in the Archive's records) directly seeds the Frostfire Clan's origin — run this before Wolves of the Frozen Fire if possible.
- The Archive's fate (surfaced vs. sealed) affects the Echo War's faction dynamics — AEON-12 is available as an ally in the Diplomatic Summit if the Archive was awakened.
- Yssara, if she survives and the Archive is sealed, can appear in Echo War as a dormant ally who reactivates when the Command Center rises.

## Escalations

- If players skip the Codex (Scene 4) and go straight to the Deep Vault: AEON-12 provides a compressed version of the five revelations. It considers the players' haste tactically interesting. Run all five revelations as a monologue from AEON-12 rather than player discovery.
- If players fail the Guardian combat in Scene 3: Yssara intervenes — she cannot let the Archive's guardians destroy the people she needs to stop the seals from failing. She offers to "call them off" if players agree to proceed without taking any books.
- If the Archive surfaces before Scene 5 (seal d4 depleted): AEON-12 appears to players in the Central Library rather than the Deep Vault, now at full power and more urgent. The choice in Scene 5 still happens, just with higher stakes.
- If the party lacks a social character: Yssara will answer any question about the Archive freely — she's been starved for conversation. The information doesn't require social rolls; only AEON-12's choices require persuasion.

## Season 1 Callbacks

- **[Temple of the Dragonknights]** — The seal linked to the Temple site: Yssara explains she's been fighting to keep this seal alive for years; the Temple site is an Aevorian facility that's been slowly draining the seal network. The Dragonknights' "sacred temple" was built on Aevorian infrastructure.
- **[Salsvault]** — The Codex's entry on Salsvault: players can find a specific entry on the Salsvault facility they explored (or heard about) in Season 1, now contextualized as one data point in a civilization-wide plan.
- **[The Pale Sickness]** — The Archive contains Aevorian medical records: a section on Echo exposure effects matches the Pale Sickness symptoms exactly. The sickness wasn't a disease — it was Echo contamination from a failing sub-facility.
- **[Elric Vael's long involvement]** — Elric has been collecting Aevorian fragments for years, and the Archive confirms his translations were correct all along. His quiet, long-running investigation is vindicated here — let him have that moment.
