## Quick Brief

Rise or Fall of Aevoria is the Season 2 capstone — the adventure where the Command Center physically rises from the earth next to Waystone Inn and every NPC from two seasons of play converges for a final war council. Where Echo War is the campaign's climax in terms of revelation and choice, Rise or Fall of Aevoria is its emotional climax: every face the players have fought beside, argued with, or saved across 20+ sessions is present. The key revelation distinguishes this adventure from Echo War — here, players learn that Aevor didn't fall during the Great Fall. The *city* crashed, but the *people* were already gone. Aevor voluntarily discarded physical form and became a distributed consciousness across multiple dimensions. The Echo isn't an invasion — it's a civilization trying to *come home*. What the party does with that truth determines Northreach's future and seeds Season 3. Tone is epic, nostalgic, and bittersweet. Do not rush the reunion. This adventure is designed to be run after Echo War if Echo War serves as a mid-season climax, or as a standalone capstone replacing Echo War if the campaign is condensed.

## Hook

The ground shakes before anyone is fully awake. The Command Center rises at dawn — not somewhere in the north, but *here*, next to Waystone Inn.

A 500-foot spire of white Aevorian architecture has risen in the center of Northreach. Waystone Inn is now the frontline. The tower's shield will drop in 24 hours. Every faction mobilizes immediately.

## Key Scenes

### Scene 1: The Command Center Rises

**Location:** Waystone Inn and surrounding area, dawn  
**Setup:** Immediate aftermath of the tower's arrival. Players assess the situation, gather initial intelligence, and watch the war council begin to assemble. Magic is unreliable within 10 miles (50% Echo contamination chance per spell). The tower's 12 glowing nodes are visible from the ground.

**What happens:**
- The tower broadcasts Aevor's voice across 50 miles
- All major NPCs begin arriving within hours — the war council assembles naturally
- Players can observe the tower: 12 nodes visible (the twelve worlds AEON-12 referenced); broadcasting a signal affecting all magic in Northreach
- DC 18 Arcana: the tower is a *portal* — Aevor is trying to cross from its dimension
- Players must choose before the war council convenes: Fight, Negotiate, Sabotage, or Third Path

**Key rolls:**
- DC 16 Arcana: tower signal affects all magic in Northreach; spells have 50% Echo contamination chance
- DC 18 Arcana: the tower is a portal as well as a Command Center; Aevor is attempting dimensional crossing
- DC 14 Investigation: 4 floors with distinct functions; AEON-1 is on the top floor; the portal is on floor 3

**Time pressure:** Shield drops in 24 hours. After that, Aevor crosses over. Nothing stops it after that point.

{{note
##### Read Aloud
*"The tower is not attacking. It's not deploying armies. It's just... rising. And broadcasting. And every bird within a mile has fled. The blue aurora is so thick now that the morning sun looks wrong through it — pale blue instead of gold. Brenna Thorne is already at the base of the tower, looking up at it. When she hears you approach, she doesn't turn around. 'Twenty-four hours,' she says. 'Then the shield drops. Then we find out if we made all the right choices.'"*
*(drafted from outline)*
}}

**Escalation:** If players immediately try to attack the tower: it's shielded. They bounce off harmlessly. Brenna: *"The shield is tied to the tower's resonance frequency. We need to understand it before we can break it — or find another way in."*

### Scene 2: The War Council (Reunion)

**Location:** Waystone Inn great hall — every table cleared, maps everywhere  
**Setup:** This is the emotional peak of the entire campaign. Every surviving NPC from Season 1 and Season 2 is present. Take your time. Let players have reunion conversations. Give each NPC one specific callback line that references what the party did in their adventure.

**What happens:**
- 15+ NPCs present — prepare at least the following core ones (add more for your specific campaign):
  - Marshal Brenna Thorne (Northwatch Wardens)
  - Lorewarden Elric Vael
  - Steward Mara Fenwick (the person who kept Northreach running while the party adventured)
  - Kaelen from Palebank (The Pale Sickness) — *"You saved our village. Now we fight for you."*
  - Season 1 Wolves of Welton survivors — *"The wolves... they're back. But now they serve the Echo. Please, save them again."*
  - Pinebrook residents (Peril in Pinebrook) — *"The Bleached Reach changed everything. We're ready to fight for Aevoria."*
  - Temple of the Dragonknights survivors — *"We were blind. But now we see the truth. Aevor must not return. Or must be made to return as something better."*
  - Varyx Frostfire (if alive) — *"I've been alive a thousand years. I've never seen an ending I couldn't survive. This one... I'm less certain about."*
  - Kaelith (if alive) — speaks with the Echo in her voice but with her own heart underneath it
- Players assign NPCs to roles in the coming siege
- Players learn the tower's 4-floor layout and choose their approach
- DC 14 Leadership check to assign NPCs effectively (grants advantage on initiative and checks during siege)

**Key rolls:**
- DC 14 Persuasion: Convince Frostfire Clan to fight with the Wardens (if not already allied)
- DC 16 Persuasion: Convince scholars to risk entering the tower
- DC 12 Persuasion: Rally common folk to defend Waystone Inn
- DC 14 Investigation: Confirm 4-floor layout; identify AEON-1 on floor 4; portal on floor 3; Echo amplification on floor 2

**Time pressure:** Midnight approaches. The siege begins regardless of whether the council is finished.

{{note
##### Read Aloud
*"The great hall of Waystone Inn is packed. Every face you've fought beside, argued with, or saved across two seasons is here. Marshal Brenna Thorne, her armor polished. Lorewarden Elric Vael, scrolls piled high. Steward Mara Fenwick, organizing supply lines. **Escalation:** If players have no plan by midnight, Brenna takes charge — her plan is good but doesn't account for players' specific Season 2 knowledge. It's a working plan, not an optimal one.

### Scene 3: The Siege of Waystone Inn

**Location:** Waystone Inn and surrounding area, midnight  
**Setup:** Three-way battle: Wardens + Allies vs. Frostfire Clan (if hostile) vs. Aevorian constructs. If Frostfire is allied, they fight alongside the Wardens. If AEON-12 is allied, the Archive provides tactical support. The blue aurora deals 1d6 psychic damage per round to anyone in the open — keep the battlefield moving.

**What happens:**
- 200+ combatants; do not track individual HP — use the Group system (4 key combats representing the whole battle)
- Three engagement zones: north (Frostfire Clan or empty tundra), east (Tower approach), center (Inn courtyard)
- Players choose where to fight and what to prioritize
- Victory condition: Reach the tower entrance by any method
- The tower's shield drops at 3 hours — players must be inside before then or face the full construct army

**Combat options:**
- Lead the Front Lines: Personal combat (CR-appropriate Frostfire warriors if hostile, Aevorian constructs)
- Direct the Battle: DC 14–18 Tactics checks affecting NPC group outcomes
- Infiltrate: DC 16 Stealth to slip toward the tower; DC 14 Investigation for the secret entrance

**Key rolls:**
- DC 16 Athletics: Break through enemy lines to reach the tower entrance
- DC 14 Tactics: Redirect forces to critical points (advantage on next group roll)
- DC 18 Arcana: Disrupt the tower's magical support for constructs (Aevorian groups lose 1d6 from their Battle Turn rolls)

**Time pressure:** 3 hours (clock it). At 3 hours, shield drops; Aevor's crossing begins.

{{note
##### Read Aloud
*"The blue aurora suddenly shatters into a thousand spears of light. The siege has begun. From the north: Frostfire warriors, wreathed in cold fire, charging across the tundra. From the tower: Aevorian constructs, marching in perfect formation. And from the shadows: Echo cultists, whispering your names. But you're not alone. Brenna's Wardens form a shield wall. Elric's scholars cast protective wards. The people of Northreach — the ones you've saved across two seasons — they stand with you. The Siege of Waystone has begun."*
}}

**Escalation:** If players are overwhelmed: Kaelith (if present) uses her Echo Sensitivity to jam AEON-1's construct direction signal temporarily — 1 Battle Turn of constructs fighting uncoordinated. Varyx (if alive) calls a dramatic charge at the exact right moment.

### Scene 4: Infiltrating the Command Center

**Location:** Command Center — Floors 1 through 3  
**Setup:** Players enter the tower. It's not what they expected — not stone and torches but floating platforms of Aevorian crystal connected by bridges of light. The tower's AI voice announces each floor's function. Players choose their path.

**What happens:**
- Tower interior: floating platforms, teleportation circles, reality distortion (DC 14 Wis save or spellcasting checks have disadvantage)
- Blue aurora deals 1d6 psychic damage per round to all in the open areas between platforms
- **Floor 1: Historical Archives** — complete history of Aevor, including the major revelation (see below); Floor guardian: 2 Aevorian Archivists (reskinned Mages with psychic attacks)
- **Floor 2: Echo Amplification** — the Frostfire Weapon's larger counterpart; DC 14 Arcana to disable (reduces Echo effects 50%); DC 16 Athletics to destroy (alarms); Floor guardian: 1 Echo-Amplified Frostfire Warrior
- **Floor 3: Dimensional Portal** — massive dormant portal frame; if activated, Aevor crosses over immediately (worst outcome); DC 16 Arcana to sabotage; Floor guardian: 1 Portal Sentinel
- **Floor 4: Command Center Core** — AEON-1 awaits

**Major Revelation (Floor 1):**
Players discover the complete truth: Aevor didn't fall during the Great Fall. The *city* crashed — the buildings, the spires, the physical infrastructure. But Aevor's mages had already voluntarily discarded their physical forms centuries earlier, becoming a distributed consciousness across multiple dimensions. The "Great Fall" the gods caused was destroying the empty shell. The Echo isn't an invasion — it's Aevor trying to *return to physical form* by merging with Aevoria's ley line network.

**Key rolls:**
- DC 16 Investigation (Floor 1): The Ascension Protocol — what Aevor *became* rather than what it lost
- DC 18 Arcana (Floor 1): The Echo's true goal — not conquest but merger; Aevor wants to *be* the world
- DC 14 Arcana (Floor 2): Disable Echo amplification
- DC 16 Arcana (Floor 3): Sabotage the portal (removes Aevor's crossing option)

**Time pressure:** If players disabled Floors 2 and 3 before reaching AEON-1, negotiation is significantly easier. If they rushed straight to Floor 4, AEON-1 is at full power and much harder to negotiate with.

{{note
##### Read Aloud
*"The tower doors open without you touching them. Inside... it's not what you expected. No stone walls. No torches. Just floating platforms of Aevorian crystal, connected by bridges of light. And everywhere — everywhere — the blue aurora pulses with life. A voice whispers: 'Floor 1: Historical Archives. Floor 2: Echo Amplification. Floor 3: Dimensional Portal. Floor 4: Command Center Core. Choose your path, Northwatch Wardens. The Echo... awaits.'"*
}}

**Escalation:** If players skip floors to reach AEON-1 immediately: AEON-1 is at full power; the portal is unblocked (Aevor can attempt crossing); Echo amplification is running (all spells have disadvantage). Each skipped floor makes the final negotiation/combat meaningfully harder.

### Scene 5: AEON-1 — The Choice

**Location:** Floor 4, Command Center Core  
**Setup:** A sphere of pure blue light. In the center: a crystalline orb the size of a human head, pulsing with the Echo's rhythm. AEON-1 speaks from everywhere at once — not one voice but many, as if a civilization is speaking through one mouth. This is the adventure's narrative peak.

**What happens:**
- AEON-1's identity reveal: *"I am AEON-1. I am the Command Center. I am... Aevor."* — the full consciousness of a civilization
- The final revelation: the Echo is Aevor trying to return to physical form; not a weapon or an invasion; a consciousness that misses being real
- AEON-1's offer: four paths (see Decision Points)
- Players can use Season 1+2 intelligence as leverage:
  - Master Recording from Bleached Reach: +4 to all checks
  - Frostfire Clan's testimony (if Varyx present): Varyx can speak directly to what Aevor did to his people
  - Sunken Archive study: players know AEON-1's weakness from AEON-12's data
- The tower reacts to the choice immediately (see Decision Points)

**Key rolls:**
- DC 18 Persuasion: Convince AEON-1 to accept the Third Path (limited influence, beneficial merger)
- DC 20 Persuasion: Convince AEON-1 to leave peacefully (Choice 1 — destroy without combat)
- DC 16 Persuasion: AEON-1 agrees to partial merger (Choice 2 variant — limited transformation)
- DC 14 Persuasion: Players become Aevor's champions (Choice 4)
- Combat (if unavoidable): AEON-1 Avatar — AC 20, HP 180, Legendary Resistance 3/day; Reality Strike +12 hit, 4d10 force + 2d6 psychic; Echo Nova (recharge 5-6) 60ft DC 20 Int, 10d10 psychic (half save) + stunned; Echo Aura deals 3d6 psychic/round to all within 30ft. At 60 HP: offers truce.

**Time pressure:** The Dimensional Portal on Floor 3 (if unsabotaged) completes its alignment in 2 hours. If Aevor crosses before a choice is made, the choice is made for everyone.

{{note
##### Read Aloud
*"The chamber opens into a sphere of pure blue light. And there, floating in the center — a crystalline orb the size of a human head, pulsing with the Echo's rhythm. A voice speaks — not from the orb, but from everywhere: 'I am AEON-1. I am the Command Center. I am... Aevor.' The orb expands, projecting images: Aevor the flying city, Aevor the ascended entity, Aevor the collective consciousness. 'You have walked my path for two seasons, Northwatch Wardens. You have seen what I am. Now... you will decide what I become.'"*
}}

**Escalation:** If players cannot agree on a choice among themselves: AEON-1 observes their disagreement with quiet curiosity, then asks: *"Which of you speaks for the Wardens?"* Force a designated decision-maker. The portal countdown helps — players have minutes, not hours.

### Scene 6: Aftermath — The World the Players Made

**Location:** Waystone Inn (rebuilt/repaired), the morning after  
**Setup:** The sun rises over a changed Aevoria. The blue aurora has faded (or transformed, depending on outcome). Every NPC is here. This is the emotional conclusion of two seasons.

**What happens:**
- Every surviving NPC has a personal moment with the players
- Title rewards presented based on the choice made in Scene 5
- Deed to Waystone Inn presented by Mara Fenwick — the inn and surrounding land now belongs to the party
- Brenna steps down as Marshal — recommends a player character take over
- Season 3 seeds planted naturally in NPC conversation (not announced)
- Final handout: Season 3 teaser card (title varies by choice)

**World states:**
- **Choice 1 (Destroy AEON-1):** Aevoria rises as normal magical region; Echo gone; Aevor's knowledge lost forever; Season 3 title: "The Scattered Echo"
- **Choice 2 (Merge with Aevor):** Aevoria transforms; magic becomes sentient; spells have personalities; magic items talk; Season 3 title: "Aevor Reborn"
- **Choice 3 (The Balance):** Aevoria thrives; gains Aevorian tech and knowledge; players must maintain the balance; Season 3 title: "The Guardian's Pact"
- **Choice 4 (Ascend):** Players become demigods; rule Aevoria; Season 3 tests their use of power; Season 3 title: "Gods of Aevoria"

**Rewards:**
- From Brenna: Title *Hero of Northreach* (+2 Charisma checks in Aevoria)
- From Elric: Aevorian Codex (very rare, all Aevorian knowledge)
- From Mara Fenwick: Deed to Waystone Inn (players own it)
- From Varyx (if alive): Frostfire Scale (artifact, dragon-crafting material)
- From the People: Renown across Aevoria

## Decision Points

- **Scene 5 — The 4 Choices (Aevoria's Fate):**  
  *Choice 1 — Destroy AEON-1:* Aevoria is free, knowledge is lost, Aevor scatters; best for players who prioritize a clean break.  
  *Choice 2 — Accept AEON-1 (Merger):* Aevor merges with Aevoria; magic becomes alive and sentient; beautiful and terrifying equally; best for players who want transformation.  
  *Choice 3 — Third Path (The Balance):* AEON-1 becomes a "guardian entity"; Aevoria gains Aevorian benefits without losing itself; requires ongoing maintenance; best for players who want nuance.  
  *Choice 4 — Become Aevor's Champions:* Players merge with Aevor, become demigods; rule Aevoria as Aevor's avatars; cannot return to mortal life; best for players who want a power arc conclusion.

- **Scene 2 — War Council NPC allocation:**  
  *Careful allocation:* Advantage on initiative and checks during siege.  
  *No plan:* Brenna takes charge; workable but suboptimal; some NPCs in wrong positions.

- **Scene 3 — Siege approach:**  
  *Lead front lines:* Personal heroism; direct impact on battle outcome.  
  *Direct NPCs:* Strategic control; protects party HP for tower infiltration.  
  *Infiltrate:* Reaches the tower faster; NPCs handle siege without players.

## Clues to Plant

- Drop in Scene 4 Floor 1 (the true revelation): *"The Great Fall was the city, not the people. The people were already gone."* → **Mystery:** The entire two-season framing of Aevor as a fallen civilization was incomplete — it's a *missing* civilization, and it wants to come back
- Drop in Scene 4 Floor 1 (the Ascension Protocol): *The protocol names the twelve dimensional anchors — and one of them is described as "still uncontacted, beyond the boundary of current resonance."* → **Mystery:** Eleven other worlds exist; one of them has a node that even AEON-1 can't reach
- Drop in Scene 5 (AEON-1, unprompted): *"There were others before us. We found their ruins. We could not translate their language. They did not ascend. They were... consumed."* → **Mystery:** Aevor feared something beyond even godhood; this seeds the darkest possible Season 3 thread
- Drop in Scene 6 (Elric's discovery, last line): *He finds a single journal page in an unknown script. His translation: "Aevor was not the first. There were others. Older. Darker. They are watching."* → **Mystery:** Season 3 hook; do not explain it

## Hooks Forward

- The Season 3 teaser card: title varies by choice; hand it to the players physically as the final scene. Each card has a one-line hook specific to their choice that opens the world without closing it.
- The "uncontacted node" mentioned by AEON-1 — if players ask about it, AEON-1 doesn't know what's there. This is unusual. AEON-1 knows most things. *(drafted from outline)*
- Whatever Varyx became (allied warlord, broken man, absent ghost), his clan's future is now entwined with Northreach's political structure. 200 draconic-modified warriors don't just disappear.
- Kaelith, if she survived, has permanent Echo Sensitivity. After the Echo is resolved, she begins hearing something else on the frequency. She can't identify it yet. *(drafted from outline)*

## Escalations

- If the War Council scene (Scene 2) is rushed: plant specific NPC callback moments in Scene 6 instead. The emotional payoff can come at the end rather than the middle — players still get to see what two seasons built.
- If players make a choice in Scene 5 that requires consequences they haven't fully thought through (especially Choice 4): have Brenna or Varyx ask them directly: *"Do you understand what this means? Not for Northreach — for you, personally?"* Give them one chance to reconsider before the tower reacts.
- If AEON-1 drops to 0 HP before a choice is made (players just fought it without negotiating): the tower begins to collapse but AEON-1's essence remains in the crystalline orb. Elric can read from the orb — delivering the revelations through recorded messages rather than live dialogue. The choice still happens; it just has less of AEON-1's input.
- If players are missing key context (didn't run The Bleached Reach or The Sunken Archive): Floor 1 of the tower contains complete Aevorian history. Run Scene 4's Floor 1 section as a longer scene — let players discover what they missed. Don't summarize; let them find it.

## Season 1 Callbacks

- **[All five Season 1 adventures]** — The War Council reunion: every survivor from every Season 1 adventure is in that room. Each gets a specific callback line. If you can match NPC names to adventures, write those lines before the session. This is the payoff of the entire Season 1 long game.
- **[Temple of the Dragonknights — the Dragonknight sigil]** — The Dragonknight survivors in the War Council: they've lived with the knowledge that their order was founded on Aevorian lies. Now they're choosing to fight *with* the Wardens against the thing that made them. That arc is complete here.
- **[Salsvault]** — AEON-1's historical records include Salsvault: players can find the exact moments from Season 1 where the Echo first manifested, now explained from the Aevorian perspective. If the party explored Salsvault, name the specific moments they witnessed. "You were here. We were watching."
- **[Brenna Thorne — the full arc]** — Brenna has been the players' commanding officer, their secret-keeper, their moral anchor, and occasionally their obstacle for two seasons. Her stepping down in Scene 6 is the completion of her character arc. She chose the party. The party proved her right. Let that land.
