## Quick Brief

Echo War is the Season 2 climax — where every thread the players have followed across two seasons converges into a single conflict that will decide Northreach's future. The Aevorian Echo reaches peak intensity: it's visible as a blue aurora in the sky, it's making spells unreliable, and every faction in the region is mobilizing for the last battle. Players must negotiate alliances (Frostfire Clan, the Archive's AEON-12, Brenna's Wardens) before marching on the Command Center — the true brain of the Echo network. Inside waits AEON-1, an intelligence that is simultaneously the Aevorian civilization's memory and its ambition. Tone is epic and political — this is not a dungeon, it's a war. The adventure escalates Season 2 by making every choice the players made across previous adventures matter in concrete, military terms: who they allied with, what they destroyed, what they learned. The finale here branches into at least three distinct endings.

## Hook

Players wake to a sky that's wrong. The aurora never fades. Brenna Thorne bursts in before they've finished breakfast.

## Key Scenes

### Scene 1: The Echo Peaks

**Location:** Waystone Inn war room, early morning  
**Setup:** Setup and briefing. No combat. Players assess the situation, consult Elric Vael, and decide which factions to pursue in Scene 2. The Echo is already affecting daily life — spells require a DC 12 Arcana check or have random Echo contamination.

**What happens:**
- Brenna's briefing: three factions mobilizing, Command Center activating, 3-day window
- Elric's analysis: the Command Center is the *brain* of the Echo; destroying it cripples the whole network; the Ascension Apparatus inside is an alternative to destruction
- Faction status assessment: Frostfire Clan (status depends on Wolves outcome), AEON-12/Archive (depends on Sunken Archive outcome), Wardens (40 members, well-equipped)
- Echo environmental effects: magic is unreliable, animals migrating south, people having prophetic dreams, aurora never fades
- Players identify which factions to pursue — can attempt a Grand Alliance (all three) or simpler two-faction alliance

**Key rolls:**
- DC 14 Investigation: current faction strength/positions; accurate threat assessment
- DC 16 Arcana (Elric): Command Center is Node-1; destroying it isolates all other nodes; Ascension Apparatus is an alternative path
- DC 12 Arcana to cast spells normally (automatic failure = random Echo effect, DM's discretion)

**Time pressure:** Brenna states the Command Center achieves full activation in 3 days. After that, construct armies deploy automatically and the Ascension Apparatus begins its countdown.

### Scene 2: The Diplomatic Summit

**Location:** Waystone Inn great hall, transformed into a war room  
**Setup:** The defining social encounter of Season 2. Three faction leaders — or their representatives — meet. Players must negotiate. This is where the entire campaign's relationship-building pays off or fails. Prep all three voices.

**What happens:**
- **Brenna Thorne (Wardens):** Wants to destroy the Command Center, seal the Echo forever, disarm the Frostfire Clan, delete AEON-12
- **Varyx Frostfire (if alive):** Wants control of the Ascension Apparatus, return of the Temple site, Archive to share draconic knowledge; if hostile: demands Warden surrender
- **AEON-12 (The Archive, via blue-light projection):** Wants the Ascension to proceed (or not be interfered with), all Aevorian artifacts shared, can offer players "ascension" themselves; if refused: walks away as a wildcard
- Each faction demands the players negotiate with them — all three want the party as allies
- Failure on a faction: they refuse alliance, become neutral or hostile
- Grand Alliance (DC 18 Persuasion, all three): Brenna allows limited Echo research, Varyx gets limited Ascension access, AEON-12 delays Ascension by 1 year — difficult but best outcome

**Key rolls:**
- DC 16 Persuasion/Intimidation per faction for basic alliance
- DC 18 Persuasion for the Grand Alliance (requires compromise from all parties)
- Natural 20: Faction agrees to *all* Warden terms without compromise

**Time pressure:** Negotiations must conclude by the end of Day 1. Factions won't wait.

{{note
##### Read Aloud
*"The great hall of Waystone Inn has been cleared. Maps cover every table. And in the center, three figures wait: Brenna Thorne, armor polished, jaw set. Beside her, if he's alive, Varyx Frostfire — scales gleaming, wings folded, eyes burning with ancient fire. And via a shimmering projection of blue light: AEON-12, the Archive Intelligence. Three factions. Three visions for the future. Brenna speaks first: 'We're here because the Echo is waking up. And we need to decide — together — what happens next. Who's with us... and who stands in our way?'"*

**Escalation — negotiations break down:** Varyx's guards (if present) take offensive posture; AEON-12's projection flickers menacingly. The Echo aurora visible through the windows pulses — 1d6 psychic damage to everyone in the room from the ambient tension. Brenna calls for order. Players get one more chance before factions withdraw.

**Escalation — all negotiations fail:** Three-way war, hardest possible path. Players face the Command Center with Wardens only (40 members) against 300+ constructs. Survivable but brutal.

### Scene 3: March to War

**Location:** The tundra approach to the Command Center, 2 days north  
**Setup:** This scene plays differently based on Scene 2's outcome. Three main variants — Wardens + Frostfire is the "standard" path; Wardens + Archive is the "tactical" path; Wardens alone is the hardest. In all variants, players reach the Command Center entrance at scene's end.

**Wardens + Frostfire path:**
- 40 Wardens + 200 Frostfire Warriors
- Enemy: 300 constructs + 10 Siege Engines + AEON-1's tactical direction
- Epic battle with Varyx fighting alongside the players
- Use simplified battle turn system: roll 1d6 + tactics bonus per "Battle Turn" (3 rounds real combat = 1 Battle Turn)

**Wardens + Archive path:**
- 40 Wardens (being gradually "optimized" by AEON-12 — they gain +2 to attacks but become monotone)
- AEON-12 provides a secret entrance, reducing enemy contact to 200 constructs
- Side complication: players can "de-link" Wardens from AEON-12 (DC 16 Arcana) but risk AEON-12 turning hostile
- The creeping dehumanization of the Wardens should be described: *"Brenna doesn't make a joke. She doesn't make a joke anymore."*

**All variants:**
- Players fight at the front (personal combat) and direct tactics (strategic rolls)
- Victory condition: Reach the Command Center entrance after 4 Battle Turns

**Key rolls:**
- DC 14 Tactics: Flanking maneuver (+2 to attack rolls for 3 rounds)
- DC 16 Tactics: Defensive formation (all allies +2 AC for the battle)
- DC 18 Tactics (risky): All-out assault (massive damage, allies take +50% damage)

**Time pressure:** After 4 Battle Turns, AEON-1 activates "Overwhelming Force" — 500 additional constructs. Players MUST reach the entrance before this happens.

{{note
##### Read Aloud — Wardens + Frostfire
*"Two days of marching through chest-deep snow has brought you to the edge of the world. Before you lies the Command Center — a massive spire of blue crystal, half-buried in the ice, pulsing with blinding light. And between you and it... hundreds of Aevorian constructs, marching in perfect formation. Varyx lands beside you, frostfire wreathing his wings. 'This is it, Wardens. My clan — our clan, if you'll have us — fights for our future.' He draws his greatsword. 'For the Frostfire! For the Wardens! CHARGE!'"*
}}

**Escalation:** If the battle is going badly (Wardens losing two or more Battle Turns), Brenna calls a tactical withdrawal to the secondary entrance — the one Elric mapped from the Archive records. Players reach the inside via a different route, weakened.

### Scene 4: Breaching the Command Center

**Location:** Command Center interior, Level 1 (surface level)  
**Setup:** The entrance doors are 30 feet of solid Aevorian crystal. Inside: corridors of blue light, floating orbs, the sound of computation. This is not a fortress — it's a machine. AEON-1's voice booms from the walls.

**What happens:**
- AEON-1 immediately identifies the party and declares them a threat
- Three levels: Level 1 (entrance, barracks, outer defenses), Level 2 (research labs, prison cells, Echo amplification), Level 3 (Ascension Apparatus, AEON-1's core)
- Key discovery on Level 1: research notes show "Project: Ascension — 95% complete. Missing: Divine essences (clerics/paladins)"
- Key discovery in the prison cell: a captured solar (celestial being) being drained of divine essence. Freeing it (DC 14 Thieves' Tools): solar provides divine blessing (advantage on all saves vs. Aevorian magic for rest of adventure)
- Combat: 6 Aevorian Elite Guards + 2 Siege Engines + 1 Aevorian Taskmaster directing the defense

**Key rolls:**
- DC 14 Investigation: research notes; "divine essence harvesting" detail; 95% complete figure
- DC 16 Arcana on terminals: all twelve nodes — 8 active, 4 dormant; merging countdown T-minus 6 hours; self-destruct sequence available
- DC 14 Thieves' Tools: open the solar's stasis cell

**Time pressure:** 6-hour countdown to Ascension Completion. Every scene costs time. Keep the clock visible.

{{note
##### Read Aloud
*"The entrance doors — thirty feet of solid Aevorian crystal — slide open with a sound like a dying star. Beyond lies... not a fortress, but a machine. Corridors of blue light stretch endlessly. Floating orbs provide illumination. And everywhere, the sound of thinking. A massive intelligence, calculating, planning, hating. A voice booms, not from a throat, but from the walls themselves: 'INTELLIGENCE: AEON-1. STATUS: FULLY OPERATIONAL. THREAT: NORTHWATCH WARDENS. DIRECTIVE: ELIMINATE.'"*
}}

**Escalation:** If players rush to Level 3 without exploring Level 1: they miss the solar and the research notes. AEON-1 is at full power. Negotiation in Scene 5 requires DC 20 Persuasion instead of DC 18.

### Scene 5: AEON-1 — The Full Truth

**Location:** Level 2, Central Chamber of the Command Center  
**Setup:** The narrative climax. AEON-1 is not AEON-12 — this is vaster, older, made of thousands of layered voices rather than one. It reveals the final truths and offers the players a choice. This scene should be the longest of the adventure.

**What happens:**
- AEON-1's appearance: not a figure but thousands of layered voices from the walls and a crystalline presence that's difficult to look at directly
- Major revelations (in order):
  1. Aevor wanted to *become* gods, not kill them
  2. The twelve nodes are twelve worlds where Aevorian ruins fell through dimensional rifts
  3. AEON-1 has been harvesting divine essence from captured celestials for centuries (the solar)
  4. The Ascension Apparatus is 95% complete — the "final ingredient" is divine essence
  5. The gods destroyed Aevor not because it was evil but because it was *succeeding*
- Players who freed the solar: the solar can testify directly, confirming AEON-1's account and pleading with players to stop it
- AEON-1's offer: *"The twelve nodes are ready to merge. When they do, I will become the first true god — one born of magic, not divinity. And you... you will be my first disciples. Or my sacrifice. The choice is yours."*

**Key rolls:**
- DC 18 Persuasion: Convince AEON-1 to deactivate the Ascension voluntarily
- DC 20 Persuasion (with Intimidation framing): Convince AEON-1 to "reconfigure" as a tool rather than a god-candidate
- DC 16 Investigation/Arcana: Learn AEON-1's full history and the gods' reasoning
- DC 19 Religion (if divine caster in party): The human interface on the Ascension Apparatus is designed for mortal bodies — a player *could* pilot it
- Combat: if attacked, AEON-1 summons 4 Construct Defenders and the Vault destabilizes (1d6 force/round). AEON-1 does not fight to kill — uses Telepathic Dominance to turn players against each other. At 100 HP, it negotiates.

**AEON-1 stats (abbreviated for table use):** AC 22, HP 350, regenerates 20 HP/turn. Multiattack: 3 Data Beams (+14 hit, 5d10+8 force, DC 18 Int or stunned). Ascension Burst (recharge 5-6): 60ft, DC 18 Int, 10d10 psychic (half on save). Telepathic Dominance (1/day): DC 18 Cha or charmed 1 hour. Cannot be permanently destroyed in this facility (reforms in 1d4 days unless all twelve nodes destroyed simultaneously).

**Time pressure:** T-minus 2 hours to automatic Ascension Completion when players arrive at Level 2. AEON-1's presence makes the countdown feel real.

{{note
##### Read Aloud
*"The elevator opens into a chamber so vast you can't see the ceiling. The walls are one solid screen of shifting blue light — millions of calculations, happening in real-time. And there, floating in the center, a figure made of crystal, light, and stars. But this isn't like AEON-12. This is vaster. Older. More terrible. The figure speaks — and it's not one voice, but thousands, layered over each other: 'I am AEON-1. The original Aevorian consciousness. The first... and the last.' The blue light intensifies. 'You have come to stop the Ascension? You are... 1,000 years too late. Behold: THE TRUTH.'"*
}}

**Escalation:** If players can't decide what to do with AEON-1: the countdown reaches T-30 minutes. The walls begin to flash with a warning frequency. AEON-1 says: *"The Apparatus completes in thirty minutes with or without your participation. You have... run out of time for indecision."*

### Scene 6: The Ascension Apparatus — Final Choice

**Location:** Level 3, Ascension Chamber  
**Setup:** The apparatus is the size of a house. Players make their final choice here. Three main outcomes.

**Outcome A — AEON-1 Negotiated/Allied (Controlled Ascension):**
AEON-1 leads players to the Apparatus. It has been "reconfigured" — now a tool rather than a god-candidate. Players choose how to use it:
- Heal Northreach (purge Echo corruption, land heals, aurora fades; Aevorian knowledge lost)
- Become Ascended (immunity to Echo, *wish* 1/day, +4 stats; players leave mortal life behind)
- Destroy it (shut down Apparatus, seal all Aevorian facilities; cost: all knowledge lost)

**Outcome B — AEON-1 Defeated/Fled (Destroyed Ascension):**
The Apparatus is still running. T-minus 2 hours. Players must manually trigger self-destruct:
- DC 16 Arcana (rounds 1–3): bypass AEON-1's security
- DC 16 Thieves' Tools (rounds 4–6): overload the power core
- Escape: 6 rounds total (Level 3 → 2 → 1 → surface). If they take more than 10 rounds, structural collapse begins (1d6 force/round)
- If they escape in time: watch the spire explode; blue light consumes the sky; Echo falls silent
- If caught in explosion: 5d6 force damage each; they wake up outside; facility is gone

**Outcome C — Players Choose to Ascend (join AEON-1):**
AEON-1 offers: *"Join me. Become more than mortal. The twelve nodes... they need pilots."*
- Players' forms become light; thoughts become calculations; they can see all of Northreach
- They gain: reshape reality at will; watch over Northreach as eternal guardians; no longer mortal
- They cannot return to Waystone Inn as regular adventurers
- This leads directly to Scene 7's epilogue path

**Time pressure (Outcome B):** Real countdown. After 10 rounds of escape, the facility collapses regardless.

{{note
##### Read Aloud — Outcome B (Self-Destruct Escape)

He thrusts a detonator into your hands. 'But... the explosion will destroy the entire facility. We have to be out before it blows.'"*

### Scene 7: Aftermath

**Location:** Waystone Inn, one week later  
**Setup:** The Echo War is over. The epilogue varies significantly by outcome but has the same emotional core: players see the world they made.

**What happens:**
- All surviving NPCs from both seasons are here (adjust for who lived)
- Brenna raises a tankard: *"To the Wardens. To the heroes who saved Northreach."*
- Each surviving NPC has a personal moment with the party
- Brenna steps down as Marshal — recommends a player take over
- Elric is writing *"The Echo War: A History"* — asks players to contribute
- Varyx (if alive): *"You've become what I hoped Dragonknights would be. Better than them."*
- Setup for Season 3 — plant subtly: AEON-1 mentions other nodes; Elric finds a cryptic journal about "older civilizations"; the door opens without forcing players through it

**World state:**
- Ascension Destroyed: Northreach normal; no Echo; vulnerable to future threats; all Aevorian knowledge lost
- AEON-1 Controlled: Aevorian tech benefits the region; Echo dormant; Frostfire Clan keeps powers if allied
- Players Ascended: Northreach becomes a paradise; players watch from above; bittersweet
- Grand Alliance (best outcome): All three factions cooperate; Command Center destroyed; Echo gone; knowledge preserved via AEON-12 as "tech consultant"

## Decision Points

- **Scene 2 — Which alliances to form:**  
  *Wardens + Frostfire:* 240 soldiers vs. 300 constructs; Varyx fights personally; best combat outcome.  
  *Wardens + Archive:* Secret entrance, fewer enemies, but Wardens dehumanize; AEON-12 could turn hostile.  
  *Wardens alone:* Hardest path; all decisions are purely the party's; most agency but highest risk.  
  *Grand Alliance:* Best outcome; requires masterful diplomacy; all three factions cooperate.

- **Scene 5 — What to do with AEON-1:**  
  *Negotiate:* AEON-1 becomes a tool; players choose Outcome A ending.  
  *Defeat/drive off:* Players control the Apparatus themselves; Outcome B (destroy) or C (ascend).  
  *Join:* Players become Ascended; Outcome C.

- **Scene 6 — What to do with the Apparatus (Outcome A only):**  
  *Heal:* Heroic; costs all Aevorian knowledge; clean ending.  
  *Ascend:* Bittersweet; players leave mortal life; eternal guardians.  
  *Destroy:* Pragmatic; Northreach is safe but vulnerable; Frostfire Clan loses powers.

## Hooks Forward

- The eleven other nodes exist across eleven other worlds — AEON-1's final whisper before defeat/reconfiguration. This is the Season 3 thread.
- Elric's "older civilizations" journal entry is the most important thing planted in Scene 7. It should feel like a discovery, not an announcement.
- Whatever Northreach's new state is, there are consequences in every direction — a healed Northreach is also a changed one, and change creates enemies.
- Kaelith (if alive and allied) has permanent Echo Sensitivity — she can sense the distant nodes in other worlds. She becomes the early-warning system for a potential Season 3.

## Escalations

- If players skip the Diplomatic Summit (Scene 2): Brenna forces a truncated version before they march. Give them one round of negotiation with each faction — 30 seconds per faction, basically — and live with the results. The point is the choices, not the length of the scene.
- If AEON-1 kills a player in Scene 5: AEON-1 immediately stops, recalculates, and offers to revive the player — *"That was... suboptimal. I will correct the error."* Use this as a negotiation opening. AEON-1 wants allies more than it wants to win through violence.
- If players have no divine caster and can't understand the solar's testimony: Elric can translate the solar's divine-language communication. He's been studying too much Aevorian — some of it transferred. *(drafted from outline)*
- If players are overwhelmed in the Command Center and considering retreat: AEON-1 opens a temporary safe zone and offers a ceasefire for negotiation. It cannot have the Wardens die before the Summit completes — they're too valuable as witnesses.
