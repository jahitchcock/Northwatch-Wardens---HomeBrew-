## Quick Brief

The Bleached Reach opens Season 2 by making the Echo impossible to ignore. Where Season 1 hinted at something watching from the shadows, this adventure puts it directly in front of the party: the sea has turned bone-white, ruins are rising from the surf, and an Aevorian coastal monitoring station is reactivating after 9,000 years of dormancy. Tone is eerie and beautiful rather than violent — a dying ocean that wants to show the players something. The adventure escalates from Season 1's scattered clues by naming the thing the players have been sensing: Aevor was watching Salsvault long before the Great Fall, and the party has been observed since the beginning. This is Arc 1 of the Echo Resurgence — the first moment Season 2's threat stops being abstract.

## Hook

Marshal Brenna Thorne receives three distress letters in one week from coastal settlements near the Bleached Reach. The party is the Warden response team. Brenna briefs them personally at Waystone Inn when the coastal scout Maris collapses through the door.

{{note
##### Read Aloud — Opening
*"The doors of Waystone Inn burst open, seawater cascading across the floor. A bedraggled scout collapses at your feet, clutching a water-stained letter. 'The coast... it's dying. The sea turned white three days ago, and now... things are rising from the depths.' He coughs up brine. 'The Bleached Reach. It's... it's awake.' Marshal Brenna Thorne kneels beside him, her face pale. 'The Echo. It's starting again.'"*
}}

Brenna provides a map (2 days' travel), the ability to message Archivist Yssara with up to 3 questions, and a warning: *"The Echo is smarter now. It's learned from Season 1."*

## Key Scenes

### Scene 1: Distress on the Waves

**Location:** Waystone Inn common room, evening  
**Setup:** Scout Maris has delivered the letters. Brenna is here. Players have time to gather information before departing.

**What happens:**
- Examine the three distress letters (all describe the sea turning white at the same time)
- Question Maris: he's been patrolling this coast for ten years and is genuinely shaken — backwards tides, fish swimming in spirals, ruins appearing overnight
- Consult Brenna: she connects this to Season 1 Echo manifestations immediately
- Brenna mentions the Temple of the Dragonknights glyphs match reports from the coast

**Key rolls:**
- DC 12 Investigation on letters: all three mention identical timing
- DC 14 Perception on letters: the salt stains glow faintly blue
- DC 16 Arcana on glowing salt: Echo resonance marker — the letters themselves are contaminated

**Time pressure:** Brenna says the settlements have been dark for 72 hours. Every day the party delays, more civilians are at risk of "walking into the sea."

{{note
##### Read Aloud
*"The three letters are laid side by side on the table. Different hands, different settlements — but the same words appear in each: 'the sea turned white.' Not 'the sea looks pale' or 'the water has changed.' Three people, miles apart, wrote the same four words on the same night. Maris's hands won't stop shaking. 'The water isn't just white,' he says. 'It's wrong. Everything is wrong out there.'"*
*(drafted from outline)*
}}

**Escalation:** If players delay more than a scene, Brenna receives word that a fourth settlement has gone silent.

### Scene 2: The Bleached Coast

**Location:** The Bleached Reach coastline, dawn, two days after departure  
**Setup:** Players arrive to the full visual impact of the manifestation. This scene is atmosphere and discovery — no immediate combat pressure.

**What happens:**
- The sea is bone-white for a mile out, with blue-white sparks trailing each slow-motion wave
- Ruins of white stone are rising from the surf — visible only at certain tides, pulsing with Aevorian glyphs
- A voice whispers on the wind in a language players have heard before (Aevorian from Temple of the Dragonknights): *"The observation continues. Subject: Salsvault. Status: Reactivation imminent."*
- Survivors from the nearest settlement (Saltmarsh) describe constructs walking out of the water — they didn't attack, they just *watched*
- Combat: one Echo-Touched Seal attacks from the surf (reskinned Giant Seal; bite deals 1d6 cold + 1d6 psychic; Echo Howl recharge 5-6, DC 13 Wis or frightened)

**Key rolls:**
- DC 14 Arcana on white water: recording medium saturated with Echo energy, contains 9,000-year-old Salsvault surveillance
- DC 13 Investigation on ruins: Aevorian monitoring station — glyphs match Temple of the Dragonknights
- DC 15 Perception on ruins: a recording device plays fragments of Salsvault's destruction

**Time pressure:** Low tide closes in 90 minutes — the underwater entrance will reseal.

{{note
##### Read Aloud
*"The sight before you defies explanation. The sea, for a mile out, is the color of bleached bone. Waves crash in slow motion, each one trailing blue-white sparks that don't fade. And there — rising from the surf like the ribs of a dead god — white stone ruins, carved with Aevorian glyphs that pulse with faint light. A voice whispers on the wind, in a language you've heard before: 'The observation continues. Subject: Salsvault. Status: Reactivation imminent.'"*
}}

**Escalation:** If players miss the tidal window, the entrance reseals and they must wait 8 hours for the next low tide — during which the station's activity intensifies and a second construct patrol emerges from the water.

### Scene 3: The Sunken Station

**Location:** Aevorian Monitoring Station Delta-7, underwater facility  
**Setup:** Players descend through the tidal entrance. Water breathing magic or held breath required for the descent; the facility interior has breathable air. This is the adventure's heart — exploration, lore, growing dread.

**What happens:**
- The entrance hatch reads "Delta-7: Salsvault Observation Post" in Aevorian
- Crystal veins light the corridors in deep blue — the facility is waking up
- Observation windows look out onto: Salsvault, *preserved in magical stasis, exactly as it was 9,000 years ago*. Players are watching the past
- Recorded voice: *"Recording 8,942-Alpha. Salsvault activity: Normal. Dragonknight presence: Detected. Echo resonance: Stable."*
- Discovery: Dragonknights were *created* by Aevor — the Temple of the Dragonknights was built on an Aevorian facility site
- Discovery: A map shows twelve observation posts like this one, all connected to an Echo network
- Combat: two Aevorian Sentinels activate to protect recording devices (reskinned Scarecrows; slam 1d10+3 bludgeoning + 1d6 lightning; Lightning Bolt line on recharge 5-6)

**Key rolls:**
- DC 16 Arcana on observation records: full Salsvault timeline; Dragonknights were test subjects
- DC 14 Investigation: living quarters, skeletal remains, personal effects of Aevorian observers
- DC 15 Arcana on Echo terminal: can ask 3 questions; DC 14 Wis save per question or 1 level exhaustion
- DC 18 Arcana: the Echo is not reactivating — it's *merging* with the original Salsvault

**Time pressure:** Each interaction with the terminal increases Echo contamination in the chamber.

{{note
##### Read Aloud
*"The entrance looms below the waves — a circular hatch etched with Aevorian script: 'Delta-7: Salsvault Observation Post.' As you descend, the water turns from white to deep blue, lit by veins of Aevorian crystal running through the walls. And then... you see it. Through the observation windows: the original Salsvault, preserved in magical stasis. You're watching the past. A voice echoes: 'Recording 8,942-Alpha. Salsvault activity: Normal. Dragonknight presence: Detected. Echo resonance: Stable.'"*
}}

**Escalation:** If players linger too long without engaging the terminal, the station's automated defenses escalate — a third Sentinel activates from a previously dormant alcove.

### Scene 4: The Echo Speaks

**Location:** Central Broadcast Chamber, deep within Delta-7  
**Setup:** The station's crystal veins reach critical resonance. Players experience a direct manifestation of the Echo — the defining moment of the adventure. The Echo is not attacking; it's *communicating*.

**What happens:**
- Crystal veins flare blinding white — players are transported (vision, not physical) to Aevor at its peak: spires of white stone, rivers of magic
- A voice speaks — not recorded, *present*: *"You have found Delta-7. You have seen the recordings. Now... you will understand why the Echo must return. Aevor did not fall. Aevor... ascended. And you... you will help me complete the process."*
- The vision shatters. Players are back in the chamber. The Echo has marked them as "assets"
- Choice: Destroy the station / Negotiate with the Echo fragment / Steal the Master Recording
- The station begins to self-destruct after this scene regardless of choice (the Echo clears its tracks)

**Key rolls:**
- DC 18 Athletics/Thieves' Tools: Sabotage crystal veins (delays Echo reactivation 1 month; Echo considers them threats)
- DC 16 Persuasion/Arcana: Negotiate (Echo shares one secret; may ask favors later)
- DC 14 Sleight of Hand/Investigation: Copy the Master Recording (players escape with complete Echo history)

**Time pressure:** The station collapses after 3 rounds — players must exit immediately after making their choice.

{{note
##### Read Aloud
*"The station's crystal veins flare to blinding life. The air itself vibrates, and suddenly... you're not in the chamber anymore. You're standing in Aevor, 9,000 years ago. You see the flying city at its peak — spires of white stone, magic flowing like water. And then, a voice. Not recorded. Present. 'You have found Delta-7. You have seen the recordings. Now... you will understand why the Echo must return. Aevor did not fall. Aevor... ascended. And you... you will help me complete the process.' The vision shatters. You're back in the chamber. But the Echo... it's inside your mind now."*
}}

**Escalation:** If players fail to exit within 3 rounds of the manifestation, structural collapse begins — DC 14 Dex saves each round or 2d6 bludgeoning damage from falling crystal.

## Decision Points

- **What to do with the Echo fragment (Scene 4):**  
  *If they destroy the station:* Echo reactivation delayed 1 month; Echo marks them as threats; other nodes accelerate to compensate.  
  *If they negotiate:* Echo shares a secret and treats them as assets; future Echo encounters have a diplomatic layer; Yssara warns this is dangerous.  
  *If they steal the records:* They carry the Master Recording (very rare lore item, resistance to psychic damage); Echo "lets" them leave — it *wants* the knowledge shared.

- **Whether to use the Echo terminal (Scene 3):**  
  *If they query it:* Gain answers to 3 questions; each question risks 1 level exhaustion; may open negotiation in Scene 4.  
  *If they leave it alone:* Avoid contamination; miss the lore shortcut; Echo regards them as passive observers.

## Clues to Plant

- Drop in Scene 2 (ruins glyph examination): *The glyph pattern matches exactly what was found in the Temple of the Dragonknights basement* → **Mystery:** The Temple was built on an Aevorian facility site; the Dragonknights were never independent of Aevor
- Drop in Scene 3 (map on wall): *Twelve observation posts marked across the region, all connected by crystal lines converging on a single point marked "Command Center"* → **Mystery:** The Bleached Reach is one node; something larger is waking up
- Drop in Scene 3 (Sentinel examination): *A mark on the Sentinel's chest reads "DS-7 / Program: Observe / Target: Salsvault / Secondary Target: Temple-DK"* → **Mystery:** The party's Season 1 activities at the Temple were observed and recorded
- Drop in Scene 4 (Echo vision, details): *Players see themselves in the vision — tiny figures inside the Temple of the Dragonknights, from above, like a surveillance feed* → **Mystery:** "We were being watched the entire time"

## Hooks Forward

- The Master Recording mentions "twelve nodes" — plant this clearly. When *The Sunken Archive* reveals AEON-12's node network, players will have already seen the number.
- The Echo's final words refer to "completing the process" — this primes players for the Frostfire Clan's belief that the Echo is "finishing what Aevor started."
- Brenna's reaction to the news ("It's learning") sets up her secret knowledge about the Frostfire Clan in the next adventure.
- Maris mentions the constructs that emerged from the water "just watched" — establish that the Echo prefers observation to aggression. This makes its eventual direct manifestation in Echo War more impactful.

## Escalations

- If players skip Scene 2 (go straight underwater): They miss the survivors' testimony. Have a survivor they find *inside* the station (hiding in a side room, memory-contaminated) deliver the key beats — the constructs watching, the white water, the ruins appearing overnight.
- If players fail the Sentinel combat in Scene 3: The Sentinels don't kill — they attempt to restrain and "scan" the players. This actually triggers the terminal interaction early (the station reads them as Aevorian-adjacent due to Echo exposure from Season 1).
- If players are too cautious to interact with anything: The Echo terminal activates on its own after 10 minutes inside the station and begins "reading" the nearest player. This forces engagement with the Scene 4 manifestation.
- If the party has no Arcana: Maris (if he came with them) recognizes the Salsvault glyph from a letter in the Season 1 archive — use his limited knowledge to surface the key connection.

## Season 1 Callbacks

- **[Temple of the Dragonknights]** — The Temple's Aevorian glyphs return: The monitoring station's script is identical, and the map shows the Temple site is a marked observation target. Players who examined the Temple basement carefully will recognize it immediately.
- **[Salsvault / Season 1 General]** — The observation windows show Salsvault in stasis: Players are watching the facility they explored (or heard about) from the outside, from 9,000 years ago. The emotional punch is "we were being watched the whole time."
- **[All Season 1 Echo breadcrumbs]** — The recording plays back Echo resonance readings from exactly the moments players triggered Echo activity in Season 1 adventures. If the DM tracked which Season 1 scenes had Echo clues, name those scenes specifically.
- **[Marshal Brenna Thorne]** — Her "I've been expecting this" reaction pays off the quiet competence she showed in Season 1. She knew the coast was a vulnerability and didn't say so.
