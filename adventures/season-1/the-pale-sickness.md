## Quick Brief

The pivotal revelation adventure of the entire Northwatch Wardens campaign. A deadly magical disease — frigid woe — is turning villagers in Palebank into ice statues. The Wardens must investigate the outbreak, track stolen vials to a bandit hideout, cross frozen icefields, and descend into Salsvault: a buried Aevorian laboratory. This is where everything clicks. Wolves of Welton, Alexi Merriksonn's disappearance, magical instability, frost patterns — all trace back to Salsvault, which reactivated 2–3 months ago. Tone: mystery and urgency escalating into cold dread, then revelation. This is the adventure that answers the campaign's biggest questions and replaces them with bigger ones.

## Hook

Lorewarden Elric Vael sends the party to Palebank Village on an urgent contract: investigate a mysterious disease that is killing residents in unprecedented ways. Village leader Elro Aldataur is waiting.

## Key Scenes

### Scene 1: Palebank Investigation

**Location:** Palebank Village — Urgon's cabin, Pelc's Curiosities, Tulgi's cabin, Irven's home  
**Setup:** The Wardens have 7 days before Tulgi Lutan dies; 10 days before Irven Liel's family follows. Every location in the village connects to the blue vials that started the outbreak.

**What happens:**
- **Urgon's cabin:** Footprints leading out (Tulgi); Urgon's body shows blue veins and internal crystallization (magical disease); residue on vials is Aevorian in origin (DC 12 Arcana)
- **Pelc's Curiosities:** Receipt linking Urgon to two blue vials; signs of forced entry — vials were stolen
- **Tulgi's cabin:** She's dying (7 days), scared, defensive. DC 15 Persuasion: admits she broke in and sold the vials to a fence connected to Croaker Cave
- **Irven's home:** Family infected (10 days); bought the vials from a traveling fence; fence last seen heading toward Croaker Cave
- **Mila Teno (Frostwatch guard):** Confirms she saw Tulgi near Urgon's cabin; provides timeline

**Key rolls:**
- DC 10 Medicine: "This disease is magical, not natural."
- DC 12 Arcana: vial residue is Aevorian — cold, unnatural, not aligned with any current school
- DC 12 Investigation: forced entry at Pelc's; stolen vials

**Time pressure:** Tulgi has 7 days. Irven's family has 10. The clock is stated to the party explicitly.

---

### Scene 2: Croaker Cave

**Location:** Smuggler hideout in cliffs west of Palebank  
**Setup:** The bandits stole the vials because they looked valuable and a Corsair fence promised coin for "anything blue and Aevorian." They had no idea the vials were deadly.

**What happens:**
- Approach: DC 13 Stealth to gain surprise; DC 12 Perception to hear arguing voices inside
- Bandits are cold, hungry, and scared — not evil, just desperate
- Social path: DC 12 Intimidation (surrender immediately) or DC 14 Persuasion (hand over vials); mentioning the disease makes them panic
- Combat: 4–6 bandits + 1 bandit captain (scale to party)
- Optional: one bandit handled a vial and is infected — powerful moral moment ("Am I going to die?")
- The stolen vials are in a locked chest (DC 12 Sleight of Hand to open)

**Key rolls:**
- DC 10 Insight: these people didn't know the vials were dangerous
- DC 10 Investigation: find the chest beneath blankets; map scrap and note inside

**Time pressure:** Recovering the vials stops further infections in Palebank. Frame this as containment, not a detour.

### Scene 3: Journey to Salsvault

**Location:** Icefields north of Palebank, via The Cold Anchor research station  
**Setup:** The Wardens must cross treacherous frozen wastes to reach the ruins. At The Cold Anchor, they meet Morgo, a veteran guide who warns them the ruins "weren't glowing last season."

**What happens:**
- **The Cold Anchor:** Morgo provides cold-weather gear, rations, climbing equipment, and a rough map; his key warning: "If you see blue light in the snow, avoid it"
- **Travel (1–2 days):** DC 12 Survival to navigate safely; DC 12 Athletics to cross unstable ice; DC 13 Constitution to resist freezing wind
- **Environmental hazards:** freezing wind (1d4 cold damage/hr unprotected), thin ice (DC 12 Dex or fall through), blue spore patches (DC 11 Con or contract frigid woe), magical instability flares
- **Random encounters (d4 every 4 hours):** ice mephits, frostmane sabertooth, frozen corpse with explorer's journal, Echo surge

**Key rolls:**
- DC 12 Arcana at arrival: "This magic predates the Godsfall. Aevorian."
- DC 12 History: "Aeor built weapons to kill gods. This place was part of that."

**Time pressure:** Every setback costs time. Remind players: Tulgi and Irven's family are dying back in Palebank.

### Scene 4: Salsvault — The Frozen Laboratory

**Location:** Buried Aevorian research facility  
**Setup:** Cold metal, blue light, drifting spores, humming machinery. Constructs still patrol the halls. Spores drift through the air. The walls hum with unstable power.

**What happens:**
- **Entrance Hall:** Aevorian glyphs, Urgon's bootprints, thin ice patches, spore clouds; 2 Flying Swords patrol
- **Research Laboratories:** Notes on "Project Woe" — a divine-resistant bioweapon; 2 Animated Armor (or 1 Helmed Horror for larger parties); constructs activate on noise
- **Containment Hall:** Sealed metal doors, shattered containment pod, burn marks — this is where the breach occurred; spore vents active
- **Construct Storage:** Dormant constructs; damaged schematic showing Preservation Chamber location
- **Preservation Chamber:** Warm golden light — the only warmth in the ruin. 1d6 intact gold vials (antidote). DC 14 Arcana: antidote was created as a failsafe — Aevor feared its own weapons
- **Central Control Room:** THE REVELATION MOMENT — logs confirm manual activation 2–3 months ago; vision of hooded non-human figure at the console; references to "Site Theta," "Site Vharos," "Site 9-B"

**Key rolls:**
- DC 10 Arcana (Preservation Chamber): gold vials = cure; blue vials = disease
- DC 15 Arcana (Control Room): "The activation sequence was manual — someone triggered it."
- DC 17 Arcana (critical): confirm deliberate activation beyond any doubt

**Time pressure:** DC 11 Constitution save (hourly) vs. exhaustion from cold; DC 11 Constitution save vs. frigid woe from spore exposure. If they disturb too much machinery, partial collapse forces quick escape.

---

### Scene 5: Return and Resolution

**Location:** Palebank Village, then Waystone Inn  
**Setup:** The party races back with the antidote. Whether they save everyone depends on how long they took. Elric is waiting at Waystone for a full debrief.

**What happens:**
- **Best outcome (returned in time):** Tulgi and Irven's family saved; 300 gp total reward; Palebank becomes Warden-friendly settlement
- **Partial success (arrived late):** Some die; 200 gp; Elro grateful but shaken; campaign takes darker tone
- **Full failure (no antidote or too late):** Palebank becomes ghost town; 0 reward; harder path into Arc 3
- **Selective curing (not enough vials):** Party must choose who lives — no guidance from NPCs; moral weight is explicit
- **Elric's debrief at Waystone:** The revelation speech. He connects all dots: wolves, Alexi, frost patterns, wand instability, frigid woe — all Salsvault. "Someone triggered this deliberately. And Salsvault is only one facility."

**Key rolls:**
- None required for resolution — outcomes flow from prior choices and timing

**Time pressure:** Final accounting of everything.

{{note
##### Read Aloud — Elric's Revelation

The frost patterns. The wand that failed. It's all connected — the Aevorian Echo is spreading from a single source. But here's what keeps me awake: the activation was deliberate. ## Decision Points

- **Choice: How to handle the infected bandits**
  *Let them go:* They might spread more vials inadvertently; moral complexity.
  *Detain them:* Logistical problem the party must solve.
  *Cure the infected one (if vials are recovered):* Costly but earns deep loyalty.

- **Choice: Prioritize speed vs. preparation**
  *Rush to Salsvault unprepared:* Risk exhaustion, frigid woe, combat at disadvantage.
  *Stop at Cold Anchor properly:* Morgo's gear and map save lives.

- **Choice: Selective curing if vials are scarce**
  *Tulgi vs. Irven's children vs. Irven's partner:* No right answer. Village reacts to the choice.

- **Choice: Destroy or preserve Salsvault**
  *Destroy it:* Echo weakens temporarily. Elric is disappointed — "invaluable research lost."
  *Preserve it:* Ongoing threat but the Wardens gain Aevorian knowledge.

## Clues to Plant

- Blue vials from the Far North → **Mystery: Salsvault is leaking Aevorian spores and has recently reactivated**
- Spore residue matches the frost patterns seen in Wolves of Welton → **Mystery: every symptom of the Echo traces back to the same source**
- Activation logs show manual startup 2–3 months ago → **Mystery: someone deliberately triggered Salsvault**
- Control room references to "Site Theta," "Site Vharos," "Site 9-B" → **Mystery: Salsvault is one node in a larger Aevorian network**
- Hooded non-human figure in the vision → **Mystery: an active antagonist is exploiting Aevorian ruins**

## Hooks Forward

- Elric's debrief closes the loop on every prior adventure and opens Arc 3: who triggered Salsvault, why now, and what other sites are waking up.
- Elric's campaign hook: "I've received reports of a cult called the Dragonknights drawn to sites of magical power — they may be seeking other Aevorian ruins." → **Temple of the Dragonknights**
- If Palebank survives, it becomes a Warden-friendly supply point and Elro becomes a regional contact.
- Aevorian artifacts recovered from Salsvault are of significant scholarly interest — multiple factions will want them.

## Escalations

- **If party gets stuck in Palebank:** Elro sends them to Mila. Mila sends them to Tulgi. Tulgi breaks down. If all else fails: Tulgi knocks on the party's door in the night. "I'm dying. You have to help me."
- **If party skips Croaker Cave:** Bandits ambush them on the road north; vials found on the captain's body. Or Elro's scouts locate the hideout.
- **If party rushes to Salsvault unprepared:** Show Morgo warning them. Show a frozen corpse as consequence of unprepared travel. Make the ice hurt before Salsvault even begins.
- **If party is getting wrecked in Salsvault:** Constructs defend and do not pursue far — they protect the facility, they don't hunt. Hidden cache: 2d4 potions of healing. Morgo can arrive a day behind as emergency backup.
- **If plague timeline is killing urgency:** Put it on the table. "You have about a week before more people die. Every delay costs lives." Don't soften it.
