# The Pale Sickness

A magical disease — frigid woe — is turning Palebank villagers to ice. The cure lies in Salsvault, a buried Aevorian laboratory that recently reactivated and is the source of every prior campaign disturbance. This is the adventure where everything clicks. Tone: mystery and urgency escalating into cold dread, then revelation.

## Hook

Lorewarden [Elric Vael](npcs/core/elric-vael.md) sends the party to Palebank on urgent contract: investigate a plague killing residents in unprecedented ways.

> *"Snow falls softly over Palebank Village. Lanterns glow behind frosted windows. As you arrive, villagers gather around a frozen statue — a dwarf locked in a final moment of terror. Elro Aldataur steps forward: 'Thank you for coming. Urgon died of something we've never seen — turned to ice. Now others are showing the same symptoms.'"*

## The Vial Chain (canonical — DM reference)

The whole investigation hangs on **two** vials Urgon brought home. Keep this straight:

1. **[Urgon Wenth](npcs/core/urgon-wenth.md)** (village blacksmith) traveled north on a trading run, reached the ruins at **Salsvault**, and brought back **two blue Aevorian vials**. He was already infected from handling them — he dies first (the frozen statue in the opening).
2. Urgon sold **both** vials to **[Verla Pelc](npcs/core/verla-pelc.md)** at Pelc's Curiosities for the coin, glad to get them out of his house.
3. Pelc resold them: **Vial 1 → [Tulgi Lutan](npcs/core/tulgi-lutan.md)** (cash); **Vial 2 → [Irven Liel Sr.](npcs/season-1/irven-liel-sr.md)'s household**, brokered by the traveling fence **[Brynn Wraithwood](npcs/season-1/brynn-wraithwood.md)**.
4. After Urgon died and the sickness spread, **Tulgi** realized the vials were the cause. She searched Urgon's cabin (couldn't open the locked chest), then broke into **Pelc's** and stole the receipt to trace the second vial — leading to Brynn and **Croaker Cave**.
5. **Brynn** runs his own northern supply line (he's been fencing Aevorian relics out of Salsvault). The two vials in the Croaker Cave chest are a *separate* batch he sourced himself — not Urgon's two. This is why the contamination is spreading through more than one channel, all of it tracing back to Salsvault.

> So: **Urgon's 2 vials → Tulgi + Irven's family.** Brynn's cache is additional. Nobody is holding the same vial twice.

## Decision Points

- **Infected bandits:** let go (may spread vials) / detain (logistical problem) / cure one (costs a vial, earns loyalty)
- **Speed vs. prep:** rush to Salsvault (risk exhaustion + frigid woe) / stop at Cold Anchor (Morgo's gear saves lives)
- **Selective curing:** if vials are scarce — Tulgi vs. Irven's children vs. Irven's partner; no right answer
- **Salsvault fate:** destroy it (Echo weakens; Elric disappointed) / preserve it (ongoing threat + Aevorian knowledge)
