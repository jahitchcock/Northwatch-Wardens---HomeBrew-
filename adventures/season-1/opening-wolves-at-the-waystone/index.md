## Quick Brief

Session 0 onboarding framework that transforms strangers into the Northwatch Wardens. The party survives a wolf attack at the Waystone Inn, gets inducted into the guild by Marshal Brenna Thorne, and receives their first contract. Tone is grounded and professional — not heroic fantasy, but frontier contractors being hired for a job. Ends with the party en route to Welton. The Aevorian Echo gets its first whisper here: unnatural frost, a humming metal buckle, something wrong in the north.

## Hook

Father Merriksonn approaches after the wolf fight and mentions the Northwatch Wardens and a Marshal named Brenna Thorne. The next morning, Brenna is already at the inn.

## Key Scenes

### Scene 1: Wolf Attack at the Waystone

**Location:** Waystone Inn, exterior and entrance  
**Setup:** 2–3 wolves (larger than normal, oddly organized) burst from darkness and attack the party. Other travelers slam the door and shelter inside. Innkeepers Richard and Lauraine Brown are trapped.

**What happens:**
- Wolves attack immediately — no warning, no preamble
- Pack Tactics: wolves use flanking instinctively
- At 3 HP or fewer, wolves flee toward the treeline
- If party protects the innkeepers, Richard and Lauraine provide free lodging and supplies

**Key rolls:**
- DC 10 Perception: notice wolves are oddly organized — not random hunger
- DC 12 Survival: tracks come from the north; wolves are well-fed, not starving
- DC 13 Arcana: thin geometric frost on wolf fur — faint magical wrongness

**Time pressure:** None — this is the tutorial fight. Let it breathe.

{{note
##### Read Aloud — After Combat
*"Silence. The snow falls harder, covering blood and paw prints. The inn door slowly opens. Richard Brown peers out, shaking. 'Thank the gods. We thought — they came so fast. I've never seen wolves like that. So organized. So... purposeful.'"*
}}

### Scene 2: The Morning After — Charter and Mission Brief

**Location:** Waystone Inn, private charter room  
**Setup:** Brenna Thorne, Mara Fenwick, and Elric Vael are waiting. The Northwatch Wardens Charter is spread on the table. This is the induction ceremony.

**What happens:**
- Brenna explains the guild: professional contractors, merit-based, no heroics
- Party signs the charter — they are now Initiate-rank Warders
- Mara distributes the standard kit (bedroll, rope, rations, lantern, crowbar)
- Elric hints that the wolves connect to larger regional patterns — Brenna gives him a look
- Contract W-17 is presented: Welton, wolves, 800 gp on completion

**Key rolls:**
- No checks required — this is pure social/ceremony
- DC 12 Insight on Elric: he knows more than he's sharing, and Brenna knows it too

**Time pressure:** If party resists joining, Brenna: "You can refuse. Your rank drops, another Warder takes the contract. That's the deal."

{{note
##### Read Aloud — Charter Moment
*"Brenna unrolls the parchment. It bears the Northwatch emblem: a compass rose with a sentinel figure. 'Read it or don't. The terms are simple: you take jobs, you complete them, you report findings, you advance. You fail, and we replace you. Questions?'"*

### Scene 3: The Road South — Echo Clue at the Creek

**Location:** Trade road south of Waystone, creek crossing  
**Setup:** 2–3 hours of travel. The Veteran Scout meets them at a creek crossing. Something is wrong with the water.

**What happens:**
- Geometric frost patterns coat the stones — too perfect, not seasonal
- Scout finds torn cloth: cold to touch, faintly humming (magical resonance)
- Scout: "I've heard reports of this. Three others described the same wrongness. Whatever it is, it's spreading."
- Optional: 2 bandit scouts demand a toll — can be fought, intimidated (DC 12), or paid (10 gp)
- Party arrives at Forward Camp by late afternoon and meets Torvin

**Key rolls:**
- DC 10 Perception: frost is geometric, impossible for this weather
- DC 12 Arcana: magical resonance — not aligned with any standard school
- DC 11 Survival (navigation): arrive early vs. at dusk

**Time pressure:** None — but Scout says "Get rest. Tomorrow we enter Welton."

{{note
##### Read Aloud — The Creek

### Scene 4: Forward Camp

**Location:** Clearing with twin pines, simple camp  
**Setup:** Two veteran Warders are stationed here. Torvin serves hot stew. This is the party bonding moment before the campaign proper begins.

**What happens:**
- Torvin and the second Warder brief the party on Welton
- Scout answers questions about the Echo, regional dangers, tactics
- Party can ask anything — this is the calm before the storm
- Scout's final word: "This is real work, not training."

**Key rolls:** None required.

**Time pressure:** None — let the party talk.

## Decision Points

- **Choice: Sign the Charter or refuse**
  *If they sign:* Full guild support, contract, equipment, and advancement path.
  *If they refuse:* Brenna acknowledges it; party gets no contract and must find their own path to Welton. Possible alternative hook via Merriksonn.

- **Choice: Investigate the frost clue at the creek**
  *If they investigate:* First Echo seed planted; Scout wraps the cloth for Brenna. Sets up recognition in later adventures.
  *If they ignore it:* Scout still notes it; the party just doesn't register the wrongness yet.

## Clues to Plant

- Creek crossing: geometric frost that doesn't melt → **Mystery: Aevorian Echo spreading from northern source**
- Humming torn cloth: cold metal resonance → **Mystery: magical interference is tactile/auditory, not just visual**
- Elric's veiled comment about "larger patterns" → **Mystery: the Northwatch already suspects something big**
- Wolves' unusual organization + geometric frost on fur → **Mystery: the same force is affecting animals and environment**

## Hooks Forward

- The wolves attacked the inn tonight. Tomorrow the party investigates wolves in Welton. The connection should feel immediate.
- Elric's pinned map — Welton is just one of several flagged locations. More to come.
- Establish that the Scout will not be a permanent NPC — she returns to other duties after Forward Camp. Party is on their own from here.

## Escalations

- **If party skips the frost investigation:** Have Scout point it out directly. "Did you notice that? The frost. It doesn't melt." Make them acknowledge it even if they don't understand it.
- **If party argues with Brenna about terms:** She doesn't argue back. She waits. "Your choice. Clock's ticking." The silence is the pressure.
- **If combat with wolves drags:** Wolves flee when bloodied. They're not suicidal — desperation, then self-preservation. Move the scene along.
- **If party wants to hunt wolves tonight before sleeping:** The Scout redirects: "Not tonight. Rest, prepare, enter Welton tomorrow on your terms."
