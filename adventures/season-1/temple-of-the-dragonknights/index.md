## Quick Brief

Season 1 capstone. Kobolds are attacking Northcrest under the command of a young green dragon named Venomfang, who has claimed the ancient Temple of the Dragonknights as her lair. The temple sits atop a ley line convergence amplified by Salsvault's reactivation, and Venomfang is explicitly drawn here by the magical power. A cult of kobold worshippers has been desecrating the temple and performing rituals that exploit the Echo. The party must rescue a kidnapped girl, stop the cult, and face Venomfang — who is ambitious and rational, not mindlessly evil. Tone: dramatic, morally complex, faction-focused. This adventure makes the Aevorian Echo's regional scope visceral — the Echo isn't just affecting the environment, it's actively attracting predators.

## Hook

Northcrest is under attack by unusually organized kobolds when the party arrives. Wounded farmer Joel Andersmith is bleeding against the inn wall. His wife and son are dead. His daughter Clementine was taken alive. The town militia is overwhelmed.

## Key Scenes

### Scene 1: Northcrest and the Andersmith Farm

**Location:** Northcrest market square, then Andersmith Farm north of town  
**Setup:** Combat with organized kobolds, then investigation at the destroyed farm to find the trail. The farm is not just scenery — the dead family makes this personal.

**What happens:**
- Market combat: 4-6 kobolds with town guard support; kobolds flee north when losing; one drops a map fragment
- Farm investigation: barn shows methodical destruction (not random hunger); claw marks too large for kobolds; son's body shows he fought back; Clementine's room shows drag marks and a bloodstained teddy bear left behind
- Trail: DC 10 Survival (blood leads northwest), DC 12 Investigation (torn clothing on branch confirms Clementine went this way), DC 11 Investigation (unnaturally dense thicket — pull the hidden vine or DC 13 Athletics to force through)
- Sergeant Sera Gelanadel of the Mages' Guild was separately sent to investigate magical resonance at the temple and is missing

**Key rolls:**
- DC 12 History: the farm was targeted, not raided — kobolds knew the layout
- DC 12+ Perception (forest path): geometric frost patterns on stones, identical to those seen in prior Echo encounters

**Time pressure:** Clementine is being held for a ritual. The cultists have hours, not days.

### Scene 2: Poisontip Cavern

**Location:** Cave complex below the temple, entrance in forest clearing  
**Setup:** Kobold territory. The cavern is organized — crude but deliberate. Torchlit. Cold. The air smells wrong. Party descends toward the temple entrance through 3-5 areas.

**What happens:**
- **Entrance guard post:** 1 winged kobold (AC 13, HP 8, fly 30 ft) shrieks alarm and pelts with spears; flees to warn deeper guards
- **Rickety bridge** over underground lake: DC 12 Acrobatics or DC 11 Athletics; failure = 1d6 fall + lake hazard; on bridge: torn parchment in Draconic — "We brought the girl to the robed ones. Not sure why they wanted her, but whatever it takes to please the master."
- **Sleeping quarters:** 2-3 kobolds asleep (surprise possible); 25 gp and 2 Potions of Healing in tents; secret door (DC 10 Perception, DC 14 Athletics) to Northcrest supply stash (return for 100 gp reward)
- **Ritual chamber / offering bowl:** Massive stone doors carved with Dragonknight emblem (snake wrapped around sword). Sealed by blood ward: cut hand into bowl (1 HP) to open; OR DC 15 Arcana; OR DC 16 Athletics to force (loud — alerts temple)
- **Staircase to temple:** Recent bloodstains confirm Clementine was taken this way

**Key rolls:**
- DC 13 Arcana (forest path): magical resonance growing stronger — rhythmic, like a heartbeat, not aligned with any current school
- DC 10 Investigation (ritual chamber inscription): "Sealed by divine will. Do not disturb. — Dragonknights, Age of the Great Fall"

**Time pressure:** Every alarm raised adds kobolds to later encounters.

### Scene 3: Temple of the Dragonknights

**Location:** Ancient burial ground built atop converging ley lines  
**Setup:** The temple has been desecrated. Ancient warrior coffins have been deliberately broken open from the outside by the cultists. Walls are painted with crude symbols. Every act of desecration weakens the ancient seals that were keeping something contained below.

**What happens:**
- **Burial Chamber:** Walking on the central emblem triggers 4 zombie Dragonknights (AC 12, HP 11, slow at 20 ft — party should dominate this easily). DC 12 Religion: the coffins were broken from the outside — this desecration was deliberate
- **Armory (Torture Chamber):** Wizard's apprentice Sera Gelanadel is magically bound, beaten, alive. One fanatical cultist (AC 12, HP 13) is mid-ritual over her. Rescuing her: she confirms the temple sits atop converging ley lines; cultists plan to amplify them; Clementine is upstairs for a ritual; Venomfang is in the upper chamber. DC 10 Investigation: +1 Spear of the Dragonknights in the weapon racks
- **Ritual Frieze Chamber:** Enormous carved battle scene — Dragonknights fighting dragon-like constructs (magical automatons, not true dragons) during the Age of the Great Fall. DC 15 History: these Dragonknights were guardians who kept ancient things sealed beneath the earth (i.e., facilities like Salsvault)
- **Cult Assembly Hall:** Clementine is suspended by magical threads at the center of a ritual circle; 5 fanatical cultists (AC 12, HP 13, arcane bolt +4 to hit, 1d8 force, range 120 ft) chanting; if cultists complete ritual in 3 more rounds, Clementine is transformed (permanent damage); interrupting causes magical discharge (DC 14 Dex save or 2d6 force on first turn)

**Key rolls:**
- DC 12 History (ritual frieze): the temple was specifically built to seal something underground
- DC 12 Religion (burial chamber): this desecration is weakening ancient protective wards

**Time pressure:** The ritual has 3 rounds before Clementine is permanently harmed. Party should feel this.

### Scene 4: Venomfang

**Location:** Temple upper lair — vast vaulted chamber, dragon's hoard gleaming, crystalline formations in the ceiling  
**Setup:** Venomfang, a young green dragon (CR 8), claims this chamber. She arrived recently, explicitly drawn by the magical power of the ley line convergence amplified by Salsvault. She is ambitious and rational — she does not see herself as a villain. She's had a long time to prepare this speech.

**What happens:**
- Venomfang's opening: "Adventurers. How expected. You've slaughtered my servants, disturbed my lair, and now you stand before me on MY pedestal. Brave or foolish? Perhaps both."
- Three resolution paths:
  - **Combat:** She opens with Poison Breath (DC 14 Con save, 12d8 poison), flies high, uses multiattack from range; at 50% HP she either negotiates or flees through a collapsed ceiling passage. Full hoard available if defeated.
  - **Negotiation:** DC 14 Persuasion (appeal to ambition), DC 13 Intimidation (show strength), DC 12 Deception (warn of dragon slayers coming). On success: she leaves in exchange for one magic item from the party's possession. This is a valid victory.
  - **Collapse the temple:** DC 15 Investigation (fractured pillars from ritual magic); DC 13 Athletics to topple a pillar, collapsing the chamber. Venomfang escapes but loses her lair. Party wins without lethal combat.
- Elric's campaign hook (debrief): Venomfang arrived because of the ley line amplification from Salsvault. This is a pattern. Other convergence points exist. Other powerful creatures are responding.

**Key rolls:**
- Venomfang: AC 18, HP 136, CR 8 — Multiattack (bite + two claws), Poison Breath recharge 5-6, Legendary Resistance 3/day, Suggestion 3/day, Hold Person 1/day
- Reduce HP to 100 and have her flee at 25% HP for Level 3 parties

**Time pressure:** Clementine and Sera need to be extracted. If the temple collapses, everyone inside is at risk.

## Decision Points

- **Choice: How to resolve Venomfang**
  *Kill her:* Full hoard (1,500 gp + magic items); cult permanently scatters; no future threat from her.
  *Negotiate:* She leaves; cult collapses without her; one magic item surrendered; she becomes a recurring character elsewhere.
  *Collapse temple:* She escapes, lair destroyed; limited treasure; she has a grudge but no base.

- **Choice: What to do with the desecrated temple**
  *Leave it:* Ancient wards are weakened; something below is less contained than before.
  *Attempt to restore wards:* DC 18 Religion or DC 20 Arcana; partial restoration only; Elric is very interested.
  *Document and report:* Elric can send scholars; unlocks future arc content.

- **Choice: Spare or capture fleeing cultists**
  *Spare:* They scatter; cult may reform elsewhere.
  *Capture:* Information about their organization; someone hired them.

## Clues to Plant

- Geometric frost on forest path matches Welton and the creek from the Opening → **Mystery: Aevorian Echo is the same phenomenon across all prior adventures**
- Venomfang arrived recently because of the ley line amplification → **Mystery: the Echo is creating magical beacons that attract powerful creatures**
- Temple frieze: Dragonknights were guardians who kept ancient things sealed → **Mystery: these temples exist to contain Aevorian facilities; desecrating them has consequences**
- Cultists performing rituals to amplify the ley lines → **Mystery: the cult has knowledge of Aevorian power — someone taught them**
- References to "Site Theta" and "Site Vharos" if party finds cult documents → **Mystery: Salsvault is one node in a larger network**

## Hooks Forward

- Elric's debrief: "Venomfang was just the first. Other creatures are responding to the Echo across Northreach. We need to find the convergence points before something worse claims them."
- The cult was working for someone — kobolds don't organize themselves to exploit ley lines. That someone is still out there.
- The temple's weakened wards are a ticking problem. Whatever was being contained below Northcrest is now slightly less contained.
- If Sera survived: she returns to her guild but will correspond. The Mages' Guild is now aware of the ley line situation.
- If Venomfang escaped: she holds a grudge and still wants magical power. She will appear again when a convenient opportunity presents itself.

## Escalations

- **If party is overwhelmed in the cavern:** Compress to the 3 key encounters (guard post, sleeping quarters, ritual chamber). Skip Areas 3-4. Get them to the temple.
- **If Clementine ritual completes before party acts:** She is transformed (DC 15 Insight at start of ritual reveals this is permanent without Greater Restoration). Play the weight of it. Joel's reaction is devastating. This is valid.
- **If Venomfang fight becomes a 10-round slog:** Have her offer negotiation at 50% HP, even mid-combat. She's rational. "You've proven yourself. This is becoming tedious."
- **If party doesn't recognize the ley line connection:** Sera explains it. Or, on their return to Waystone, Elric has maps connecting this to the Palebank reports. The clue is not missable.
- **If party wants to ally with Venomfang:** She won't serve, but she might acknowledge mutual interest. A dragon who has tacitly agreed not to attack Northcrest in exchange for being left alone is a valid outcome. Enforce the tribute term.
