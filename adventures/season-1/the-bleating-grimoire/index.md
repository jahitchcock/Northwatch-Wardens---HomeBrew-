## Quick Brief

Comedy palate-cleanser between heavier adventures, but with genuine campaign weight. An elven enchantress named Vaelora Moonwhisper has been trapped as a sheep for 18 months by her bitter former apprentice Theron Blackwood, who stole her Wand of Polymorph. The wand is malfunctioning — transforming things unpredictably rather than as intended — which is proof that Aevorian Echo corruption is destabilizing magical artifacts across Northreach. Tone: absurdist comedy layered over a real horror ("what if ALL our magic tools start failing?"). Run this when players need to laugh before the next serious arc.

## Hook

A sheep with an air of wounded dignity approaches the party carrying a scroll in its teeth. Reading the scroll or casting Speak with Animals reveals Vaelora Moonwhisper, Enchantress of the Silver Wood, who is furious, desperate, and extremely put out about being livestock.

## Key Scenes

### Scene 1: The Woeful Arrival

**Location:** Town tavern, inn, or marketplace  
**Setup:** Vaelora finds the party and explains her situation. She is acerbic, proud, and occasionally bleats mid-sentence. Her scroll contains the basics; she fills in the rest.

**What happens:**
- Vaelora explains: Theron Blackwood stole her wand 18 months ago, polymorphed her
- The wand was meant to transform predictably — it now creates random bizarre changes
- Tower is 2 hours east through forest; Theron has polymorphed guards
- Theron employs a half-orc enforcer named Krull
- Party offer: 100 gp up front + 200 gp on restoration + 1 uncommon magic item from her collection

**Key rolls:**
- DC 10 Insight: she's genuinely terrified beneath the bravado
- DC 13 Arcana: "The wand's corruption is unprecedented. Something is interfering with the enchantment itself."

**Time pressure:** None yet — but Vaelora warns that Theron's paranoia makes him dangerous when cornered.

---

### Scene 2: Krull's Challenge

**Location:** Street or road leaving town  
**Setup:** Krull (half-orc, CR 2) arrives with two intelligent polymorphed wolves and a polymorphed brown bear. He wants the sheep back.

**What happens:**
- Krull's opening: "Theron sent me to collect the sheep. She's property now. Hand her over."
- Can fight, intimidate (DC 13), or pay off (50+ gp buys departure)
- If Krull is defeated and interrogated: reveals Theron "hasn't slept in days" and "keeps talking to himself"
- Vaelora after combat: "We should reach the tower before he prepares further."

**Key rolls:**
- Krull: AC 14, HP 32, two greatsword attacks, Reckless trait
- Polymorphed wolves: AC 13, HP 11, bite + knockdown
- Polymorphed bear: AC 11, HP 34, claw multiattack

**Time pressure:** If Krull escapes, he warns Theron; adds 1-2 guards to tower.

### Scene 3: Blackwood Tower Infiltration

**Location:** Tower compound in forest — three levels grown from a massive oak tree  
**Setup:** 3-4 polymorphed ape guards playing cards at the base. The tower has malfunctioning magical defenses throughout.

**What happens:**
- Infiltration options: assault, stealth (DC 12), distraction, or social engineering via Vaelora's contacts
- Ground level: 2 Flying Swords behaving erratically (sometimes attacking each other)
- Second level library: Animated Armor suits, Theron's paranoid notes scattered about
- Optional polymorphed cat servant who can provide help if freed
- DC 12 Arcana (any level): identify that constructs are destabilized by same corruption as the wand

**Key rolls:**
- DC 12 Stealth: bypass ground guards
- DC 12 Arcana: disable magical traps
- DC 10 Investigation: find secret passages

**Time pressure:** Every failed Stealth check may alert more guards. Vaelora becomes increasingly sarcastic about party's competence.

{{note
##### Read Aloud — Tower Approach

### Scene 4: Theron Blackwood and the Wand

**Location:** Third-level laboratory  
**Setup:** Theron is bloodshot, unkempt, holding a wand that pulses with unstable light. He's been awake for days.

**What happens:**
- Theron monologues about 15 years of unrecognized service before combat
- Combat: opens with Mirror Image, uses polymorph ray (DC 14 Wis save), Misty Steps to reposition
- Negotiation possible (DC 12 Persuasion, DC 13 Deception, DC 14 Intimidation)
- Wand backfire chance: on rolls of 1-4, caster OR random target polymorphs into random beast for 1d4 hours
- Wand explosion path: if used repeatedly, wild magic surge (d8 table: sheep, frog, chicken, wolf, bear, unchanged, flight 1 min, minor benefit)
- Restoration: use wand (DC 12 Arcana), Dispel Magic, or Greater Restoration

**Key rolls:**
- Theron: AC 12, HP 44, CR 3 — Suggestion, Misty Step, Counterspell, Dispel Magic
- Wand backfire DC: roll d20 when wand used; 1-4 = backfire

**Time pressure:** If Theron reaches 0 HP without negotiation, he uses wand as last resort — backfire likely.

She flexes her fingers, then looks at the party with something approaching respect. 'You actually did it. I... thank you. Perhaps I've misjudged adventurers after all.'"*

## Decision Points

- **Choice: Defeat Theron or negotiate**
  *Defeat and execute:* No future threat. Vaelora restored. Wand destroyed or kept (dangerous).
  *Negotiate:* Theron lives, becomes recurring antagonist. Vaelora grateful but nervous.
  *Wand explodes:* Theron likely polymorphed. Vaelora may remain sheep temporarily. Must seek alternative cure.

- **Choice: Keep or destroy the corrupted wand**
  *Keep it:* Enforce backfire mechanics strictly every use. It's a liability.
  *Destroy it:* Elric is relieved. "It's too dangerous to exist."
  *Study it (DC 18 Arcana):* Reveals external magical frequency — "something very old and powerful." Points toward Salsvault.

## Clues to Plant

- Wand malfunction: random polymorphs instead of intended targets → **Mystery: Aevorian Echo corrupts magical artifacts region-wide**
- Theron's notes about "instability growing worse" → **Mystery: the interference is escalating, not fading**
- Elric's debrief (back at Waystone): "Every wizard's tools are becoming unreliable. We need to find the source." → **Sets up Pale Sickness urgency**

## Hooks Forward

- Elric's debrief makes explicit that artifact corruption is regional, not isolated — sets the stakes for The Pale Sickness.
- If Vaelora is restored, she becomes a magical research contact (can Identify items, access her library).
- If Theron escapes, he becomes a recurring antagonist with a grudge.
- The wand can be delivered to Elric for study — he extracts partial information: "an ancient, cold frequency."

## Escalations

- **If party won't engage with the sheep's scroll:** Have Vaelora headbutt the nearest party member and bleat urgently until someone pays attention.
- **If Krull fight drags:** Resolve in 1-2 rounds. Krull flees at 10 HP. This is a warm-up fight, not the main event.
- **If party goes fully lethal in tower:** Tone check — Vaelora will be increasingly horrified. "He was a pawn! Just like I was!" This is valid to play for the moral complexity.
- **If wand backfire turns someone into a sheep:** Play it straight. Vaelora: "Yes. That's exactly what it feels like. Now you understand. Can we perhaps retrieve my wand from the man who did this to me?"
