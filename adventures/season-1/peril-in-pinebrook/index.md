## Quick Brief

An introductory adventure with real emotional stakes. The merchant village of Pinebrook sits on the frontier, far from protective armies. When a patrol discovers a baby silver dragon barely alive, frozen in ice, the party must navigate icy caves, outsmart desperate mercenaries, and ultimately face the dragon's mother — a powerful entity who could be Pinebrook's greatest ally or its doom. The Aevorian Echo appears here as the cause of the unnatural cold freezing the lair: magical weather destabilization spreading from a northern source. Tone: uplifting and heroic, but grounded — frontier towns can't defend themselves, and mercy has consequences. Run this to establish that dragons are people, not just monsters.

## Hook

Captain Emmajeen Kole of Pinebrook's militia (all six of them) approaches the party, exhausted and clearly out of her depth. Three days ago her patrol found something frozen in ice one hour north. She needs help before an adult dragon decides the village is the problem.

{{note
##### Read Aloud — Kole's Briefing
*"Thank you for coming. I'm Captain Kole. We have a situation we can't handle alone. Three days ago, one of our patrols found something frozen in ice about an hour north of here. It's a dragon. A baby dragon. Alive, but barely. And something forced it into that ice. Something dangerous."*

*She pauses. "We have two choices, and they're both terrible. Leave it — and the mother will eventually decide Pinebrook is the problem. Or try to help — and face whatever put it there. I don't have soldiers strong enough. That's why I'm asking you."*
}}

## Key Scenes

### Scene 1: The Frozen Discovery

**Location:** Icy cave complex, 1 hour north of Pinebrook  
**Setup:** The cave mouth is ringed with jagged icicles. Inside, everything is coated in thick ice glowing faint blue-white. The environment is unnaturally cold — deeper cold than autumn should produce. Kole provides cold-weather gear and warns about humanoid tracks inside.

**What happens:**
- Cave entrance: sloping icy floor, DC 12 Acrobatics or Athletics to descend safely (rope/pitons grant advantage)
- Second chamber: three Living Icicles guard a pillar of solid ice. Within the pillar — a baby silver dragon, 10 feet long, silver scales shimmering through the ice, curled in a fetal position
- Freeing Nytha: DC 14 Strength (brute force), fire magic (DC 10 Arcana to melt safely), Dispel Magic (DC 12 Arcana), or tools (DC 11 Dexterity)
- Once freed: Nytha is child-like, confused, trusting. Calls party "rescuer." Speaks Common.
- Optional discovery: a dead dragon egg beneath another ice formation (a sibling who didn't survive)

**Key rolls:**
- DC 12 Arcana: the cold radiating from the cave is magical, not environmental
- DC 10 Insight: Kole is hiding something — uncertainty about what caused this, not deception

**Time pressure:** None yet — but Nytha is urgently distressed and wants to find her mother.

{{note
##### Read Aloud — Nytha Found
*"The slope opens into a vast chamber. In the center, suspended within a pillar of solid ice, is the baby dragon — silver scales catching the light even through the ice, tiny wings wrapped around its body. As the ice gives way, the dragon tumbles out with a surprised cry. It looks up at you with eyes full of wonder and fear. 'M-mother?' it mews. 'Are you... my rescuer?'"*
}}

### Scene 2: The Egg Snatchers

**Location:** Lower lair chamber, while comforting Nytha and heading toward the exit  
**Setup:** Three human mercenaries — hardened but desperate, not evil — emerge from deeper in the cave. A wizard in a city offered them 500 gp for a dragon egg. They are not cackling villains; they're people who need money.

**What happens:**
- Mercenaries freeze when they see the party with the baby dragon; hands move to weapons
- Can fight, negotiate (DC 12 Persuasion / DC 13 Intimidation), or bluff (DC 12 Deception to claim the egg is dead)
- If killed: Nytha is distressed — "Why did you hurt them? They were just trying to survive..." (moral moment)
- If defeated and questioned: reveal they were hired by "a wizard in the city" (future hook)
- Nytha is helpless in combat — party must physically protect her

**Key rolls:**
- Mercenaries: AC 12, HP 18, shortsword +4 to hit; Pack Tactics; flee at 5 HP

**Time pressure:** Nytha grows more agitated the longer they're underground. Her mother is circling above.

{{note
##### Read Aloud — Snatchers Arrive
*"As you prepare to ascend with Nytha, voices echo from deeper in the cave — angry and urgent. 'The egg must be here somewhere! The client will pay 500 gold pieces for a dragon egg. We need that money!'*

*Three figures emerge: hardened mercenaries, weathered and desperate-looking. They freeze when they see you — and the baby dragon. Their hands immediately move to weapons."*
}}

### Scene 3: Rorn and the Reunion

**Location:** Lower lair main chamber  
**Setup:** Rorn (Hysvearorn), an adult silver dragon, fills the chamber — 60 feet of silver scales gleaming like starlight. She was trapped by the unnatural ice and could not protect Nytha. She is dangerous, honorable, and deeply grateful. She will NOT attack unless the party harms Nytha.

**What happens:**
- Rorn bows her head when she sees the party with Nytha — a gesture of genuine respect
- Nytha rushes to her; the reunion is profound and visible
- Rorn's account: "A force of magic — ancient and cold — came from the north. It was not natural. Something has awakened beneath the earth."
- Her dead firstborn: "Lost to the cold before the barrier formed. Nytha was my second; fortune blessed me that she survived." (emotional weight — the Echo is destructive)
- Bahamut's Blessing: Rorn touches a horn to each party member's forehead — +1 to all attack rolls and saving throws for 24 hours; once per day if reduced to 0 HP, regain 5 HP instead

**Key rolls:**
- No checks required — this scene is pure resolution
- DC 10 Insight: Rorn is choosing to trust the party because her child is alive. This is a conscious choice.

**Time pressure:** None — let this breathe.

{{note
##### Read Aloud — Rorn Speaks
*"'I am Rorn, called Hysvearorn in the old tongue. I was trapped in this lair by unnatural ice — magic that froze even my flames. I could not escape to protect my daughter. But you have done what I could not.'*

*She bows her head — actually bows, a gesture of respect. 'For this, you have my eternal gratitude.' Her eyes glow with divine light. She lowers her head and touches her horn gently to your forehead — each of you. You feel warmth, peace, and a sense of being blessed by forces far older than this ruin."*
}}

## Decision Points

- **Choice: Engage or avoid Nytha**
  *Help the dragon:* Adventure proceeds; Rorn becomes a dragon ally; Bahamut's Blessing.
  *Refuse:* Rorn, increasingly desperate, attacks Pinebrook in 2-3 days. Pinebrook is destroyed or severely damaged. Party can receive a desperate messenger as alternate entry.

- **Choice: Handle the egg snatchers**
  *Kill them all:* Nytha is distressed; moral weight is explicit. Future antagonist thread severed.
  *Intimidate or negotiate them away:* They report to their wizard-employer; potential future hook.
  *Capture:* Logistical problem; Pinebrook can hold them; Kole is grateful.

- **Choice: What to do with Rorn's information about "something awakened"**
  *Investigate:* Points directly toward the northern source. Strong hook for The Pale Sickness.
  *File it away:* Elric picks it up in debrief.

## Clues to Plant

- Unnatural cold in cave — geometric frost on walls, too perfect for weather → **Mystery: magical cold spreading from a northern source (Salsvault)**
- Rorn's testimony: "ancient and cold, from the north, something awakened" → **Mystery: Aevorian Echo is regional, not local**
- Dead dragon egg: even protected creatures are not safe → **Mystery: the Echo is actively lethal, not passive**

## Hooks Forward

- Rorn's promise: "If ever you face true evil, speaking Nytha's name will bring aid." — potential future dragon intervention.
- The wizard-employer who hired the mercenaries is still out there; someone is collecting dragon eggs and Aevorian artifacts.
- Elric's debrief notes Pinebrook's unusual cold matches other frontier reports — "something in the north is causing this."

## Escalations

- **If party refuses to enter the cave:** Kole describes sounds of the adult dragon above — increasingly distressed, circling closer. She gives the party a day before she expects the dragon to make a move.
- **If Nytha is harmed:** Rorn's response is nuclear. This ends the adventure as a catastrophic failure. Don't engineer this — let it be a genuine consequence if players choose it.
- **If party wants to fight Rorn:** She has 210 HP, CR 16, and is not going to die to a level 1-3 party. She will subdue and lecture. The point is mercy, not murder.
- **If Living Icicles overwhelm the party:** They're mindless — they don't pursue outside the ritual circle. Party can retreat, rest, and return. Kole can provide additional cold-weather gear.
