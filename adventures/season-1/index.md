## Aevorian Echo Clue Chain

Plant these clues across the season to make the revelation feel earned:

1. **Opening (Waystone):** Geometric frost at creek; humming metal buckle; wolf fur has frost → *Something is wrong in the north*
2. **Wolves of Welton:** Aevorian symbols at Westly's Farm; transmutation magic residue; wolves describe "a light, a surge" → *Ancient ruins; something reactivated*
3. **The Bleating Grimoire:** Corrupted wand; random polymorphs; Elric's debrief → *Magical artifacts failing region-wide; an external frequency*
4. **Peril in Pinebrook:** Rorn's testimony about "ancient cold from the north" → *It's coming from a specific place*
5. **The Awakened Fawn:** Environmental Echo spread through biological absorption; the frost is in the soil, in roots, in anything that grows where frost patterns appear → *The Echo doesn't need artifacts to spread*
6. **The Pale Sickness:** Salsvault — confirmed source; deliberate activation; network of sites → *The revelation*
7. **Temple of the Dragonknights:** Ley line amplification attracts Venomfang; desecrated wards weaken containment → *The consequences are expanding*
