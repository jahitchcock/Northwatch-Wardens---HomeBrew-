## Quick Brief

Comedy palate-cleanser with genuine campaign weight. Finethir Shinebright, a pompous elven Master Transmuter, has been trapped as a sheep for two years by his bitter former apprentice Ahmed Noke, who stole his Wand of True Polymorph. The wand is malfunctioning — transforming things unpredictably rather than as intended — which is proof that Aevorian Echo corruption is destabilizing magical artifacts across Northreach. Tone: absurdist comedy layered over real horror ("what if ALL our magic tools start failing?"). Run this when players need to laugh before the next serious arc.

## Hook

A sheep with wounded dignity and pompous bearing bounds toward the party carrying a scroll in its teeth. Reading the scroll or casting Speak with Animals reveals Finethir Shinebright, Master Transmuter and scholar of the highest order, who is furious, desperate, and extremely put out about being livestock.

## Key Scenes

### Scene 1: The Accidental Messenger

**Location:** Town tavern, inn, or marketplace  
**Setup:** Shinebright has finally found adventurers willing to communicate. He explains his situation with a combination of arrogance, desperation, and occasional bleating.

**What happens:**
- Shinebright explains: Noke stole his Wand of True Polymorph two years ago, polymorphed him into a sheep
- The wand was meant to petrify — it now creates random bizarre transformations instead
- Tower is 2 hours east through forest; Noke has polymorphed guards
- Noke employs a half-orc enforcer named Guz
- Party offer: 100 gp up front + 200 gp on restoration + 1 uncommon magic item from his tower collection
- Shinebright is a "noble liar" — commits to deals he fully intends to keep

**Key rolls:**
- DC 10 Insight: he's genuinely desperate and scared beneath the arrogance — he doesn't think the party is capable, but has no other options
- DC 13 Arcana: "The wand's malfunction is unusual. Standard polymorph spells don't fail like this. Something is interfering with the magic itself."

**Time pressure:** None yet — but Shinebright warns that Noke's paranoia makes him dangerous when cornered.

---

### Scene 2: Guz's Ambush

**Location:** Street or road leaving town  
**Setup:** Guz (half-orc, CR 2) arrives with two intelligent polymorphed wolves and a polymorphed brown bear. He wants the sheep back.

**What happens:**
- Guz's opening: "The sheep is Master Noke's property. He wants it back. NOW. Give it to me, or I take it by force."
- Can fight, intimidate (DC 13), or pay off (50+ gp buys departure)
- If Guz is defeated and interrogated: reveals Noke "hasn't slept in days" and "keeps talking to himself"
- Shinebright after combat: "Well done. We must reach my tower before he learns we're coming."
- If Guz is killed: Shinebright horrified — "I didn't want him dead! He was a victim of Noke's magic too!"

**Key rolls:**
- Guz: AC 14, HP 27, greatsword two attacks, Reckless trait
- Polymorphed wolves: AC 13, HP 11, bite + knockdown (DC 11 Str or prone)
- Polymorphed bear: AC 11, HP 34, claw multiattack

**Time pressure:** If Guz escapes, he warns Noke; adds 1-2 guards to tower.

### Scene 3: Noke's Tower Infiltration

**Location:** Tower compound in forest — three wooden platforms grown from massive oak trees  
**Setup:** 3 apes (polymorphed mercenaries) playing dice at the base. 1 polymorphed brown bear in the outhouse. The tower has malfunctioning magical defenses throughout.

**What happens:**
- Infiltration options: assault, stealth (DC 12), distraction (DC 12 Deception), social engineering
- Ground level: 2 Flying Swords behaving erratically (sometimes attacking each other)
- Second level library: Animated Armor suits, Noke's paranoid notes scattered about
- Optional polymorphed cat butler or squirrel maid who can provide help if freed
- DC 12 Arcana (any level): constructs destabilized by same corruption as the wand

**Key rolls:**
- DC 12 Stealth: bypass ground guards
- DC 12 Arcana: disable magical traps or destabilized spell circles
- DC 10 Investigation: find secret passages or alternate entry

**Time pressure:** Every failed Stealth check may alert more guards. Shinebright becomes increasingly sarcastic about party's competence.

### Scene 4: Ahmed Noke and the Wand

**Location:** Third-level laboratory  
**Setup:** Noke is bloodshot, unkempt, holding a wand that pulses with unstable light. He's been awake for days.

**What happens:**
- Noke monologues about decades of unrecognized service before combat
- Combat: opens with Blur, uses polymorph ray (DC 15 Wis save), Scorching Ray as area denial
- Negotiation possible (DC 12 Persuasion, DC 13 Deception, DC 14 Intimidation)
- Wand backfire chance: on rolls of 1-3 on d20, caster OR random target within 30 ft polymorphs into random beast for 1d4 hours
- Wand explosion path: if used repeatedly, wild magic surge (d8 table: sheep, frog, chicken, wolf, bear, unchanged, flight 1 min, minor benefit)
- Restoration: use wand (DC 12 Arcana), Dispel Magic, or Greater Restoration

**Key rolls:**
- Noke: AC 12, HP 45, CR 3 — Fireball, Scorching Ray, Counterspell, Blur
- Wand backfire DC: roll d20 when wand used; 1-3 = backfire

**Time pressure:** If Noke reaches 0 HP without negotiation, he uses wand as last resort — backfire likely.

## Decision Points

- **Choice: Defeat Noke or negotiate**
  *Defeat and execute:* No future threat. Shinebright restored. Wand destroyed or kept (dangerous).
  *Negotiate:* Noke lives, becomes recurring antagonist with grudge. Shinebright grateful but nervous.
  *Wand explodes:* Noke likely polymorphed. Shinebright may remain sheep temporarily. Must seek alternative cure.

- **Choice: Keep or destroy the corrupted wand**
  *Keep it:* Enforce backfire mechanics strictly every use. It's a liability.
  *Destroy it:* Elric is relieved. "It's too dangerous to exist."
  *Study it (DC 18 Arcana):* Reveals external magical frequency — "something very old and powerful." Points toward Salsvault.

## Clues to Plant

- Wand malfunction: random polymorphs instead of intended petrification → **Mystery: Aevorian Echo corrupts magical artifacts region-wide**
- Noke's notes about "instability growing worse" → **Mystery: the interference is escalating, not fading**
- Elric's debrief (back at Waystone): "Every wizard's tools are becoming unreliable. We need to find the source." → **Sets up Pale Sickness urgency**

## Hooks Forward

- Elric's debrief makes explicit that artifact corruption is regional, not isolated — sets the stakes for The Pale Sickness.
- If Shinebright is restored, he becomes a magical research contact (can Identify items, access his tower library).
- If Noke escapes, he becomes a recurring antagonist with a vendetta.
- The wand can be delivered to Elric for study — he extracts partial information: "an ancient, cold frequency."

## Escalations

- **If party won't engage with the sheep's scroll:** Have Shinebright headbutt the nearest party member and bleat urgently until someone pays attention. Escalate to audible telepathy if needed ("I am not. A. Normal. SHEEP.").
- **If Guz fight drags:** Resolve in 1-2 rounds. Guz flees at 10 HP or fewer. This is a warm-up fight, not the main event.
- **If party goes fully lethal in tower:** Shinebright becomes increasingly uncomfortable. "He was a pawn! Just like I was!" Moral complexity is valid.
- **If wand backfire turns someone into a sheep:** Play it straight. Shinebright: "Yes. That's exactly what it feels like. Now you understand. Can we perhaps retrieve my wand from the man who did this to both of us?"
