## Quick Brief

A roadside encounter that becomes a moral weight. The party rescues a fawn from a direwolf, only to discover the fawn can speak Common — she gained sapience hours ago by eating grass near geometric frost patterns. She is terrified, newly conscious, and has no language for what she is experiencing. This is the campaign's first direct glimpse of the Echo's cost: her father died where the frost touched him. She gives the party his antlers — the earliest documented casualty of ambient Echo spread in Northreach — and flees into the wilderness. Tone: gentle, strange, quietly devastating. This session makes the Echo personal.

## Hook

On the road to Pinebrook: a direwolf chases a spotted fawn across the path. The fawn is small, unsteady, and bleeding from a gash on her flank. The direwolf is large, determined, and closing fast. The party is the only thing between them.

## Key Scenes

### Scene 1: The Chase

**Location:** Northreach Wilderness, road to Pinebrook  
**Setup:** Direwolf (use wolf stats, AC 13, HP 11, CR 1/4) pursues the fawn. The fawn cannot fight. Combat or deterrence is immediate.

**What happens:**
- Direwolf fights if party intervenes; flees if reduced below 5 HP
- If party drives it off without killing it: the wolf retreats, watching from the treeline before vanishing
- The fawn collapses once safe, shaking, unable to stand for 1d4 rounds
- She speaks in fragmented Common — simple words, no grammar, emotion overwhelming structure

**Key rolls:**
- DC 12 Nature: direwolf behavior is unusual — it pursued with *intent*, not hunger
- DC 14 Perception: wolf tracks show it followed the fawn for miles, not opportunistic hunting
- DC 10 Animal Handling: calming the fawn requires patience; she flinches at sudden movements

**Time pressure:** The wolf will kill the fawn in 2 rounds if the party does not act.

---

### Scene 2: The Conversation

**Location:** Roadside clearing  
**Setup:** The fawn recovers enough to speak. This is the heart of the session — discovery, not combat.

**What happens:**
- The fawn explains in fragments: she ate grass near "cold wrong ground" this morning; then she could talk; then she could *think*
- She describes her father's death: "Big antlers. Lay down. Didn't get up. Cold."
- She does not understand death. She does not understand grief. She understands he is gone and she is alone.
- If asked about the frost: "White in the dirt. Pretty. Then... loud. Inside."
- She asks if the party is "like me now" — she has no concept of other sapient beings

**Key rolls:**
- DC 10 Insight: she is hours old as a thinking being; every emotion is raw and immediate
- DC 14 Arcana (if party examines the frost area): faint transmutation aura; geometric crystalline patterning in soil
- DC 12 Medicine: the gash is superficial; the shaking is shock, not injury

**The Gift:**
Before leaving, the fawn walks to a nearby field and returns with **a large set of antlers** — her father's. She nudges them toward the party. She does not have language for "gift" or "memorial." She only knows these were his, and she does not want them to be alone.

{{note

### Scene 3: The Antlers

**Location:** Immediate aftermath  
**Setup:** The party now carries the Father's Antlers. This scene is for investigation and realization.

**What happens:**
- The antlers are large, heavy, and carry a faint geometric frost pattern in the marrow
- Under Detect Magic: faint transmutation aura, crystalline and inward-growing
- DC 14 Arcana or Nature: the bone shows signs of ambient Echo absorption — the frost grew *into* the marrow
- DC 16 Investigation: this pattern matches no known creature in the region; the geometric structure is wrong, mathematical, Aevorian
- The antlers hum faintly when brought near other Aevorian artifacts or sites

**Consequence:** The party carries the earliest documented Echo casualty. Elric will recognize this if shown. The antlers will become narratively significant when the fawn returns.

## Decision Points

- **Choice: Comfort or interrogate the fawn**
  *Comfort:* She opens more, shares the frost location, warns about "cold wrong ground." She leaves calmer. Future encounters are gentler.
  *Interrogate:* She shuts down, frightened by intensity. She still gives the antlers, but flees faster. Future encounters require more trust-building.

- **Choice: Keep or discard the antlers**
  *Keep:* John incorporates them into armor (as written). They become the Antlers of the First Fallen — a homebrew magic item.
  *Discard/leave:* The antlers remain in the wilderness. The fawn may retrieve them later, but the party loses the magic item and the narrative weight.

## Clues to Plant

- Fawn's description of "cold wrong ground": **Mystery: Echo spread is environmental, not just artifact-based**
- Geometric frost in the soil: **Mystery: the frost patterns are Aevorian in structure**
- Father's death from exposure, not violence: **Mystery: the Echo kills through ambient spread, not just direct contact**
- Antlers' crystalline marrow: **Mystery: biological absorption of Echo energy is possible — and lethal**

## Hooks Forward

- **The Fawn Returns:** She will find the party again (see Campaign Bridge Notes). When she sees the antlers in John's armor: *"Those were his."*
- **Elric's Reaction:** When shown the antlers, Elric goes quiet. His model was that artifacts are the primary vector. The fawn breaks his model — environmental spread means the Echo doesn't need carriers anymore.
- **The Direwolf:** Its origin is undefined. Possible answer: Flame's pack, perceiving new sapience as a territorial threat. Hold for future revelation.

## Escalations

- **If party attacks the fawn:** She does not fight back. She dies confused, asking why. This is a valid (horrific) path — play it without comment. Consequences: the antlers are never given; the fawn's story ends here; a future ally becomes a ghost.
- **If party ignores the chase:** The direwolf kills the fawn. The party hears her final word — "Help" — and then silence. The wolf retreats with the body. The antlers are never found.
- **If party tries to keep the fawn:** She refuses. She is not a pet; she is a person, hours old, who does not understand ownership. She will escape at first opportunity. She may return later on her own terms.
