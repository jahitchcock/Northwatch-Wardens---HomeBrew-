## Note on Bleating Grimoire vs Wild Sheep Chase

Two comedy adventures exist in Season 1 — both feature a wizard polymorphed into a sheep:

- **The Bleating Grimoire** — Campaign-original version. NPCs: Vaelora Moonwhisper (elf enchantress, sheep), Theron Blackwood (villain apprentice), Krull (half-orc enforcer). Uses Suggestion/Misty Step spell set.
- **The Wild Sheep Chase** — Adapted from a published one-shot. NPCs: Finethir Shinebright (high elf transmuter, sheep), Ahmed Noke (villain apprentice), Guz (half-orc enforcer). Uses Fireball/Scorching Ray spell set.

Run one or both depending on campaign pacing needs. They serve the same arc purpose and are interchangeable as palate-cleansers.

## Note on The Pale Sickness / Frozen Sick

These are the same adventure under two names:
- **Frozen Sick** is the original module from *Explorer's Guide to Wildemount*
- **The Pale Sickness** is the campaign-integrated rename

`the-pale-sickness.md` contains the full table-reader content. `frozen-sick.md` is a reference stub pointing to it.

## Aevorian Echo Clue Chain

Plant these clues across the season to make the revelation feel earned:

1. **Opening (Waystone):** Geometric frost at creek; humming metal buckle; wolf fur has frost → *Something is wrong in the north*
2. **Wolves of Welton:** Aevorian symbols at Westly's Farm; transmutation magic residue; wolves describe "a light, a surge" → *Ancient ruins; something reactivated*
3. **Peril in Pinebrook:** Rorn's testimony about "ancient cold from the north" → *It's coming from a specific place*
4. **Bleating Grimoire / Wild Sheep Chase:** Corrupted wand; random polymorphs; Elric's debrief → *Magical artifacts failing region-wide; an external frequency*
5. **The Pale Sickness:** Salsvault — confirmed source; deliberate activation; network of sites → *The revelation*
6. **Temple of the Dragonknights:** Ley line amplification attracts Venomfang; desecrated wards weaken containment → *The consequences are expanding*
