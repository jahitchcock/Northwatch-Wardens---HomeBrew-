## Quick Brief

The campaign's inciting incident. The party discovers that wolves plaguing Welton village are not animals — they're sapient, awakened overnight by a magical surge that also killed a missing sorcerer. The adventure is fundamentally a moral dilemma: exterminate intelligent beings, negotiate a treaty, or deceive both sides. Every path has lasting consequences. The Aevorian Echo makes its first real appearance here — the wolves are a symptom of something ancient reactivating in the north. Tone: grounded mystery escalating into moral weight. This is the adventure that makes players ask "what kind of people are we?"

## Hook

The Northwatch Wardens contract W-17: 800 gp to eliminate wolves plaguing Welton. Party arrives in a valley town with anxious faces and a desperate council.

## Key Scenes

### Scene 1: The Hunted Road

**Location:** Road to Welton, 150 feet from Welton valley  
**Setup:** 6–8 wolves attack two shepherds and their flock. Party is 150 feet away and can intervene. The wolves fight with tactical coordination, not animal aggression.

**What happens:**
- Wolves prioritize sheep first, then party when threatened
- Pack Tactics: wolves flank constantly
- At 3+ wolves killed, pack morale breaks; survivors flee toward the western woods
- Shepherds (Davol and Wren) are grateful guides to Welton afterward

**Key rolls:**
- DC 10 Insight: wolf attacks show planning, not blind hunger
- DC 12 Nature: "This level of coordination is unusual for normal wolves"
- If wolves are examined after death: scorch marks, strange markings, faint magical aura

**Time pressure:** Shepherds will die if party doesn't act. Combat is immediate.

### Scene 2: Welton and the Council

**Location:** Shepherd's Crook Inn, Welton  
**Setup:** The village council is already in session arguing about export quotas vs. starvation. Party's arrival is welcome relief.

**What happens:**
- Council gives full briefing: 3+ weeks of attacks, escalating, organized
- Missing shepherd: Featherock recovered at the inn — he heard wolves argue in Common
- Missing sorcerer Alexi Merriksonn (Father Merriksonn's brother) vanished when attacks started
- Corel (shepherd, council member) offers to guide the party to the wolf territory
- Council vote mechanics established: Merriksonn YES, Corel LIKELY YES, Tillus Merrion SWING

**Key rolls:**
- DC 12 Arcana: someone suggests "magical tampering"
- DC 12 Persuasion (Featherock): he confesses he heard wolves *debate* whether to eat him

**Time pressure:** Village is economically collapsing. Council needs action "soon."

### Scene 3: Seeking the Pack

**Location:** Western woods and approaches  
**Setup:** Open scene — party chooses their method. Corel is available as guide (+4 to Survival checks).

**What happens:**
- Option A: Direct Tracking (Survival DC 14, DC 10 with Corel)
- Option B: Bait trap with Corel's flock — wolves come in 1–2 hours
- Option C: Westly's Farm investigation — finds buried Aevorian symbols
- Optional: Wounded owlbear encounter (DC 12 Nature: the wolves attacked it — they fight apex predators now)
- Arrival: wolf den has decorations, crude drawings, organized camp — "it looks almost like a home"

**Key rolls:**
- DC 13 Arcana (Featherock's ambush site): "Transmutation magic was cast here — recently"
- DC 15 Arcana (Westly's Farm): symbols match pre-Fall Aevorian designs
- DC 13 Stealth: approach den undetected; overhear Bolt and Flame arguing inside

**Time pressure:** If players stall on tracking, Corel offers directly: "Set a bait trap. We'll draw them in."

### Scene 4: The Den — Parley or Combat

**Location:** Wolf den cave complex  
**Setup:** This is the heart of the adventure. Party meets Bolt (blue-glowing eyes, calm, formal) and Flame (orange fire-marks, aggressive, contemptuous). The choice of combat or negotiation determines the world consequence.

**What happens:**
- Bolt speaks first if party approaches stealthily
- Bolt explains: Alexi came through, pack defended territory, a magical surge changed them overnight
- Bolt's proposals: relocation OR territory treaty (western woods for protection from predators)
- Flame challenges everything Bolt proposes — tension between them is real
- If party mentions Featherock: Bolt shows genuine regret ("It was mercy, by our standards")
- Council vote path: party returns to village and must convince 3+ YES votes

**Key rolls:**
- DC 12 Insight: Bolt and Flame fundamentally disagree — their conflict could break the pack
- Council persuasion: DC 12–15 depending on argument (economic appeal reaches Tillus)

**Wolf stats (if combat):**
- Standard wolves: AC 13, HP 5, CR 1/8 — pack tactics
- Bolt & Flame: AC 14, HP 47, CR 2 — Bolt has Lightning Breath, Flame has Fire Breath (recharge 5-6)
- Bolt calls ceasefire at 50% HP: "This benefits neither of us!"

**Time pressure:** Flame will force combat if party stalls. Bolt: "I await your decision. Though my sister grows impatient."

## Decision Points

- **Choice: Kill, negotiate, or deceive**
  *Kill all wolves:* 800 gp immediately. Father Merriksonn quietly disappointed. No future wolf allies. Mystery "what caused this?" remains open.
  *Negotiate treaty:* 800 gp after 30-day probation. Father Merriksonn gives Alexi's spellbook. Bolt becomes a one-time future ally (Flame remains resentful).
  *Deceive village:* 800 gp immediately. Father Merriksonn makes DC 12 Insight check. If he catches the lie, relationship becomes complicated.

- **Choice: Believe Featherock or dismiss him**
  *Believe him:* He recovers faster; becomes a credibility source if party negotiates with wolves. "See? I wasn't mad."
  *Dismiss him:* No bonus when wolves first speak. He stays traumatized.

## Clues to Plant

- Featherock's testimony (wolves argued in words): **Mystery: magical awakening caused by external force, not natural**
- Alexi's disappearance matching attack timeline: **Mystery: Alexi triggered or encountered whatever caused the surge**
- Aevorian symbols at Westly's Farm: **Mystery: ancient ruins in the region; something was buried here**
- Transmutation magic residue at ambush site: **Mystery: Aevorian magic specifically, not any current school**
- Bolt's description of "a light, a surge": **Mystery: Salsvault activation — will be confirmed in The Pale Sickness**

## Escalations

- **If party can't decide**: Flame forces the issue. She attacks or threatens to attack. Bolt shouts "Hold!" — but the party has to respond now.
- **If council vote stalls at tie**: Father Merriksonn and Corel both vote YES. Party needs one more. Tillus is the swing. If truly tied 3–3, Corel makes a final passionate speech and tie breaks for treaty.
- **If party wants to kill all wolves including pups**: Bolt and Flame fight to the death. Every wolf. This is a valid (dark) path — play it without comment.
- **If party has no way to communicate with wolves**: Any basic magic (Speak with Animals scroll, found on road shepherd Davol, or purchased from Leanor) works. If truly no option, Bolt addresses the party in broken Common unprompted.
