## The Aevorian Echo Thread

All entities connected to the Echo — organized by how/when the connection is revealed.

### Echo-Connected Locations

- **Waystone Inn** — The standing stone is a pre-Fall Aevorian communication beacon, reactivating; inn foundations contain Aevorian metal and crystal; Elric has secretly documented resonance events; the old well connects to intact Aevorian construction beneath the building
- **Westly's Farm** — Sits above a sealed Aevorian biological research station whose automated systems are reactivating; Westly is being passively "marked" by residual magic; the water has tasted metallic for weeks
- **Welton (old woods, north)** — A partially buried Aevorian structure with biological research materials; the wolves are Aevorian experimental subjects being directed through a device here; a controller has established a base here
- **Pinebrook** — Active dead drop points for Blackwake Corsair operatives; an abandoned logging camp sits above an Aevorian structure entrance; the Echo's influence is indirect, flowing through the conspiracy's supply chain
- **Palebank Village** — Collection point for Aevorian artifacts; frigid woe is an Aevorian bioweapon reactivated from Salsvault; pre-Calamity stonework discovered beneath the village (Olan Mesk's secret)
- **Salsvault** — Most concentrated physical Echo manifestation in Season 1; ancient Aevorian biological research facility that developed anti-divine bioweapons; its command center is attempting to communicate with other Aevorian sites; connects to a larger "northern drop-off facility" beyond it
- **Temple of Dragonknights** — Aevorian research complex sealed beneath; focused on draconic bioweapons; the Dragonknights built a containment structure over it; the Frostfire Clan maintained the seals for a millennium
- **Noke's Tower** — Shinebright is a dedicated Aevorian researcher with a basement archive, captured artifacts, biological samples, and countermeasure prototypes; the tower's observatory monitors Aevorian activation events across the region
- **Bleached Reach / Delta-7** — Aevorian coastal monitoring station, reactivating after 9,000 years; contains 9,000-year-old surveillance recordings of Salsvault; one of twelve connected observation nodes
- **Sunken Archive** — Aevor's central knowledge repository; guarded by Archivist Yssara for 1,000 years; contains the Codex of Ascension and AEON-12, the Archivist node of the Echo consciousness
- **Ember Crag** — Frostfire Clan stronghold; the Frostfire Weapon broadcasts to the Temple site and other facilities; draws power from the Echo network
- **Nullwood Expanse (border)** — The Silent Court's magical silence is an active Echo-suppression field built into ancient Aevorian wardings; the wardings are showing strain as the Echo strengthens

### Echo Discovery Chain

Ordered by when players encounter each Echo clue across Season 1 → Season 2:

1. **Opening** → Geometric frost on creek stones that doesn't melt; humming torn cloth (magical resonance); wolves with geometric frost on fur — the Echo is spreading north to south
2. **Wolves of Welton** → Aevorian symbols at Westly's Farm; transmutation magic residue at ambush site; Bolt describes "a light, a surge" — something old is active underground
3. **The Bleating Grimoire / Wild Sheep Chase** → Corrupted wand malfunctioning due to external frequency; "something very old and powerful" — the Echo is destabilizing magical artifacts region-wide; Elric: "Every wizard's tools are becoming unreliable"
4. **The Pale Sickness** → Frigid woe vials are Aevorian in origin; Salsvault is the source; activation logs show manual startup 2–3 months ago by a hooded non-human figure; references to "Site Theta," "Site Vharos," "Site 9-B" — Salsvault is one node in a larger network; Elric's revelation: "Someone turned it on deliberately"
5. **Temple of the Dragonknights** → The temple was built to *contain* an Aevorian facility beneath; Dragonknight tombs are ward anchors; a draconic bioweapon research complex is sealed below; the Dragonknight spirits know the sealing methods
6. **The Bleached Reach** (S2) → Delta-7 monitoring station reveals the party has been observed since Season 1; twelve observation posts are connected; the Temple site is a marked target; "Aevor ascended rather than fell"; players are marked as "assets"
7. **The Sunken Archive** (S2) → AEON-12 reveals: the Echo is a distributed consciousness across multiple worlds; twelve nodes exist; Aevor wanted to become gods; the Command Center node is what players have been tracking; the Dragonknights were Aevorian test subjects from the beginning
8. **Wolves of the Frozen Fire** (S2) → Frostfire Clan are Project: Frostfire survivors; the Temple was their home for a millennium; the Wardens' Season 1 investigation broke the seals the Clan maintained; the Frostfire Weapon's Phase Two reveals the same intelligence coordinates all sites

## Secret Threads (DM Only)

### Thread: The Informant Network

Brenna Thorne's past informant (who got 4 wardens killed) still operates in the region under a different name. Mara Fenwick has criminal contacts from her past as a guild accountant for a thieves' organization. Darvin Crook (Welton's Shepherd's Crook innkeeper) unknowingly sells village news to a southern contact. At least one Waystone Inn staff member is a Corsair informant; a stable hand reports on arrivals and departures. Kaelith was once a Warden contact inside the Frostfire Clan.

### Thread: The Warden Leadership's Hidden Truth

- **Brenna Thorne** was born Brenna Ashcroft; fled after uncovering military intelligence corruption; founded the Guild partly as a response mechanism for an escalating Aevorian threat she has never named aloud; maintains encrypted correspondence with southern intelligence contacts
- **Mara Fenwick** embezzled funds from a thieves' organization to help establish the Wardens; her old organization may still be looking for her; maintains coded underworld contacts tracking Aevorian artifact movement
- **Elric Vael** was expelled from the Cobalt Soul for forbidden Aevorian research; holds smuggled Cobalt Soul texts; suspects an entity that survived Aevor's fall is deliberately reactivating ancient systems; has been understating his certainty to Brenna to force faster action

### Thread: Aevorian Contamination Among Wardens

- **Takk Oaksplitter** participated in a mercenary raid that recovered Aevorian artifacts, resulting in scholars' deaths; recognizes artifacts surfacing in the campaign; haunted by a dying researcher's last words
- **Baleth Cindermoon** (Lorewatch warlock, not yet introduced by name) has a patron that is an Aevorian entity that survived the Great Fall — and that patron is causing the Aevorian Echo; Baleth is beginning to suspect this but is addicted to the power and bound by pact
- Multiple field Wardens (Corel, Galvena, Ariodh, Guz, Shinebright, Father Merriksonn) each have prior direct exposure to Aevorian magic carrying trauma, knowledge, or hidden capabilities

### Thread: The Buyer's True Identity

The Buyer serves a larger power whose goals align with the Aevorian reactivation agenda. The Loremaster of the Archivists of Aevor (Kethis Vraal) has been secretly corresponding with the same anonymous buyer and rationalizing it as "funded patronage." The Unmasked Parliament's Architect has been hired to construct something using specifications that match Aevorian design principles — the commission traces back to the same entity driving the Echo. The Buyer's specific identity and faction is a Season 2+ reveal.

### Thread: The Croaker Cult's Patron

Hulil Lutan knows the cult's patron and has begun having strategic doubts about the endgame — the operation is larger than she was told. ### Thread: The Frostfire Clan's Grievance

The Frostfire Clan are Aevorian Project: Frostfire survivors — Dragonknights who were captured after the Great Fall and subjected to draconic modification. The Temple of Dragonknights was their home for a millennium. The Wardens' Season 1 investigation at the Temple broke the seals the Clan had maintained, waking the Echo from their perspective. Brenna Thorne has known about the Clan for years and chose not to act. Kaelith was once a Warden informant. Varyx's "birthright" claim over the Temple has legal merit — the Wardens did investigate the site.
